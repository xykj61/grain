## You can begin where you are

Grain is a computer that answers to you. Your words stay on your machine. A **citizen** here is any person who wants that, roughly from the middle-school years upward, in any country, with any careful companion model they already trust.

The public copy is the one a stranger clones. The first hour begins with:

```sh
git clone https://github.com/grain-os/grain.git
```

That line is already a shipped step, with its own checked ending, in [the first hour](../../tutorials/the-first-hour.md). This page adds no second command. It tells you what the work is for, so the hour has a reason before it has a toolchain.

The bound is simple, and TAME would write it the same way: the public copy carries the teaching and the programs. It carries no one's private name, no one's key, and no one's money. Real payment, real identity, and publishing that public copy stay with the person who holds them.

## Four words for how a small thing ships

[Simple, Lovable, Complete](../../../context/SIMPLE_LOVABLE_COMPLETE.md) is how Grain lets a thing into the world.

**Simple** means one promise, drawn small enough to finish. **Lovable** means a person is glad to hold it. **Complete** means the promise closes: what it says it does, it does, inside the edge it drew.

Civic style asks what that rewards. A small finished thing rewards the person who kept the promise. A large unfinished thing rewards the announcement. Grain rewards the first.

## Two products, one set of facts

**Linengrow** is the side a person stands on. You offer a small valuable record, you say what use you allow, you see the return that was promised, and you can carry the whole record away. The ladder is [the Linengrow itinerary](../../../construction/LINENGROW_ITINERARY.md). The first whole on that ladder is a receipt you can read: what, who, why, value, and until when, on one card.

**Dimeroll** is the books beside that receipt. The same signed facts become a journal a steward can follow. Offer, consent, use, return, expiration, and correction stay distinct. Dimeroll holds no money and opens no payment rail. The ladder is [the Dimeroll itinerary](../../../construction/DIMEROLL_ITINERARY.md).

The two meet on purpose and stay apart on purpose. One source of facts. Two meanings. A person in Kenya and a person in Kansas read the same card, because the card is about consent and value, and the local law of money stays in that person's own country.

## Fair trade, in this tree and in the world

On the [front door](../../../README.md), **Fair Trade** means the hands and the land behind a thing are treated as well as the thing itself. That is Grain's own sentence, and it is adoptable anywhere the sentence is true.

The world's Fair Trade seals belong to their own bodies. Grain can keep a record of a claim. A record is a join between what was required and what a cooperative actually holds, which is the exploration in [the fair-trade certificate](../../../active-designing/date/20260812/20260812-193727_ayre-journey7-fair-trade-exploration.md). A seal on a bag is that certifier's work. Using Grain does not print their mark.

What the choice rewards: a buyer who can see the hands, and a producer whose certificate cannot float free of the trade it names.

## Biocyclic vegan ecology, and the United States

Grain's own name for the land-ethic is **Atthowe ecological**: fertility from plants, compost, green manures, and the rotation itself. Its kin in the wider world is biocyclic vegan agriculture. The place-neutral page is [Every Climate Has a Fiber](../../../foundations/20260824-003828_every-climate-has-a-fiber.md). The crop changes with the climate. The care does not.

The Biocyclic Vegan Standard is a public global standard. CERES, a control body, describes it as available worldwide since November 2017 and accredited by IFOAM. The publisher's own door is [biocyclic-vegan.org](https://www.biocyclic-vegan.org/about-us/). Grain does not issue that seal.

In the United States, a coordinator site, [Biocyclic Vegan Agriculture Certification USA](https://biocyclicagricultureusa.com/farmers), describes applications for farms already working without animal inputs, and a public note in February 2026 named New York and California as the first two states. That is their announcement. A farmer checks their door and the standard's own door. A reader in another country asks the network partner for that place, or begins the practice with no seal at all. The ethic travels. The certificate is local.

What the choice rewards: soil fed by what the field can return, and a receipt that can say what went into the ground. The reward is the soil and the record together. A badge alone rewards the badge.
