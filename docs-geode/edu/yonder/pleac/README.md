This room is where the Glow standard library eats its own cooking. Each page states a small, real task; a desk in the page's chapter folder does it; a witness in `tools/` proves it; and the page ends with the exact green line a pilot's screen should say. Recipes are numbered by chapter and land only proven -- a page without its green line is a draft, never a resident. Findings belong on the page: when a recipe discovers a seam or a missing spoon in the stdlib, the page names it plainly, because the cookbook's second job is telling the kitchen what to build next.

**The laws of the room.** One page, one recipe. Desks keep the comment-column law and the stem law of the runner they ride. Witnesses assert positive and negative space. Chapters accrete; recipes never rewrite -- a better way lands as a new numbered recipe beside its elder.

**Gratitude.** The cookbook's shape honors the elder PLEAC project -- the Programming Language Examples Alike Cookbook -- and the Perl Cookbook tradition it grew from, both named here with thanks; every recipe in this room is our own, written fresh against our own stdlib.

*May every page end green, every finding feed the kitchen, and the cookbook grow one honest bite at a time.*
