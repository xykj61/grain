## The Name -- a Slate of Three, One Lean

**Cloverfold** -- the lean, offered with its reasons said once. Clover is the meadow's quiet regenerator, feeding the soil it grows in -- ecological to the root and universal on every continent. And *fold* is both the pasture's gathering place and this tree's oldest law made visible: **the app's standing is literally a fold over the log**, so the name teaches the architecture every time it is spoken. Beside it on the slate: **Meadowkeep** (stewardship in one breath, rhyming with *kindness that keeps*) and **Fernwake** (the old green unfurling, a wake of growth). All three stand clean in the tree; the seat is Keaton's; Cloverfold serves as working title until the word.

## What Cloverfold Is, in One Breath

A small Pool app where **makers receive, kindness routes, and merit keeps**: a vegan maker's page, a sanctuary's door, a seva host's calendar -- and every enacted kindness at any of the three becomes a two-handed fact on MUR (was MALA), folded into standing, pinned by WOV at a nib, and steerable toward more kindness. It is the merit ledger wearing clothes, the onboarding walk with a destination, and the whole Glow userland exercised in one lovable toy that is not a toy.
