## The press voice

**The voice is Kyri**, as everywhere else in this tree. Two pieces here still named **Riyo** and two
named **Quin** as of `20260827`; both are elder chapters of the same voice, and the pages that
recorded them were correct on the day they were written. Living Voice lines read Kyri now, and
dated bodies keep every word.

**The register is the radiant twilight zone**, seated `20260827` on Keaton's word. It is Radiant
Style at dusk -- everything Radiant asks, kept, and the light lowered: calm, spacious, warm, and
never gloom. The full guide is [`TWILIGHT_STYLE.md`](../context/TWILIGHT_STYLE.md), and its motion
is a pendulum -- a line that **aims** and names the essence, then a line that **dissolves** and lets
the image open. A press piece written this way trusts the reader to lean in rather than reaching out
to grab them, which is the difference between an announcement and an advertisement.

The register suits this room for a plain reason. Everything here speaks to someone meeting this work
for the first time, about something that took a long time and was made carefully. Brightness would
oversell it. Dusk lets the thing be its own size.

## Where the register stops, and why

**A page a lawyer or a family must read first keeps its plain daylight.** Two pieces in this room
are gated on a human hand -- the Siya Fund LLC founding announcement waits on the Wyoming filing and
an attorney's read, and the Livermore name announcement waits on a Nevada court's order and a family
reading it aloud together. Both carry bracketed slots a person fills.

Those pages stay in their plain register until the humans who are gated on them have read them.
Changing how a legal announcement *sounds* while it waits for counsel would put a style pass ahead
of the review it exists to receive, and the register can follow the approval rather than lead it.
Twilight's own guidance already says as much: spend the benediction where it is earned, and let
ordinary and technical pieces end plainly.

## The one rule this room never bends

**Nothing leaves without a hand.** A custody gate stands between every page here and the world, and
every agent stops at it. A press release is the one artifact whose whole purpose is to be believed by
people who take it on trust, so the person it speaks for is the person who sends it.
