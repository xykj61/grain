## Siya Fund PBC Launches as a Mother-and-Son Public Benefit Company, Leading the Seed Round of Grain Energy PBC

**[City, Wyoming]** — Siya Fund PBC, a Wyoming for-profit public benefit corporation, announced its founding today by **Sara Livermore, Founder and Chief Executive Officer**, and **Keaton Dunsford, Founder and Chief Technology Officer**. The fund takes its name from **Siya** — a beloved name of Sita — and carries *seva*, selfless service, as its stated public benefit: patient capital for regenerative, plant-kingdom-kind, open-source civic technology.

As its first act, Siya Fund PBC will serve as the **lead seed investor in Grain Energy PBC** (grain.energy), the Wyoming public benefit company building Grain — an open-source computing project whose work is developed in public, witnessed line by line, and licensed for every hand. "The reward should point at the good," said Livermore. "We are structuring both companies so that what we measure and what we cherish are the same thing." Dunsford added: "Every kindness in our systems is a fact that keeps. Siya Fund exists so the builders of those systems can eat while they build them."

Both companies are organized under Wyoming's for-profit public benefit corporation chapter (W.S. Title 17, Chapter 31), which asks a company to name its public benefit in its articles and to balance that benefit alongside shareholder value. Siya Fund's benefit statement, investment criteria, and annual benefit reporting will be published openly at **siya.fund**.

**About Siya Fund PBC** — a family-founded public benefit investor supporting open-source, ecological, and service-first technology. **About Grain Energy PBC** — the public benefit company behind Grain, at grain.energy, on Instagram and X at @grain.energy and @grain_energy, built in the open with the groupproject36 collective.
