## Keaton Sealy Livermore: a Founder Takes His Family's Founding Name

**[City]** — The co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of Seva Fund PBC will carry the name **Keaton Sealy Livermore**, following a legal name change granted in the State of Nevada. He takes his place beside his mother and co-founder, **Sara Sealy Livermore**, Chief Executive Officer of Seva Fund PBC — a mother-and-son public benefit company whose very name means selfless service.

The name reaches back on purpose. **Livermore** carries the family line to Livermore Falls, Maine, where the founding family stands in the town's own story. **Sealy** carries it to Sealy, Texas, and the founding family whose name the town bears — a heritage the family holds today as equity stewards of the Sealy family's energy interests. "A name is the shortest story a family tells," said Livermore. "We chose to tell ours whole."

The family's technology work carries one more quiet tribute: the short name of the Mycelium contract language in the Grain project — **Myc** — honors Michael, with love.

Seva Fund PBC and Grain Energy PBC continue their work in the open at **seva.fund**, **seva.center**, and **grain.energy**.
