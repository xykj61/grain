# Code of Conduct

**Language:** EN · **Style:** Radiant (see [`context/RADIANT_STYLE.md`](context/RADIANT_STYLE.md))
**Status:** Living · **Kin:** [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [`context/CIVIC_STYLE.md`](context/CIVIC_STYLE.md)

Grain is a custody-first, civic project. It is built so that ordinary people — families, small collectives, civic hands — can keep their own words in their own hands. The way we treat each other here is meant to match the software: plain, honest, and kind.

## The standard we hold

- **Be kind, and assume kindness.** Read the most generous version of what someone wrote before you answer. A question is not an attack; a mistake is not a betrayal. The tree ends its own prose with *thank you everyone* for a reason.
- **Be honest, and say why.** State what is true, name a gap when you find one, and give the reason behind a strong claim — the same discipline the code holds ([`context/TWO_ROOMS.md`](context/TWO_ROOMS.md)). Honesty told warmly is the whole standard.
- **Prefer *yet* to *but*.** Disagree with the idea, never diminish the person. Contrast that builds is welcome; contempt is not.
- **Protect the newcomer.** The person on their first day is exactly who this project is for. Explain the coined word, link the reference, and leave the door plainer than you found it.
- **Respect custody and consent.** Do not share another person's private information, keys, or identity. What belongs to someone is theirs to give, never yours to take.

## What is not welcome

Harassment, demeaning or discriminatory comments, personal attacks, sustained disruption, unwelcome attention, sharing others' private information, and any conduct a reasonable person would read as designed to make a participant feel unsafe. None of it belongs here, in any venue where the project gathers.

## Scope

This applies wherever the project's work happens — the repositories, issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any space a participant is representing Grain.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness conduct that breaks this standard, report it privately to the maintainer. Because this is a young project with a single named maintainer ([`context/THREATS.md`](context/THREATS.md) is honest that this is a single point of trust), the quiet channels are the surest: a private security advisory on the repository, or a direct message to the maintainer through the contacts named in the project's identity records. A report is read by a person, and held in confidence to the extent the situation allows.

## How it is handled

The maintainer will read the report, listen to those involved, and respond in proportion — a word, a request to stop, an edit, or removal from the project's spaces when that is what safety requires. As the project grows past one hand, this section grows with it: the succession and stewardship questions are open and named ([`context/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`](context/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)), and a fuller enforcement process will be seated rather than assumed.

## With thanks

This page owes its shape to the many open communities that wrote their conduct down first — the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) most of all. We keep our own voice here, yet the debt is real.

*May everyone who arrives be met the way the grain runs — whole, plain, and kind.*
