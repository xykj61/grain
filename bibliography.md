## What This Is

Every outside teacher the Grain has learned from, gathered in one root home: who they are, what their gift became in our hands, and where their own words rest whole. The silo rule governs the whole file — a teacher's ideas enter the body of our work re-voiced and made ours, while the teacher's own words stay unaltered in `gratitude/`, and their names appear in the work only in thanks. This bibliography is the map of that gratitude.

Human orientation stays at [`MAP.md`](MAP.md). Filing law stays at [`ORGANIZING.md`](ORGANIZING.md). The shelf of unaltered sources stays at [`gratitude/`](gratitude/README.md).
