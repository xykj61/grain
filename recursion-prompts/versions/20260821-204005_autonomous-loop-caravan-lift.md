## What changed in this filling, and what did not

**The route did not move.** The Microkernel Target double-seat still leads, and the Caravan ladder is still the work in front of it. What changed is the **vocabulary the work is written in**, and one thing the loop now owns that it did not before.

**Three things changed, all of them law rather than direction.**

1. **The mark law is seated, and it forbids the loop's most habitual gesture.** A lap is marked by a **one-clock stamp and a plain name** -- never an ascending letter. The ladder work continues, and the word for it is **`lift`**, not `fold`: `fold` keeps the filing sense (a room folding to `date/YYYYMMDD/`), and lifting a family of rungs out of a ladder is its own act with its own word. **Do not open `fold AJ`.** Open *the lift of whatever family the queue names*, and let the commit subject carry the name the way it always has -- `caravan: lift the courier cluster, eleven at once` needed no letter to be clear. Count the lifts when a count is wanted: `git log --oneline --grep="caravan: lift" | wc -l`. Law: [`../../.claude/rules/stamp-and-name.md`](../../.claude/rules/stamp-and-name.md).

2. **The carry meter reads two numbers now.** `carry` counts lines; `CARRY_DELEGATES` counts the three-line forwarders a lift leaves behind -- 2,365 of them as of this filling. Carry alone can be satisfied by **moving** lines rather than removing them, and a meter that can be satisfied by relocation will eventually be satisfied that way. Read both. When carry falls and delegates hold, lines genuinely left the tree; when both move together, the lap traded a cost for a cheaper-looking one, and the round should say so plainly rather than claim the fall.

3. **Seventeen guards stand, and they are yours to run.** Run them at the head of a cold start rather than trusting them: **a guard earns confidence by being run, not by being written** -- two of the newest were red the first time they were asked. The roster and what each holds is on the operator card under *Standing equipment*.
