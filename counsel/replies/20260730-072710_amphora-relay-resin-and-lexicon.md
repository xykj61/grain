## How the Resin Persists

Counsel's own working directory resets between sessions, so files cannot be held here. The resin therefore persists by parity rather than by storage, exactly as Amphora would have it. Three carriers hold it together: the **baton** grows a `resin` limb that restates the full pending set every pass, so no single lost paste loses the list; **memory** holds the practice and the roster's shape across conversations; and each pass **re-mints** any bead the bundle needs, since re-minting a document from a stated shape costs little and guessing costs correctness.

## The Equinox-Close Protocol

At the close of each equinox the resin crosses whole. The bundle is cut under the discipline sheet: basis naming its pier and both hashes, cut home-side with the exit status captured before any pipe, verified where it was built, copied to the mount as its own motion, and a stamped manifest written beside it. The manifest names every bead, its class, its destination, and the word standing behind any personal-lane bead. The far side verifies before fetching, proves each living document's elder text is a prefix of the incoming form, lands the workshop beads at their homes, holds the personal beads unless a word covers them, runs the witnesses on metal, and reports verbatim in the baton shape. Each bead that lands leaves the pending roster on the next baton, and the resin empties honestly.
