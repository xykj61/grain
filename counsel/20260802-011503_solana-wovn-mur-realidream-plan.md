## Why this plan exists

Ship identity already leans on **Solana `.sol` SNS** as an external liveness proof (`active-designing/20260729-231500_fund-star-ship-and-the-myc-anchor.md`). Micropayments and dual-chain settlement need a named Grain shape before any contract byte lands. This plan names that shape under TAME: bounds first, witnesses first, custody last.
