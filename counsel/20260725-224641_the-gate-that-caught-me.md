## What the Witness Cannot See

Running the scan by hand surfaced something worth recording, because it changes how much weight a green may carry.

The amended pass passed `claim_preserve` while still silently dropping two facts: **"eight" generator hops** and **the `OTHER_SENSORS` emission**. Neither registers, because the extractor's `NUM` class matches digits and never spelled-out numerals, and its `PROPER` class requires a capital followed by lowercase, so `ALL_CAPS_IDENTIFIERS` are invisible to it. Counsel restored both by reading the diff — a hand, not a gate. This is the assumption in `THREATS.md` doing its work: *a green witness means what its assertions say, and no more.*

Two candidate ratchets follow, and both are proposals rather than steps in the relay, since widening a blocking gate wants its own deliberate lap with a known-good fixture beside a known-bad one. Add a `WORD` token class for spelled-out numerals from *one* through *twenty*. Add an `IDENT` class for `[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{3,}` runs. Until they exist, the honest posture is the one this lap just demonstrated: a style pass over any page carrying counts or identifiers still earns a human read of the diff, and the witness catches what it catches.
