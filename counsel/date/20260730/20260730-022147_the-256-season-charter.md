## The Shape

Each journey turns one full Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle across its four quests, with the leverage lens applied at Plan:

## Meters Protocol

Every counsel codeblock carries the meter line: sundial with its band and its door, fascia with its grade and delta. Pinned readings say pinned; fresh readings say fresh; the counsel never invents a number. Fascia entered the season at **72, down fourteen from 86** — so the season opens with a recovery emphasis: journey scopes stay narrow until fascia stands at 80 or better, and the Act quest of every journey asks whether the delta moved the right way. ## Reds Under Toyota

A red is an andon pull. The line stops, the three fields are written — what went wrong, what caught it, what it taught — and the allocation books before the next round opens, reds-first. A red that repeats earns a five-whys pass down to root cause. Every red that teaches a mechanical lesson accretes a poka-yoke witness, beginning with `reds_ledger_monotone` from red 30. The three wastes are scanned each Act quest: muda in tokens and laps spent without landing, mura in uneven batching between counsel and bench, muri in any journey scoped past what fascia can carry.

## Compute and the Fair Exchange

Counsel compute goes where its leverage is highest — design, writing, judgment, packaging — and mechanical laps flow to the bench, which runs local and unmetered. The working ceiling is two counsel sessions per journey, open and close, paired into one for light journeys: roughly 64 to 128 counsel sessions across the whole season, against 256 rounds of bench work. Heavy drafting batches at journey boundaries; meters stay pinned between fresh runs; every artifact is written once and accreted. This is the fairest exchange the counsel can name under Keaton's budget: the expensive mind spends itself only on what only it can do. The **Murr Mand** reading — Mur-constellation economics joined with the three M — stands parked for Keaton's correction.
