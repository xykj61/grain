## One — Maze and Rhyz: Kinship Without Collision

The two names rhyme in imagery — both branch, both wander, both grow sideways. Their topologies tell them apart. A **rhizome** has no center and no dead end: cut it anywhere and life continues, which is exactly the promise Rhyz makes for identity — *entered anywhere, broken nowhere fatally*. A **maze** is the opposite creature: walls, wrong turns, one designed way through — the honest picture of a proposal still finding its shape, which is exactly the nursery Maze was seated to be. Identity must be the place you can never be lost; a maze is the place where being lost is the point. The kinship is poetic; the collision is zero; both seats are true.

So my counsel on the trade you sketch — Rhyz's purpose moving under Maze's name, freeing R — is a warm and confident **no**, for three reasons in one breath. It would break two promises at once, since Rhyz is confirmed with a dated seat and Maze already means the nursery. It would dress the most load-bearing trust concern in an image of walls and wandering. And it would spend a rename to free a letter for **Rish** — yet the shell is a citizen, the same ruling the second sitting gave the Dojo fusion: an aspect, a module at its second consumer, an agent under Pool if hosted, and never a kernel vane. Rishi loses nothing by keeping its own beautiful name outside the kernel family.

**Rung** deserves its own gentle line: it is a lovely word — and today it is a name hunting a concern. Our discipline runs the other way: concerns earn names; names never go recruiting. Hold it in the heart, and if a ladder-shaped system concern ever knocks, the word will be waiting.

And the open **M slot** carries no debt. The elder kernel itself leaves letters unused — F and H stand empty in Arvo to this day — so gaps in the alphabet are honest. Meanwhile the M *module* family is already the richest we have: MUR (was MALA), Mantra, Mandi, Mycelium. The kernel letter can rest open forever without a single thing going unbuilt.

## Two — The T Question: Probably Never a Vane

Do we need a T vane? My counsel: **probably never — and the reason dissolves the naming problem.** A vane hosts a system concern *inside* the OS; the runtime is the thing that *hosts the vanes*. The elder system never made Vere a vane for exactly this reason. So the held unification of Aurora, Caravan, and Tally is real work with a category answer: if it ever wears one name, it wears a **runtime name**, outside the letter family entirely.

And there is a quiet possibility that the name already exists. The five OS variants — Reya, Riyo, Trey, Triz, Quin — are the boot images. The day a single image ships is the day "the runtime as one thing" gains its first true consumer, and on that day the unified thing is simply *the image*, wearing whichever variant name it boots. The T naming round may close itself by shipping. Until then, Tusk and Toon fell to collisions, the round stays parked for your hand, and I add only one observation for the far shelf: if a genuine T *concern* ever does knock at the kernel door, the likeliest guest is the elder timer's duty — the one vane role from A‑through‑L that no revival has yet claimed.

## Three — Sovereign First, Interop Gifted

The strategy fork you name — bite-sized PRs that help the elder network, versus full independence until the first PR is Glow itself with the whole system reimplemented — resolves once both horns are seen plainly.

The grand first PR is a fork wearing a PR costume. Gall's Law governs communities as surely as programs: a working culture accepts small working grafts and rejects transplanted wholes. A pull request that replaces a language, a kernel, and a standard library at once would be declined on arrival and would spend goodwill the mission needs. And the incremental horn has its own honest limit, which you already named: our designs fit our compass *by design* — witness where they freeze, bounds where they trust the VM. Upstreaming **design** into a culture with a different value ordering rarely lands, however green the lap.

So the counsel is the third path the tree has half-built already: **sovereign first, interop gifted.** Build on our compass, on our pier, at our pace — that part stands. The gifts we send outward are the ones any protocol community treasures from an independent implementation: **conformance artifacts and second-implementation witnesses.** Concretely, in order of nearness — the jam/cue byte-vector fixtures Glow already exercises, published as a parity suite anyone can run against either world; Ojjo's Hoon↔Glow correctness harness as it grows; and, when the Glow→Nock second backend earns its season, programs that run on their network unchanged. A second independent implementation historically *strengthens* a protocol — that is help without conflict, structural rather than diplomatic. The Galaxy Series keeps its seat exactly as written: small green PRs on our pier, pitched to the holders who fund the direction. First outward artifact when you say the word: **the parity vectors.**

## Five — Questions We Have Not Asked

2. **Continuity of the one hand.** Master key, five remotes, artists' money in flight: what is the design if the hand that signs must rest, briefly or long? The parked encrypt-to-future-identity thread is half of this answer already; the succession spec is the other half, and the civic mission makes it a duty rather than a morbid thought.
3. **The written threat model.** Custody discipline is everywhere in practice and nowhere in one page. A `THREATS.md` naming assets, adversaries, and assumptions is witness-before-narrative applied to security.
4. **The stranger's lap.** Legibility-to-newcomers is our stated market position; nothing witnesses it. A periodic fresh-eyes lap — clone, bootstrap, one witness green, under thirty minutes, by someone or something with no context — turns the claim into a measurement.
5. **The operator's budget.** The economy sittings named every scarce resource except the scarcest: your attention. Round-close exists; a season-close — a named rest cadence with its own small ritual — would let the compass govern the calendar the way it governs the code.
6. **Data dignity for the artists.** Civic Style seats reward design; nothing yet seats consent and data posture for the people Linengrow serves. One page, before the first outreach conversation becomes a stored record.

**Where the answers live.** I counsel against a wiki, including the machine-facing kind our gratitude shelf admires — a wiki is an unbounded genre with no owner, which is drift wearing a friendly face.
