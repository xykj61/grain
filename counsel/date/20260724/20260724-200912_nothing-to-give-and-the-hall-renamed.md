## One — The First Principle: Nothing to Give

A real case sets the frame, reported the day before this counsel's parent memo. A person faced federal charges for allegedly handing a border officer a duress passcode that wiped a hardened phone, under a statute reaching anyone who takes action to impair the government's lawful custody of property. Our stated demo base is that same class of hardened device. The lesson is not defiance; it is architecture.

> **Build nothing that destroys, and build so there is nothing to destroy.**

The honest answer to a lawful search should be *"there is nothing here to give,"* rather than *"there was something, and it is now gone."* The first is a property of the design; the second is an act that a statute can reach and a prosecutor can dramatize. We reach the first by carrying little and encrypting what we carry — aparigraha pressed all the way down into the threat model.

## Two — The Three Techniques, in Our Own Voice

**Minimize first.** The data never collected cannot be seized, subpoenaed, breached, or misused. Every feature states, before it stores anything, the least it must remember to serve its one purpose — the same discipline as naming a bound before a loop. Collection is the exception that must justify itself, rather than the default.

**Encrypt with the key held elsewhere.** Sensitive records rest under per-record keys that live off the device, so a seized machine holds ciphertext that was never plaintext there. The operator cannot produce plaintext it never held — the strongest possible answer to a demand, because it is a true one.

**Delete by destroying the key.** Crypto-shredding — already our redaction primitive — doubles as lawful deletion: destroy one per-subject key, and the ciphertext becomes noise while the log's shape and chain stay whole. Paired with a **published retention and legal-hold regime** — routine, scheduled, announced, and suspended the instant a hold arrives — ordinary deletion stays provably ordinary.

The forward edge, held for its own season: **capability-scoped disclosure** — proving a fact without revealing the data beneath it. *Over eighteen*, rather than a birthdate; *this donation occurred*, rather than an income. Linengrow's zero-knowledge receipt, generalized into a house style. The data proven-without-collecting is the safest data of all.

## Three — Safe by Default, Informed Escalation

Configuration is a gift for **preference** and a hazard for **safety**. A person should shape the texture of their machine freely — and should never meet a toggle that quietly lowers their own protection, because a safety switch a frightened person can flip under pressure is the duress trap wearing a friendlier face.

So the doctrine, which is TAME's own order carried to the settings screen — safety first, the joy of a personal machine second:

- **Protection is the floor, not a setting.** Minimization and encryption hold for everyone, always, with no switch that turns them off.
- **A choice that trades away protection is a deliberate, explained, logged escalation** — never a checkbox. It states plainly what it gives up, and it writes itself to the audit log.
- **Everything that is pure preference stays free and reversible** — theme, cadence, register, the personal feel of the hall.

## Four — Where Settings Live: Seva for Feel, Mona for Weight

"Settings" names two different things, and the custody principle sorts them by one test:

> *If I change this, could it weaken who may see my data, or how long it is kept?*

**No → it belongs to the viewer.** Theme, notification cadence, reading register, the texture of the space you inhabit. Free, reversible, unlogged. These are experience concerns, and they live in the viewer vane.

**Yes → it belongs to Mona.** Retention windows, disclosure scopes, who-may-see-what, any policy carrying legal or custody weight. Changing one is an escalation, and it writes to the audit log. These are authority concerns, and Mona was seated for exactly authority, scope, audit, and retention.

The split needs no new vane. The two we hold divide configuration honestly along the one line that matters — and the split is itself the custody principle applied to the settings screen.

## Five — The Hall Renamed: Sala Becomes Seva

The viewer vane — the unified surface, the Glow-side parallel to the elder Landscape — carried the name **Sala**, *śālā*, the hall you inhabit. The name names the *place*. **Seva** names the *posture of that place toward the person in it*: selfless service, the surface that serves rather than merely houses. For a viewer — the one vane a user actually touches — the posture is the truer thing, and Seva sits squarely inside the five pillars this direction rests on.

Two honesties, stated plainly rather than smoothed:

**Seva is a hand-seat, not a corpus draw.** SEVA does not appear in the seated four-letter corpus, so it cannot be waymark-derived; it is chosen by hand, the way SETU was, and recorded as such. Its collision lap is otherwise clean: zero hits in the elder kernel, and one benign mention inside the one-clock naming spec, rather than a claim.

**The rename is real work, not a sweep.** Sala lives across roughly twenty places — the lexicon, four specs, `glow/README`, several Glow generators and shape files, and eight live witnesses (`sala_view_witness`, `glow_sala_b0_*`, `sala_backchannel_witness`, and their kin). So the rename earns accrete-never-break discipline rather than a blind find-replace:

- Seat `Seva` in the lexicon **beside** `Sala`, with Sala marked *renamed-to-Seva, retained as alias* until the migration completes — accrete before break.
- Migrate names as files are touched, plus one deliberate pass over the eight witnesses so the parity surface renames atomically rather than half-and-half.
- Swap `SALA → SEVA` in the `waymark_derive` exclude roster, and **add both** for one season so a ladder can draw neither during the transition.
- Rename the witness files (`sala_* → seva_*`) in the same pass that renames their contents, so a green run proves the rename whole.
- A short claim brief records Seva confirmed, Sala retired-as-alias, with the migration's GREEN as its witness.
