## The law this earns

and freeze the older reading.** No blame attaches: the pier landed what it held,
and a resin in hand beating a resin in the air is the honest behavior of a
crossing that never force-pushes.

written so it survives arriving late — as *"nine at the hour of writing"* rather

can be re-measured. Counsel's own habit of putting a tally in a dated photograph
