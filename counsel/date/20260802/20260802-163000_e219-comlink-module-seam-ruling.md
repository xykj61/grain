## Why R1 is safely separable

The pier's ladder is well drawn, and its first rung is the one rung that keeps the module seam closed. `shape-comlink-ipv6-dual-stack` names a policy — dual-stack on — and a witness that reads the host bind. Three walls make the cut honest, and they belong in the R1 seat itself. **Bind, never dial:** R1 witnesses that a host *can* offer both families and refuses an IPv4-only bind when the policy says otherwise; it does not open a connection to anything beyond the loopback the wire selftest already crosses. **Values apart, never braided:** the newer address record stands beside the elder one as a sibling shape — a destination is one family or the other, stated plainly, never a clever union. **The fallback is bounded:** if R1's descendants ever choose between families, the wait carries a named ceiling under Tally, because an unbounded wait is a wait-without-bound wearing a feature's clothes.

## Where the seam truly is, and what it costs to cross

The seam is not IPv6. The seam is **the frame** — the moment a datagram gains a field, a nonce, or a meaning it did not have before, every peer must agree at once, and the elder wire's format becomes a promise under renegotiation. That is R3's territory and, in a different way, R2's.

So the ruling draws the line there. **R2 (shell bootstrap)** may proceed as a *seam of the honest kind* only if it leans on the host's own client as a named outside process and never re-speaks an authentication protocol inside our wire — a seam leaned on openly, never absorbed silently. The moment a Kumara handshake is proposed inside Comlink instead, that is a frame change and wants its own check-in with a written format version. **R3 (roam window)** is a frame change by construction: a roam rule needs a sequence number that is never reused, and a nonce space is a wire promise. Its check-in should arrive carrying three things — the named width (`u64` at the wire boundary, per the tree's own width law), the exhaustion behavior as a *named error* rather than a silent wrap, and the replay refusal proven by a plant that the guard actually bites. **R4 (predictive echo)** is client-local by the pier's own framing and stays outside the seam entirely, which is exactly right: a UX fold that never forks the wire.
