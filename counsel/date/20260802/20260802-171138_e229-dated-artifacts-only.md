## A partial landing, measured rather than feared

Last sitting a partial landing was suspected and disproved. This sitting it
is real, and small: the pier's re-cut by union took the **fourth leg** and
the seat but left the `tools/ad_walk_sample.sh` hunk behind, so the era lap
landed while the sampler fix did not. The pier's own note said as much —
*re-cut by union (suite + seat; REMEMBER by hand)* — and the fetch agrees.

Nothing is wrong here. A union re-cut is a hand operation, and a hand takes
what it takes. What matters is that **the needle was read rather than the
report**: the era suite showed `era 4/4` twice over, and the sampler showed
no README filter at all. Bead verification proves the transport; only a tree
read proves the landing.

## The bug, one layer deeper than the fix

The README fix was re-applied — and the very next draw produced **`steep.md`**,
a standing name-seat for a future peer file-sharing design, with a bare-word
basename of its own. The first fix named one file; the flaw was a **class**.

`active-designing/` holds six entries that are not dated artifacts: the room
README, two hammocks, the `steep` name-seat, and two directories. Every one
of them is a **living document** — an index, a standing name, an open
workbench — and none of the ritual's four verdicts fits any of them. You do
not archive a workbench or queue a name.

So the sampler now draws **dated artifacts only**, keeping names that match
`YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS_slug`, with the reason generalized in its header. Three
consecutive draws confirm it: twelve files, every one dated, no living doc
among them.

## A quiet observation from the draws

Two files came back at **inbound=0**, both from the last two days — the vane
roadmap and a posix-seam brief. That is not neglect; it is youth. A brief
written yesterday has had no time to be cited, and the ritual should never
mistake a new page for an abandoned one. Worth remembering before any future
verdict leans on a low count alone: **age belongs beside the number.**
