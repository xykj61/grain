## The observation becomes an instrument

rather than abandoned — and left the thought in prose. Prose is where good
observations go to be forgotten, so it is an instrument now: **every sampler
line prints the artifact's age in days beside its inbound count**, derived
from the artifact's own stamp.

The ritual's arithmetic changes accordingly, and the header says so: no
verdict past KEEP may rest on a low count without its age standing next to

opposite meanings, and only the age tells them apart.

and now each one can be judged on two facts instead of one.
