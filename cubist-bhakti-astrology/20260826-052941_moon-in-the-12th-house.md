## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page opens by naming the twelfth the house of dissolving, and gives that word
four facets: seclusion and rest, the private and the hidden, loss and letting go, and, at its
height, liberation -- the small self released into something vast. Set the Moon there and the
feeling heart takes the house's direction: drawn inward, drawn beyond, tender and imaginative,
longing for a peace the surface of life rarely hands over.

Then the page teaches its central fork, and insists on its unity first. The pull to dissolve is
one energy, and it can run two ways. Aimed upward it becomes the longing for transcendence --
ego released, rest found in the larger stillness, the page's own image of the great swan
gliding free on open water. Aimed the other way, the same pull seeks escape: a melting into
comfort or withdrawal that numbs rather than frees. The art of the placement is entirely in
the aim, because the capacity is the same capacity in both directions.

The cost and the gift come from one source. So porous a heart absorbs the moods around it,
carries the world's sorrow as its own, needs more solitude and rest than most, and may keep
its deepest feelings hidden even from itself. The page's medicine is gentle and precise: aim
the dissolving, rather than build walls against it -- choose the practices that quiet and
restore over the habits that only numb. And the same thinness of boundary is the grace:
compassion felt easily and deeply, rich imagining, prayer, meditation, art, service to those
who suffer, and a letting-go of self that can ripen into genuine spiritual freedom. In a
reading, the page counsels, honor the sensitivity and the need for rest as real before
offering the fork -- and offer it softly, as an invitation the heart takes up again and again.

## The habit it feeds

The fork translates into a working tree without any forcing, because a tree meets it at the
close of every round. Release aimed upward is the deliberate close: the log written, the work
laid down by name, the rest taken as rest -- the tree already keeps this direction in its own
words, *prune with care, release what no longer serves*, and in the fallow that follows a
harvest. Release aimed at escape is the drift: the unbounded midnight fiddle that neither ships
nor restores, motion that numbs the tiredness instead of answering it. The page's medicine
transfers whole -- bound the rest and aim it, rather than wall it off -- because a rest with a
name is a practice, and a rest without one is a leak. The house's second facet transfers too,
and literally: the private and the hidden are honored here as real need, the way this tree
keeps personal readings withheld from the seed and holds its tenderest source material in a
silo that enters no repository at all. A porous heart chooses what enters its quiet room; so
does a careful tree.

## The pair it wears

By day this placement wears **dream**; by night, **hush** -- the aether row's dual seat in the
fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the dual column adapts and dissolves onward and
aether's own sense, at the fixed seat beside it, is sound. The why is nearly the page's own
sentence. The dual seat's motion in the palette's comment -- *dream dissolves* -- is the
twelfth house's very verb, and aether is the subtle row, the one that hears rather than
grasps, which is how a boundary thins. Dream is the placement's day: the rich imagining, the
prayer and the art, the dissolving aimed upward toward the vast. Hush is its night, and keeps
Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom: the quiet room where solitude is welcomed as medicine,
the clear water gone still after the swan has settled. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word aims
the heart beyond itself, and the night-word is what remains when even the dream is laid down,
a hush with peace inside it. Of the fifteen pairs, this row waits for the teachings that
honestly dream -- and this page dreams from its first line, so the seat receives it gladly.
