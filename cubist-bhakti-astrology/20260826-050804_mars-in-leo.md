## The teaching, kept whole

Mars is the planet of action, courage, and force, and Leo is the Sun's own house -- a fixed
fire sign, ruled by a friend of Mars. Set there, Mars stands strong and dignified: the
drive that elsewhere outruns its own aim arrives here with the aim already in hand. The
classical page names the result a will that carries its honor inside the act. It protects.
It leads by showing rather than by pushing. It fights for what it loves, and keeps its love
out of the line of fire. Because Leo and the Sun stay so close to the self, action here
becomes part of identity -- there is a hero in the placement, and the hero's bravery earns
trust because it rises from settled ground rather than from fear.

Fire is the element of dharma -- purpose, integrity, right action -- and three fires meet
in this placement: Mars, Leo, and the Sun behind them both. Courage supplies the motion,
Leo supplies the steady identity and the clear direction, and the deed at its best becomes
a beacon others steer by. At the center of the sign the old sky-watchers placed Regulus,
the heart of the lion, and called it a royal star, a keeper of the sky's order. That is the
high form: the commander who takes orders from the throne and spends strength answerably,
where the reckless soldier spends first and asks after.

The page meets the shadow honestly. Such power can quietly decide that worth lives in the
deed alone, due for re-earning with every act, and can ache when applause goes elsewhere.
The medicine the page gives is gentle: the fire already belongs to its bearer, lit whether
or not anyone watches. Strength goes deepest to school in the hours that offer no victory
-- patience, surrender, the blade left sheathed -- and even a born leader bends to plain
work. The Bhagavata's way closes the teaching: do the deed with whole courage, hand over
its fruit, and rest in the doing. The classical page reads all of this inside an example
chart, where the placement shares the tenth house -- the chart's noon height, its most
public sector -- with the Sun, and counsels action that shelters and lifts, leadership that
inspires, and a quiet heart kept under the mane.
