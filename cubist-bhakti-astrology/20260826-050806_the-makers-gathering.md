## The teaching, kept whole

Some charts gather their craft into one room; that is the whole of the pattern, and the
classical page names its three companions plainly. Mercury and an exalted Saturn stand
together in Libra under Hasta, the skilled hand, while the Moon beside them wears Chitra, the
architect's star -- word, discipline, and the designing heart sharing the third house, the
house of hands. Each thread carries its own gift into the room. Mercury gives the articulate
word trained to the workbench, speech that knows what the tools are for. Saturn at his Libra
best gives the fair steward's patience: craft mastered over years, abundance that blooms late
and then stays. And the Chitra Moon gives the vision the other two serve -- what the hands
can build, the heart first imagines whole.

The page then opens one door outward. When the gathering's sign is also an angular house of
the visible chart, the workshop opens directly onto the public square: the making becomes the
vocation, and the vocation keeps the maker's temperament -- slow, fine, honest, built to
last. The counsel the page closes on is simple and kind, three lines a friend would say:
protect the workshop hours, trust the late blooming, and let each finished piece be released
with satisfaction before the next is begun.

## The habit it feeds

The three counsels are already a method for a working tree, and the translation asks no
forcing. Protect the workshop hours: a round is a bounded room, and the bound is what makes
the craft possible -- the tree calls this slower to go faster, strict tools early, the quiet
in which fine work happens. Trust the late blooming: Saturn's gift is the witness discipline
itself, proof accumulated patiently over many small rounds, abundance that arrives late and
then stays green. Release each finished piece with satisfaction before the next is begun:
that is the finishing edge word for word -- ship one real thing before naming the next, close
the round, let the send carry it. And the three companions map onto the tree's own three
rooms: Mercury the mechanism sentence and the commit body, Saturn the witness and the bound,
the Chitra Moon the design essay that imagines the module whole before a line is written.
Word, discipline, and the designing heart share one repository the way they share one house.
Devotion completes the loop: the craft was given, so finishing is thanks.

## The pair it wears

By day the gathering wears **harvest**; by night, **fallow** -- the earth element's mutable
seat in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the harvest turns and earth's own
sense, at the fixed seat beside it, is aroma rising as incense. The why is the page's own
closing image. The counsel is a farmer's counsel spoken to a maker: trust the late blooming,
and let each finished piece be released with satisfaction -- that is harvest, the day-word,
the slow abundance Saturn ripens and the open door through which the finished work goes out
to the public square. And the dark twin keeps Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. Fallow is
the protected workshop hour itself: the field resting full of next year's strength, the bench
swept clean between pieces, the quiet in which the Chitra Moon imagines the next whole thing.
Aim, then dissolve: the day-word carries the finished piece out through the open door, and
the night-word lets the door close gently, keeping the ground rich for the making to come.
