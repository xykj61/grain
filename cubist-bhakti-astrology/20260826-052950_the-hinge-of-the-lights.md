## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page describes a birth that arrives at the day's own turning. The Sun stands at
the western horizon, minutes from setting, while the Moon has newly risen in the east -- the
sky handing day to night at the very hour of arrival. The tradition reads this as a threshold
temperament: a life lived between the public glare and the private feeling, carrying the
Sun's last brightness and the Moon's first tenderness together, both lights held in a single
breath.

The counsel the page gives is gentle and whole. The Sun near the descendant teaches
partnership -- the self meeting the other as the light goes down, visibility at the day's
edge. The risen Moon teaches a heart already aloft, receptive, gathering the night early.
And the reading asks for integration rather than a choice: each pole keeps its own light,
and the gift is one craft with two motions -- address offered in the hours that listen,
feeling kept for the hours that rest. The hinge is honored as a hinge. It turns, and both
doors stay hung.

## The habit it feeds

The hinge is already a working rhythm; the translation asks for no forcing. Every round in a
working tree ends at this same turning: the day's half is the send -- the witness shown
green, the work pushed where companions can see it, the Sun's last visible brightness -- and
the night's half is the session log, the quiet record written as the round sets, the Moon
rising over what the day meant. The counsel transfers whole: let neither half cancel the
other. A tree that only ships forgets what it learned; a tree that only reflects shows
nothing at its western edge. The hinge teaches the handoff itself as the craft -- one commit
carrying both the public proof and the private reasoning, day handed to night with both
lights still burning. The threshold temperament, read as method, is simply this: stand at
the close of a round without dropping either light, and let the turning be the work.

## The pair it wears

By day this teaching wears **sound**; by night, **silence** -- the aether element's fixed
seat in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where sound is aether's own sense and
the receipt's comment says the pairing plainly: sound rests in silence. The why is the
page's own counsel. The hinge-born are asked to speak while the world still listens -- that
is sound, the day-word, the Sun's edge where the self meets the other in the open -- and to
feel while the world rests, which is silence, the night-word, the risen Moon receiving what
the day said. Aether is the honest row here because the teaching hears: its whole craft is
knowing which hours listen and which hours rest, and the space between the two horizons is
aether's own room. The fixed seat is earned by the counsel's one firm ask -- hold. The hinge
turns, and the one who stands at it stays; steadiness at the threshold is what keeps either
light from canceling the other. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word carries the voice out to
the western edge, and the night-word lets the voice go, keeping the hearing.
