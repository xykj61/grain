## The teaching, kept whole

Dharma is most often heard as duty, religion, or morality, and the classical page grants each
of those at the surface. Underneath sits the root: dharma is the essence of a thing, the
quality held so fundamentally that, were it removed, the thing would stop being itself. The
dharma of water is wetness. The dharma of fire is heat and light. From that root the familiar
meanings unfold on their own: what a thing is decides what it is meant to do, so a parent's
essence carries the feeding of a child, a student's the study, a teacher's the teaching --
and living from one's nature holds a person together, while living against it comes apart a
little. Morality, read this way, rewards itself without any judge watching.

The page then teaches two surprises. First, dharma differs from being to being: on a bicycle
the pedal is the pedal and the wheel is the wheel, neither more important, each honored for
being itself -- so ranking people against one standard is a confusion, and meeting each as
themselves is the truer kindness. Second, one person's dharma shifts across a life: most
duties are incidental, born of the present season and company, while a few are permanent, and
when duties collide the guidance is to layer them -- incidental toward the outside, permanent
toward the core, the incidental yielding where they conflict. A duty completes when those
served release us, a trusted guide who knows us personally helping us read the season; the aim
is honest effort rather than flawlessness, and the effort itself grows us. At the very core
sits the essence of a conscious being -- to perceive, to experience truly -- found within the
life actually being lived. And non-harming, read through dharma, becomes the devotion of
minimizing one's footprint: choosing the gentler path where a gentler path exists.

## The habit it feeds

The essence teaching is already a working method, and the tree keeps it daily. The dharma of a
module is its invariant: the bound and the assert name the quality the module would stop being
itself without, which is why the discipline states invariants first and proves them at every
edge -- take the wetness from water and it is something else wearing water's name. The pedal
and the wheel are the value model: modules composed side by side, each honored for its own
role, the runner running and the allocator bounding, ranked against their own natures rather
than each other. The layering of duties is the tree's own memory order: living pins shift with
the season the way incidental duties do, while foundations and dated testimony hold the
permanent core, and where they conflict the living line yields to the record -- accrete, never
break, said in the classical page's grammar. And a duty released by those it served is a round
closed on a green witness: the work is done when the one it serves says landed, with the
footprint teaching setting the tempo -- small rounds, bounded allocations, pruning with care,
the gentler path taken where a gentler path exists.

## The pair it wears

By day dharma wears **taste**; by night, **nectar** -- the water element's fixed seat in the
fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where taste is water's own sense and sweetens at
night to nectar. The why is the page's first example. The dharma of water is wetness, and
taste is the sense by which a thing confesses what it is: sweetness is sugar's essence on the
tongue, and sugar parted from its sweetness is gone the way de-wetted water is gone. The fixed
seat fits the teaching exactly -- the cardinal seat initiates and the dual seat adapts,
while the fixed seat holds the quality that stays, which is the page's whole definition of
essence. And the dark twin keeps Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. Nectar is the deepest
layer of the page: the essence of a conscious being is to experience truly, and nectar is
taste fully received, the hollow experience made sweet again from within the life actually
being lived. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word knows each thing by its essence, and the
night-word lets the knowing deepen into the experience the essence was for.
