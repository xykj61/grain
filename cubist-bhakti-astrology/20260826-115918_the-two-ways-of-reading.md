## The teaching, kept whole

The Veda opens to two readings, and knowing which one you hold clears a great deal. The first
is Purva, the earlier way, and it concerns action: what to do, what to leave undone, and when.
Its heart is the practical craft of a good life -- health, long years, children, a sound
society, food and music and love. The second is Uttara, the later and ultimate way, and it
concerns understanding: perceiving reality as it truly is. Its heart is insight, and its aim
is liberation.

The two form a sequence within a person more than a sequence in history. First a life wants
its actions right; later, with that footing, the deeper question opens -- what these things
truly are, and why. Do the first well and the second grows from it on its own, because
someone who uses a thing rightly is already on the way to knowing what it is. Astrology sits
inside this frame as a limb of the first, practical Veda: a tool for timing, for how a
tendency is likely to unfold. The stars keep company with the practical, blessing-granting
powers of marriage and work and health, rather than with the supreme three -- the craft was
built to serve the questions of action, and the old star-lore is peopled accordingly.

Both aims stay honored. The first leans toward prosperity and pleasure, the second toward
liberation, and every path ripens upward in its own time. For the craft, the frame is a gift
of humility: a reading that knows what kind of tool it is gives clearer counsel, and leaves
the deeper journey open for the person to walk in their own way.

## The habit it feeds

The two ways translate into a working tree without any forcing, because the tree already
keeps them as rooms. The Purva room is the round: what to do, what to leave undone, and when
-- the lap plan, the timing call, the witness run before the claim. The Uttara room is the
essay: what the thing truly is, written so it would still be worth reading if the code it
describes were deleted. The teaching's order holds in the tree exactly as it holds in a life.
Ship the round rightly and the essay grows from it, because using a module well is already
the first draft of understanding it. And the frame's humility is a working discipline too: a
witness proves its one bounded claim and stops, the way a reading answers the practical,
tendency-shaped question and leaves the whole path open. A tool that knows its scope counsels
clearly; a tool that claims the whole journey counsels no one for long.

## The pair it wears

By day this teaching wears **sound**; by night, **silence** -- the aether element's fixed
seat in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where sound is aether's own sense and
the palette's comment says the pairing plainly: sound rests in silence. The why is the
teaching's own shape. The first way speaks -- an injunction sounded into the day, telling a
person what to do and when, the working word of the practical craft. The second way listens
past the word, toward a perception no ordinary means can hand over. And the dark twin keeps
Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. Silence here is the word at rest rather than the word
refused: the counsel has been spoken, the action taken rightly, and understanding gathers in
the quiet the way meaning gathers after a sentence lands. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word
says when, and the night-word receives what the saying was for.
