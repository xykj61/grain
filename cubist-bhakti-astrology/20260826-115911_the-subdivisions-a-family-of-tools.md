## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page opens on a question people keep asking as a rivalry, and answers it as a
toolbox: the birth chart and its subdivisions are a hammer and a screwdriver rather than two
hammers. The birth chart shows the observable reality -- how a person works, the traits met
at face value. A subdivision shows the mechanism beneath -- why a person likes what they
like, fears what they fear, reaches for what they reach for. One names the trait; the other
names its root. The order of reading is gentle and fixed: surface first, because a *why*
needs a *what* to answer about, and the *what* lives in the birth chart alone.

Three quieter clarities follow. Dignity is held per chart: a planet may sit exalted in one
subdivision and fallen in another, each dignity speaking only to its own chart's subject,
with one whole-planet measure gathered across all the subdivisions belonging to the planet
itself. The ninth chart, widely called the chart of the spouse, is truly the chart of dharma
-- of living in one's essence -- and speaks to marriage through its own seventh house
because moral duties are woven through the closest bonds. And a subchart is a chart entire:
its houses, lordships, conjunctions, and aspects all real, weighed in that order. Last comes
the deepest teaching: a division carries meaning only where its geometry draws a shape, and
where there is no shape there is no symbolism.

Two honest notes ride with the table, telling teaching and arithmetic apart in so many
words. The shapes themselves are arithmetic: dividing by four draws a square, by seven an
opposition, and tracing a base sign against the first of its divisions is geometry anyone
can redo. The meanings are teaching: that the sextile drawn by three is lifted into a trine,
that the trine broken by five is why that chart speaks of fortune fractured, that the trine
drawn by nine is the trine of essence itself -- each is the tradition reading the shape, and
the page says which is which. The page writes no Sanskrit chart names and no divisor past
these; this twin carries only what its source states.

## The habit it feeds

The teaching translates into a working tree without any forcing, because it is already a
debugging method. Read the surface first: a symptom is scoped from what a witness observably
does before any module is opened to ask why, since a cause read without its trait floats
free of anything to explain. Hold dignity per chart: a module strong under one witness and
weak under another carries no contradiction, only fitness to purpose, and the whole-tree
reading belongs to the module itself rather than to any single meter. Keep the weights in
order inside every chart: where a thing sits matters most, what it governs next, what it
touches after. And keep the geometry test as the meter's own law: a measurement earns its
place only when its derivation draws a shape -- a bound with a reason, a count with a
geometry -- and the finest division stops at the apparent size of the lights themselves,
because a reading finer than the instrument can observe is a fabrication wearing decimals.

seat in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the mind initiates and aether's
