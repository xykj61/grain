## The teaching, kept whole

The tenth house is the high noon of the chart -- the point directly overhead, the open sky, the
most visible seat in the whole circle. It holds public life: career and calling, standing and
contribution, being seen for what one does. The Sun is light and visibility and the central
self, so when it takes this seat it is entirely at home; of all the places the Sun can hold,
the classical page counts this among the most powerful. The light has found the highest window.

Such a life is, in some real sense, meant to be seen. The placement carries a strong pull
toward purpose -- the sense that the work is a calling, that a summit stands worth climbing and
a contribution worth making. The tenth is also a house of movement, an open sky in which one
can rise, and the Sun here gives both the wish to rise and the steadiness to do it: leadership,
authority, the quiet center other things come to turn around. Then arrives the part the page
holds closest. The fame of the tenth, at its truest, follows contribution -- recognition that
comes because one has become a pillar others rely on, because one has given something of worth.
The house builds, and the old understanding asks that the building be done with dharma: rising
by genuine usefulness, the light meant to illumine rather than merely to be admired.

A Sun read whole asks for its Moon, for the two move always as a pair -- the soul and the
heart, the light and the one who feels it. In the page's example chart the Moon stands exalted
in Taurus, fertile and steady, giving the bright climb a settled inner ground to act from; the
outer summit and the inner home support one another when both are tended. And the page closes
on an honest shadow: a Sun this high can tempt a person to measure whole worth by public
standing. Its answer is the Bhagavata's way -- the work given freely, the fruit released -- so
the brightness stays clean and the heart's home stays warm beneath it.

## The habit it feeds

The tenth-house Sun is already a working method, and the tree wears it without any forcing.
The highest window of a tree is its front door: the README, the published seed, the green
witness shown where anyone can run it. Recognition follows contribution here exactly as the
page teaches -- a module becomes known by being the pillar other modules import, and the proof
ships before the announcement, so the light illumines before it asks to be admired. Building
with dharma is the same discipline in work clothes: usefulness first, every claim bounded,
every rung honestly earned. The Sun-and-Moon pair keeps the pace honest -- the visible send by
day and the quiet field tended beneath it, the outer climb resting on an inner ground that
stays warm. And the shadow note draws the boundary: a tree's worth is measured by what it
proves and serves, with attention left to arrive as fruit, offered onward as freely as it came.

## The pair it wears

By day the tenth-house Sun wears **rockstar**; by night, **hermit** -- fire's cardinal seat in
the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the rockstar ignites and fire's own sense,
at the fixed seat beside it, is vision. The fit is earned twice over. The Sun is fire itself,
and the tenth is the chart's high-noon angle -- a cardinal seat by the house's own nature -- so
planet and house point together at the same square of the grid. The rockstar's day is the
page's first face: the light in the highest window, career as calling, a life seen for what it
gives. And the hermit's night keeps the page's own caution as a gift rather than a warning:
the climb rested, the fire banked to coals in the quiet room where the exalted Moon keeps
house, the work offered and the fruit released. The seat is shared in this room, and worn here
on the placement's own grounds. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word climbs to the summit and
shines; the night-word walks down whole, keeping the warmth.
