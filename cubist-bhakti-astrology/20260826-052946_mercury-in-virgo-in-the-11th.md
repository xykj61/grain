## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page opens with exaltation, and teaches it as something better than comfort: a
gentle push -- being asked to stretch toward something fully within reach, with real resources
to meet the stretch. Pushed too hard, a planet struggles; pushed just enough, it rises past its
usual best. Mercury's case is the lovely one, because Mercury alone is exalted in a sign it
rules. In airy Gemini the planet of intellect rests so comfortably that nothing stretches it.
In earthy Virgo, its other home, the earth element gives it just enough weight to grow grounded
and serious -- so it keeps all its brilliance and sets down its childishness, refined without
ever leaving home.

The earth shapes the intelligence four ways, and the page names each: organized, loving order
and system; prepared, planning rather than scrambling; thorough, cutting no corner and
overlooking no detail; and patient, with the calm stamina for long, focused study. This is a
mind that manages -- people, resources, itself -- that makes the checklist and quietly works it
to completion, that keeps a steady practical sense of money, and that tends to dress the way it
thinks: clean, tailored, well-ordered.

Then the page opens the eleventh house, the place where the wider world gives back: community
and networks, warm socializing, exchange that brings gain, the arts and their enjoyment,
refined talent and simple pleasure. Woven together, planet and house harmonize four times over
-- interaction in the house of socializing makes the well-connected; skill in the house of
talent makes the expressive; recreation in the house of pleasure makes the delightful company;
exchange in the house of exchange makes the effective trader. And because this Mercury sits in
Purva Phalguni under Aryaman, its brilliance is a devoted brilliance -- given to real bonds
rather than merely clever connections.

## The habit it feeds

The gentle push is already a sizing rule, and a working tree can run it without any forcing.
Comfort teaches little: a round made only of what the bench has done before stretches nothing.
Overreach breaks: a round past the bench's resources ends in struggle rather than rising. The
exalted round is the stretch taken at home -- inside a module already ruled, with the witnesses
and bounds on the bench -- so the push is met and the work comes back better than its usual
best. The four earth qualities name the disciplines that meet it: prepared is the bound named
at construction, thorough is the assert at every edge, organized is the one filing shape,
patient is the long green run watched to the end. And the eleventh house names where the
finished work belongs: shipped into the network, where exchange turns skill into gain and
companions into collaborators. Aryaman closes the loop -- the connections kept are the
trustworthy ones, bonds rather than contacts.

## The pair it wears

By day this placement wears **mind**; by night, **mystery** -- the aether element's cardinal
seat in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the mind initiates and aether's own
sense, at the fixed seat beside it, is sound. The fit is earned in the teaching's own noun: the
classical page titles itself a bright, well-connected mind, and Mercury is the planet of
intellect at its very best -- the day-word is the page's own subject, the mind initiating
connection, making the list, brightening the gathering, its gift for words living one seat away
where aether hears. The night twin keeps Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. The thorough
Virgo mind wants everything known and correct, and night hands it the one thing the checklist
leaves open: the remainder past the counted edge, held gently. Mystery is the same mind at
rest, its patience -- earth's own calm -- turned toward what is still arriving, the unsolved
kept like a friend expected rather than a defect found. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word counts
what is, and the night-word lets the count breathe at its edge, keeping the wonder.
