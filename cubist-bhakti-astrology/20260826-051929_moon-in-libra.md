## The teaching, kept whole

The Moon is the feeling heart, and Libra is Venus's air sign -- the place of exchange, balance,
and refined perception. Set the receptive Moon there and the heart comes out attuned: tuned to
what flows in and what flows out, whether the flow is a taste, a sound, a sum of money, or the
give and take between two people. This heart reads the world through its senses and its scales.
It notices the exact seasoning in a dish, the true color of a room, the small difference between
the fine thing and the ordinary one, and it answers beauty with a real, discerning pleasure.
Libra is the sign of partnership, so the same fineness reaches the human scale: an instinct for
fairness, the ability to set one's own wishes beside another's and honor both.

The classical page names the shadows kindly, as the costs of feeling the fabric of things
closely. Attunement turned up too high becomes a burden -- the loud sound too loud, the sweet
too sweet, a small imperfection spoiling a whole experience. High fineness can breed high
expectation, easy disappointment, and a worth measured by the finer things one gathers; in
partnership the scale can tip toward pleasing until one's own agency quietly slips. Each cost
eases when the balance is held from within rather than chased. At its best the placement weighs
self and other as equals and loves from that fairness; it reads a room, a market, a mood with
intuitive ease, so it thrives in commerce and in every craft that must know what people want.
The scale that is Libra's emblem is also an old cash register, and this heart understands flow:
its fortunes, like the Moon and like money, move rather than sit, and it does best treating that
movement as its element.

## The habit it feeds

The attuned heart is already a working method, and it translates without forcing. The gift is
taste: the eye that catches the small difference between the fine thing and the ordinary one is
the same eye a tree wants on everything it touches -- prose read aloud, a bound questioned, a
name weighed before it seats. The shadow teaches the calibration. Fineness turned all the way up
refuses ordinary work, the way the too-loud and the too-sweet spoil the meal, so the habit is to
let attunement inform rather than overwhelm: a reading that reports and advises, a fineness that
ratchets on touch instead of barring the door. The partnership scale teaches the second half --
a review keeps the author's intent on one pan and the reader's need on the other, names its own
lean plainly, and honors both. And the old cash register closes the loop: fortunes move rather
than sit, so work moves too -- small rounds that land soon, value kept flowing in and out rather
than hoarded toward a grand one that waits. Bhakti holds it together: weighing another's wish as
carefully as your own is a form of love, and the fairness is the devotion.

## The pair it wears

By day this Moon wears **senses**; by night, **trance** -- the air element's dual seat in the
fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, the row where the arc moves, touch is air's own sense,
and the senses play. The why is the page's own vocabulary. Its nouns are taste, color, sound,
scale -- the senses at work -- and its whole motion is two-sided: in and out, self and other, the
two pans of one beam, which is what the dual seat holds. The grid keeps a cardinal air pair too,
and the arc is a fine word for Venus's initiating air; yet this page teaches through the senses
by name, so the twin wears the teaching's word and earns the seat by the give and take rather
than by the sign-count. The dark twin keeps Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. Trance is the
attunement with the weighing set down: the same fineness absorbed rather than measuring, the
too-loud softened, the too-sweet resting as sweetness again, the scale balanced and still. Aim,
then dissolve: the day-word goes out to meet the world flavor by flavor, and the night-word lets
the flavors merge, keeping the ease.
