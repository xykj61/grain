## The teaching, kept whole

Every sign carries a set of themes, and every theme holds two expressions -- a bright face and
a shadowed one. The classical page opens with two worked pairs: a sign of leadership can rise
into noble command or harden into domination, and a sign of care and order can flower into
patient excellence or tighten into anxious perfectionism. The themes stay fixed; which face
they show stays open. Knowing what decides the face is one of the quiet keys of the craft.

The page then hands over its one instrument: a sign is like a guitar. A guitar always sounds
like a guitar, and it sounds entirely different depending on how it is played. So a sign
keeps its essential nature in every chart while leaving the real question open -- how that
nature will be voiced for this particular person. What decides the voicing is dignity, the
overall welcome the sign receives in the chart, gathered from a few honest sources: the
dignity of the planets that aspect or join the sign, weighed together; the dignity of the
sign's own ruler, counted with special weight, since a sign leans most on its lord; and the
supportive light the sign's lord and the ascendant's lord shine upon it, which always helps.
A richly dignified sign leans toward its bright face, a poorly supported one toward its
shadow, and most charts live along the range between rather than at either end. The point of
the measuring is a balance rather than a verdict: every chart holds some of both faces, and a
true reading speaks to each in proportion -- the strength to lean on, the shadow to tend.

## The habit it feeds

The teaching translates into a working tree without any forcing, because a module has two
faces too. The same bounded parser can read as patient excellence or as anxious
perfectionism; the same supervising loop can read as noble command or as domination of every
neighbor it touches. Which face shows is decided the way the chart decides it -- by the
welcome the thing receives: the health of what joins it (its callers and imports, weighed
together), the dignity of its own lord (the witness that rules it, counted with special
weight, since a module leans most on its proof), and the supportive light of the rooms that
cite it. So the habit is the review done as a balance rather than a verdict. Measure the
welcome before judging the nature; a red mark on a poorly supported module names its support,
and the fix is more often dignity -- a witness, a citation, a caller repaired -- than a
rewrite of the nature itself. And when the reading is spoken, speak to both faces in
proportion: the strength to lean on first, the shadow to tend beside it, so the module's
keeper hears the whole truth rather than a flattering half. A grade tells what could be
better; only a wrong thing is a red. That distinction is this teaching wearing work clothes.

## The pair it wears

By day this teaching wears **vision**; by night, **night** -- the fire element's fixed seat in
the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where vision is fire's own sense and its dark
twin opens into night. The seat is earned twice over. It is the fixed seat because the page's
hinge sentence lives there: the themes are fixed, and only their voicing varies -- a nature
held constant while the playing changes is the fixed column's whole meaning. And it is the
vision pair because the craft the page teaches is entirely an act of seeing: dignity is
gathered light, the lords shine supportive light upon the sign, and the reader's task is
never to rename the sign but to see which way it is being played. By day, vision reads the
bright face in full light and names the strength plainly. By night, the same sense turns to
the shadowed face and keeps Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. Night is vision with the
lamps down: the shadow seen gently, told kindly, held as a face of the same instrument rather
than an accusation. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word points at what is strong, and the
night-word lets the judgment go, keeping the seeing.
