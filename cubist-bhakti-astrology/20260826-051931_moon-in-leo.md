## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page seats the receptive Moon in the Sun's own house, where dignity, warmth,
and the wish to shine keep the hearth. A heart placed there becomes self-aware: it feels its
own presence, watches and knows its own moods, gives in large measure, celebrates gladly,
and asks openly to matter to the people it loves. The page is careful with the self-regard
underneath -- it reads as a true relationship with one's own worth rather than vanity, and
it carries a natural nobility that asks to be met with respect. Appreciation feeds this
heart nearly the way food does, and being overlooked cuts deeper than it should.

The shadow arrives when the watching runs short. The page names three slides -- wounded
pride, a hunger for validation, a heaviness that settles where recognition was expected --
and two quiet curdlings beside them: self-awareness thickening into self-preoccupation, the
wish to shine tightening into a need for steady applause. Named gently, this is a bright
heart that mislaid one fact: the lamp burns the same before an empty room. The medicine the
page gives is a single move inward -- worth drawn from one's own well, dignity held real
with or without a crowd to confirm it. Secure, the heart turns magnanimous: generous
because it is unafraid, encouraging because another's shining threatens nothing of its own,
able at last to lead with warmth and give without asking. In a reading, the page counsels
honoring the warmth and the dignity first, and only then, softly, naming the invitation
inward, so that recognition becomes a welcome gift rather than a needed proof.

## The habit it feeds

The medicine translates into a working tree without any forcing, because the tree already
lives by it. Worth drawn from within is witness-before-narrative: a module's dignity is its
green witness, and the witness runs the same in an unattended loop at three in the morning
as it does before a watching demo. Applause stays welcome -- a kind review, a companion's
thanks -- yet the proof already ran, so praise arrives as gift rather than as verdict. The
shadow has its tree-shape too: work that ships only where it will be seen, counts quoted
from memory because the real run felt unglamorous, a round that waits for an audience before
it lands. The Leo Moon's cure covers all three -- run the measurement, write the log for the
years rather than for the room, and let the record hold its worth in the dark. And the gift
completes the loop the way the page says it does: a module secure in its own proof turns
generous, documenting itself kindly and lending its parts, because another module's shining
takes nothing from a witness that is already green.

fixed seat in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, the column where each element
