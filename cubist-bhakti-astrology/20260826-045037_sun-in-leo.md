## The teaching, kept whole

The Sun rules Leo, so this placement is the ruler returned to his own house. The classical
page opens there: sign and light agree, the meeting carries almost no friction, and out of
that agreement comes a clear and warm sense of self. The page counts this among the most
harmonious seats the Sun can take.

The first gift is confidence, and the page is careful to say how quiet a gift it is. It has
two parts: self-direction, a mind that knows itself without waiting for instruction, and
self-assurance, a rest in oneself that doubt stops gnawing at. From that ease flows
decisiveness, and from decisiveness the capacity to lead -- leadership taught at its root as
the willingness to decide, to carry the weight of choosing while others prefer to be
carried. Then comes steadiness. Leo is a fixed sign, and the Sun is the sky's most faithful
body, rising each day where it is expected and holding the year's tempo whole. Together
they give loyalty and reliability; people learn to set their own rhythm by such a person.

The deeper note turns the light inward. The Sun is the self and Leo is the self's own
kingdom, so the life carries a strong interest in the self -- held lightly, that interest
ripens into self-knowledge, and with devotion and study beside it, toward the deeper Self
the Bhagavata Purana teaches. The page closes its brightness with an honest shadow: pressed
too hard, this Sun can grow domineering, slow to follow, impatient with small faults, and
quick to over-commit. The medicine it offers is gentle -- temperance, and the memory that a
true king still kneels.

## The habit it feeds

The placement translates into a working tree without any forcing, because its gifts are
already disciplines. Steadiness first: the Sun rises each day at its appointed place, and a
tree keeps that faith as one clock, honest stamps, and the round that lands today -- the
witness others set their rhythm by is the Sun of its module. Decisiveness next: leadership
as the willingness to decide is the closing line of every pass, one next step named plainly
and carried rather than deferred. Confidence as accurate self-knowledge is a survey done
kindly -- measurement beats memory, and a tree knows itself by counting what it already
does well. Even the shadow instructs. Quick to over-commit is scope creep wearing a crown,
and the page's temperance is the tree's own bound: one keystone per round, everything
bounded at construction, the king kneeling to the budget he named himself.

## The pair it wears

By day this placement wears **vision**; by night, **night** -- the fire row's fixed seat in
the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where fire's own sense is seeing. The seat is
the tradition's arithmetic before it is a preference: Leo is the fixed fire sign, and the
grid holds exactly one fire-fixed seat, so the placement lands there the way a sum lands.
Chitra already wears this pair among the twins, and the sharing is said plainly: fifteen
seats hold twelve signs and twenty-seven stars, so honest seats are shared by design.
Chitra wears the seat as light returned -- a jewel, brilliance received by whoever looks.
The Sun in Leo wears it as light at the source -- the lamp itself, seeing as the Sun's own
act, self-knowledge as that light turned toward where it stands. The jewel and the lamp
need each other, and one seat holds both faces, cubist-fashion. By night the word is
**night**, and Twilight's rule holds -- calm euphoria rather than gloom. Night is the sky
that lets one flame be enough: the audience set down, the warmth kept, the flame tended
rather than displayed. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word carries the light out and inward at
once, and the night-word rests it without asking to be seen.
