The classical room reads the sky the way the tradition hands it down. This room reads the
same sky again, at an angle, and by devotion -- bhakti names the love the reading serves. Cubism paints one subject from several sides at once. So a page
here takes a classical teaching -- a nakshatra, a house, a yoga -- and shows its faces
together: the teaching, the measurement, the working habit it feeds, and the pair of words
it wears in the Kyri 6 palettes, light terra by day and dark euphoria by night.

## What the sweep does

The classical room holds ninety-five pages (counted `2026-08-26`). The sweep walks them and
reimagines each worth carrying: New Gauge for the bounded claim, Civic for what the reading
rewards, Tame for the discipline, Twilight for the register, and Kyri 6 for the lens -- the
fifteen-seat palette grid and its tanmatra senses are this room's own working vocabulary.
The classical pages stay where they stand. Personal readings stay withheld from the seed,
exactly as the classical room already keeps them.
