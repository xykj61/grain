## The teaching, kept whole

The sixth house holds difficulty and its overcoming: daily work and service, health and its
troubles, conflict, obstacles, and the effort it takes to set things right. Set the Moon there
-- the feeling heart in the house of struggle -- and the emotional nature engages challenge
itself. This heart feels most alive with a problem to solve, a wrong to correct, a hard thing
to master. It sharpens against opposition, thrives in friction that would wear another heart
down, carries burdens others set aside, and takes a quiet pride in what it endures.

The classical page names the shadow gently. The same engagement can turn restless: a heart
tuned to problems can find the flaw before the good, worry over health and small troubles,
manufacture conflict where none is needed, and press hard on itself and others, mistaking
criticism for care. The medicine the page offers is rest allowed -- notice when the fight is
won, and warm the critical eye into a caring one. And the gift stands past the shadow: this
placement transmutes suffering into contribution, hardship into skill, and conflict into
progress that helps many. A reading honors the strength and the service first, then softly
offers the invitation. The heart that meets the fight and, having won it, heals -- that is the
whole turning of the placement.

## The habit it feeds

The sixth-house Moon is already a working discipline, so the translation asks no forcing. The
heart that notices what is wrong and feels the pull to fix it is the reds-first reflex: name
the fault plainly, book the fix, and work it before new work opens. The deep satisfaction in
overcoming is the fix closed honestly -- a witness run green on metal, never a claim. The
medicine is the boundary the habit needs most: the green witness is the bell that says the
fight is won, and rest after the bell is part of the discipline rather than a lapse. A heart
so accustomed to struggle that it manufactures conflict is, in a tree, the sweep that goes
hunting for unrelated drift -- so the fix stays a ratchet, tightened where the work is already
open, and the line rests when the line is green. The critical eye warmed into care is review
as service: name what the module does well before naming the flaw, the same order the classical
reading keeps. Devotion completes the loop -- the work is service, the burden carried is an
offering, and the healing that follows the fight is the point of the fight.

## The pair it wears

By day this placement wears **river**; by night, **tide** -- the water element's cardinal seat
in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the river cuts and water's own sense, at
the fixed seat beside it, is taste. The why is the teaching's own shape. The Moon is the
watery heart, and the sixth house is the stone: a river meets rock and answers with neither
retreat nor rage -- it keeps flowing, and the wearing is the winning. The channel it cuts
becomes the bed that carries water onward to fields, which is the gift face exactly: struggle
turned to service, hardship worn into a shape that helps many. And the night word is the
Moon's own, since the tide is the one water the Moon visibly moves. The ebb is rest on
schedule -- the fight handed back to the sea at dusk, whole and calm, returning with the
morning. Notice when the fight is won and let peace return: that is the tide's entire nature,
said as water. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word presses patiently against the stone, and the
night-word lets the sea take the struggle back, keeping the channel.
