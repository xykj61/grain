## The teaching, kept whole

The nodes begin as geometry. They are the two points where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's,
the places where eclipses are born, and from that single fact the classical page unfolds their
whole nature. One node reaches up and out, amplifying, drawing things into the open. The other
draws down and in, deepening, pulling things inward. Both keep the eclipse in their character:
sudden and radical change, and the pull of what feels beyond our control. Wherever they fall,
they exaggerate, they compel, and they polarize.

On the Taurus-Scorpio axis they do all of this to one fertile tension. Both signs stand fixed,
so their shared strength is commitment and depth -- a person neither shallow nor easily moved.
Earth and water together bring fertility and richness. Yet the same two elements hold a real
opposition, and the nodes amplify it into a felt divide: the practical against the emotional,
the rational against the intuitive, the real against the imagined, and finally substance
against form. The healing the page teaches is a weaving rather than a verdict. Substance and
form give each other life -- the inner force generates a form so that it can exist and act in
the world, and the form expresses and empowers the force. And the two nodes sit asymmetrically
in a life: the upward node marks the side a person naturally wants more of, the deepening node
the side they are inclined to want less of, so the axis tilts by placement while the lesson
stays one lesson. Read it as an invitation to integration, and the nodes become a teacher of
wholeness.

## The habit it feeds

The felt divide translates into a working tree without any forcing, because the tree already
legislates its healing. Substance against form is the code against the prose, the mechanism
against the metaphor, the green witness against the essay that says why it matters -- and the
tree's own laws answer as the page answers: weave, rather than rank. The mechanism sentence
comes first and the metaphor rides on it; the doc moves in the same commit as the code it
describes. The inner force generates a form so it can act in the world -- a design lands as a
module; the form expresses and empowers the force -- a witness proves the design true. And the
node asymmetry names a builder's own tilt kindly. Every hand has a side it amplifies, the
proof or the prose, and a side it quietly draws down. The habit is to notice which pull runs
and let the next round book the neglected bank, so the round ships whole: the thing made, and
the thing said, each giving the other its life.

## The pair it wears

By day this axis wears **river**; by night, **tide** -- the water element's cardinal seat in
the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the river cuts and the tide answers it in
the dark. The why is the page's own geometry. The nodes are born of the Moon's path, and the
tide is the Moon's pull made visible in water: the flood reaching up and out, the ebb drawing
down and in, two motions of one water that no shore ranks. That is the night face -- the pull
felt as beyond control, kept calm, trusted to turn. The day face is the divide already healed:
a river is water and earth giving each other life, the current cutting a channel so it can
move through the world, the channel carrying and empowering the current. The signs stand
fixed, and the pair sits at the cardinal seat on purpose -- the nodes are the movers upon
that fixed ground, and the teaching is about the pull. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word
carves its one channel forward, and the night-word lets the water rise and fall without
choosing a direction, keeping both.
