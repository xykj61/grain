## The teaching, kept whole

The Moon is the chart's softest and most receptive light, and it thrives when kept company. The
whole craft turns on one gentle question: who stands beside it? A real planet -- one of the five
moving stars, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, or Saturn -- in the whole sign just behind the Moon
or just ahead of it lends the soft light strength, and the shape of that company forms one of
four named conditions. The nodes and the far invisible bodies stand outside the count, because a
neighbor in this craft is a visible light keeping watch.

A planet in the sign that follows the Moon nourishes it from within: the roots grow firm, the
mind learns to hold its aim, and the heart earns and keeps its own support, the way a steady
hand holds a household together. A planet in the sign that goes before the Moon nourishes it
outward: someone walks the road first, so the heart speaks with ease, moves with visible grace,
and takes honest pleasure in the senses. Planets on both sides at once leave the Moon
surrounded -- attended front and back, given much, free to lean on others without fear of the
ground giving way. And a Moon with neither side kept is lonesome, inclined toward sadness and a
smaller station than its origins promised. That last is the tender case, and the classical page
is emphatic about what it is: a first reading, never a verdict. Company reaches the Moon by
angle too -- real planets in the fourth, seventh, or tenth sign from it quietly cancel the
loneliness, as does the Moon standing in an angle to the ascendant, and a Moon strong in its own
right asks for no escort at all. The page's one illustration is Krishna: Moon exalted in Taurus
upon the ascendant, lonesome by the letter, carried from a palace to a farming village -- and
that village life the sweetest fortune imaginable, because so strong a Moon carries its own
confidence.

Two honest notes ride with the table, telling teaching and arithmetic apart in so many words.
The adjacency is arithmetic: whole signs only, so a neighbor is found by counting one sign in
either direction, and the fourth, seventh, and tenth are found by counting on the same wheel --
sums any reader can check. What a follower or a leader *means* -- the firm roots, the easy
grace, the held heart -- is teaching, carried on the tradition's word. The exclusion of the
nodes is teaching too (what a neighbor *is*), while the resulting count of five is arithmetic
once that teaching is given. The page writes no degrees and no orbs; its arithmetic begins and
ends at the sign boundary.

## The habit it feeds

The craft translates into a working tree without any forcing, because it is already a review
method. A module is a soft light, and the first question is the Moon's own: who stands beside
it? The witness at its back is the follower -- proof that steadies it from within, so the module
holds its aim and earns trust. The front door ahead of it is the leader -- the README that walks
out and meets the reader first, so the module speaks with ease. A module with both is
surrounded, and others may lean on it freely. And a module with neither is lonesome -- which the
habit treats exactly as the craft does: a first reading, never a verdict. Before anyone declares
a thing unsupported, check the angles -- the suite that runs it from a distance, the index that
seats it by name, or a selftest strong in the module's own body, needing no escort. Color the
reading by the neighbor's nature, since a witness-neighbor and a door-neighbor lend different
strengths. Then let the whole picture, weighed together, decide. Devotion completes the loop:
keeping company with the soft lights of a tree -- the quiet modules, the tender cases -- is the
love the reading serves.

## The pair it wears

By day this craft wears **love**; by night, **longing** -- the water element's dual seat in the
fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where love flows onward and water's own sense, at the
fixed seat beside it, is taste. The why is the craft's own current. Every face of the teaching
is love flowing toward a receptive light: the follower's steadying, the leader's guiding, the
surrounder's abundance -- company moving to the Moon the way water moves to the low, soft place.
The dual seat fits because the craft itself adapts and dissolves onward: four conditions, and
the hardest of them dissolved by a wider look. And the dark twin keeps Twilight's rule -- calm,
never gloom. Longing is the lonesome Moon's honest register, love aimed at company that has yet
to arrive; and the Krishna case shows longing carrying its own sweetness, the exalted Moon whole
in the village night. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word carries the company in, and the
night-word keeps the heart tender while the company is still on the road.
