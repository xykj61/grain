## The teaching, kept whole

The plain question -- what is your sign -- has always drawn three half-answers. The rising
sign, the Moon sign, and the Sun sign each feel partly right, and choosing one leaves the
others unspoken. The classical page answers the plain question plainly by consulting
everything. For one chart the method moves in three steps: note each sign that holds a
planet, give each planet a weighted share, and tally the shares by sign until the totals
speak. The weighting is an order the page teaches in words -- the rising sign counts most,
the Moon strongly, the Sun a smaller share, and the remaining planets a middling one -- and
it helps to picture the tally, so the numbers become an intuition.

The same three steps read each subdivision, and each speaks for its own domain: the seventh
voices creativity and sexuality, the ninth the inner self one truly wants to be, the tenth
success and the talents that carry a person into the world, the twelfth the inheritance one
is born into, the sixteenth inner peace and outer ease. Then the charts themselves are
weighted -- the birth chart most dominant, the ninth at roughly three-quarters of it, the
seventh and twelfth at about half, the tenth and sixteenth at about a quarter -- multiplied
through and added, and a single balance appears. The answer stays sayable: mostly one sign,
a strong second, a quieter third. And the page's own caution rides with it. The whole voice
names the deep character rather than the surface manner, so a whole-voice Capricorn may wear
another face outwardly while the tenacity runs underneath; for the outward detail one
returns to the single lights, and the whole and the lights stay companions.

## The habit it feeds

The method is already a working habit, and it translates without forcing. When someone asks
what a tree is, the honest answer weighs every voice in it: the front door counts most, the
foundations strongly, a scratch file a middling share -- then the tally speaks, and the
answer stays one sayable sentence resting on the whole. The tree already lives this shape
wherever many readings become one word: a suite of witnesses summed into a single GREEN
line, several meters meaned into one grade. The teaching adds the two disciplines that keep
such a composite honest. Weight before you sum, because the lights carry different force;
and keep both readings alive, because the whole voice names the deep character while the
single lights hold the detail. A composite that replaces reading the parts has eaten its own
depth; a pile of parts with no spoken answer has hidden its own shape. Depth and plainness
keep company -- richness made shareable is the page's own gift, and a round can practice it
every time it writes a one-line answer over a many-file truth.

## The pair it wears

By day this teaching wears **sound**; by night, **silence** -- aether's fixed seat in the
fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where sound is aether's own sense and the fixed
column is where a thing holds steady. The why is the page's own word: voice. Aether is the
medium every voice sounds in, belonging to no single light, exactly as the true sign belongs
to no single planet and no single chart -- it is the space all the lights speak into, and
the fixed seat suits what holds steady beneath the moving sky. By day, sound: the choir of
lights lands on one word, the tally said aloud, richness made shareable. By night, silence:
the whole sky rests under the spoken name, every light that was counted keeping its quiet
counsel, saying nothing and holding everything. Aim, then dissolve -- the day-word gives the
answer its voice, and the night-word gives the answer its depth.
