One placement, four faces, held together the way a cubist canvas holds a face: the teaching
as the classical room keeps it, the counts the page itself carries, the habit those counts
feed in a working tree, and the pair of words the placement wears by day and by night. Bhakti
names the love the reading serves -- and this page's whole subject is love, read again at an
angle, as an act of devotion.

## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page opens by naming what each party brings. Venus carries love, beauty, union,
the five senses, value, and the art of relating. Gemini is air and intellect -- multiplicity,
exchange, communication, curiosity, and the crossing of thresholds. Set them together and the
page's central turn appears: at its highest, Venus longs to dissolve every difference and
merge into oneness; in Gemini it does the opposite and equally beautiful thing, holding union
*through* difference. Many bright nodes, woven into one living connection. The page gives
this Venus its truest name in a single image: a pollinator rather than a possessor.

So this Venus loves through language. Its charm is conversational -- curiosity about another
that lets them feel seen and heard, which the page counts among the deepest human needs. Two
people can fall for each other through talk alone, before ever meeting. The placement stays
youthful and playful, holds contradictions gracefully, speaks in metaphor, and finds delight
in an intimate conversation. Its emblem is the sand mandala the monks make and release in a
single day: impermanent, and as sacred as anything carved to last. Love here can be light and
still real, brief and still holy.

The shadows are named with care, and kindly. Air rarely settles, so rootedness -- the stuff
loyalty and endurance are made of -- arrives late. This Venus tends to *think* its love
rather than feel it; the same charm that delights can tip into performance, and words can
quietly stand where feeling belongs. Depth can read as airless, and the first impulse when
things grow heavy can be to drift toward a fresh spark. The page holds all of this gently:
the placement is benefic and without malice, the shadows appear only where self-awareness
has yet to grow, and its native receptivity makes it a wonderful student of love.

## The habit it feeds

A working tree already loves the way this Venus loves: through language. Session logs, review
passes, the round that carries understanding between modules -- the weave is conversational,
and the pollinator is the honest picture of a good collaborator, spreading what it learns
rather than possessing what it built. The shadow translates just as directly. Prose can
replace proof the way banter replaced feeling: a confident claim standing exactly where a
witness belongs. So the five lessons land on disciplines the tree has already seated. Talk to
touch is witness before narrative -- a green run on metal is love brought down into the body
of the work. Novelty to patience is the finishing edge: circle back and land the round begun,
rather than leaving a trail of bright unfinished ones. Pleasing to truthful is the honest
red, reported plainly and once. Stay with the heaviness is reds-first, booking the allocation
until the fix closes. And value without applause is measurement beats memory: the count
stands whether anyone watches. The page's closing breath -- speak a little less and feel a
little more -- arrives here as: narrate a little less, and measure a little more.

## The pair it wears

By day this placement wears **senses**; by night, **trance** -- the air element's dual seat
in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the senses play and air's own sense, at
the fixed seat beside them, is touch. The fit is the sign's own address said plainly: Gemini
is air, and Gemini is a dual sign, so element and column both belong to it before any
choosing begins -- and the day-word is nearly the page's first line, since the five senses
are among the six domains Venus carries. The senses at play are this Venus in daylight: the
bright exchange, the many nodes, the pollinator's rounds. And the dark twin keeps Twilight's
rule -- calm, never gloom. Trance is the same conversation deepened past its own words, the
clever surface dissolved into one slow current, the lesson *stay with the heaviness* held as
sweetness rather than weight. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word plays across the whole bright
field, and the night-word gathers the field into depth -- one seat away from touch and
presence, which is exactly where the lessons point.
