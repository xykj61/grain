One subject, four faces, shown together. The classical room reads the exalted Moon the way the
tradition hands it down; this page turns the same teaching in the dark and lets each face catch
what light there is. The subject is Taurus itself -- the ground -- and the Moon that comes to
rest on it.

## The teaching, face on

The Moon is the mind, the body, the feelings -- the inner weather, always moving, always seeking
a place to settle. Taurus is the ground that answers the seeking: fixed, grounded, the sign of
enduring abundance, where even as one thing is used up, more is already growing. When the Moon
arrives here the tradition calls it exalted -- its highest, most supported placement -- which is
a quiet way of saying that everything this planet longs for, this soil provides.

The fruit is peace of mind: a mind steady as a bull, held through its own rise and fall. On the
dim days and on the brimming days, the same earth answers -- still good, still held, still loved.
From that fullness comes the real capacity to love, since a person who feels full has something
to give, and from that steadiness comes a calm precise usefulness, the way fine fast work asks
for a steady hand.

The classical page keeps two honest edges, and this page keeps them too. First, the shadow:
needs so well met can soften the hunger that sends others into the depths, so reach here comes
best from chosen purpose rather than inner lack -- a gentle cost, held gently. Second, the
correction: exaltation speaks of the mind's contentment, never of the size of a bank account,
and a placement is a language and a mirror rather than a verdict. The living remains the
native's own.

## The habit it feeds

A working tree keeps this teaching without knowing its name. The steady ground under a demanding
lap is the suite that runs green, the bound named at construction, the reference that resolves --
the tree's own Taurus, the quiet assurance that there is enough and more is growing. On that
ground a hand does fine fast work calmly, exactly as the teaching says a settled mind can.

And the shadow translates whole: a board that is all green can lull. When nothing aches, the
push to dig can soften, so this tree chooses its depth on purpose -- crux-first, from the living
card -- rather than waiting for a lack to drive it. Contentment as a base for effort, never a
reason to drift: the exalted Moon's own lesson, landed as a scheduling discipline. Devotion is
the room's angle, and here devotion looks like tending the ground so the work can rest on it.

## The pair it wears

**Aroma by day, incense by night.** The palette grid in `../kyri/receipt.rye` seats five element
rows by three modalities, and Taurus is the tradition's fixed earth -- so this teaching wears the
grid's own fixed-earth cell, the one seat that matches it modality for modality, element for
element. The fixed column carries each element's own sense, and earth's sense is smell: aroma,
the sense that reaches memory most directly, the way this Moon holds its ground through every
rise and fall.

At night the pairing deepens rather than changes. Aroma becomes incense -- the same sense,
offered. Incense is fragrance given upward on purpose, which is bhakti in one image: the settled
fullness of the exalted Moon turned from comfort into offering. By day the ground simply holds;
by night the ground burns sweet, and the holding becomes a gift.
