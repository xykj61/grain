One star, four faces, held together the way a cubist canvas holds a face: the teaching as the
classical room keeps it, the counts the page itself carries, the habit those counts feed in a
working tree, and the pair of words the star wears by day and by night. Bhakti names the love
the reading serves -- this page reads Ashvini as an act of devotion, and says so at the door.

## The teaching, kept whole

Ashvini opens its page as the first of the lunar stars, and it carries the nature of a horse:
swift and beautiful. The classical page unfolds everything from those two words. Swift means
quick, and quick means done soon -- a bright, efficient intelligence that finds the short clean
path. Beautiful means enjoyable, and beauty means health, and health means strength. The star
lives turned outward, warm and confident, glad among others; a horse walks into a gathering
and leaves with companions.

Then come the two secrets the page teaches, each depending on the other. First: recognize the
gift already carried. Every being holds some real asset, because every being is a spark of the
divine -- the frame the classical page holds from the Srimad Bhagavatam -- so confidence is
simply accurate self-recognition. Second: find the courage to use the gift in the open. Music
played alone at midnight stays a private sweetness; the same music on a stage, out where
people gather, changes everything. And the result follows on its own: companions arrive, each
carrying an excellence the others lack, because wholeness belongs to the divine alone. Find
the gift, show it, gather the people, move together. That is the whole turning of the star.

## The habit it feeds

The two secrets translate into a working tree without any forcing, because they are already a
method. The first secret is a survey done kindly: measure what the tree already does well
before longing for what it lacks. Accurate self-recognition is a count, and a count is
something a round can run. The second secret is the stage: a green witness shown in the open,
a round shipped small and soon, work published where companions can find it. The module kept
private at midnight gathers no collaborators; the same module sent, with its proof visible,
draws exactly the hands and modules that carry what it lacks. Swiftness sets the tempo --
prefer the short clean path, the small round that lands today over the grand one that waits.
Devotion completes the loop: the gift was given, so showing it is thanks, and the companions
who gather are the answer to the offering.

## The pair it wears

By day Ashvini wears **rockstar**; by night, **hermit** -- the fire element's cardinal seat in
the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the rockstar ignites and fire's own sense,
at the fixed seat beside it, is vision. The why is the page's own image. The second secret
places the musician on a stage, out where people gather: that is the rockstar's day, the gift
shown in the open, recognition drawn toward vitality. And the dark twin keeps Twilight's rule
-- calm, never gloom. The hermit is the same musician in the quiet room at midnight, the gift
at rest rather than hidden: strings tuned, strength gathered, the next stage already glowing
somewhere ahead. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word rides out to meet the gathering, and the
night-word lets the gathering go, keeping the music.
