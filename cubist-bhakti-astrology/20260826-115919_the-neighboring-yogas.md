## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page carries one formula across the chart's three most important lights. A real
planet -- one of the five moving stars -- standing in the whole sign just behind a focal point
becomes its supporter; one in the sign just ahead becomes its neighbor; company on both sides
at once becomes the surrounder. The point takes on the nature of its company. The Moon,
softest of the three lights, feels this most fully; the Sun feels it clearly; the ascendant,
the horizon itself, feels it least, registering only the strongest, both-sided form, which the
tradition calls the scissors.

At the Sun the tradition draws a line the Moon's reading left undrawn, between soft planets
and hard ones. Soft company lends the Sun gentleness: fortune, ease, patience, command, and
integrity from behind; renown worn modestly, generosity, and a warmth people love from ahead;
and on both sides beauty of manner, an endearing voice, taste, eloquence, and wealth. Hard
company lends the Sun its edge, a sharper and more self-guarding coloring, skilled in
difficulty and in conflict. The line stays honest on both sides: hard names tough and
forceful, soft names gentle and forgiving, each strong in its own field, and the naming
carries description rather than verdict. Above the whole method stands its clearest case, the
wholly auspicious: a soft supporter free of any hard aspect, whose unblemished influence
yields virtue, contentment, fortune, respect, gentle nature, loving speech, and wealth. The
grammar throughout is a spectrum rather than a switch -- the purer the company, the fuller the
result -- and the closing principle is plain: the flavor a focal point takes on follows the
purity of its neighborhood.

## The habit it feeds

The formula translates into a working tree without any forcing, because it is already a
review discipline. Read the neighborhood before you speak: before judging or moving a module,
read what stands just behind it -- the modules it builds on, its supporters -- and what stands
just ahead -- the callers that depend on it, its neighbors. References are promises, so the
grep for inbound citations is the chart cast before the change. The soft-and-hard line maps
kindly too: the gentle door document and the strict refusing witness are both company, each
strong in its own field, and a tree wants both flavors standing near its important lights.
Purity shades confidence the way the spectrum teaches -- a module held on both sides by green
witnesses is read boldly, mixed company mutes the claim -- and the sensitivity gradient
orders the care: living prose takes flavor from every neighbor, a witness registers company
clearly, and a sealed ledger, horizon-still like the ascendant, answers only the strongest
configuration around it.

## The pair it wears

By day this teaching wears **taste**; by night, **nectar** -- the water element's fixed seat
in the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where taste is water's own sense, the
tanmatra the fixed column carries. The why is the source's own closing word: these yogas
describe the flavor a focal point takes on from its company, and flavor is tasted. The fixed
seat fits twice over -- a fixed word sustains, and the teaching's stillest light, the
ascendant, holds still while the sky turns around it, flavored by what stands beside it. The
day-word even sits inside the source's own gift list, where both-sided soft company grants
taste itself. And the dark twin keeps Twilight's rule -- calm, never gloom. Nectar is the
purest form read at night: the unblemished supporter distilled to sweetness, loving speech
aged into something a reader can drink. Aim, then dissolve: taste discerns the neighborhood
sip by sip, and nectar is what remains when the company runs pure.
