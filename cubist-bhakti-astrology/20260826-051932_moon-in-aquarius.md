## The teaching, kept whole

The Moon is the feeling heart, and Aquarius is Saturn's fixed air -- the sign where the wish to
move meets the wish to hold steady. The classical page sets these two together and reads a woven
heart: an emotional nature that finds itself by weaving outward into others rather than by
gathering inward. Aquarius stands opposite Leo, so where Leo consolidates one bright single
self, Aquarius distributes that self across a group and lives gladly as one thread in a larger
weave. The group's likes become its own likes; its loyalty runs deep and asks little of the
spotlight; the page hears it as the steady bass line that holds a song together.

Two tender edges live inside the warmth, and the page meets both gently. The first is dilution:
a self so woven into others can lose track of its own outline, growing dependent on approval.
The second rises from Saturn's rulership, whose clear unsentimental analysis, turned inward, can
drain the ordinary shine of things and leave a quiet dissatisfaction wearing calm's clothes.
Named kindly, this is a spare honest heart that has looked closely and found the shine wearing
thin. The medicine the page gives is twofold -- companionship, and a remembered sense of the
heart's own worth -- since an air heart truly needs friends to talk with and lean on. At its
best the placement gives genuine selflessness, a Spartan simplicity that lands as contentment,
and a tactile artist's touch the page pictures as the potter at the wheel: patient hands, exact
pressure, a feel for timing and repetition that turns plain clay into something quietly
beautiful. Keep one private thread of selfhood intact inside the weave, the reading counsels,
and the woven heart becomes both indispensable and free.

## The habit it feeds

A working tree is full of woven hearts, and the teaching names them without any forcing. The
bass-line module is the one every other module leans on -- the copy routine, the bound check,
the witness harness -- serving the whole song while asking for no spotlight, and the tree runs
on exactly that loyalty. The two shadows translate whole. Dilution is the module so woven into
its callers that its own outline blurs: the medicine is the page's own -- keep one private
thread, which in a tree means the module keeps its own named bounds, its own invariants, its own
witness, even while serving everything. And Saturn's drained shine is the audit that runs so
long alone it forgets why the work was sweet: the medicine again is companionship, which this
tree already practices as the check-in -- the round that ends by talking to someone. The potter
at the wheel is the ratchet: patient, exact, repeated pressure, the same witness run again and
again until plain clay holds a shape. Devotion is the room's angle, and here devotion looks like
the thread's kind of love -- carrying the weave, softening the room, and staying whole enough to
keep giving.

## The pair it wears

**Touch by day, presence by night.** The palette grid in `kyri/receipt.rye` seats five
element rows by three modalities -- cardinal, fixed, dual -- and Aquarius is the tradition's
fixed air, so this teaching wears the grid's own fixed-air cell, the one seat that matches it
element for element, modality for modality. The fixed column carries each element's own sense,
and air's sense is touch: the tanmatra the source itself reaches for when it hands the gift to
the potter -- patient hands, exact pressure, the tactile sensitivity the page names outright.
The day-word is the fit twice earned: the seat matches the sign, and the sense matches the gift.

At night touch deepens to presence, and the pairing tells the placement's whole secret. Presence
is touch with the hands at rest -- the companion felt in the room rather than heard, the bass
line sensed in the body, the thread that holds the weave without announcing itself. That is the
woven heart at its truest: being with the group is the gift, before any single thing it does.
Aim, then dissolve -- by day the hands shape the clay, and by night the same nearness simply
holds, warm in the dark, needing nothing back.
