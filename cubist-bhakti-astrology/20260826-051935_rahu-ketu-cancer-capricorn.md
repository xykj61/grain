## The teaching, kept whole

The classical page begins in the sky itself. The lunar nodes are the two places where the
Moon's path crosses the Sun's, and eclipses are born there -- so the nodes carry the eclipse's
own character: change that arrives suddenly and goes deep, and the felt presence of forces
larger than a person's steering. One node lifts up and out, amplifying whatever it touches and
carrying it into the open; the other draws down and in, deepening and compressing. Wherever
the pair falls, the page teaches, they exaggerate, compel, and polarize -- and on the axis of
Cancer and Capricorn they press upon one of the heaviest tensions the zodiac holds.

The two signs agree before they argue. Both are cardinal, so their shared gift is ambition and
initiative; water and earth meet here, and under Saturn's steady hand the meeting reads as
weight and momentum -- a freight-train quality, hard to start and harder to stop, that bears a
person straight toward a goal. Yet the elements also hold a true opposition: water runs deep
and inward, earth stays practical and close to the surface. The nodes magnify that difference
into a lived divide -- the internal against the external, feeling against practicality,
imagination against hard realism -- the tender Moon set beside austere Saturn.

The healing the page gives is a joining. Let the two sides serve each other: the outer gives
shape to the inner, and the inner gives soul to the outer. A feeling needs a form to become
real, and a form needs a feeling to become worth having. Some carry this axis outwardly,
making public what others keep private; some carry it inwardly, drawn toward contemplation and
the quiet interior life -- and the outward node marks the side wanted more, the inward node
the side wanted less, so the tilt follows the seating. When a person stops splitting the two
worlds and lets each complete the other, the same nodes that pulled them apart become drive
and presence, and the long work of joining them is the one theme running under all of it.

## The habit it feeds

The divide translates into a working tree without forcing, because the tree already lives on
this axis. The inner life here is the reasoning -- the design essay, the session log's think
lines, the why beside every bound; the outer life is the artifact -- the code that runs, the
witness GREEN on metal. The page's healing is a rule this tree has already seated in drier
words: docs and implementation stay synced, changed in the same commit, because a claim needs
running code to become real and running code needs its why to be worth keeping. Shape and
soul, joined at every send. The freight-train momentum is the round rhythm itself, and the
teaching says plainly what to do with a thing hard to stop: write the stops down before
starting -- the checkpoint recorded before the cut, the gate named in the closing line. And
the two nodes are the tree's own two gestures, each sanctioned and neither left to run alone:
one amplifies and carries into the open -- the round shipped, the seed published, the proof
shown where companions can find it -- and one draws down and in -- the fold, the prune done
with care, the release of what no longer serves. Held together, the two gestures are momentum
seeking integration, which is how the page asks the whole axis to be read.

## The pair it wears

By day this axis wears **grain**; by night, **mist** -- the earth element's cardinal seat in
the fifteen-pair grid of `kyri/receipt.rye`, where the row reads *the grain seeds* and the
night list answers with earth's own dark. The fit begins in the grid: the tradition seats
Capricorn as the cardinal sign of earth, and both signs of this axis stand in the cardinal
column, so the pair rests exactly where the axis touches the ground. Then the pair earns it in
the teaching's own words, because this one seat holds both elements the nodes press together.
Grain is the outer life given shape -- the seed hard and practical, Saturn's patient earth,
the form a feeling must take to become real. Mist is the inner life arrived at night -- water
laid softly over the same field, Cancer's tenderness diffused across Capricorn's ground, the
soul a form must hold to be worth having. One seat, two elements, day and dark: the pair
performs the joining the page teaches. And the night word keeps Twilight's rule, calm and
never gloom -- mist is the field still present, its edges gentled, the harvest resting rather
than lost. Aim, then dissolve: the day-word drives the seed into the ground, and the
night-word lets the ground breathe upward, keeping the water and the earth as one weather.
