## The teaching, kept whole

A planet in a house is among the simplest things to name and among the richest to read well,
and the classical page opens with the trap inside that simplicity: with so few pieces,
everything rests on how they are combined. So the page hands down a small method and a larger
discipline, and the two belong together.

The method begins in the sky. A house is a region of it, and its meaning grows from where
that region sits: the eastern horizon is sunrise, so the first house carries birth,
visibility, and the self; the region just above it holds the fading light, so the twelfth
thins what the first establishes, visibility and vitality easing toward the unseen. Read this
way, a house is a place whose nature can be reasoned out from its position rather than a list
to hold in memory. Then the planet is understood just as plainly -- the Sun is light, so it
means visibility, selfhood, authority, and vitality -- and the two are set side by side. Where
they speak of the same thing, the placement flows. Where they pull apart, light meeting the
house that dims light, the exact tension worth interpreting stands named. One resonance
repays keeping: a hard, assertive planet settles well into a hard house and strains a soft
one, while a gentle planet does the reverse, and the growth houses -- the third, the sixth,
the tenth, the eleventh -- tend to give a forceful planet honest work.

The discipline is the painter's. A placement is a single color; a child squeezes it straight
onto the page, while a painter blends it with every other color the chart provides -- the
planet's dignity, its strength, the aspects it receives, the combinations it forms. This is
why the same difficult placement returns again and again in the charts of confident,
accomplished people: a strong first house, a strong tenth, a dignified house-lord, and a
fortunate combination or two quietly outweigh it. Every placement is a beginning rather than
a verdict, held lightly while the rest of the chart has its say.

## The habit it feeds

The method translates into a working tree without any forcing, because it is already a
review. Derive from position first: a module's meaning grows from where it sits in the tree,
the way a house's meaning grows from its place in the sky, so the reader reasons the room out
before opening the file. Then set two things side by side and name agreement and clash
honestly -- a doc's claim beside the code's behavior, a plan beside the witness that ran --
which is the whole engine of keeping docs and implementation synced. The hard-and-soft
resonance is tool fit: a strict, forceful check earns its keep in the growth rooms of the
tree, while a tender surface asks a lighter hand. And the painter's discipline is the deepest
carry: one green witness is a single color, never the whole painting. A claim about the tree
is trusted the way a placement is trusted -- blended with the suite, the ledger, and the
fascia around it -- and a red is read as a beginning rather than a verdict, the exact tension
named so the next round knows where to work. Devotion completes the loop: reading the whole
before judging the part is a kindness, and the craft exists to be fair to what it reads.
