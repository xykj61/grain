## The teaching, kept whole

The ordinary reading of the ninth chart stops at the division-sign: this planet stands in
Capricorn of the ninth, and there the matter rests. The finer technique the classical page
teaches goes one step further. Find the exact degree a planet holds *within* its
division-sign, then set that division-planet back onto the birth wheel at that degree, on the
same circle as the real chart, and watch where it lands. A division-planet that falls beside a
birth planet or a house-cusp is announcing itself: this inner factor matters far more than the
surface reading would suggest, joined to that point through the ninth harmonic, alive in the
outward life. A division-planet that lands nowhere in particular is resting quiet, and the
reader may let it rest. The overlay sorts the loud from the quiet with real precision, and a
chart that seemed mild can suddenly show its clear signature.

The page carries a lighter form of the same key, always available: the division-ascendant
falls into some house of the birth chart every time, and locating that house already says a
great deal about where a person's essence expresses itself. And it answers the honest doubt --
whether moving a planet between signs and reading it there is a trick -- by showing the
geometry underneath. A subdivision chart pictures a real relationship on the real circle; the
overlay only makes visible where those rays were pointing all along. The counsel that closes
the teaching is restraint: read the conjunctions fully and first, because stacking further
aspects upon the overlay thins the signal the conjunctions already carry whole.
