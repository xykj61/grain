## Five Commitments, Spoken in Our Own Words

**One -- the ledger and the law live apart.** The record of who holds what is a bare, append-friendly store; every rule about *how* holdings change lives beside it and can be amended by governance without ever rewriting a record. Data accretes; law amends. This is Weave's own heart, seated at the identity ledger's scale.

**Two -- a point is a single-keeper record.** Each Kumara point lives as one record on the settlement ledger, carrying a unique name, a keeper, and a version that climbs with every change. A record kept by one hand rides the fast path -- no global ordering spent on one keeper's own affairs -- and only the few surfaces the whole world must read together pay the price of shared consensus. The held question beneath this commitment is the sharpest of the sketch: *how small can the shared surface be -- and could it be nothing at all?*

**Three -- powers are capability records.** The keeper's key alone can transfer a point. Beneath it stand limited siblings, each a small transferable record of its own: a **management capability** that rotates networking keys and tends sponsorship, a **spawn capability** that issues children where scarcity allows, and a temporary **transfer capability** that makes handoff a deliberate two-step. Because a record's name can stand wherever a keeper's would, a point may keep its own capabilities -- identity as a small household of records under one roof.

**Four -- networking keys rotate on their own counters.** The signing key that Comlink speaks with lives as a versioned field on the point, climbing a **key counter** on ordinary rotation and a **reset counter** when continuity must start over -- and neither counter ever moves the keeper's key. A compromised ship heals itself while its deed sits cold.

**Five -- sponsorship is a default, never a cage.** Every point below the root ranks carries a parent link: arithmetic gives the default parent for free, the link records the chosen one, and **escape** re-parents at the child's word. Service earns the tree; the tree never owns the child.

## Held Open, on Purpose

The **scarcity shape** -- whether Grain keeps the elder ranks of 28 - 216 - 232 or reshapes scarcity for its own network -- is a word for Keaton, weighed in paper two. The **bare-keys counterweight** -- identity as nothing but keys, with no scarcity and no ledger at all -- stands beside the sketch as the honest rival every commitment must beat on merits. The **human-name surface** waits with the custody park, whole. And every capability, counter, and link above is a *descriptor*, never a coin: any of them that wants a true name of its own is a naming round for its own day.

## What Paper Two Takes From Here

The tilaks: the point record, each capability record, the rotation record the wire will read, and the parent link -- each as a type-mark in `til/`, each with one pedestal in `sur/`, all shaped so the fast path stays fast and the shared surface stays ashamed of its own size.
