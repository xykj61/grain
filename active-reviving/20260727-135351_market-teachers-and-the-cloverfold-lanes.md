## The Method

Study what the world already loves, strip each pattern to the concept underneath, and revive the concept on our own organs -- two-handed facts, folded standings pinned at nibs, single-keeper identity, and jurisdiction-aware, moderated commerce. Each lane below is one such concept, one sentence of what it truly is, and one sentence of how it lands here. Every lane is a future lesson or organ of the example app, never a clone: the teachers taught the *need*; the organs decide the *shape*.

## The Thirteen Lanes

**Listings with trust.** A marketplace of large, careful purchases lives or dies on verified truth about the thing listed -- here, a listing is a signed fact-family with its provenance foldable, and trust is the seller's merit standing read at a nib. **Payroll and benefits, kindly.** Paying helpers lawfully is jurisdiction work -- Mandi's JURISDICTION block plus a fact-per-payment MALA lane, exports shaped for the CPA. **The professional graph.** Who has worked with whom is just countersigned facts between two Kumara points; reputation is a fold, never an editable profile. **Receipts that file themselves.** Expense truth is a photographed fact with a class, two hands, and a clock -- the accounting lane's raw grain. **Crowd-gifts for need.** One door, one story, gifts as steering facts into a named pool, the ledger public by construction. **Crowd-launch for making.** Pledges as escrowed facts released on witnessed milestones -- the merit ledger's steering pattern wearing a maker's apron. **The wholesale bazaar.** Many makers, one catalog, terms by jurisdiction -- Mandi's native room. **The tiny-price shelf.** Very small purchases demand very small friction; the fast path's whole argument, with moderation guarding the floor. **Live-shopping rooms.** A stream where showing and selling are one motion -- an outfit (chosen, moderated circle) with a cart, every sale a fact in real time. **Creator tips in the moment.** Applause as micro-steering, folded into the maker's standing while the song still plays. **Books kept honest.** Double-entry is two-handed facts by birth; the ledger lane *is* the bookkeeping product. **The CPA's window.** Professionals need read-only, provable views -- a WOV pin handed to an accountant is an audit that cannot be quietly edited. **The dashboard of one's own money.** A pilot's whole financial verse folded into one calm page, standing at a nib, every number recomputable by the pilot's own hand.

## What the Lanes Share

One skeleton under all thirteen: **fact in, fold out, pin at a nib, steer forward** -- with Kumara signing every hand, Mandi naming every ground, and moderation (the outfits law, Arc V) keeping every room lawful and kind. This is why one small app can teach all of them: the lessons change clothes; the skeleton never changes.

## Landing Order

Lessons three through six of the series already cover the skeleton itself; the lanes then land as **electives, one lane per elective page**, in whatever order the collective's first-readers find most wanted -- the books-kept-honest and CPA-window pair first by counsel's lean, because the companies this very round founds will need them soonest, and vegan-dogfooding our own bookkeeping is the merit ledger's most honest first meal.
