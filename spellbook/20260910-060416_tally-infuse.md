**tally infuse is a planning spell.** It reads a bounded declaration of what a fleet should be
carrying, compares that against the itinerary standing today, and rewrites only the lines that
differ. The output is plan text a person reads. The spell runs as a hand and an agent working a
card together, and it compiles nothing.

The word is built from two seated parts. **Infuse** comes from Brix, where it already means the act
of running a declaration until the world matches it -- `brix/infuse.rye` carries that meaning in
running Rye, and the foundation beside it states the formula. **Tally** comes from the garden
allocator, where bounds live: a region with a stated start, length, and end. Together they name one
gesture -- *infuse an itinerary from a declaration where every list has a ceiling.*

## The formula

```
declaration + itinerary  ->  tally infuse  ->  itinerary'
tally infuse(itinerary') ->  itinerary'          (idempotent: a second pass moves zero lines)
```

Idempotence is the whole promise. A pass that moves lines on every run is a pass whose reader learns
to skim it, and a card nobody reads steers nothing.

## Single-stranded -- one referent per strand

**This is the point of the spell.** A strand carries exactly one referent, and a field holds exactly
one strand. Sunflower's task list, Skate's rendered frame, and Kumara's identity key are three
different referents; braided into one field they arrive as one word where three facts stood, and the
reader receives the loss silently.

| Strand | Its one referent | What it writes into the card |
|---|---|---|
| **task** | a Sunflower task, in TAME shape | the lap line -- what this ship works next |
| **surface** | a Brushstroke frame lowered for a drawn view | the render note |
| **identity** | one Kumara key held by one seat | the signer |
| **route** | one Comlink place, and its hops | the reach |
| **enclosure** | one Dexter room or Pond application | where the lap runs |
| **order** | one Mycelium cord -- the agreed history | the settled fact |
| **seat** | one council row, of the 5 x 3 grid | the rota angle |

**Seven strands, and seven is the ceiling.** An eighth strand waits for its own seating word rather
than riding in beside a sibling.

Two habits keep the strands apart. A field naming two referents splits into two fields before the
pass writes it. And a strand keeps its name across the whole card, so *identity* means the Kumara
key on every ship's block and carries the surface nowhere.

**Alias-sameness rides here undisturbed.** Surf and Skate are peer synonyms for one surface, so both
words reach the same strand and neither outranks the other. Several lawful names over one referent
stay single-stranded; one name over several referents is the braid this spell refuses.

## How a lap runs the spell

1. **Read the declaration.** Name the seven strands and their ceilings, one strand per line.
2. **Read the itinerary standing now**, per ship, bounded at 8 ships.
3. **Compare strand by strand.** A strand agreeing with the card stays untouched.
4. **Label each part's room** from the roster above -- running, design, or split -- so every planned
   lap names which side of the door it works on.
5. **Write only the differing lines**, at most 16 per ship.
6. **Run the pass again.** A second run moving zero lines closes the gesture; a second run moving
   lines names a strand still carrying two referents.

## What this spell leaves alone

**It ships nothing.** `send` carries work to the piers, `kg` continues a lap, and tally infuse writes
plan text and stops there. The custody gates hold exactly as they stand.

**It grades rather than gates.** A card whose strands read cleanly earns a good reading; a braided
field earns a finding and one molt frame, bounded at depth 2. Reds keep their own discipline and
their own ledger.

**It renames zero modules.** The roster reads the tree as the tree is; a part sitting in the design
room today keeps its name and its room until its own seating word arrives.
