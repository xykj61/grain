## Three readings a wall over this owes

**The character class is one reading of two, and it needs the second.** A token here runs 7 to 40
hexadecimal characters carrying at least one of `a`-`f`, which is
[`nib-honesty`](../tools/fixtures/n/nib_honesty_scan.sh)'s own lesson from REDS
`%782`, where an all-decimal generator constant read as an advertised commit.
That clause is necessary and it is not sufficient: **`ed25519` is seven
characters, every one of them hexadecimal**, and it stands beside the word
*parent* in every hardened-derivation body this tree has written. This paper's own
scan header claimed the character class kept it out, and the pen proved otherwise
on the first run. What keeps it out of the gate is the **relation filter** -- those
sentences never say *this commit's own parent*, so they read `loose` and the wall
never sees them. A token rule reads shape; telling a curve name from a commit takes the relation.

**Resolvability grades a checkout rather than a claim.** Six of the ten name a hash this pier
holds nowhere -- pre-rebase objects that resolved on the ship that wrote them and
vanished when the rebase replayed the commit. Measured across four checkouts of
one repository on `20260917`, five of the six resolve on none of them and one
resolves on exactly one peer. So **resolvability is per-checkout**, and a wall
built on it answers differently on eight ships for one body. The scan reports the
genre and never uses a peer's object store to grade a peer's prose.

**And the hook is the wrong door.** At `commit-msg` time `git rev-parse HEAD` is
the prospective parent for a fresh commit and the commit being *replaced* for an
amend, and nothing the hook can read tells those two apart. A hook-side wall would
refuse every correct amend or welcome every wrong fresh commit. This is the
structural twin of REDS `%803` one rule over, where a writer could not derive
whether the hand about to type was amending or committing afresh, and the repair
was to make the hand say which. Here the hook runs before the commit exists, so
the hand it would ask has yet to act. So the reading happens **after the fact**, where the parent is
a fact rather than a forecast, and
[`tools/hooks/commit-msg`](../tools/hooks/commit-msg) is read and never edited.

## The gate is forward-only, and that is the shape rather than a weakness

History is testimony. The ten standing today are pushed, signed, and unrewritable
by this tree's own first law, so a wall counting them would red on every ship for
a fault no lap may repair -- and a guard that reds on work nobody can do is a
guard somebody turns off. The scan takes `--anchor REF` and counts the three claim
genres over `REF..HEAD` alone. The witness spells the anchor once, as the HEAD it
was seated on, so the wall stands at zero the day it lands and reds on the lap the
eleventh arrives.

The three genres are gated **together**. A ghost, an abandoned branch, and a live
ancestor named as the parent are one fault wearing three faces: a hash written
before the rebase that ended up naming something other than the parent. A lap
controls all three with the same move.

## The cure a lap takes, in one line

**Leave the parent hash out of the body and let the card carry it.**
`construction/ITINERARY.md` is repointed by the step that knows -- the amend after
the final rebase, or the follow-up's own read of HEAD -- and
[`remember-git-nib`](../.claude/rules/remember-git-nib.md) already orders that
step. The rule says it about the card; the same ordering owes the body, and this
guard is where the body's half gets written down.

A body that wants to name the parent anyway may: the wall asks only that the hash
be the true one, which means writing it **after** the rebase that decides it.

## What this does not reach

**The nine genres it declines to grade.** A sentence about a peer's parent, a key
derivation's parent, or a past fault's parent reads `loose` and is counted rather
than judged. The `quoted` genre -- a sentence naming another commit by hash, so
the relation belongs to that commit -- is a **proxy, named as one** in the scan's
own header. It over-forgives, which is the safe direction for a wall and a looser
one for a census.

**Whether the body should carry a hash at all.** That is a convention question and
it is Keaton's word. This proves only that a body naming one names the right one.

The ten that stand keep every character they wrote, and the eleventh meets a wall
on the lap it arrives.
