## What was asked, and what was found

[The Bounded Torus](20260910-060204_the-bounded-torus-moonshots.md) ranked twelve moonshot rows on
`20260910`, by what a lane could start on this pier that month. Ten errata have landed against it
since, each reporting a measurement on metal, and several recommend a re-rank or a re-aim in their
own words. The ranking table still reads as first written, which is correct: dated testimony keeps
every word it wrote.

So the order the twelve measurements support stands scattered across ten separate errata. This lap set out to derive that
order from the errata's own recommendation sentences, and to gate that the result is a permutation
of the twelve.

**What they support is a partition rather than a permutation, and the arithmetic says so.** The recommendations speak for **four** of twelve
positions and collide on **two** seats. `permutation=no`.

## The mechanism

**An erratum speaks in a class, and the table speaks in a position.**

*Last of the twelve* is a demotion. Two rows may both be demoted; only one may be twelfth. Rows 2
and 4 were demoted for the same stated cause -- a radius over a population carrying no usable
distance -- and their errata say so to each other explicitly. Neither sentence was ever a claim
about which of the two goes below the other, because no measurement asked that question.

Transcribing a class into a table demands a tiebreak, and that tiebreak would be invented rather
than measured. Inventing
one is the failure this reading exists to refuse: the transcribed table would carry twelve seats
and four measurements, and a reader could not tell which eight seats were judgment from `20260910`
wearing a measurement's clothes.

**And five of the nine speaking rows stop short of naming a seat.** Three errata report a reading
and stop there -- rows 6, 9 and 10. One asks for a re-aim alone (row 11). One asks for a breach
(row 5). These are dispositions about a row's *content*, and a content finding has no
opinion about ordering.

## What can be stated

A partition. Each of the twelve falls into exactly one disposition class, read from its latest
erratum:

| Class | Count | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| **unread** -- no erratum has been written | 3 | 1, 3, 7 |
| **reported** -- an erratum landed and recommends nothing | 3 | 6, 9, 10 |
| **re-aimed** -- keep the row, change what it claims | 1 | 11 |
| **seated** -- a measurement names a position | 2 | 8, 12 |
| **demoted** -- a measurement sends it last | 2 | 2, 4 |
| **superseded** -- breached in the living page | 1 | 5 |

The six counts sum to the roster, which the witness gates rather than assumes. That sum is the
whole substance of the weaker statement: a partition is a statement only while each row falls into
exactly one class.

**This is the honest product of ten measurements**, and it is less than an order. It says what has
been learned about each row without claiming a comparison nobody made.

## Assumptions, named

**That "last" is a class rather than a seat.** This is a reading of English, and a reader may
disagree. Read the other way, rows 2 and 4 collide with each other directly rather than with row
7, and the count of collisions falls to two by a different road -- the refusal survives either
reading, which is why the ambiguity is named here rather than adjudicated in the instrument.

**That the latest erratum on a row speaks.** Row 5 carries two, `20260912.042053` and
`20260915.175000`, and the later one read the earlier before writing. The control proves the
tiebreak by planting two clauses on one row.

**That the ranking table is the standing statement.** It is the only ordering the page publishes,
so a row whose erratum stays silent about position keeps the seat the table gave it.

## What would falsify this

**An erratum landing on each of the three unread rows, naming a distinct seat, with the six
existing recommendations re-read into distinct seats.** That is `permutation=yes`, and the
instrument reaches it -- the control plants exactly that page and watches the verdict read `order`.
A refusal proven only in the refusing direction cannot be told from an instrument that always
refuses.

**A parse failure.** A clause the page carries and the reader passes over would mean this is a
reading of the reader's blind spot rather than of the page. Gated at zero.

**A thirteenth row.** The roster is counted on every run rather than written into the reader, and
the control proves a thirteen-row page reads thirteen. A hard-coded twelve would pass in silence on
the day a row is added.

## Confidence

**High on the finding.** Four named seats against twelve positions falls well short of a permutation,
and the two collisions each have three independent roads proven in the pen.

**Medium on the partition's usefulness.** Six classes over twelve rows is a real statement and a
coarse one. Whether a lane can plan from it is a judgment this page leaves open.

## What this leaves open

**The order itself.** This rung proves the errata supply a partition alone, and leaves the order to a later lap. A lane
wanting a ranked table must either measure the comparisons the errata left for later, or state plainly
that the remaining order is judgment -- which is what the `20260910` table already honestly was.

**The three unread rows.** Rows 1, 3 and 7 carry no erratum. Rows 1 and 3 were ranked first and
second on the grounds that they could run this pier that week; six days on, neither has a landed
reading, and that gap is itself a finding nobody has measured.
