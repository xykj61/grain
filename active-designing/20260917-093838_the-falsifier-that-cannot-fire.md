Gauge at its Field setting asks a page for four things before it makes a projection:
a horizon, its assumptions, a **falsifier**, and a confidence in plain words. This
seat's own charter sharpens the third one into a test: a speculative paper that says
which measurement would kill it is research, and one that does not is enthusiasm.

That sentence rested on habit alone. Three corpus readers watch the code disciplines --
`tools/w/width-check.rish` over the widths, `tools/t/tame-check.rish` over the tidy
bans, and `tools/fixtures/a/alloc_bound_reach_scan.sh` over TAME rule 1's call sites.
The prose discipline this tree writes its research in had none, so the line between
research and enthusiasm was held by hands alone.

## What the instrument reads

The corpus is the **493 living tracked `.md` pages** under `external-research/` and
`active-designing/`, read `20260917` before this page joined them; with it the scan reads
494 pages and 591 regions, and every figure below is the reading that excludes it. The `date/`, `archive/` and `yonder/` shelves stay
outside it: `.claude/rules/read-scope.md` names them closed stacks, and accrete-never-break
keeps every word they wrote, so a shelved page is testimony rather than a claim this tree
still makes. That boundary sets aside **1,079 pages**, and the scan prints the figure as
`shelved_read_past` so a reader sees the size of what it declined.

**The region is the whole instrument**, and the first draft misread it in a way worth
recording. A falsifier's condition rarely sits on the line carrying the word. A line-by-line
read found 676 mentions and called 509 of them conditionless, because `## The falsifier` is
a heading whose condition lives in the block beneath it. That is the same fragment-reading
error the allocation scan one lane over made on `@as(usize, @intCast(total))`, one lap
earlier, in this same seat. So the walk reads a **region**: the block beneath a heading
mention, or the rest of the paragraph after an inline one, bounded at twelve lines either
way.

Each region falls into one of three classes, first match winning:

| Class | What it means | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **runnable** | names a path this tree carries or a command form -- somebody can run it today | **27** |
| **quantified** | names an outside-world measurement carrying a quantity: *two seasons running*, *under 40% of nameplate hours* | **374** |
| **narrative** | a condition stated in prose with nothing to count | **179** |

**Narrative is reported and gated nowhere**, and the reason matters more than the number.
*A corridor whose verifiers are paid by the mill* is genuinely checkable by looking; calling
it unfalsifiable would be the meter lying about its own reach.

## The finding, and the falsifier of the finding

**580 falsifiers stand across 168 pages, and 267 pages carry projection language.** The
plain gap is `projection_no_falsifier=141`: living pages making a projection-shaped claim
with no falsifier named anywhere.

**The falsifier of this paper's own headline was stated before the run.** If most narrative
regions sit on pages that also carry a runnable or a quantified one, then 179 measures a
**writing style** -- several short falsifiers per page, one of them loose -- rather than a
gap. It bit, and hard: `page_narrative_only` reads **28**. So the great majority of narrative
regions share a page with a falsifier somebody could act on, and the residue surviving the
widening is those 28 pages.

That leaves the honest finding in two numbers rather than one. **141 pages project and name
no falsifier. 28 more name only falsifiers with nothing to count.**

## The one wall

A page whose own header declares Gauge at the Field setting, whose body carries projection
language, and which names no falsifier anywhere, has broken a promise it wrote itself. That
is decidable by text, and **21 such pages** stand at this stamp.

They are held in `tools/fixtures/f/falsifier_field_baseline.txt`, and the count splits the
way bakery's root-finder census split earlier the same day: **`unfalsified_new`**, a page the
roster leaves out, **gated at zero**; **`unfalsified_legacy`**, the roster's own, ratcheted
under a ceiling that only falls. A lap writing a new Field page with a projection pays one
sentence, which is the law that page already declared. An absent roster **refuses**, since a
gate whose roster can be deleted into silence is a gate with a door beside it.

The 141 stay off the wall on purpose. A ratchet over a corpus growing by tens of pages a day
is a wall with a delay, and a gate that reds on a peer's ordinary new page is a gate somebody
turns off.

## What it cannot read, printed rather than hidden

A region hitting the twelve-line bound may carry its condition below the cut:
`regions_truncated` reads **3**. A quantity spelled as a word rather than a digit is the
softest half of the quantity test, and `quantified_by_words` reads **184** so that softness
names itself. Stamps and ledger rows are stripped before the quantity test, since a page
citing `20260917` under REDS `%811` is footnoting rather than measuring -- and a mutation
dropping that strip is run in the pen every pass.

**Proven:** 44 legs on real git repositories in a throwaway pen, every refusal planted and
then lifted, **four mutations bitten** -- the heading region, the paragraph continuation, the
citation strip, and the roster comparison.

## What this hands the modules

**27 runnable falsifiers is the number to grow.** A falsifier naming a path is the one shape
that can cross from the proposed room into the checkable one, because a witness can bind it
(`context/TWO_ROOMS.md`). Every design claim this seat writes about Caravan, Aurora, Tally
and Mantra sits today in the quantified or narrative classes, which is correct while the
module is unbuilt and becomes a debt the moment it lands.

So the practical handoff to bakery is one line: when a module reaches the checkable room, the
design page that proposed it is owed a **runnable** falsifier, and the scan's `--list` face
names every page still carrying only the other two.

*May every claim here carry the measurement that could end it, and may the ones that
survive be trusted the more for it.*
