## The finding, in one line

**A reading persuades and a refusal decides**, so a red that recurs after a reading was diagnosed
correctly and answered one rung too low.

## What happened, with the numbers

One class of red, three firings, all inside twenty-six hours:

| Firing | Stamp | Cost, measured |
|---|---|---|
| First | `20260824.082144` | two generated pages stale, found by the next cold roster (REDS %188) |
| Second | `20260825.092953` | 37 paths staged; **60 guards, 57 green, 3 red** (REDS %220) |
| Third | `20260825.132121` | 19 paths staged; **61 guards, 58 green, 3 red** (REDS %223) |

The shape repeats exactly. A round stages its work and stops before `git commit`.
[`../tools/hooks/pre-commit`](../tools/hooks/pre-commit) regenerates `README.md`'s metrics block and
`docs-geode/libraries/README.md` **at commit time**, so both pages keep whatever they last said
until a commit refreshes them -- on the third firing, a front door advertising 1,690 witnesses over
a tree holding 1,692. The third firing added a reading the first two lacked:
`nib_honesty` answered `git_nib=absent`, because the operator card carries the literal `NIB_HERE`
until a commit exists whose hash can replace it, and only the commit makes one.

## The ladder, named

Each answer was correct for its rung, and each rung is a different kind of thing.

**Rule.** REDS %188 wrote it down: *a lap ends at the commit, never at `git add`.* It also proved
something durable -- **enforcement of the close sits outside what a guard can do**, since such a
guard would have to run after the lap ends, and the lap ends last. That reasoning holds today and
this document leaves it standing.

**Reading.** REDS %220 asked a narrower question: enforcement is impossible, so what about
*measurement*? `tools/fixtures/standing_equipment_run.sh` began printing `staged_uncommitted` before
its first guard, so the count arrives on line one of the lap rather than eleven guards later. That
is a genuine improvement, and it is why the third firing was diagnosed in seconds instead of
minutes.

**Refusal.** The class fired again **eleven hours** after the reading landed. A reading informs
whoever reads it, and the agent reading `staged_uncommitted=19` is the one that has just arrived --
so by construction the reading reaches the lap that inherits the mess rather than the lap that made
it. The rung above asks a third question: enforcement belongs to a moment nothing runs in, and
measurement lands with the next reader, so what can a guard **decide**?

It can decline to open the next lap over the wreckage.

## Where the refusal is legitimate, and why exactly there

A refusal earns its place only where the reading it stands on is unambiguous. Here, one moment
qualifies: **a full-roster pass, which is how a lap opens.** Staged paths at that instant belong to
whoever ran last, so the count carries exactly one meaning.

Two cases sit outside that moment and pass free:

- **`--hot`** is how a round says the staged paths are its own -- the after-`git add` pass REDS %174
  asks for, where the same count is the round's own work and speaks only for it.
- **A guard asked for by name** is no lap open at all. It is a hand checking one thing.

One flag and one structural distinction, and the count stops there on purpose. A roster of
exemptions becomes the hiding place the refusal exists to close, which is the lesson the ales choir
paid for one room over on the same day: it registered its own name rather than adding a second
exemption beside the first.

The refusal fires **ahead of the tree digest and ahead of the first guard**, since a pass reports
what it has measured, and twenty minutes of guard verdicts over a tree the lap is about to change
answer a different question from the one the lap has.

## What holds this honest

Both directions are proven on metal, inside a real git repository
[`../tools/fixtures/standing_equipment_control.sh`](../tools/fixtures/standing_equipment_control.sh)
creates and throws away:

| Reading | Proves |
|---|---|
| `clean_cold_reads_zero` - `clean_cold_passes` | a clean cold open runs its guard and answers ok |
| `staged_cold_refuses` | one staged path refuses that same pass |
| `staged_cold_refuses_early` - `staged_cold_skips_digest` | no guard line and no digest print above the refusal |
| `staged_hot_passes` - `hot_still_reads_staged` | `--hot` passes the same tree and still reports the count |
| `staged_named_guard_free` | a guard asked for by name runs free over a dirty index |
| `hot_composes_with_all` | `--hot --all` still selects every tier |

**Nine behaviors added, forty-three in all, ten refusals bitten**, each asserted by
[`../tools/s/standing_equipment_witness.rish`](../tools/s/standing_equipment_witness.rish). The
green side matters as much as the biting one: a guard proven on both sides is distinguishable from
one that bites everything, and a guard that leaves ordinary work alone is a guard people keep.

## What this does not reach

**The close itself stays unenforced**, exactly as REDS %188 concluded. A round can still stage its
work and walk away; what changes is that the next lap declines to spend twenty minutes measuring
the result and instead names the repair in one line.

**A round that passes `--hot` at its own cold open** silences the refusal, and nothing stops it.
That is the shape of every honest gate: a named door rather than a wall with a hole in it. The flag
appears in the round's own shell history and in the loop recipe, where a reach past its purpose
stays plain to read.

**Whether a lap's work is any good.** This asks one question -- did the last lap finish -- and stops
there.

## The transferable rule

When a class of red returns after a reading was added, resist adding a second reading. Ask instead
which party the reading reached and whether any moment exists where its meaning is unambiguous. If
one does, the guard belongs there and it belongs as a refusal, with the case it must leave alone
proven beside the case it bites.
