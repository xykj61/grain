## What this explores

DJINN's live concept at `bit-trading-company.com` (read `2026-08-25`; the copy there is
placeholder while DJINN works the words) demonstrates a whole design language Grain's own
front door could learn from: a page whose identity, texture, and hierarchy are carried by
**characters on a grid** rather than by images. This page reads that mechanism beside the
Linengrow first-cloth direction and asks what a Grain website woven the same way would be.
Everything here is a proposal for DJINN's design lead; his repositories carry no license, so
each borrowing below names a concept, never a byte.

## The mechanisms worth learning

Five, from the live page and the survey beside it. **Character density carries what color
usually carries** -- the mark on the company page is a diamond built from dots, colons, and
asterisks, legible in monochrome on anything that renders text. **The mark is ASCII** -- the
logo travels as characters, so it survives every terminal, diff, and copy-paste intact.
**Sections are numbered plainly** (00-05) with generous whitespace, hierarchy by structure
rather than ornament. **The page can never be blank** -- ase.lat restores its interface
unconditionally 2.5 seconds after a failed boot, and the company page degrades to plain
semantic text with JavaScript off. **Tuning is a live loop** -- SCAPE's press-T panel exports
a config to paste over the whole file, iteration as a first-class mechanism.

## The Grain turn -- cloth, not terminal

A character grid is a loom. Each cell holds one thread-crossing, and that reading turns the
Linengrow palette into a weave a browser can render natively:

- **Stone and Hearth stay the two grounds** (`#DDD3BD` light, `#2A2520` dark), and the linen
  texture becomes literal: a low-density lattice of `.` and `:` characters in the thread
  color, the CSS weave recipe re-spoken as text.
- **Elevation stays weave fineness** -- a card weaves one thread finer than the page, which
  in character terms is a denser glyph or a tighter cell.
- **ASCII passages sit in the meters face** (Source Code Pro, ligatures off), while body
  prose stays Vollkorn -- the cloth is drawn in characters, the reading is done in type.
- **Warmth over monochrome** -- where DJINN's page runs monochrome, Grain's cloth keeps the
  umber-warm thread of the first-cloth page, so the grid reads as linen rather than as a
  green-screen homage.

The register matters: the foundations write in fields, fiber, grain, and crossing, and a
front door woven from characters says the same thing structurally that
[every climate has a fiber](../foundations/20260824-003828_every-climate-has-a-fiber.md) says
agriculturally -- the material is humble, local, and everywhere, and the craft is in the
weave. Regeneration as a design language: nothing on the page a plain text file could not
carry.

## The mark -- from placeholder to woven

[`../assets/grain-logo.svg`](../assets/grain-logo.svg) is the tree's standing placeholder --
a quickly generated sephirot-tree figure, seated during the AHOY front-door season and
honestly low-quality by its own record. The proposal: the mark's next life is a **character
figure** -- the sephirot tree, or a grain stalk, drawn on the same grid as the cloth, in the
same thread color, shipped as text beside its SVG rendering. A mark made of characters would
finally match the tree it names: plain ASCII, diff-stable, renderable by anything, and
drawable by hand in a session log. This seat is DJINN's to sketch and Keaton's to word.

## What would send this back

A rendered page that reads as terminal nostalgia rather than as cloth. A weave lattice that
reads as noise at either 1x or 2x density. Or DJINN choosing a different direction entirely
-- his lead is the gate, and this page is one offering on the table, glad to be outdrawn.
