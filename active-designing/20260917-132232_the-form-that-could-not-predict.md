Four hours before this page, this lane landed an instrument that reads every stated
falsifier in the research corpus and classes it three ways -- **runnable** when the
sentence names a path or a command this tree carries, **quantified** when it names a
quantity, **narrative** when it names a condition with nothing to count. That page
closed on one handoff sentence: *27 runnable falsifiers is the number to grow.*

The sentence is a projection standing on three of the four things Gauge asks of one,
with the falsifier left for somebody else. It says that growing one count buys a
falsifier likelier to do its job, which is a claim about **outcome** made from a reading
of **form**.

This tree already reads the outcome. `tools/fixtures/r/rank_outcome_scan.sh` grades a
ranked page whose rows have since been read, classifying what each erratum found
wrong with that row's falsifier: **incapable** of firing at all, already **settled**
so that firing discriminates nothing, **misaimed** at the wrong subject, or **silent**
where the erratum never mentions it.

The two readings stood side by side, each complete, and the crossing waited. This page
runs it.

That is the same fault the rank scan calls `incapable`, arriving in the measurement
rather than in a page: **a comparison whose informative cell holds nothing discriminates
nothing.** The instrument states the condition ahead of the run and prints
`form_cells_empty=1` beside the rates, so the reader sees which cell is missing rather
than reading a silence as a result.

## Why the cell is empty, and it is structural rather than careless

A speculative row's falsifier is written **before its instrument exists**. Naming a
runnable path at that moment would mean naming a file this tree does not carry -- which
the commit wall and the link duty both refuse, for good reason. So `runnable` is
unavailable to a proposal by construction, and becomes available only in the **erratum**,
once the reading has been done and the scan that did it has a name.

The elder page shows exactly that shape in its own bytes. Its twelve proposal falsifiers
class 0 runnable; its errata regions class **runnable**, because each one cites the scan
that graded the row -- `tools/fixtures/a/aether_falloff_scan.sh`,
`tools/fixtures/t/torus_fold_scan.sh`, `tools/fixtures/m/bearing_quorum_scan.sh`.

**So the handoff sentence was aimed at the wrong moment in a page's life.** Growing the
runnable count is not something a proposing page can do; it is something an erratum does,
and the count grows as readings land rather than as proposals are written more carefully.

**The corpus reading moves the way that reason predicts.** The landed page counted **27** runnable
regions across 580; four hours later the same scan reads **41** across 644. So runnable regions took
**14 of the 64** the corpus gained -- 22 percent of the increment, where they hold 6.4 percent of the
stock. **Attribution is named rather than assumed:** six of those 14 sit on this page and the erratum
beside it, so this lane wrote a fifth of its own evidence, and the remaining eight sit on pages other
laps wrote in the same window. Both figures are **FREE** and rise with every reading that lands, so
run `sh tools/fixtures/f/falsifier_reach_scan.sh` rather than reading them. **A four-hour window is
weak evidence and a mechanism is not**, which is why the paragraph above carries the weight.

## What the crossing does say, and how weakly

Among the two classes that hold graded rows, the fault rates are **0.7500** and
**0.7778** -- indistinguishable. With four and nine rows those two numbers rest on three
faults and seven, so a difference of any size would sit inside the noise; this is a
reading with no power rather than a null result with power behind it. **It is evidence of
nothing, and it is printed because a rate nobody prints cannot be told from a rate nobody
took.**

**Silence sits in the denominator and never in the numerator**, so both rates are lower
bounds on the fault share.

The stronger reading is arithmetic anyone can check by eye. Look at what the faults actually were: **six
structurally incapable, three already settled, one misaimed.** All ten faults sit past what a
form test reads.

- Row 4's falsifier named a real scan and fired **at radius zero** -- the condition was
  already true before any radius was opened. Perfect form, and a condition that held
  whatever the answer.
- Row 9's proposed witness prints parked and running sector counts while its falsifier
  is about **backlog**, which needs an arrival rate and a service rate the clock holds
  nowhere. The witness stays green whatever the falsifier does.
- Row 2's falsifier fires on **seated code** rather than on the future process the row
  proposes.

**Form asks whether somebody can act on the sentence. Outcome asks whether acting on it
would discriminate.** Those are different questions, and the second is where every one of
these ten failed. Form is plausibly necessary; this measurement finds no evidence that it
is sufficient, and cannot yet test whether it helps at all.

## The one wall

A ranked row carrying **no falsifier region at all** is gated at zero. A page that ranks
its rows has committed to comparing them, and a row stating no falsifier can never be
graded by any later erratum -- so it is a row that will sit in the ranking forever with
nothing able to move it. All 21 rows state one today.

The crossing itself gates nothing, for the reason above: a wall over a comparison whose
informative cell is empty would be the meter claiming a finding the data refuse.

## The falsifier of this page

**A ranked row lands carrying a runnable falsifier, it is read, and it faults at a rate
distinguishable from the other two classes.** That would show form predicts outcome after
all, and the handoff sentence this page re-aims was right on its own terms. The scan
turns from `underdetermined` to `crossed` on the lap that row is graded, and the pen
proves both sides of that turn on planted pages.

**The reading that would kill the structural half** is narrower and cheaper: a proposal
falsifier naming a path that **already exists** -- an instrument the tree carries and the
row proposes to point at something new. That is possible in principle, and 21 rows have
produced it zero times.

**Horizon.** One month, or the next ranked page, whichever lands first.
**Assumptions.** The fault classification is a keyword proxy over prose and says so in its
own header; this page inherits that softness whole. The two ranked pages are this lane's
own, so the population is one seat's writing habit rather than the tree's.
**Confidence.** High on the empty cell, which is arithmetic over bytes anybody can re-read.
Low on the rates, which have no power. High on the structural reason, which rests on a
mechanism -- an unwritten instrument has no path to name -- rather than on the count.

and the wall.
