the Framework laptop joined with two hands of its own. No new words were coined -- **each

## The collision law, unchanged and doubled

Lanes are the collision law: a find in a sibling body's lane becomes a line on
`construction/ITINERARY.md` rather than that hand's lap. A star and its planet divide one
domain -- Mind designs and Mystery ships it to new machines; Sound holds the core and Silence
holds the hands-on half; Dream lowers the interface and Hush the infrastructure -- so the
sharpest lane seam runs *inside* each orbit, and the planet defers to its star where the two
could brush. Two bodies now share each of two machines, so the `%291` habit is charter law:
**every lap opens with ListAgents, names any peer before a file moves, and messages it when
lanes could brush.** The two Glow halves share one language and never one file, and language
custody stays Dream's. One checkout per body stays Keaton's open door from `%291`; until his
word, the habit and path-limited commits carry two hands on one tree.
