## The question

Does **The receipt you can read** require an Amphora vessel, or does the vessel belong to the later
portable bundle?

The living surfaces disagree. The accepted contract says Amphora enters a later milestone. The
first Dimeroll milestone names Amphora among its modules. Copal has also landed a witness that seals
the synthetic receipt fixture as opaque Amphora cargo, refuses changed ciphertext and an unknown
vessel version, and restores the fixture byte for byte.

## Source chain, newest to oldest

The landed `amphora_receipt_bundle` witness proves a sound vessel seam. It begins from the fixture
file; it does not consume bytes emitted by the first receipt application or feed restored bytes into
the two product projections.

The accepted [product contract](20260912-201126_the-receipt-you-can-read-contract.md) places Kyri,
Tally, Mantra, Linengrow, Dimeroll, Brushstroke, Skate, and Brix in the first whole. It explicitly
places Amphora later. Its acceptance cases require one admitted fact to reach both product readings.

The earlier [custody packet](20260912-191503_the-oldest-premise-under-the-readable-receipt.md)
revived one product condition: emit the authenticated receipt through a local save or standard-output
seam, then read those same bytes back into Linengrow and Dimeroll. It also says this condition adds
no module.

[The Sealed Crossing](../foundations/20260703-201612_the-sealed-crossing.md), written `20260703`,
gives Amphora its durable boundary. A Bron or Kyri record is the letter. Amphora is the crate that
carries one or more letters across a custody boundary and returns them whole.

The oldest deciding premise is [Four Promises a Tool Can Keep](../foundations/20260628-133212_four-promises-a-tool-can-keep.md),
written `20260628`. Its third promise is to hold lightly: keep the door out open. That promise asks
for a receipt the holder can take, rather than for a particular container around it.

## Judgment, oldest to newest

The product owes the holder receipt bytes in the first whole. A bounded local file or standard-output
seam can keep that promise, and reading those bytes back into both projections proves that the door
out is real. Amphora answers a later question: whether a sealed collection crosses a custody
boundary and arrives whole.

The disposition is **yondered**. The landed vessel witness remains valuable and whole as preparation
for **The bundle you can carry**. It does not close any of the first contract's eight acceptance
cases, because it starts from a fixture rather than the application's emitted receipt and never
reaches either product projection.

## Handoff

Incense owns the accepted contract and should keep Amphora outside the first module residences.
Dimeroll's first milestone should drop Amphora from its module list or state that the landed vessel
is later preparation rather than part of completion. Copal's code and dated testimony remain whole.
No module assignment changes in this packet.
