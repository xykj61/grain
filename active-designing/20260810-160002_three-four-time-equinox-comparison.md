## The idea

The **calendar** already bounds work in powers of two — Quest 4, Journey 16, Equinox 64, Season 256. The **3/4 time signature** adds a *rhythm* on top of that bound: a way to hear, at each new equinox, whether the melody is rising or falling.

Music's 3/4 time keeps three beats to a measure. Here the measure is **three equinoxes** — the one just closed, the one before it, and the one before that. At every new equinox we read the triad:

- **Winning** — the last equinox scored better than the one before it (the line is rising).
- **Competitively losing** — the last equinox scored worse than the one before, *and* that one was already below the one before that (the line is falling two beats running).
- **Holding** — mixed: a rise after a fall, or a fall after a rise; the melody has not committed.

Three beats give the smallest window that can show a **direction**, not just a step. Two equinoxes tell you up or down once; three tell you whether the trend holds.

## What is measured

Each equinox closes with a score — the honest, witness-backed numbers already printed by `prin`:

- **fascia health** (the clutter/soundness meter),
- **reds** (open wrongs, fewer is better),
- **sundial** (recursion-prompt confidence),
- **green witnesses** shipped over the equinox's 64 rounds.

The score is a reading, never a fabrication — `prin sundial` and the fascia meter already produce it. The 3/4 reading simply lays this equinox's score beside the previous two.

## How it evolves the molt/debride pattern

Molt and debride tend the tree *within* a round; the 3/4 time signature tends it *across* equinoxes. Where a molt asks "is this file superseded?", the 3/4 reading asks "is this whole equinox better than the last, and is the trend our friend?" It turns tending from a per-file act into a seasonal rhythm — a heartbeat the calendar can keep.

## What it is not

- **Not a fabricated leaderboard.** Every beat is a witness-backed number or it is not counted (Two Rooms).
- **Not a punishment.** A falling triad names where to spend the next equinox's care; it never shames a round.
- **Not automatic yet.** This seats the concept and its reading; a witness that computes the triad from the equinox scores is its own future lap.
