## Why this document exists

The autonomous loop has proven itself past every plan it was handed — the Surface & Intelligence equinox (**BUHR**) grew from a four-voice crux to **eleven journeys** end to end, each rung witnessed on metal, because whenever the fixed itinerary ran dry the loop opened its own next journey honestly. That is the good problem: the bench climbs faster than the calendar names the rungs.

So this is a **calendar large enough to stay ahead of the bench** — a fractal of **1,024 rounds** that names the road from where the tree stands (language proven · identity settled · surface and intelligence met over one graph) out to a sealed, harvested, commons-bearing whole. It is **double-seated**: it doubles the largest allocation to admit a fresh priority without moving one stone of the Compass itinerary already in flight (Lexicon **double-seat**). The Compass Season's own four equinoxes stay exactly as seated; this itinerary threads *through* them and out past them, so an autonomous run always has a higher-Lindy crux waiting rather than only one more journey to accrete.

## The fractal — 4 · 16 · 256 · 1,024

The tree already nests **Round · Quest · Journey · Equinox · Season** by powers of two. This itinerary reads that nesting at the calendar scale a full road wants:

| Level | Count | Holds | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Season** | **4** | a whole arc with one durable promise | — |
| **Journey** | **16** | 4 per season — one crux each | ×4 |
| **Quest** | **256** | 16 per journey — a themed band of work | ×16 |
| **Round** | **1,024** | 4 per quest — one keystone, one send | ×4 |

A **round** stays the atomic unit the loop already fills: one keystone, witnessed, sent as its own signed increment. A **quest** is four such rounds on one theme. A **journey** is sixteen quests around a single crux. A **season** is four journeys carrying one durable promise. The loop never needs the whole grid in view — it needs only *the next crux*, and this document keeps one always named.

## The filling law (how the loop reads this)

1. **Lindy-first, crux-first** orders everything ([`../.claude/rules/lindy-first-crux.md`](../.claude/rules/lindy-first-crux.md)) — the most durable journey of the open season first; within a journey, the hardest still-tractable quest; within a quest, the round that opens the rest.
2. **Reds preempt.** A booked red ([`../.claude/rules/reds-first.md`](../.claude/rules/reds-first.md)) is fixed before the next durable round, across any journey or season boundary.
3. **A quest is filled, not invented.** When a quest opens, the loop names its four rounds as a self-approved design round in `active-designing/`, then climbs them — exactly as BUHR's journeys were opened and filled.
4. **The gates are the fence.** Every custody gate in REMEMBER stays the maintainer's hand; a round that reaches one stops and surfaces (`GATES-ONLY`), never crosses.
5. **Double-seated means additive.** This itinerary rewards *finished, proven, bounded* work that stands beside the fixed Compass plan; it retires nothing already seated.

## The four seasons (crux-first within the road's own dependency order)

### Season 1 — **The World** (finishes Compass Equinox 4, **TACT**)

*Durable promise: a stranger reaches a working, signed, publishable instance without the maintainer's hand on each step.* The intelligence and surface met over one graph in BUHR; now the graph meets a person.

- **Journey 1 — Ship-Pilot.** Crux: a guided first-run pilots a new keeper from zero to a running Pond, the whole arc witnessed. (Ties HAWM · SOURCE onboarding.)
- **Journey 2 — Publishing.** Crux: an owner-signed publish of a Tablecloth artifact or a Realidream page — this journey's serve rung reaches the **Comlink-served custody gate** and surfaces there for the maintainer's word.
- **Journey 3 — Grainphone.** Crux: the glass surface on GrapheneOS reads the living Pond as a first-class client (HAWM ladder; install stays the operator desk).
- **Journey 4 — Commerce.** Crux: a fair-value trade recorded honestly in Dimeroll — facts only, disbursement counsel-gated.

### Season 2 — **Yield** (Harvest Equinox 1–2, **GISM · AYRE**)

*Durable promise: every module the tree built stands on real data, not a demo catalog, and trades fairly.*

- **Journey 5 — Real corpora.** Crux: the demo catalogs (`recall_lap1`, the pinned markdown) become real corpora the voices read; book every red the real bytes surface.
- **Journey 6 — Provenance.** Crux: a real document carries a Kumara-signed, content-addressed chain of custody end to end (Tablecloth-keyed on real artifacts).
- **Journey 7 — Fair-trade (AYRE).** Crux: a certificate a store *satisfies* — the Brix-declaration pattern (J7r3) carried to a real cooperative record.
- **Journey 8 — Cash-first.** Crux: books that balance for Linengrow PBC / Siya Fund — the accounting spine, custody and disbursement counsel-gated.

### Season 3 — **Commons** (Harvest Equinox 3, **DAHL**)

*Durable promise: keepers meet each other with consent, and a community can shape its own sky.*

- **Journey 9 — Skate.** Crux: the social layer over Pond identities — a shared surface entered by consent, never by default.
- **Journey 10 — Membership.** Crux: the one surface that needs quorum (names, groups) proven at more than two keepers (extends `settlement/names.rye`).
- **Journey 11 — Council skies.** Crux: loadable topologies a community loads like a game — odd-quorum fractal (3·9·15·27), modality roles (the breach's held horizon).
- **Journey 12 — Data-dignity.** Crux: data outlives the keeper with dignity — succession, consent, the keeper's rights, custody-first.

### Season 4 — **Seal** (CION Meta-Season · Harvest Equinox 4, **VOLS · LOWE · OFFY · GRAD · KOFF**)

*Durable promise: the tree's own labels, history, and connective tissue are named, healed, and guarded — then it rests.*

- **Journey 13 — Survey (VOLS).** Crux: name every living count-up ID — each site, each gap — before a single relabel.
- **Journey 14 — Molt (LOWE).** Crux: relabel every living now-line to chronological/semantic form, dated testimony untouched.
- **Journey 15 — Debride (OFFY).** Crux: word-gated cuts of dead count-up tissue, a cairn stacked before each cut.
- **Journey 16 — Seal & Rest (GRAD · KOFF).** Crux: witness the whole conversion, seat the labeling-law guard, consolidate, read the 3/4, close the saga on a benediction.

## Alignment — this itinerary retires nothing

Every journey above **threads a seated waymark** rather than coining a rival one: TACT closes the open Compass finishing edge; GISM · AYRE · DAHL · KOFF are the Harvest equinoxes already drawn; VOLS · LOWE · OFFY · GRAD are the CION equinoxes already drawn ([`../.claude/rules/waymark-ladders.md`](../.claude/rules/waymark-ladders.md)). The Compass Season's fixed itinerary stands; this reads across it and out, so the loop's Lindy-first crux is always the next durable thing on the *whole* road, not merely the next rung of the equinox it happens to be inside.

When a season here opens its first journey for real, that journey draws its own waymark by the ladder law before its rounds are numbered — this document names the road; the ladder names each rung.
