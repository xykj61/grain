## The Walk in One Breath

A pilot arrives with an internet connection and nothing else. Eight stations later they hold a keypair born on their own machine, a ground block naming their own law, a voice on the bare lane, and — if and only if they choose the ledger tier — a bound point with its healing drill already rehearsed. The walk never touches what is theirs to touch: **every coin, key, wallet, and signature is the pilot's own hand, at every station, in every jurisdiction.**

## Station Zero — the Ground

Before any key exists, the pilot names their law. Two slots, never one blur: **law-of-the-ground** and **identity-of-the-wire**.

```
JURISDICTION {
  county/region:   Washoe County        # the pilot's own
  state/province:  Nevada
  nation:          USA
  treaty-layer:    international law
}
WIRE {
  kumara-keys:     <fingerprints, filled at Station One>
  keeper-address:  <filled at Station Three, or "bare lane">
}
```

The reference instance above is one pier's truth; the blank twin beside it in the template is every other pilot's. What the ground block governs: commerce words, tax words, and any registrar of human names. What it never governs: the shape of a key, a bind, a counter, or an escape — those are the wire's, identical on every ground.

## Stations One and Two — Keys, Then a Voice

**One.** A Kumara keypair is born on the pilot's own machine, and the private half never travels — not to a service, not to a helper, not to this walk. The pilot records both fingerprints in their WIRE block by hand, reading them aloud once, because a fingerprint spoken is a fingerprint checked.

**Two.** With keys alone, the pilot already *is* someone: they can sign, verify, and speak on the **bare lane** — membership by degree begins here, at cost zero, with nothing purchased and nothing owed. Many pilots may live whole lives at Station Two, and the walk says so with a straight face.

## Stations Three and Four — the Keeper and the Bind

**Three** *(optional — the ledger tier begins).* The pilot brings a **keeper address** of their own, from an instrument of their own choosing; the walk's only requirement is capability, never brand: the address must be able to sign a transaction that carries a bind. The custody paragraph repeats here in bold, because here is where it earns its bold.

**Four.** The **bind** is signed from both sides — the keypair signs the address, the address signs the key — and the pilot verifies the marriage themselves: both fingerprints, both signatures, checked against the WIRE block before anything else proceeds. A bind is deliberately a two-step; hurry is the only adversary this station has.

## Stations Five Through Seven — the Point, the Drill, the Receipt

**Five.** A point arrives by receiving or by spawn, per the scarcity shape (**held for Keaton's word**); its sponsorship default is arithmetic and free, its chosen parent is a link, and **escape is a right the child exercises alone** — the walk teaches the escape before it praises the tree.

**Six — the healing drill.** On day one, while nothing is wrong, the pilot turns the **key counter** once and watches the rotation record climb — so the day something *is* wrong, healing is a habit, not a scramble. The reset counter is explained and left untouched; the deed sleeps through the whole drill.

**Seven — the receipt.** The pilot leaves holding a small card: the JURISDICTION block, both fingerprints, the bind's name, the counters' values, and where each of these lives on their own machines. Nothing on the card is a secret; everything on the card is enough to check everything else.
