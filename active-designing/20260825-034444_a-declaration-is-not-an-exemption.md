# A Declaration Is Not an Exemption

## The finding, in one sentence

**A meter that instructs a repair which would make the artifact worse is the thing to fix.**

That is the test, and it is worth carrying past the round that found it. When a reading looks wrong,
ask what the number tells a writer to *do*, and whether doing it would help anyone. A low grade
that points at real work is a good grade. A low grade whose only available repair damages the page
is a fault in the ruler.

## Where it showed up

The report card scores cross-references per hundred words against a budget: one for a Door page,
three for a Field page. That budget is written for prose, and it is right for prose.

On an index the links **are** the content. `docs/README.md` carries 10 words of prose and 5 links,
which reads 50 per hundred words and floors its Reach at zero. The only repair available is to pad
the page with prose until its denominator can carry its own rate -- on a page whose own closing line
asks that these pages stay few.

The same day, `construction/CHECKPOINTS.md` sat at Truth 60, one point off the F gate that reads a
whole card as F. It got there by writing `date/YYYYMMDD/name` twice, which is a **shape** -- letters
standing where digits would go. The naming law seats that spelling and asks for it by name: build an
illustration from placeholders and it stays honest; build one from a real-looking stamp naming no
file, and it reads as a real citation to every reader and every tool. The page followed the law
exactly and the meter charged it forty points.

Both pages were correct. Both meters were incomplete.

## The shape of the fix, and the trap inside it

The tree already had the answer for one of these. `declared_ceiling` reads a page's **own** limit off
its **own** header -- `**Ceiling:** <=40 lines` -- rather than carrying a table of which pages are
allowed to be long. So the card can read a page's own declaration the same way: `**Depth:** routing`,
`**Kind:** crushed index`.

And there the trap opens. **A self-declared exemption is a door, and a door beside a wall makes the
wall a habit.** Any page could type `**Kind:** index` into its header and stop being measured. That
is worse than the fault it repairs, because a wall people can walk around teaches everyone to walk
around it.

**The two-condition form** is what keeps it a reading. A page is read as an index when it

1. **declares** itself one, in its header alone, and
2. **measures** as one -- under 100 words of prose.

Neither half is an exit. A 3,000-word page declaring routing stays graded, because it has the
denominator to carry a rate. A 20-word page declaring nothing stays penalized, because a short page
is not automatically an index.

**Say why 100.** A rate expressed per hundred words is extrapolation below one full unit of its own
denominator. That is the fault itself, stated as a number, rather than a threshold picked for feel.

## The measurement that decided it, taken before the code was written

Four living pages declare an index in their header. Their prose word counts:

| Page | Declares | Prose words | Read as |
|---|---|---|---|
| `docs/README.md` | `**Depth:** routing` | 10 | index |
| `docs-geode/libraries/README.md` | `**Kind:** crushed index` | 13 | index |
| `docs-geode/press/README.md` | `**Kind:** crushed index` | 13 | index |
| `docs-geode/edu/README.md` | `**Kind:** crushed index` | 193 | **graded** -- and already an A |

The fourth row is the whole argument. A page that calls itself an index and carries real prose keeps
its grade, and it was already earning an A at 3 cross-references per hundred words. The pair
discriminates on data the tree already held, which is what separates a reading from an opt-out.

## Proving it from both sides

A guard proven only in the passing direction cannot be told from a bypass. So the control plants
each half against itself:

- a declaring **short** page reads as an index, and its density is reported;
- a declaring **long** page stays graded, **and actually loses reach**;
- a **short** page declaring nothing stays graded, **and actually loses reach**;
- a declaration in the **body** rather than the header reaches nothing;
- a placeholder shape costs nothing and is **named** on the card;
- a **fabricated** stamp -- digits naming no file -- still costs twenty points, and is still named.

That last one carries the same weight as all the others together. It is the behavior that must
survive the change, and the naming law warns about exactly that failure. Keeping it is what makes
the placeholder reading a reading.

Pointed at a copy of the card carrying the old rule, the control **refuses 13 of its 17 new readings
and passes exactly 4** -- and those 4 are the ones describing behavior that must not change. That
count is the evidence the control bites.

One planted case had to be repaired before it meant anything. The first index probe put its links in
a bullet list, and the reach reading skips list lines, so the links were never counted and the probe
passed against the old rule too. A reading that passes for the wrong reason is worth less than no
reading, because it looks like coverage.

## What this reaches past the report card

Any meter this tree writes will eventually meet an artifact that is correct and unusual. Three
questions carry over:

**What repair does the number instruct?** If the answer damages the artifact, the meter is the work.

**Can the artifact already say what it is?** This tree writes declarations into headers as a habit --
`**Ceiling:**`, `**Depth:**`, `**Kind:**`, `**Status:**`. A meter can read them, and reading a page's
own words is cheaper and more honest than a roster somebody maintains by hand.

**What second condition keeps the declaration from being a door?** There has to be one, and it
should be the measurement the reading was already taking. Here the declaration says *what the page
is* and the word count says *whether the rate can be computed at all* -- two independent facts, so
neither alone opens the door.

## What this does not reach

**Whether an index with 13 words is a good index.** The card reports its density now rather than
scoring it, and a reader still has to look.

**The register reading's own denominator.** `docs-geode/libraries/README.md` reads Register 0 from a
single surviving sentence, which is the same extrapolation problem one reading over. It is named on
`construction/ITINERARY.md` and wants its own measured round rather than a patch at the end of this
one.
