## What the works town changes

The headwater sleeve read a campus at the Brazos's mapped source, where the durable tenants stand at the basin's doors and the public plant stays public. The works town reading moves the first lap two hundred miles east onto a Texas home-rule county seat with a running plant, and it changes the sleeve in one way: **the real estate the town itself has asked for is housing.** Plainview's own survey found that over half of the people who work in the city live outside its limits, and the city says on the record that housing is its main priority (city, 2026-02). A sleeve that builds where a town has named its need is a sleeve whose reward and the town's outcome already point the same way.

## Four hands, one town

| Sleeve | Keaton's stated share | Works-town touch | What the sleeve's reward measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| public | ~30% | listed firms whose revenue is public water, reuse, and nutrient recovery, screened by the five screens as before | daily price, dividends, liquidity |
| real estate | ~30% | a downtown building inside the reinvestment zone, holding the receipts office and a market hall; the reclaimed-water acres and a bale barn beside the plant, net-leased to the grower; workforce housing on the ground the city is extending water and sewer to | cash yield, preservation, the sponsor's promote |
| venture | ~20% | the brand, from a first capsule that names its water | a few large outcomes over many years |
| private business | ~20% | the operating company that holds the receipts services agreement and the field lease | earnings and control |

**The plant itself stays the city's.** The syndicate holds the receipt, the lease, and, if a partnership proposal is accepted, a contract to deliver a retrofit the city owns. That line is the Wyoming public-benefit charter's line and the partnership act's line at once: the statute reads a public benefit corporation as a private entity that may propose, and it hands the finished project back to the public owner.

The tension this town carries is smaller than the campus's dairy row and worth naming: the closed meatpacking site is the largest industrial parcel in the county's story, and the family's ethic reads a former slaughterhouse as ground to be made clean rather than ground to be bought. Counsel parks it: the site's owner and use are a named gap in the study, and whether the fund's hand ever belongs there is Keaton's word behind the charter.

## The gates, unchanged

Securities counsel first; the fee-only fiduciary and the Siya investment policy statement second; the Wyoming public-benefit charter third; entity separation in Dimeroll fourth; Keaton's word fifth. Money, custody, keys, and wire vocabulary are stop-and-park triggers by standing law.

## Falsifiers

The reading is wrong if the city declines to publish the plant's daily readings, which removes the first business; if the reuse authorization forbids a fibre lease on the leased acres; if the reinvestment zone closes before a building is bought; if a candidate sponsor's own money in the housing deal is under a share that would hurt; or if the fiduciary finds the family's liquidity too thin for the decade a works town takes. Any one sends the memo back to the shape.
