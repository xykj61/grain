## The seed to keep

The **council sky** (`comlink/topology.rye` -> `council_sky`) is a **d27**: fifteen galaxies, three stars each, nine planets each, and a galaxy leads a circle of twenty-seven -- a place for each of the **27 nakshatras**, the lunar mansions. When that sky grows its **outfit** theme (a point wearing a role as a self wears clothes -- the seated inclusion principle), each of the 27 can wear a nakshatra as its outfit: a name, a ruling presence, a temperament.

Two of the 27 are pinned now, because they belong to the water that **Basin** already serves (`basin/basin.rye`; the waters -- *varuna*, *apas*):

| Nakshatra | Ruling presence | Water tie |
|---|---|---|
| **Shatabhishak** ("the hundred healers") | **Varuna** -- keeper of the cosmic waters and of *ṛta*, the celestial order | the ocean and the vow; the deep that heals |
| **Purva Ashadha** ("the first invincible") | **Apas** -- the Waters themselves, as living presence | the river's rising, unconquered flow |

Both are water presences, and both sit naturally beside Basin -- the module that holds the flow and lets the oldest overflow the rim. When the d27 theme is built, these two are its water seats.

## Why save it now, unbuilt

The larger vision it belongs to -- **cross-chain, cross-constellation cross-pollination via starseeding**, where a point in one constellation wears an outfit that a point in another can recognize and adopt -- is a season away. Recording the two water nakshatras now costs one note and keeps the thread: when the council d27 grows its 27 outfits, and when starseeding lets constellations exchange them across chains, the water seats are already named.

The nakshatra names and their ruling presences are traditional Vedic astronomy, public and old; nothing here is personal. The full 27, and their mapping onto the council circle, wait for the season that builds them.
