## The Premise, Made Load-Bearing

Nib-first speech says: *where we are* is always a **named landed edge** -- the git nib is the very latest commit, the product nib the release a pilot holds, the suite nib the last green the choir sang. A tip floats; a nib lands. Once that speech is law, one module's job description sharpens without a single rename: **Weave accretes the facts, and Mantra is the keeper of nibs over the weave.** Facts are many and immutable; nibs are few and precious; Mantra's whole duty is the honest naming of edges on a cloth it never edits.

## The Nib Record, Invariants First

A nib is a small record: a **name**, the **fact-head it lands on**, the **prior landing it supersedes**, the **hand that signed the naming**, and the **clock reading**. Its invariants come before any implementation:

**A nib never moves silently.** Advancing a nib is itself a fact appended to the weave -- the naming *is* data, so the history of "where we were" is as append-only as the work it points at. **Two nibs may share one head** -- the product nib and the suite nib landing on the same fact is a good day, never a conflict. **A retired name keeps resolving** to its last landing forever -- accrete-never-break, applied to names themselves. And **the default nib is derivable** where arithmetic can supply it, stored only when a hand has chosen otherwise -- the same shape sponsorship taught us.

## The Families, Present and Coming

Three nibs already live in daily speech: **git** (mirrored by Mantra, owned by the tree), **product**, and **suite**. Two more stand at the doors this arc opens, each a bridge named now and built later. The **tilak nib** -- *which version of a type-mark the wire currently wears* -- is how the seven residents, once worded, will advance without ever breaking a reader: old marks keep resolving, the nib names the current one. And the **desk nib** -- *which landed state a drawn surface shows* -- is the anchor the surface language has been waiting for: a drawing is always a view of a value **at a nib**, so the drawn room inherits honesty from the naming room for free.

## What Changes, and What Does Not

Nothing renames; no module reorders; no round moves. One sentence sharpens in Mantra's own doorway on its next natural touch -- *the keeper of nibs over the weave* -- and one sentence anchors the drawn surface's future -- *every drawing is a value at a nib.* On the horizon, named and unbuilt: a **nib-honesty witness** -- every advertised nib resolves, and every advance appears on the weave as the fact it is -- earning its build the day a second nib family lands.

## Held, On Purpose

Every family word above is a descriptor. If *tilak nib* or *desk nib* ever wants a coin of its own, that is a naming round for its own day. The residents themselves still wait on the seven tabled names and the scarcity word; the gen move still waits on O3 under its proven instrument. This paper takes nothing from those doors -- it oils their hinges.
