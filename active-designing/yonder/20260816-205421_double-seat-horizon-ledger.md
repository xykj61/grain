## How to read this ledger

Each row names an idea, how well it **fits** the seated values, its proper **home** if it advances, and a one-line **recommendation**. Rows near the top are the most durable and the most tractable; rows near the bottom are larger, further from the seated mission, or waiting on a prior thing to exist. Nothing here is a commitment -- it is a place to stand so the ideas stop living only in a chat.

## Tier 1 -- buildable now, excellent fit (the open-media-primitive family)

This family already began: **QOI** for images (HUNK) and the **OGG/Vorbis-shaped audio wire** (ALES). These rows finish it, and they are the two I would reach for next.

- **Open RGB/HTML color algebra in Rye** -- *Fit: excellent - Home: a module (`.rye` primitive), no season - Rec: build soon.* A pure sRGB / hex / HSL color primitive is **floor code that does not wait on DJINN's Bit Design System** -- only Brushstroke *rendering* is paused, not color math. Smallest tractable crux in the whole pile.
- **Open permissive fonts + a font codec (QOI-for-fonts)** -- *Fit: excellent - Home: a journey, double-seated (Lexicon + a rule/foundation) - Rec: seat as a journey.* Sits right beside QOI and the audio wire as an open, bounded, permissively-licensed media primitive across weights, styles, and families (serif - sans - mono - caps). On-brand and Lindy.

## Tier 2 -- aligned but heavier, or research-first

- **Open video codec (QOI-for-video)** -- *Fit: good, heavy - Home: horizon double-seat - Rec: start smallest.* Begin with an **intra-frame container** (QOI-per-frame, self-describing, content-addressed) -- literally "QOI for video" -- for Photos, Skate, Realidream, and Pond, rather than a full inter-frame codec. Landscape / portrait / square are container metadata, not new math.
- **HuggingFace / model-format compliance** -- *Fit: research - Home: one external-research round - Rec: study before building.* Check whether `safetensors` (bounded, mmap-friendly) and `GGUF` pass TAME civic/style; likely yes. Adopt an existing open format before authoring our own.
- **Open-weight ML: math - parser - training - output for Lattice - Lantern - Scribble - Ember** -- *Fit: aligned, season-scale - Home: a full season - Rec: Keaton's call.* This is Quin's inference Q-vane made real -- genuine, but a whole season of bounded math, a parser, and a training/output pipeline. Do not open it as a side effect of another round.

## Tier 3 -- premature, or a values fork to decide consciously

- **Own model store / forge / marketplace** -- *Fit: premature - Home: after the ML season - Rec: defer.* Building a forge before there are models to store is backwards. Revisit once the ML season has output worth hosting; name it then from the favorites list or a fresh clear word.
- **Sig/Sui-style validators in Rye vs. Mycelium + a token** -- *Fit: values fork - Home: Mycelium (already seated) - Rec: focus Mycelium; question the token.* Reimplementing Solana's Sig or a Sui validator suite is enormous and off-mission. A token-aimed-at-exchange-listing pulls toward custody, wallets, and listings -- exactly the **manual custody gates** -- and sits against the custody-first, civic, families-and-collectives values the Urbit-reframe rule set. Worth a conscious decision, not a slide-in.
- **Self-hosted compute / datacenter analytics** -- *Fit: aligned, low urgency - Home: horizon note only - Rec: park.* Fits the personal-server dream; nothing to build now.
- **Sports-team HR / roster management (e.g. an MLS or NBA/WNBA club)** -- *Fit: a vertical of existing HR - Home: Dimeroll / Linengrow PBC (WADE) - Rec: Keaton's business call.* WADE already seats *Linengrow PBC HR & accounting* plus Dimeroll; a pro-sports org would be a **client vertical** of that, a go-to-market leap rather than an engineering decision.

## The values check, named once

Three rows -- an exchange-listed token, datacenter analytics-as-a-service, and pro-sports SaaS -- pull toward a growth/commerce posture the reframe rule consciously set aside in favor of families, small collectives, and civic builders. They are not wrong to want; they deserve a deliberate *"yes, we are widening the mission,"* rather than arriving as quiet double-seats. The color module and the open-fonts journey carry no such tension -- they simply finish the open-media family already begun.

## What this ledger is for

So that a wide, generous session of ideas lands somewhere durable and ordered, and the next planning pass reads *what next?* the same way at every scale -- most durable first, hardest-solvable-that-is-tractable next -- instead of ten seasons all shouting at once. Seat a row when it is truly its turn; until then, it rests here, alive.
