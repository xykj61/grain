# What part of service can be counted

**Language:** EN
**Stamp:** `20260824.165106`
**Style:** Gauge, **Field** setting
**Voice:** Kyri
**Room:** Design essay -- worth reading with the code deleted
**Kin:** [`../context/GAUGE_STYLE.md`](../context/GAUGE_STYLE.md) -> *Quality assurance* - [`../.claude/rules/quality-assurance.md`](../.claude/rules/quality-assurance.md)

## The question

A report card takes four readings of an artifact. Three of them are counted or half-counted.
**Service** -- does this help the work actually in front of us -- is judged, and the tool that takes
the other three prints the inputs and stops.

That was the right call on the day it was made, and it deserves a harder look than "judgement is
hard to automate," because the interesting part is *which* half resists counting and why.

## What the tool already hands a reader

Three inputs, all counted today: how many **living** files cite this artifact, whether the **live
card** names it, and whether the **projection manifest** carries its room. A reader takes those and
answers four questions worth twenty-five points each -- is it **named**, is it **reached**, is it
**current**, and which side does it **carry**.

Line the inputs up against the questions and the split is sharper than it first looks.

**Named is nearly counted already.** A live line, a standing law, or a roster reaching the artifact
is a graph question, and the tool answers a coarse form of it now. Making it exact means asking
whether any of the citing files is itself live rather than merely tracked, which is a walk of a
graph the tree already builds for other guards.

**Reached is a distance.** Not *is it cited* but *does someone arriving at the live work pass
through it*. That is the shortest path from the live card to the artifact through the citation
graph, and it is entirely countable. A distance of one is a card that names it. A distance of six is
a page nobody arrives at by walking.

**Current is countable only in its cheap direction.** The tool can see when an artifact last
changed, and whether every path it cites still resolves -- which the Truth reading already takes.
Whether its *description of the tree* is still honest needs a reader or a run, and no graph reaches
that.

**Carried is a lookup.** Which side an artifact serves -- the public projection, the working
repository, or both -- is written in the manifest, and the tool reads it today. What stays judged is
whether that side is the *right* one, which is a decision rather than a fact.

## The trap, named before it is walked into

**A counted Service would reward being cited, and citation is not service.** A page every rule
points at scores high whether or not anyone reads it, and a page that quietly carries a season --
the one card an unattended run opens first -- may sit at distance one from nothing at all because
nothing needs to cite what everyone already opens.

This is the failure Civic Style names in its own domain: **a metric rewards something, and the thing
it rewards is rarely the thing anyone wanted.** A Service score built on the citation graph rewards
being pointed at, which is an excellent proxy right up until somebody knows it is the score.

## The shape proposed, therefore

**Count two of the four and leave two judged, and say so on the card.**

- **Named** and **Reached**: counted, from the citation graph, fifty points between them.
- **Current** and **Carried**: judged, fifty points between them, with the counted inputs printed
  beside them as they are today.

A Service reading that is half counted and says which half is the honest shape. It also keeps the
scale from drifting: a fully counted Service would make the composite look more objective than it
is, and a report card's whole value is that it tells you which of its numbers you can lean on.

## The alternative, given its best case

**Leave Service entirely judged.** The argument is real: a reader taking all four questions holds
the artifact in mind at once, and splitting the reading into a counted half and a judged half
invites the judged half to be skipped because the number already arrived. A partly-counted score is
easier to accept without reading than a blank is.

**The answer, and it is not a full answer.** Print the counted half as *inputs* rather than as
*points* until the graph walk has been measured against a hand-taken reading on twenty artifacts. If
the two agree, the split earns its place. If they disagree, the graph was measuring citation and
this essay was wrong.

## The trade-off accepted

**Accepted:** a half-counted Service is easier to trust than it deserves.
**The bill:** the card prints which readings were counted and which were judged, on every line,
forever. A grade that hides its own provenance is worse than no grade, and this is the price of
counting anything here at all.

## Two rooms

Everything above is **proposed**. What is **proven** is the reading as it stands -- Service judged,
inputs printed, composite withheld until a reader supplies the number.
