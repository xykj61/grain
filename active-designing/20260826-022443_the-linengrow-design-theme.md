## The name, and what it instantiates

**Linengrow Design Theme** is the seated name for this tree's own theming layer -- the analog,
on our side of the collaboration, of DJINN's **Bit Design System**. His system is the template
pattern; ours is the instantiation in this tree's materials: Stone and Hearth grounds, the
woven-linen texture, the Vollkorn-Public-Source type trio, and the rota's five element rows as
the theme's own axis. One honesty note carried from the survey: DJINN's public repositories
ship without a license today, so the pattern reaches us as concepts until his own word licenses
more -- and this theme is written fresh either way, in `.brush` under TAME.

## The shape law -- rounded, and only rounded

Every themed enclosure -- card, key, chip, pane -- is **rounded: never square, never circular,
never elliptic.** The corner is a true arc blended into straight sides: enough straight edge to
read as a made thing, enough curve to read as a soft one. One named radius token per scale
step, so the softness is a dial rather than a mood. The falsifier is visual and honest: a tile
that reads as a box, a coin, or an egg sends its radius back to this page.

## Texture, shading, softness -- the tileable brushstroke

The surface is a **tileable brushstroke**: one small hand-shaped stroke tile, repeating without
visible seam, carrying the linen weave at cloth scale and the painter's hand at close scale.
Shading is woven rather than dropped -- elevation stays weave fineness, per the first-cloth
page -- and softness is the one dial that moves radius, stroke pressure, and shadow together,
so a profile turns one knob and the whole surface agrees.

## Motion -- the one-second body

The whole page arrives as one motion: **one second, whole-body**, the Skate default, tunable
per profile and falling to zero for a reader who prefers stillness. Parts arriving separately
is the falsifier; the page is one cloth and moves as one.

## Five encapsulations -- the rota thresholds as theme sets

The theme ships as **five encapsulations, one per element row**, each a `.brush` set named for
its threshold page and voiced by its palette pair (light terra by day, dark euphoria by night):

| Encapsulation | Threshold | Day voice | Night voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **aether** | the row that hears | mind - sound - dream | mystery - silence - hush |
| **air** | the row that feels | arc - touch - senses | spiral - presence - trance |
| **fire** | the row that sees | rockstar - vision - risk | hermit - night - surrender |
| **water** | the row that tastes | river - taste - love | tide - nectar - longing |
| **earth** | the row that smells | grain - aroma - harvest | mist - incense - fallow |

Each encapsulation keeps the same shape law and motion law and varies only accent, stroke, and
temperature -- five rooms of one house rather than five houses. The mapping from palette word
to visual token is DJINN's to draw; this page seats the frame and the names.

## What would send this back

A licensed word from DJINN that reshapes the template relationship. A radius family that reads
as one of the three refused shapes on real screens. Or the five encapsulations diverging until
they stop reading as one house -- the falsifier the table exists to catch.
