## The reading

Nineteen documents stand on the DOOR roster of
[`../tools/fixtures/p/prose_register_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/p/prose_register_scan.sh), held at
the strictest ceiling this tree writes: at or under 20% negative sentences, one cross-reference per
hundred words. Measured `20260910.191740`, **seven of the nineteen said so at their own door**.
Twelve stayed silent: two carry only a title where a `**Style:**` line would stand, and ten
declare a register while leaving the setting open. A reader arriving at `tally/README.md` could learn which ceiling holds that page only by
opening the scan.

The roster is a promise, and for twelve pages it was a promise the page itself left unspoken.

## What changed

Eleven of the twelve now name their setting in their own front matter. Seven took one clause --
`**Style:** Gauge (see ...)` became `**Style:** Gauge, Door setting (see ...)`, which is the
spelling ten of the roster's pages already wrote. `bat/README.md` and `mycelium/README.md` gained a
front-matter line beside their siblings'. `README.md` and
[`../docs-geode/tutorials/the-first-hour.md`](../docs-geode/tutorials/the-first-hour.md) declare
**Bhakta at the Door setting**, which says both true things at once: Bhakta is the register, Door is
the dial Gauge reads it on.

The twelfth is `foundations/20260823-034321_the-return-that-feeds-everyone.md`, and it keeps every
word it wrote. Its basename carries a one-clock stamp, so it is testimony, and testimony keeps its words
whatever a meter asks. The roster may hold a founding statement to the front-door share -- a
*reading* takes nothing away -- and asking that page for a clause would be an edit to a dated
artifact.

## The mechanism

`prose_register_scan.sh` gained one function and one gate. `door_setting_verdict()` answers
`testimony | declared | unnamed | absent` for each rostered path: a dated basename answers
`testimony` and is counted apart, and every other page is classified by the Style line beneath it.
The scan prints `door_setting_declared`, `door_setting_undeclared`, `door_setting_testimony` and an
`undeclared:` line per page, and refuses with `verdict=door_setting_undeclared` while the count
stands above zero. It reads **18 declared, 0 undeclared, 1 testimony** at this stamp.

The Style line beneath that verdict is read by the one reader
[`../tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh`](../tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh) publishes --
`declared_style_line_of()` and `QA_HEAD_LINES` -- lifted at run time with `sed`, the way
[`../tools/fixtures/q/qa_setting_declared_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/q/qa_setting_declared_scan.sh)
already lifts it. Three instruments now read one line one way. A second copy here is the exact
fault the lap before this one repaired, where two readers of one line disagreed for weeks while
announcing that they agreed. A scan that cannot reach the reader **refuses**, since a silent
reading and an absent one look identical from outside and only one of them is a fault.
`PROSE_CARD_READER` names the card, so a pen can hand in its own.

## Walled here, counted there, and the difference is consent

`qa_setting_declared_scan.sh` counts the same question across **330 living pages and gates
nothing**, for a reason that still holds: a page at large may honestly name no setting, Gauge's own
law says most know their own, and a gate that reds on ordinary work is a gate somebody turns off.

A page NAMED IN DOOR has already agreed to a ceiling. Saying which one costs it one clause and
gives every reader the page's own answer, so the roster earns a wall where the population earns a
count. **The wall says a door DECLARES its setting. It says nothing about the meter READING that
declaration**, which is the derivation question standing on Keaton's word since `20260910.175434`.

## Proof

[`../tools/fixtures/p/prose_register_control.sh`](../tools/fixtures/p/prose_register_control.sh)
runs **43 legs, `control_failed=0`**, eleven of them new. Six read the classifier on planted pages
-- a named Door setting, a Field setting, a silent page, the key written inline after another key,
a declaration below the head bound, and a lowercase spelling. Two prove the citation is live: a
card that is gone and a card that publishes no reader each refuse. Two more show the wall from both
sides on one warm page in a throwaway git repository -- undeclared refuses, and the same bytes one
clause later walk free -- because a refusal proven only in the passing direction cannot be told
from a bypass.

Both halves were mutation-proven before the lap closed. Dropping `door_undeclared` from the
verdict condition reads `door_gate_bites=no`; dropping the testimony arm makes the scan demand a
clause from a dated foundation and answer `verdict=door_setting_undeclared`. The exemption is
load-bearing rather than decorative.

## What this does not reach

**Whether a declared setting is the RIGHT one for its page.** The wall reads that a door names
Door; a page could name it and read badly at it, which is what the share beside it is for.

**The 114 living pages that declare a style and name no setting.** They are counted, ranked and
priced by their own census, and the ruling that would move them is not this lap's to make.
