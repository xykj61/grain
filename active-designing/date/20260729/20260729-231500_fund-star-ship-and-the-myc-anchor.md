## 2. The myc definition — siloed from the money teaching

The study's five claims, folded into our universals:

**No fixed total supply — supply is a fold, not a cap.** A hard cap is the quantity-anchor mistake the teaching spends two books dismantling. Instead: `myc_supply = Σ issued − Σ taxed`, a pure fold over the append-only log of signed multisig facts. Anyone recomputes it; nobody declares it.

**The star tax is the demand engine and the anchor at once.** *Taxes drive money*: myc is wanted because a reserved star name is payable only in myc, to the twelve. And *the anchor is a price*: the multisig pins the **star reservation price** as the system's fixed point — our buffer stock is names rather than labor. One instrument, both jobs, which is the kind of compression the tree prizes.

**Issuance is functional and bounded.** The multisig issues to a *named purpose* (a fund's seated work), never to a balance — and per our own rule two, **issuance per equinox carries a named ceiling** in the brix, changeable only by a recorded 12-multisig fact. The bound is ours, not the teaching's; the teaching supplies the purpose-test, our universals supply the ceiling.

**Recommended opening numbers, honestly labeled a proposal:** genesis issuance **12,288 myc** (1,024 per fund — powers of two, one journey of journeys each); star reservation price opening at **64 myc**, re-affirmed by the multisig each equinox close (e63 · e127 · e191 · e255, the bundle's own rounds); per-equinox issuance ceiling **4,096 myc**; **targeted drift zero** — the measurable is the star price's stability across re-affirmations, and a multisig that must raise the price is the inflation signal, visible in the log before it is visible anywhere else. Numbers are one word from different ones; the *shape* — fold-supply, price-anchor, tax-drain, bounded-issuance, purpose-issuance — is the seat.

**What the measurable rewards:** names reserved and kept, not volume issued. A multisig paid in its own tax has no reward for issuing beyond purpose — the Civic Style alignment, closed in the architecture.

## 3. Peer discovery — the Sui adaptation fused with the elder's Ames

**What each brings.** The modern lane contributes *discovery as gossip over a committee*: peers exchange signed self-descriptions (address, keys, freshness), keep bounded tables, and prefer recently-verified liveness. The elder lane contributes *identity-first routing*: a permanent ID **is** the address; encryption to the ID rather than the transport; peers relay for one another; the network forgives moved transports because the name never moves.

**The fusion, in our vocabulary:** a ship's Kumara key is the address (elder insight); what gossips is a **signed self-description fact** — transport hints, freshness stamp, star lineage — folded into each peer's bounded table (modern insight). Stars serve as **first-hop relays and introducers** for their ships, the way the elder's mid-tier routes for its children; the twelve funds publish root descriptors. Liveness is re-verified on our one clock; stale descriptors age out at a named bound.
