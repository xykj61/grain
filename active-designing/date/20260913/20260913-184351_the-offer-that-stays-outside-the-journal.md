## The matter

The first Dimeroll milestone carried two different thresholds. Its Simple line said one verified
offer receipt enters an entity's journal. Its product wall kept offer value distinct from recognized
value, obligation, and settlement. The accepted receipt contract later made the boundary executable:
an offer arrives as evidence with `recognition_status=unrecognized-offer` and
`journal_entry_count=0`.

## Forward judgment -- oldest evidence first

The itinerary's own product wall settles the meaning: an offer can inform the books before it
changes them. The accepted contract adds the checkable representation and refusal edge. The first
milestone should therefore classify the verified receipt as evidence and show why no journal entry
exists yet. Recognition belongs to a later use, obligation, settlement, or correction fact whose
accounting rule has been named.

**Disposition: MOLTED.** The living Dimeroll itinerary now says the first verified offer enters an
entity's evidence intake with zero journal entries. Its Lovable line pairs the receipt card with a
plain recognition reason rather than a journal line. The dated design testimony remains unchanged.
