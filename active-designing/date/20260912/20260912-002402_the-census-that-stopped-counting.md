## The part worth keeping

**The headline never signalled the drift.** Rows read twenty at every one of those commits and reads
twenty today. So a reader who spot-checks the census -- and a reader checks the total, because the
total is the cheapest thing to check -- finds agreement, and carries that agreement to the breakdown

A census whose total stays right while its parts drift costs a reader more than one that drifts in
plain sight. A figure that drifts visibly earns a repair on the lap somebody notices. A figure that
keeps agreeing buys trust for the sentences around it every time it is checked.

## The three states, promoted from prose to a reading

The elder paragraph drew a distinction that stayed in words alone, and it is the distinction that
makes its **six** honest where a plain count of unrostered tools reads seven:

- **Rostered** -- the row names a tool carrying its own `path` row in
  `construction/standing-equipment.kyri`, so a lap runs it.
- **Driven** -- the row names an unrostered tool *beside* a rostered one, which is the table's own
  reading of `tools/t/tame_style_scan_bans.rish`, *driven by* `tools/t/tame_style_check.rish`.
- **Unreached** -- no lap runs anything the row names.

Sharing a row is the claim, so a tool standing elsewhere in the table is reached only where its own
row reaches it. The pen proves that discrimination both ways: one row naming both tools reads
`driven`, the same two tools split across two rows read one `unreached`, with the roster untouched
between them.

**The wall and the ratchet are placed on purpose.** A table citing a tool that is gone tells a reader
to run something that cannot run -- a promise standing on nothing, repairable on the lap it lands. A
row no lap runs is often correct and reasoned, so a wall there would red on rows nobody may repair,
and a wall that reds on honest work is a wall somebody turns off.

**Names beside sizes.** Both fault populations print their members every run rather than behind a
`--list` verb. That is this lane's own finding of `20260911`, one room over: a reported population
printed its size and sent its names to a verb whose two callers neither ran, and a bare count is
visible the way a locked door is.
