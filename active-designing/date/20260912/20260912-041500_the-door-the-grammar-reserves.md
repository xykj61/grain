## The reading

Gauge sets its dial by reader, and the dial runs through the code. **Meter** sits beside a bound,
where the comment says why the number is that number. **Door** sits at the head of a module, where
somebody arriving cold deserves a plain sentence about what the thing is for.

Zig, and Rye after it, writes that setting into the grammar. A `//!` line is a module doc comment
the compiler accepts only at the top of a file, so the door is a **position the language reserves**
rather than a habit a reader has to infer. A `//` block above the same code is prose; a `//!` block
is the same prose in the place every tool can find.

Read `20260912.035749` over the 1,973 tracked authored modules -- `git ls-files '*.rye'` less
`vendor/`, `gratitude/` and `old/`, which are somebody else's dial:

## What the ten actually were

**Eight of the nine carried real Door prose.** `comlink/query_wire_retention_cost.rye` opens on a
WHY paragraph naming the design question the program settles -- whether a caller keeping an answer
should keep the decoded struct too, or decode again on every read -- and says how it counts its own
retired instructions. `linengrow/retting_timer.rye` names its two commands, its append-only log, and
where the log lives. Each was a door a reader walked through and every tool read as a wall.

**The tenth carried its prose inside `main`.** `rye/tests/version_test.rye` explains
`builtin.zig_version` in a comment attached to the statement below it, which serves a reader already
inside the function and answers nobody arriving at the file. It gained a head this lap.

**Three of the nine are one data table reached three ways.** `brushstroke/font8x8_data.rye` is the
file; `linengrow/font8x8_data.rye` and `tools/rye/font8x8_data.rye` are symlinks to it, which the
tracked listing reports as three paths and the disk holds as one. It opens on a single attribution
line naming its public-domain source -- that line IS the door, and it simply wore the ordinary
marker. The attribution text stands word for word and only the marker moved, so one edit answered
all three paths. Worth saying plainly: the reading counts PATHS, and a symlinked module is counted
once per path. That is right for a meter asking whether a reader arriving at a path meets a door,
and it means the ten silent paths were nine distinct files.

**All ten paths stand repaired -- nine files -- and the four that build as programs were rebuilt on metal** through
`rye/bin/rye build` with `RYE_ZIG=vendor/zig-toolchain/zig`, so the compiler's acceptance of the
form at each head is measured rather than assumed. The font table was proven through its importer:
`tools/h/hunk_skate_witness.rish` compiles `brushstroke/skate_grid.rye` and reads GREEN.

## Why nobody had met this

`tools/fixtures/c/comment_dial_scan.sh` has counted the form since `20260824`, and it gates nothing
**on purpose**. Its own header says so: *a measurement taken to answer a question is finished when
the question is answered*. That is a good rule and it was kept. The consequence is simply that the
reading stood for eighteen days with no lap meeting it, which is the shape the two-rooms doorway
census named one room over -- a finding repaired by hand twice in two days and regrown both times,
because the repair lived at census time rather than at write time.

So this is a second instrument rather than a widened first one. The measurement keeps its scope and
the wall gets its own.

## What the wall gates, and what it lets past

`tools/fixtures/r/rye_module_door_scan.sh` holds `silent` at **zero** -- a wall rather than a
ratchet, on the doorway guard's own line: nothing structural holds a living module's head silent, so
a floor above zero would be slack rather than a fact. For the common shape the repair is one
character on a line that already existed.

Two exemptions stand, each named and each counted:

- **Dated testimony.** A module whose own basename carries a one-clock stamp is testimony, and
  accrete-never-break keeps every word it wrote. One stands today,
  `construction/20260703-202312_crossing_manifest_seed.rye`, a graduated elder seed kept for its
  inbound references.
- **A pen fixture.** A module under a `fixtures/` path is a planted control, and a control must stay
  free to carry the fault it plants -- the same reason the ASCII document family reads `fixtures/`
  past, since its own mojibake control must keep its high bytes. Sixty-one stand.

Both are **printed as counts**, so an exemption that quietly widened shows as a number rather than
as a cleaner tree. A reach floor sits under the whole reading, since a gate held at zero cannot tell
a clean collection from a dead instrument, and each exemption must match at least one path: an
exemption matching nothing has stopped being an exemption, and the reading below it cannot be
trusted either way.

## Three things the control taught by refusing

**A rule spelled twice drifts the first time somebody widens one copy.** The scan first found its
exempt classes with a grep and its remainder with the matching inverse grep -- one rule in two
places. The mutation that widens the fixture exemption to every path moved only the first, and the
gated corpus did not collapse. Each rule is spelled once now and its complement is taken with
`comm`, so the mutation bites.

**A dead line kept answering.** The first draft computed `gated` twice, once by counting the gated
list and once by subtracting the exempt counts from the total. Both agreed on the living tree by
arithmetic accident. In the pen the subtraction read **-1**, which is what surfaced it.

**A number that reads the same either way proves nothing.** The first mutation flipped a line the
rewrite had already removed, so `cmp` found the copy unchanged -- and a control that cannot tell an
inapplicable mutation from a passing one would have reported the check proven. The legs assert the
mutation **applied** before they assert it **bites**.

## What it costs, and what that bought

The first draft read one module per `grep`, which is 1,911 processes and **27 seconds**. One `awk`
over the whole gated corpus reads the same tree in **one**. A guard on the lap tier has to be
affordable or it quietly becomes a cadence tier -- and a cadence tier is exactly the shape this
reading exists to refuse, since there the hand that wrote the module is not the hand that meets the
refusal. A stranger repairs somebody else's door, up to five rounds later.

The whole witness runs in **5 seconds** including its 45-leg control.

## What this does not reach

**Whether the sentence behind the door welcomes anybody.** A `//!` line restating the filename
counts here exactly like a paragraph a stranger could follow. Position is checkable; the welcome is
graded by `sh tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh`, which is a different instrument answering a
different question.

**The other settings of the same dial.** `comment_dial_scan.sh` reads 12,076 asserts standing under
no `// invariant:` line and 242 modules asserting with no invariant line at all. Those are Meter
readings, they are larger, and they are their own lap.

**Rishi, Glow and shell.** Each has its own comment grammar, and none of them reserves a position
the way Zig's `//!` does, so a door meter for those languages would be reading a habit rather than a
grammar. Named here as a gap rather than assumed closed.
