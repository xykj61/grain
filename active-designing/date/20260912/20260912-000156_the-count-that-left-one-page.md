# The Count That Left One Page and Stood in Another

**Stamp:** `20260912.000156` -- **Setting:** Gauge, Field -- **Voice:** Kyri
**Room:** checkable -- the reading below is gated by a witness, and both directions are proven on the standing tree.
**Status:** Landed
**Seat:** the council rota's Earth row, which breathes in -- read the concrete fact at the door before any argument about it.
**Instrument:** [`../tools/fixtures/c/crushed_index_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/c/crushed_index_scan.sh) under [`../tools/cr/crushed_index_witness.rish`](../tools/cr/crushed_index_witness.rish), pen [`../tools/fixtures/c/crushed_index_control.sh`](../tools/fixtures/c/crushed_index_control.sh)

**A guard took a count out of one page's title, and the same count stood the next day in another
page's row, and it stood there unread.** That is the whole finding, and both halves are dated.

## What was already held

`tools/fixtures/c/crushed_index_scan.sh` guards the shipping shelf's indexes, and it had already met
this subject four times. Three of the four firings were counts in **prose**, each repaired by hand by
moving the number into a table something derives. The fourth stood in a **title**, and the guard
closed that door on `20260911.122211` by refusing the **form** rather than the value:

> a declared index's basis is a room other hands grow, so a number in its name is a forecast

`docs-geode/lessons/README.md` opened `# Lessons -- eight walks` and gave that count up on
`20260911.135729` (commit `423357f27`). Its title reads *reading walks for someone meeting computing
itself* today, and `title_count_claimed` has read zero since.

## What stood unread

`docs-geode/README.md` -- the front door of the same shelf, the page a newcomer opens first -- carried
the row

```
| `lessons/` -> lessons/README.md | Eight walks for a reader meeting computing itself | ... |
```

**The count stayed on the shelf. It left the one line a guard read.** A row on an index makes the
identical claim about the identical basis: the lessons room holds eight, in a cell a reader trusts
exactly as far as they trust the title above it. Beside it stood a second, the press row, spelling
*three announcements, one magazine piece, one public projection and one paired telling* over a room
of seven members.

Every other reading in that guard reads past a row by construction. The member walk asks whether
each member has a row; the door check asks whether each link opens; the stamp check reads a stamp
cell. **A count typed in a row about another page's room sits outside all three.**

## Why the value reading cannot hold this, measured

The guard already owns a value reading -- `generated_count_disagrees`, landed `20260909.151033` --
and it fails here twice over.

**It reads digits alone.** Its pattern is `[0-9]+`, and it landed against a genuine fault spelling `38 rooms`.
Every count of a docs-geode room sits **below thirteen**, which is exactly where English prose writes
the word rather than the numeral. So the reading has stood green while reading past every form this shelf
actually writes.

**And a row reaches for a synonym.** The lessons index's own first column reads `Lesson`; the front
door's row said `walks`. The press index's column reads `Announcement`; its own abstract said
`pieces`. A noun match that sees both is a synonym roster somebody edits, which is the one thing
that file was designed never to become.

**A genre breakdown raises the cost further.** *three announcements, one magazine piece* is **true** of a room
holding seven, because it counts a genre rather than the room. A value check against the member count
refuses honest prose. The form refuses both without reading either.

## Why the form reading had to be narrowed, also measured

A bare form rule -- any cardinal on a row -- over-reaches, and the numbers say so. Over every living
non-testimony Markdown page it reads **91 rows tree-wide** and **10 on this shelf**, of which **2** are
the class. Read across the five declared indexes alone it reads **3**, of which 2 are the class.

**Those three figures are free**, measured `20260912.000156`: nothing holds them still, and each rises
whenever anyone writes a table row anywhere in the tree. Only the last reading is **walled** -- the two
the guard gates. So run the wide readings rather than trusting these, and the command is the rule
itself, with the room test and the plural test each lifted out in turn.

Two exclusions bring it to 2 of 2, and each is a false refusal that was actually produced rather than
imagined:

| Passes free | Why | Where it was found |
|---|---|---|
| a cardinal with a **singular** noun | a depth or a manner, never a census of more than one thing | the front door's wiki row, *rather than one room deep* |
| a cardinal inside **link text** | a piece's title quoted in a cell; the row names it rather than counting | the front door's blog row, *Eighteen times, two agents did the same job* |

**Both sides opt in.** The row must sit on a declared index, and must link another declared index's
door. That is what keeps the reading off English: the refusal is about a promise two pages made.

The exactly-one case (*one page stands*) reads singular and walks free here. The title reading holds
it on the page's own front, which is where a room of one announces itself.

## What landed

`row_count_claimed`, gated at zero. Run against the standing tree it named both sites by name and
refused; with the two rows repaired it reads zero and the verdict walks free -- the RED proven on real
history rather than only in a pen.

Four sites were repaired. Two were the wall's own population, and two were body prose on
`docs-geode/press/README.md` that no reading reaches, taken on touch:

- the front door's lessons row, to *Reading walks for someone meeting computing itself*
- the front door's press row, the genres kept and the cardinals dropped
- the press page's abstract, which opened on a **total** of the room it indexes
- the press page's repair paragraph, which said *the count moved out of the prose and into the table*
  while leading with a four-cardinal breakdown of the same room

**That last one is the sharpest fact in the lap.** The sentence recording the repair outran itself by
a genre: the tally left, the breakdown stayed, and the page argued for a discipline it had half kept
for two weeks.

## The pen

**71 legs pass.** Twelve are new, every refusal planted and then lifted, and the plant is
deliberately **true on the day it is written** -- `two pages` over a room holding exactly two -- since
a value check passes that plant right up until the day a third page lands, which is the fault.

**Two mutations were run and both bite.** Drop the plural requirement and the singular leg catches it;
leave link text in and the quoted-title leg catches that. A guard whose exclusions no leg defends is a guard
whose next widening removes them by accident.

## What this does not reach

**Body prose.** Two of the four repairs are sentences that stand on habit rather than on a wall. The
guard's own head already states why a value check cannot be widened there: a page quotes its own past
counts -- *this page said three for two weeks* -- and testimony reads identically to a live claim.
Those quotations keep every word.

**Whether a row says anything true** about the member it names. This proves the counting.

*May every number we write stand where something can check it, and may the page that gives one up
never hand it quietly to the page next door.*
