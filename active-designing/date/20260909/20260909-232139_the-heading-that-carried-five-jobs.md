A guard in this tree ends its head with a block titled **WHAT IS NOT PROVEN**, naming in plain
words the claim it declines to make. `tools/c/compass_station_scan.sh` wrote *that the rose names
the right six stations* and stood green over that gap for thirteen days, until a lap read the
sentence and repaired the floor beneath it. That repair raised a general question, recorded on the
card as open: **every scan carries such a block and nothing walks that queue.**

This page walks it once, and the answer is a deflation worth having. **The blocks are mostly not a
queue.** One heading is carrying at least five different jobs, and only one of the five is work
anybody could take.

## What was counted

**84 tracked files under `tools/` carry the block, one block each.** Both figures are exact and
free to move, so run them rather than reading them:

```
git grep -l "WHAT IS NOT PROVEN" -- tools/ | wc -l
git grep -c "WHAT IS NOT PROVEN" -- tools/
```

The corpus splits roughly half and half between witnesses (`tools/<letter>/*.rish`) and the scans
and controls they drive (`tools/fixtures/**`), and a scan and its witness usually spell the same
declination twice -- deliberately, since either may be read alone.

## What the blocks actually say

Each block was read. Four markers were then counted across them with rerunnable greps; **the
markers overlap on purpose**, because a single Caravan block declines a judgment, names a sibling,
and waits on a machine all in one sentence.

| Marker | Blocks | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Declines a judgment -- *the RIGHT one*, *is GOOD*, *worth reading*, *a reader's job* | **47** | not a gap; the guard naming its own edge |
| Waits on a machine or instrument that is absent | **13** | gated elsewhere, by hardware or by a second host |
| Waits on a seat, a law page, or Keaton's word | **11** | gated on a decision |
| Names a sibling guard that already owns the question | **8** | single-strandedness working |

**Forty-seven of eighty-four decline a judgment**, which is the finding. *That an A page is a good
page*, *that a fixed-width choice is the RIGHT width*, *that a document says anything USEFUL* --
these are not owed work. They are a guard refusing to launder taste through a number, which is the
strongest sentence in the whole corpus and the reason the convention earns its place.

**Thirteen wait on a machine.** Nine of those are one cause: the Caravan family's *no kernel
booted*, which the seated Microkernel Target chapter already owns. The rest name a second host or
an absent instrument -- a BSD pier, `minisign`, a readable energy counter, qemu.

**Eight hand the question to a named sibling.** `tools/t/tame-check.rish` sends the assert question
to `tame_style_check`; `tools/p/phantom_path_witness.rish` sends dangling references to
`dated_path`; `tools/s/seed_link_witness.rish` sends resolution to `tracked_link`. Each is a
factorization note wearing a gap's clothes, and reading them as a backlog would propose building
what already stands.

## The queue that is genuinely a queue

Five blocks name work a lap could take, and each names it in the guard's own words:

- **`tools/f/foundations_link_witness.rish`** -- *that an anchor names a real heading*.
  `tools/fixtures/f/foundations_link_scan.sh:19` strips the anchor and checks the path alone, so
  **no guard in this tree resolves a `#heading` anywhere**. A link that opens the right file at the
  wrong place reads green everywhere today.
- **`tools/d/dated_path_witness.rish`** -- references whose basename exists nowhere are counted
  rather than explained, and *telling those apart needs a history walk this rung does not take*.
  Some are renames, some are files that never landed, and only the first kind is repairable.
- **`tools/g/glow_literal_law_witness.rish`** -- the fourteen readers are compared on the leading
  zero alone; *overflow, sign, an empty string* stay unasked.
- **`tools/s/shipped_binary_claim_witness.rish`** -- *that every command in the manual runs*, named
  in the block itself as a reader's walk and a separate round.
- **`tools/e/exec_bit_witness.rish`** -- *that a shebang names an interpreter this machine has*.
  The tree proves it can run what it says to run, and stops one question short of whether the
  interpreter is installed.

Nothing here is taken. Naming five is the deliverable; four of the five are one bounded lap each.

## Why the heading came to carry five jobs

The block was drawn for one job -- **stop a reader from over-reading a green** -- and it does that
job for all five kinds equally well, which is exactly why they gathered under it. A reader meeting
*no kernel booted* and a reader meeting *that an A page is a good page* are both correctly warned.

The cost lands one level up, on anybody asking what the tree owes itself. There the five kinds want
five different answers: a declined judgment wants nothing, a sibling reference wants a reader
following it once, a machine gate wants a chapter, a seat wants Keaton's word, and only the fifth
wants a lap. **A single word answering two questions is the braid `single-stranded` names**, and
this is that shape in a documentation convention rather than in a variable.

**No new field is proposed.** Adding a class token to 84 blocks would ask every future guard to
classify its own gap at the moment it is least able to -- and the tree already has a place where
work is ranked, which is the card. The honest repair is what this page is: read the corpus once,
name the five that are work, and let the other seventy-nine keep doing the job they do well.

## The limit

**The classification is judged, and one reader made it.** The marker counts rerun; the reading
behind them is mine. A second reader would move blocks between the classes at the margin --
`tools/s/shared_pen_witness.rish`'s *that any pen is ever actually contended* sits between a
declined judgment and a structural impossibility, and either reading is defensible.

**Nothing here proves a guard is honest about its own edge.** A block could decline the small thing
and stay quiet about the large one, and only reading the guard's code would catch that. This page
read the blocks.
