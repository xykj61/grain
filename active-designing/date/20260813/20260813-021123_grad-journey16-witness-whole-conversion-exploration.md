## The crux

**The conversion is complete — and the seal proves completeness, not merely cleanliness.**

Completeness is measurable now: `vols_classify_scan.sh` over the live tree derives
`prose_gaps` by classifying every count-up site against the guardlist in force. A prose gap
is a site carrying a bare `lap N` that is neither guarded nor a kept witness handle —
exactly the drift LOWE relabeled. The live count is `0`. Sealing `prose_gaps == 0` makes
"the molt is finished" a standing, witnessed fact rather than a REMEMBER claim, and turns
the seal into a whole-tree guard: a future bare-ordinal prose identity born *anywhere*
raises the count above zero and bites, not only one born inside an already-watched file.

This is honest to handles: a new legitimately-named witness handle (a `foo_lap1.rish`
whose ordinal lives in the *filename*) classifies as a handle, never a prose gap, so the
seal governs drift without forbidding the sanctioned keep.
