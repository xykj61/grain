## What stood open

`context/TWO_ROOMS.md` asks a forward-facing page to name its register at the door -- **checkable**,
**vision**, **mixed**, or **research for understanding** -- and `tools/t/two_rooms_doorway.rish`
counts the pages that decline. The guard carries two numbers rather than one, because a page never
changes class: a stamp is in its basename or it is not. Dated testimony keeps a floor of **3**, since
accrete-never-break can never let anyone repair those three. The living half has always had a floor

sixteen repaired on the amending lap itself -- and left **39**: 33 in `active-designing/` and 6 in

## The 39 held three shapes, and only one of them needed 30 judgments

**Thirty are ALES fill captures** of the Lotus rungs, and their sameness is a reading rather than an
assumption: **30 of 30** carry both a `## What the witness proves (GREEN on metal)` section and a
`## The road on` section. So each holds a proven half beside a named horizon, and `mixed` is what
their own structure says. One token over thirty pages is one honest reading; the same line typed over
thirty pages of differing shape would be a shortcut wearing a reading's clothes.

**Three design pages are mixed for their own reasons.** `20260814-fill-constel-naming-law.md` seats a
law `tools/f/fora_name_witness.rish` binds, and names the fake-pier harness as a later round.
`proven-seat-guest-hammock.md` and `seam-season-hammock.md` are living ledgers whose green rungs and
pinned nibs stand beside parked rulings and a named Caravan horizon.

**The six lineage silos carried no Status line at all** -- the one shape in this family where
the door was silent rather than answering a different question. Each restates an outside whitepaper
in our own words under `context/SILO_TECHNIQUE.md`, and each names its two halves apart in its own
headings: *What Is Load-Bearing* and *What to Read as Vision*. All six read **research for
understanding**, and this is the first room where that fourth token is the honest answer for every
page in it.

## The reading, and the wall

`doorway stampless=138 living_silent=0 living_ceiling=0`, GREEN, over 1,267 pages in seven rooms
with 619 folded shelves reached. `tools/fixtures/t/two_rooms_doorway_control.sh` stands at **70
behaviors**, `control_verdict=ok`, each refusal planted in a pen and then lifted.

**A ratchet at zero is a wall**, and this one earns the name honestly: nothing structural holds a
living page silent, since a living path may always be repaired. So the next living page written into
these seven rooms without a room token reds on the lap it lands -- which is what the baton already
asks of every ship, in the clause written *where a habit is set, rather than only in the guard that
counts the damage afterward*. The habit and the wall now say the same thing.

## What this does not reach

**The dated floor of 3.** Those three pages are testimony, and a guard walled at 3 over a cell no lap
may touch is the same shape `dated_path` already reds on every ship's receipt. Moving it is Keaton's
word.

**The `yonder/` shelf.** Its 262 post-seating pages, 11 of them naming no room, were never inside the
elder reach, so pulling them in would widen the subject rather than restore it. That also waits on
his word.

**Whether a token is the right one.** The guard reads a word; a reader judges the register. Thirty-nine
doors now speak, and what they say is checkable only by reading the page behind them.
