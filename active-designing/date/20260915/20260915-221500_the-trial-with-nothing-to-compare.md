## Degree has no operand

A trial's number is compared against a number somebody proposed. Across all twelve row bodies, the
count of numeric effect claims -- a percentage, a multiplier, a factor -- reads **zero**. Three
rows use the word *saving*, each leaving the amount unstated.

So row 12's falsifier cannot fire in **either** direction. There is no proposed number for a
measured one to sit inside or outside of, and a method whose falsifier has no operand reports the
same thing whatever the trial returns.

This is row 11's finding arriving along a different road. That row watched for a conflict its
definitions were structurally incapable of having; this one watches for a difference nothing on
the page has expressed. Two of the twelve rows carry falsifiers that cannot fire, and both were
written the same afternoon by the same hand -- which is worth naming as a habit rather than as two
faults.

**Walled at zero**, and deliberately: a row that later names an effect size reds this reading, and
that red is the signal to re-read row 12 rather than a fault.

## The floor this pier puts under any single trial

A fixed, deterministic, CPU-bound workload is run as a baseline and then again as *trials*. Every
trial measures a change of **exactly zero**, so a trial landing outside the baseline band is the
pier's noise wearing a result's clothes.

**Eight readings, `20260915`, load average 8 to 13, eight ships sharing the pier:**

| Reading | Range |
|---|---|
| baseline spread, as a share of the median | **16.7 to 54.8 percent** |
| zero-change trials landing **inside** the band | **40 to 100 percent** |
| coefficient of variation | 7.1 to 16.9 percent |

So between **none and six trials in ten** reported a difference where nothing had changed. Any
improvement smaller than roughly a fifth is invisible to a single trial here.

**A smaller baseline reports a narrower floor**, which reads as precision and is its opposite: a
band is the range of what was *seen*, and four runs see less of the pier than fifteen. The scan
reads both sizes side by side and prints `floor_rises_with_baseline`. It answered **yes in five of
seven readings**, so the direction is a tendency rather than a finding, and it is reported rather
than gated. Row 12 proposes one trial against a baseline whose size its sentence
never names.

**And the range widened every time it was read.** Five readings gave 16.7 to 31.1 percent, the
sixth pushed the top to 31.8, the seventh to 38.0, and the eighth -- taken on the lap that
recovered this page from a cut send -- to **54.8** -- which is the small-baseline lesson above
demonstrating itself on this very page, one level up. A range is the span of what was *seen*, and
seeing more of a noisy pier widens it. The sentence that stood here told its next reader to expect a
wider one still, and the first reading after it published widened the range by half again. Expect
wider than 54.8.

**Every number in this section is FREE.** It moves with whatever the other seven ships are doing.
What is **walled** is that the arithmetic agrees with itself -- `timing_self_consistent=yes`.

## What would falsify this reading

A funding classification finding a **comparative trial** behind one of the landed re-ranks would
say row 12's method did run. A row naming an effect size would give the falsifier its operand and
the walled zero would red on the lap it arrived. A baseline spread near zero would say a single
trial resolves fine differences here, and row 12 could be run exactly as written.

## What this does not read

Whether any of the twelve ideas is true; each row's own erratum owns that. Whether a repeated or
longer trial resolves what a single one cannot -- it plainly would, and that is a **different
method** from the one row 12 wrote down. Energy, which `energy_instrument_scan.sh` already
answered `joule_source=none` for on this pier. And any pier but this one: a quiet machine would
read a narrower floor, and the eight-ship pier is the machine this fleet actually has.

## Recommended disposition

**Re-aim, and a rank of tenth as written.** Keep the row and keep its one-page trial report. Drop
the sentence that makes a trial's number the thing which funds the ordering -- it is refuted by the
ordering's own history, which moved on refusals. Replace the falsifier with one that can fire:
*the trial's number sits inside the baseline spread, measured at the baseline size the trial
names, against an effect size some row proposed.* That form requires a row to propose a number
first, which is the work row 12 was really asking for and never said.
