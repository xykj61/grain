## The five doors

A family keeping its plant out of its own field has five ways through, and this tree has walked all
five. Three were already named; two are named here.

1. **Move the plant** -- REDS `%775`. The plant leaves the shape the guard reads and takes a form
   the guard skips by construction; the derived-spine control moved its unshared numbers to a
   five-digit run for exactly this reason.
2. **Read past the fixtures** -- `tools/fixtures/e/empty_document_scan.sh`. The population excludes
   every `fixtures/` path by rule, and the exclusion is printed rather than silent.
3. **Count your own control, in a counter that says so** -- `instrument_absence_scan.sh`, whose
   `blind_captures_in_fixture` exists to hold exactly the sites inside its own control, apart from
   the field, with the exemption proven in both directions by name.
4. **Plant in a separate root, handed to the proof by an override** -- `tools_py_ban`. The living
   reading walks `find tools`; the plant stands under `context/fixtures/`, and the selftest reaches
   it by setting `TOOLS_PY_SCAN_ROOT`. The living population never walks that root at all.
5. **Plant under a name the population's own filter excludes** -- `copy_sameness`. The scan reads
   `find . -name 'tally_copy.rye'`; the plant is `tally_copy_drifted.rye`. One basename apart, and
   the guard cannot see its own control however deeply it walks.

Doors 4 and 5 are the quietest of the five, and that is what makes them worth naming. Doors 1
through 3 leave a trace a reader meets -- a moved file, a printed exclusion, a counter with a name.
Doors 4 and 5 leave the field reading exactly as it would read with no plant in the tree, so the
guard is clean and **nothing anywhere says why**.

## What this does not reach

**Whether a door is a fault.** All five are legitimate moves, and door 4 is arguably the cleanest
design of the five -- a plant in its own root cannot inflate a population by accident. The probe
classifies; it never gates.

**Whether an `unmoved` plant reading is a compliment.** It says the plant stands outside that
guard's population. It says nothing about whether the guard would have been right to read it.

**A family with no plant and no control.** `living_docs_lint` is unread, and no widening of this
instrument reaches it: there is nothing to take away. Naming what a proof for it would even plant is
work for whichever lane owns that guard.
