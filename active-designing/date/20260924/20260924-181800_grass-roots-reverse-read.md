## What this foundation gathers

[`The Roots Beneath the Work`](../../../foundations/20260628-140812_the-roots-beneath-the-work.md) gathers seven separate essays into one account of why the modules exist. It connects person-held identity, a plain workspace, four promises, a shared commons, learning, a humane daily rhythm, and a device that can forget. The composition rewards a reader who asks what a technical choice serves. Each essay still carries its own narrower claim.

## What the first receipt can carry

The [accepted receipt contract](../../../active-designing/date/20260912/20260912-201126_the-receipt-you-can-read-contract.md) describes one local, synthetic offer. Bounded admission and replay give the holder a record whose terms and expiration can be checked. Linengrow reads an offer; Dimeroll reads evidence of an unrecognized offer with zero journal entries. Those distinctions reward truthful records without treating an offer as a payment.

The contract does not establish person-held production keys, a public commons, a teaching path, a life rhythm, or the proposed device. Earlier reverse readings placed those promises at their later product seams. This foundation helps judge those seams together; it does not add another field to the first receipt.

## Disposition

**Standfasted as a composition; no molt, repair, or module handoff.** Keep the seven essays separate and this page as their map. For the first receipt, the checkable reward is bounded, inspectable evidence. The larger rewards remain product principles until their own acceptance work supplies a witness.
