## The older premise

[`A Conversation with Serena`](../../../foundations/20260702-202612_a-conversation-with-serena.md)
asks whether a ledger can hold grace. Its answer draws a boundary: a book can preserve evidence of
an act of care, while the act's human worth lives beyond the book. This is an imagined, dated
conversation, so its words stand as testimony. The product question it raises is current: what
does a value receipt reward a reader for believing?

## The present seam

The [accepted first receipt contract](../../../active-designing/date/20260912/20260912-201126_the-receipt-you-can-read-contract.md)
admits one synthetic offer at one stated value. Mantra replays that fact into two separate product
readings. Linengrow shows the holder's offer and promised return. Dimeroll takes the same fact as
evidence, marks the offer unrecognized, and writes zero journal entries. An offer has been made;
use, obligation, and settlement have not happened. The cold roster on `20260925` passed
`receipt_contract_ceiling`, `receipt_product_braid`, `linengrow_receipt_offer`,
`dimeroll_receipt_offer`, `mantra_receipt_offer_snapshot`, and
`mantra_receipt_offer_refusal_chain` with `tree_moved=no`.

The [Dimeroll product wall](../../../construction/DIMEROLL_ITINERARY.md) keeps offer value,
recognized value, obligation, and settled value distinct. That separation rewards a truthful
account of what happened and what is still owed. It also leaves a limit visible: the receipt
measures a declared exchange and its evidence, not devotion, care, or a person's worth. A future
care application would need its own consent and purpose before recording acts; this first receipt
cannot establish that policy.

## Disposition

**Standfasted as a product boundary.** Keep the foundation's distinction between evidence and
human worth in view when the value and books milestones grow. The current offer and zero-entry
intake already respect the narrow boundary. No product type, amount, or journal rule changes in
this packet. The next owner of a care or giving product should test whether its records help a
person receive care without turning a count of acts into a ranking of people.
