## The decision

Two moves, seated together:

1. **Pause the sky.sol purchase ambitions** — held as a **saved background option**, not abandoned. The on-chain financial layer (sky.sol, an MMT-style L2, treasury and governance designs) stays a real north star the project may take up if it chooses, on Keaton's word and with licensed counsel first. Pausing costs nothing and frees the near-term focus.
2. **Prioritize `xykj61` as the dev star** — the development identity and the near-term center of gravity. The work that ships now is software and honest documentation under the `xykj61` hand, not token or treasury purchases.

## Why it is shaped this way

This follows straight from the pick-the-first-slice reasoning: the crypto and monetary north stars fail the *sellable-and-safe* bar for a first slice, while the dev work and the trust-directory wedge are whole, low-risk, and ready now. Naming the pause plainly keeps the ambition safe on the shelf, so nothing is lost and the focus stays clear.

## What this does not do

- **It does not delete or foreclose sky.sol.** The option rests, resumable by name.
- **It does not touch any key, wallet, or on-chain holding.** Those are Keaton's own hand and stay untouched.
- **It is not counsel's ruling.** Any eventual on-chain step still waits on licensed securities, money-transmission, and tax counsel first.

*The near stars first: build under the dev hand where trust is cheapest to earn, and let the on-chain sky wait, whole and saved, for a season that chooses it.*
