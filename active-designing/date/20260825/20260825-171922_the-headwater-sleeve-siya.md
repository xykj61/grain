## What the region changes

The Kansas City reading put the real-estate sleeve under a mill's roof and leased it to a fibre operator. On the high plain the durable tenant is a different kind of business: **the works and the businesses that stand at the basin's doors**, whose receivables are water bought over twenty years, chemistry captured for a federal programme, and nutrients recovered under a state specification. The fibre corridor here is the campus's *water customer* -- the beneficial use that makes reuse water worth its permit -- so the sleeve reads the campus first and the field second.

## Four hands, one campus

| Sleeve | Keaton's stated share | Corridor touch on the high plain | What the sleeve's reward measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| public | ~30% | listed firms whose revenue is public water, remediation, digestion, and mineral recovery -- the sector list Keaton pasted, read by role, screened by the five screens | daily price, dividends, liquidity |
| real estate | ~30% | the ground and roof beside the works: a digester-and-nutrient plant at the barn door, a degumming line at the outfall door, bale barns on the reclaimed-water acres, net-leased to operators | cash yield, preservation, the sponsor's promote |
| venture | ~20% | the brand, from a first capsule that names its water | a few large outcomes over many years |
| private business | ~20% | the grower-and-baler operating company on the reclaimed-water lease, or the nutrient-recovery operator | earnings and control |

**The public plant itself stays public.** The treatment plant is a utility authority's, built with a 75% federal share; a private syndicate touches it only through the private businesses that stand beside it -- a design-build-operate contractor's yard, a digester the dairies co-own, a PFAS-treatment vendor's site -- and never through the plant's own ground. That line is the Wyoming public-benefit charter's line as much as the authority's.

The digester row is the honest tension in this memo. A nutrient plant at the barn door serves the county's dairies, and the family's own ethic keeps dairy off its table. Counsel names it and parks it: the door exists whether or not this fund touches it, the harm it removes is real, and whether the fund's hand belongs there is Keaton's word behind the charter.

## Falsifiers

The reading is wrong if the plant's residual is sealed as deep-well injection into a formation shared with the brackish supply, which fails the custody screen for every business beside it; if the reuse permit forbids a fibre field on reclaimed water; if a candidate sponsor's own money in the deal is under a share that would hurt; or if the fiduciary finds the family's liquidity too thin for the decade a works campus takes.
