## The matter

The accepted receipt contract sends one `ReceiptOfferFact` through Kyri, Tally, Mantra, Linengrow,
and Dimeroll. Its public boundary needs one settled answer ahead of implementation: does the
`schema` field version an encoding, or does it name the kind of value crossing the seam?

## Reverse walk -- newest to oldest

The `20260912` contract puts `schema` first in the fixture and the public type. It also requires an
unknown schema to refuse before append. The `20260826` Earth threshold says a value crossing a seam
wears its name on its face. Its oldest deciding premise is the ratified `20260703` foundation
[`The Marked Value`](../foundations/20260703-202312_the-marked-value.md): a type-mark names the kind
of cargo, unknown marks refuse whole, and converters arrive only when a real crossing earns them.

## Forward judgment -- oldest evidence first

The marked-value premise now has metal beneath its first rung: Amphora accepts exactly two root
marks, writes each mark into its manifest line, and refuses an unknown mark whole. The receipt
contract already has the corresponding product shape. `grain.receipt-offer.v1` names one public
kind, travels with every encoded fact, and is checked ahead of durable state changes. The first
receipt can therefore use the existing schema field, registry footprint, and four public types.

**Disposition: STANDFASTED.** Treat `ReceiptOfferFact.schema` as this milestone's type-mark and keep
the contract's unknown-schema rejection. This preserves the oldest premise while holding the first
whole to one earned crossing. Incense owns any wording change to the accepted product contract;
Patchouli and Pheromone can implement the boundary through their existing module seats.
