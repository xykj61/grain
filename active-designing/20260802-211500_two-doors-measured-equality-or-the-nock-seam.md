## Where a2 leaves us

The fold is a running whole. One rune reaches two domains — a range and a bounded named sequence — and each domain carries two reducers whose identities are kept honestly apart. The bound comes from the closed-shape allowlist rather than from any hardcoded count, every desk names its own verb where a witness can see it, and the pair path delegates to the bytes that first served it. Nine deciding gates stand beneath, across five families.

What remains are two doors, and they are not the same size.

## Door one — the equality rune

`gth` speaks ceilings. It cannot speak *exactly*. That single gap is measurable, and here it is: **eight seated pedestals name an exact number and therefore cannot decide today.**

Aurora's living stages, seed length and signature length; Caravan's supervisor exit meanings; and all four of Mantra's counts — Line's three fields, Weave's two, Diff's two, Store's three directories. Each of those is a shape that names a number the tree already trusts, standing beside a gate it cannot yet have.

The work is bounded and its shape is known. One rune in the grammar, parsed beside `gth`, with an emitter that compares rather than orders — then eight small gates that the era lap already knows how to hold. It is a2's own unfinished business rather than a new frontier, and it finishes something the season began.

## Door two — the Nock seam, which is not what it sounds like

The roadmap has carried *"Glow value model + Nock seam"* as hardest-solvable number one since before this season, and the phrase reads like a mountain. **It is not a greenfield reach, and measuring the tree says so plainly.**

`nock/` already holds an interpreter: **1,097 lines, opcodes zero through eleven**, with the primitive core, the macro layer, and the jets each carrying their own witness. All four laps — core, dec jet, lap two, lap three — **run green on this tip**. Seventy-seven documents in the tree already reason about Nock; seven Rye files already speak it.

So the door is not *build an interpreter*. It is narrower and much more interesting: **the seam between a Glow value and a Nock noun** — how a closed tuple becomes a cell, how a fold becomes a formula, and which direction the trust runs across that join. That is a design question first and a code question second, and it is exactly the kind of question Gall's Law says to ask from a working whole rather than from an ambition.

## What counsel leans, offered once

**Equality first.** It is eight gates behind one small rune, it completes what a1 and a2 already started, and it costs the season nothing it cannot afford. The Nock seam then gets discussed from a tree where every seated number can decide — which is a better place to stand than one where eight of them still cannot.

That is a lean, not a verdict. The Nock seam is genuinely ready to be *talked about*, and if Keaton would rather spend the evening on the deeper question, the interpreter is already there to reason against.

## What neither door touches

R2 through R4 still want a word and a fresh check-in each. The glob, Q58, class-and-rooms, seat 128, shred execute, Class O, the geode, the old root zips, bar5 and the SEA paste stay exactly where they are.
