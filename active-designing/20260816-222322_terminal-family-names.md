## Why these belong together

A terminal you own (Dexter), a chat that runs on it (Scooter), and the intelligence that may one day answer inside it (the vane) are one family: the command line as a first-class Grain surface, bounded and proven, growing on Pond. Naming them together keeps the family coherent as each is built.
