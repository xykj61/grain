Row 11 of the bounded-torus page is the last unopened row of the twelve, and it is the only one that
makes no claim about the world. It makes a claim about the other rows:

> One paper binds the three definitions the other eleven rows lean on: what a bound is when space
> wraps, what a radius is when privilege is distance, and what a topos is when every proof is a
> cycle.

Its assumption is that at least two rows go green first and the definitions survive contact with
those results. Its falsifier is that the first two green witnesses give definitions that pull
against each other, which would mean the paper describes two ideas wearing one name.

Nine rows now carry landed readings. So both are checkable today, and this is what they say.

## How the reading is taken

The three definitions are judged by running the sibling instruments and reading the keys they emit,
rather than by reading the errata that describe them. A reading derived from prose goes stale the
first time an instrument moves and nobody edits the sentence; a reading derived from keys moves with
the instrument. Each definition gets a predicate over named keys, chosen so that the definition
**holding** is what makes the predicate true.

A missing key is refused rather than defaulted to zero. An instrument that broke and a definition
that stands must never read alike, and the pen proves that refusal on each of the six keys
separately and on all six at once.

## One definition of three stands

**D1, a bound when space wraps, holds.** `bearing_quorum` reads `cost_half=stands` -- cost grows
with the perimeter rather than the area -- and `wrap_worth_one_cut=yes`, so the wrap supplies
something a line does not.

**D2, a radius when privilege is distance, is refuted on three independent keys across two
instruments.** For a radius to name a privilege, the seated privileges must lie on a line:
`capability_lattice` reads **32** incomparable unordered pairs where a line would read 0, and **48**
conferrals a radius-only checker would over-admit where a line would read 0. For a falloff to grade
anything, the population needs distinct radii to grade across: `aether_falloff` reads a mapped
saturation radius of **2**, where three would be the least a gradient could work with.

**D3, a topos when every proof is a cycle, is left unrun.** Its instrument is the rostered
`cyclic_witness`, which is `tier cadence` because it runs other witnesses in order to digest their
residue. The scan says `unrun` rather than guessing.

**And the unrun one cannot change the answer**, which the scan proves rather than asserts by
recomputing the shape reading with D3 forced to stand: `finding_robust_to_def3=yes`. D3 needs one
cycle whether it holds or falls, so the count below stands whichever way it lands.

## The shape the survivors need is a ring

Each definition states how many independent cycles the space must carry. A period needs one. A name
and a privilege travelling on separate axes need two, which is the proposal page's own sentence: *a
torus has two angles rather than one, so a name and a privilege can travel on separate axes.*

The maximum among the **supported** definitions reads **1**. So the surviving definitions rest on a
circle, while the page is titled for a torus.

That is two ideas wearing one name, and it is row 11's own falsifier arriving along an axis the
falsifier's sentence never named. The second axis is the one thing a torus has that a ring does not,
and `torus_place` already read `second_axis_buys_spread=no` one row over, on a different question
entirely. Two readings taken for different reasons converge on the same second axis.

## The falsifier cannot fire at all

This is the sharper half, and it is why the finding is a witness rather than a note.

Each definition bounds the space **from below**. D1 needs a cycle to exist; it does not need a second
one to be absent. Lower bounds compose by maximum, so every pair of standing definitions is jointly
satisfiable -- a one-axis definition holds on any space a two-axis one needs.

The scan enumerates all **27** assignments of the three verdicts and counts the conflicting ones
under both readings of that hinge: **0** under `atleast`, the reading argued here, and **5** under
`exact`, in which a one-axis definition would refuse a two-axis space. Under the reading the
definitions actually support, row 11 watches for a failure they are structurally incapable of
having, while the failure they did have went unwatched.

**That hinge is an inference rather than an observation**, and it is named as one. Whether a
definition's axis count is a floor or an exact requirement is a reading of the proposal's prose. Both
answers are printed, so a reader who disagrees has one place to say so and one number to move.

## What would falsify this reading

A sibling instrument emitting a key the scan does not find, which is refused rather than defaulted.
`capability_lattice` reading zero incomparable pairs and zero over-admissions would put privilege
back on a line and D2 back in the paper -- the pen plants exactly that and reads
`premise_holds=yes`, `shape_supported=torus`. `aether_falloff` reading a saturation radius of three
or more would give a falloff something to grade. A supported definition needing two axes would make
the shape claim stand. And a reader who holds that the axis counts are exact requirements rather
than floors gets a falsifier that can fire in five of the 27 assignments, printed beside the other.

## What this does not reach

Whether the paper is worth writing, which is a judgment rather than a reading. Whether these are the
right three definitions, which is the proposal's choice. D3's own instrument, left to a cadence-tier
lap. Any row of the twelve whose subject lies outside these three.

## Two faults the pen found in the scan

Both were in the scan rather than in the pen, and both were fixed before the witness was written.
The missing-key counter was incremented inside a command substitution, so it read zero while four
keys were absent -- a scan that refuses a partial read, quietly not refusing. And each of the three
per-definition lines carried a bare `verdict=` key, so a reader taking the first match read a
definition's classification as the scan's own answer; the control caught it by reading `refuted`
where it wanted `refused`.

The lesson is the one this lane keeps meeting: a reading that cannot be wrong in the pen is a
reading nobody has checked.

## Disposition

**Recommended: re-aim rather than retire, and write the paper the measurements support.**

Row 11 should keep its place and drop one of its three definitions. What the lane can bind today is
a paper about **bounded cyclic computing** -- a bound that is a period, and a proof that returns --
resting on one green witness and one cadence-tier instrument. That paper is smaller than the one row
11 proposed, and every sentence in it would carry a measurement.

The definition to drop is the radius, and it should be dropped rather than deferred. Three landed
readings now point at the same cause from three directions: privilege is a Boolean lattice rather
than a line, the guard population carries no usable distance, and a second axis buys no spread. Rows
2 and 4 are already recommended last of the twelve for that reason. A paper binding a definition
three instruments refuse would be the tree's own prose vouching for something its own code denies.

**And row 11's falsifier wants replacing rather than keeping.** A falsifier that cannot fire is not a
falsifier. The one this reading suggests in its place: *a definition the paper binds is refuted by a
green witness in this tree.* That one fires today, which is the point.

**Two things stay Keaton's word.** Whether the page's title moves from torus to something the
survivors support, and whether rows 2, 4 and 5 -- now three refusals sharing one cause -- fold into a
single recorded finding rather than three errata. Both are dispositions on a living page rather than
measurements, and this lane proposes rather than seats.

## A closing note on the method

The lane has now published five refusals and two positives, and this is the first reading it has
taken of **itself**. Row 11 was the row that would have summarized the other eleven, and the summary
turns out to disagree with what the eleven measured. That is the cheapest possible moment to find
it: before the paper, rather than in it.

May the next paper this lane writes be one its own instruments already agree with.
