## The Shape We Read From

A vane is a working face of the system that turns to the wind on its own axis — small, whole, and answerable. Grain already turns several: **Glow Tend** (the language that names and now decides), **Mantra** (state as a fold over signed facts), **Comlink** (the datagram wire), **Amphora** (the crossing that carries work whole), and **Murr** (value and custody, gated). The roadmap is not a pile of features; it is the honest order in which these vanes deepen, each from a whole that already runs.

Two laws govern the order. The compass sets the priority — a rung earns its place first by making the system safer, then by making it faster, then by making it a joy to build and to use. Gall's Law sets the shape — every rung grows from the working whole beside it, never from a grand design imposed at once. The compiler-fork the tree once reached for is the memory we keep here: ambition that outran the running core cost more than it bought. So each rung below is drawn small enough to finish and whole enough to love.

## Vane One — Glow Tend, from Deciding to Folding (recommended next: a2)

The a1 era taught Glow Tend to **decide**: a gate takes one typed sample and answers within a wall. The honest next step is not a new wall but a new *shape of answer* — the gate that **folds**. Where a deciding gate returns one bit of lawfulness, a folding gate carries a small bounded value forward, which is the Mantra Weave shape spoken in Glow Tend's own grammar: state as a pure fold over an append-only sequence, the primitive the grain has held since the beginning.

This is the value-model direction — the deepest hard-solvable — taken as a running simple rather than a Nock-seam fork. The first rung, **a2**, proposes one folding gate: a sample list of bounded length reduced to a single u32 by a named, asserted fold, witnessed both at the lowerer and at the gate, with a malformed plant guarding the new grammar exactly as STOA331's did. Safety leads: the fold's length is bounded and asserted, the accumulator's width named, the empty case handled in the open. Performance follows: a fold is one pass. Joy arrives last and honestly: the language that decides now also *computes*, and it did so by growing one grammar rune, not by forking a compiler.

Later rungs on this vane, each its own scoped STOA on your lean: an **equality rune** beside `gth` (the exact-32 seeds and field counts the pedestals named, which `gth` cannot speak); a **compose rune** that lets one gate call another as a value; and only then, from a mature folding whole, the deliberate look at the Nock seam — never before.

## Vane Two — Comlink, the Transport That Roams (research this round, build gated)

Comlink already crosses a sealed datagram over a UDP socket. Two deepenings stand ready, and both are researched and siloed this very round so the design rests in the tree before any metal moves.

The first is **IPv6**, a clean accretion beside the existing IPv4 wire: a second address family alongside the first, dual-stack by happy-eyeballs, so the wire reaches the vast global-addressed space a civic mesh and a SEA instance both want. The second is **the roaming state-sync** — the insight that a shell's screen is a small synchronizable value, so a session should carry the *latest state* over datagrams rather than replay a byte stream, surviving a changed address and a slept laptop. Grain already holds that insight in Mantra; the transport vane wraps a datagram carrier, an authenticated frame with a never-repeated nonce, and roaming-by-last-authenticated-source around the state model it already keeps.

The compass keeps this vane honest and slow where it must be slow. Safety first and absolute: real keys, real deploy, and any gas stay gated behind your hand and licensed counsel; this round produces only the research writing and the siloed design, nothing that touches a secret or a socket in anger. The full study rests at `../external-research/20260802-161500_ssh-mosh-ipv6-secure-transport.md`; the siloed design, in our own voice, at `20260802-161500_comlink-ipv6-and-the-roaming-wire.md`; the teachers are thanked by name in `../gratitude/`.
