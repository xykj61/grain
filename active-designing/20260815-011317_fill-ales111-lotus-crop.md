## The crux, chosen Lindy-first

ALES2's **cut** removes the named span and keeps everything around it — the clip shrinks by exactly the span. This rung names its exact complement: **crop**, which keeps the named span and removes everything else. *Trim to Selection* — *Crop* — is the button every editor ships: circle the one phrase worth keeping, drop the rest, and the take becomes exactly that phrase. Where cut answers "remove this," crop answers "keep only this." The two partition every edit of a span into its two honest readings, and naming crop beside cut completes that pair.

## The rule, stated once

`crop(clip, start, count)` keeps the `count` samples starting at `start` and drops every sample before and after — the clip becomes exactly that span, its length exactly `count`. It refuses `BadRange` when the span falls outside the current samples (checked without underflow, `count` against the remainder) before any write, reusing `timeline.EditError` whole. One in-place forward pass bounded by `count` slides the kept span to the front, then the length is set to `count`; no allocation, no second buffer.

## The four laws

1. **THE CROP LAW** — after `crop(clip, start, count)`, the clip is byte-for-byte the samples `[start, start+count)` of the original and its length is exactly `count`, checked against a hand-built vector at the head, the middle, and the tail.
2. **THE COMPLEMENT LAW** — the crux that ties this rung to ALES2. Crop keeps exactly what cut removes and removes exactly what cut keeps: on one source, `crop(start, count)` yields the middle span, while `cut(start, count)` yields the head-plus-tail around it, and splicing the crop's result back at `start` into the cut's result reconstructs the source byte for byte. Proven side by side in one selftest.
3. **THE SUBSET / PEAK LAW** — crop is a pure subset (it invents no sample, reorders none, and only drops); the peak magnitude re-measured through ALES13 is **non-increasing** — cropping away the loudest sample strictly lowers the peak, cropping a span that still holds it holds it. The length is exactly `count`, never more.
4. **THE DEGENERATE / REFUSAL LAW** — `crop(0, len)` is the identity; `crop(start, 0)` yields the empty clip; the empty clip is its own crop; a `start` past the samples or a `count` past the remainder refuses `BadRange`, before any write, the clip untouched.

## Honest scope

Software only, purely local. A bounded in-process buffer of i16 PCM on one bench, siloed to `lotus/`. It reseats an existing span to the front and shortens the length — it invents no sample, changes no value, and reads no byte past `len`. No real sample rate, no network, no keys, no funds, no real device, no real speaker.
