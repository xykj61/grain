## The callings

Eight, each a discipline Caravan or Tally already lives, named here so a table row can wear one
word. The first five are the optimization spine's laws; the last three carry Gauge and Civic
into the fusion.

- **bound-in-shape** -- the budget lives in the structure's own coordinates; raising it convenes
  the compiler rather than the debugger.
- **wrap-named** -- a wrap is a meaning, never a net; periodic says so, linear asserts instead,
  and a refusal wears a named error.
- **replayable-fold** -- the same inputs in the same order leave the same state, proven by
  running it twice and comparing every byte.
- **derived-not-stored** -- what is derived from the declaration can never disagree with it; no
  side table exists to drift.
- **choir-and-bijection** -- every witness registered, every registration real, the roster
  measured rather than remembered.
- **door-and-meter-comments** -- a module opens with a plain sentence for a stranger; the reason
  beside a bound stays exact.
- **civic-name-the-reward** -- a surface that moves value says what it rewards, and whether that
  matches the outcome anyone wants.
- **gauge-register** -- every figure carries unit, date, and source; a health number is a
  measurement rather than a rumour.

Three patterns repeat across the table, inherited from the eight-modules reading: where a
coordinate already names its storage, the move is a witness that the coordinate stays honest;
where a roster grew one case at a time, the move is a classification into few families with a
parity proof; where many hands will someday contribute, the move is the signed fact and the
replayable fold, rehearsed on planted data now.

## The expansion law

Sound's widened lane is this charter's table. The order of a lap:

1. **Core first.** The lap opens on Caravan-Tally confidence: their witnesses green on this
   bench, the choir's bijection whole. Confidence read, the microkernel road and the spine keep
   first claim (move four stands next).
2. **Then the table.** Confident, the lap takes the constellation's next unpicked move,
   Lindy-first crux-first, honoring each row's gate as written.
3. **Then the seated shelf.** The lap may also take other seated or booked dev-itinerary work
   that competes with neither Mind (cardinal, Arbor -- Brushstroke and Surf) nor Dream (dual,
   the pier -- the Glow rune lowering). A sibling-lane find becomes an ITINERARY line rather
   than a lap.
4. **The gates stand whole.** Keys, funds, chains, hardware, publishing, and %6 stop every
   star equally; the seed cadence rides with Dream alone; the Pond accrete-only boundary above
   binds every documentation touch.

## Why it is shaped this way

A thirty-seat lane could scatter a bench across thirty half-moves; the table prevents that by
making every move one bounded round that closes on a witness or a graded page, and the
expansion law prevents it from another side by keeping the core's claim first. The callings are
the compression: eight words a lap can hold, proven somewhere real before they were asked for
anywhere new. What a seat cannot yet carry under the callings stays outside with gratitude --
a horizon named and left alone -- rather than dragged across the doorway half-heard.
