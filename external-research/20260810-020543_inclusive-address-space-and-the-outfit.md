## The Elder's Inclusion

The elder network's address space is **inclusive**, and this paper's whole subject is that one word. Its galaxies are the first of its stars, and its stars are the first of its planets -- a galaxy *is* a star *is* a planet, the same low number playing three roles. Sponsorship falls out of arithmetic: a planet's sponsor star is its number modulo the star count, a star's sponsor galaxy its number modulo the galaxy count. A galaxy, modulo anything at or above itself, is itself -- the root that sponsors its own roles.

The gift of inclusion is that nothing is counted twice. Were the spaces disjoint -- galaxies, then separate stars, then separate planets -- a universe would hold the sum of all three. Inclusive, it holds only the largest: every point is a planet, and the galaxies and stars are simply the planets that also wear those higher roles. This is the **minus-1 effect**: a galaxy does not add a fresh point when it also serves as a star; it already *is* one.

## Applied to Grain

Grain's d12-d60 sky takes the elder's law on its own counts. Twelve galaxies; five stars to a galaxy; twelve planets to a star. Inclusive:

- the **star space is sixty** -- the twelve galaxies among them, so `star = number mod 60`;
- the **planet space is seven hundred twenty** -- the sixty stars among them, so `galaxy = number mod 12`;
- the **universe is 720**, not 792, because a galaxy is never counted apart from the star and planet it also is.

A galaxy `G`'s five stars are `{G, G+12, G+24, G+36, G+48}` -- itself first. A star `S`'s twelve planets are `{S, S+60, ..., S+660}` -- itself first. The sponsor chain is pure modulus, and it climbs to a galaxy that roots itself.

The odd-quorum sky (fifteen galaxies, three stars, nine planets) takes the same law: a star space of forty-five, a planet space and universe of **405**, a galaxy leading a d27 of twenty-seven -- a place for every nakshatra. One law, any counts; the geometry is data (a **Sky**), and inclusion is part of the law, not the numbers.

## The Outfit

Inclusion gives a name to a warm idea. Because a galaxy is also a star and a planet, it **wears** each lower role as an aspect of itself -- and the sixtieth division, the **d60**, is classically the reading of *prosperity, appeal, fame, and fortune*: the many faces a fortunate life turns to the world. A point's `decode` names its *primary* role, the highest it holds; a `plays` test asks whether it may wear a given one. A galaxy plays all three; a pure planet, only its own.

This is the same shape the social skin wants. One identity need not wear one face: it may keep several **outfits** -- a genre, a theme, a mode for each side of a life -- and show the one that fits the hour, never forced into a single profile or a single feed. The address space and the wardrobe are one idea: an identity is whole, and it is many. What the topology proves in numbers, the surface will offer in cloth.
