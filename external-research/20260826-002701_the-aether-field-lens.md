## The four electricians, reported

**Tesla** told the American Institute of Electrical Engineers in May 1891 that electricity and
ether phenomena are one and the same. He went further in "Man's Greatest Achievement," drafted
in 1907 and published later in the Milwaukee Sentinel. There he wrote that all perceptible
matter comes from a primary substance filling all space -- the akasha, or luminiferous ether.
Prana, the creative force, stirs it into form, and matter returns to formlessness when the
stirring ceases. He took the Sanskrit terms from his 1896 acquaintance with Swami Vivekananda
(Kak, LinkedIn essay; Swarajya; reads 2026-08-26). His standing splits cleanly in two. The
engineering -- polyphase AC, the induction motor, resonant radio work -- is foundational and
fully mainstream. The substantial ether was set aside by physics after the Michelson-Morley
experiment (1887) and special relativity (1905), and he declined relativity to the end.

**Steinmetz** was General Electric's chief consulting engineer. He gave AC engineering its
phasor mathematics and its law of hysteresis, and both are textbook to this day. His "Four
Lectures on Relativity and Space" (McGraw-Hill, 1923) accepted relativity and treated the field
as a condition of space itself. The revival tradition claims him chiefly for his transient-wave
work, and for teaching that energy travels in the field around the conductor rather than inside
the copper. On that point the mainstream agrees with him entirely; Poynting proved the flux
picture in 1884. He is the bridge: a field-first teacher standing wholly inside accepted
physics.

**Eric Dollard** is a living researcher writing in the Tesla-Steinmetz lineage. He teaches a
four-quadrant theory of AC through versor algebra, a rotation-based method extending the phasor.
He holds dielectricity and magnetism to be two aspects of one aether. He also claims
longitudinal magneto-dielectric waves that travel faster than light (versoralgebra.com; "The
Theory of Anti-Relativity," gestaltreality.com; reads 2026-08-26). He publishes through A&P
Electronic Media and Borderland Sciences rather than peer review. The superluminal claims stand
unverified, and they conflict with established electromagnetism. His durable offering is smaller
and real. He sends readers back to Steinmetz's original texts, and he takes the capacitor's
dielectric field as seriously as the coil's magnetic one.

**Ken Wheeler**, writing as Theoria Apophasis, self-published "Uncovering the Missing Secrets of
Magnetism" (2nd ed., 2014; archive.org read 2026-08-26). He holds magnetism to be spatial and
radiative, and dielectricity to be counterspatial and inertial. He calls field lines a
perceptual artifact. He calls the orbiting-particle electron a fiction, reading charge as a
relation in the field. He names the ether apophatically, as counterspace, and cites Plotinus
openly as his method's source. His standing: fringe, with zero peer review, and critics find the
terminology idiosyncratic. Two of his denials run against measurement. Millikan counted charge
quantization in 1909, and single-electron detection is routine laboratory work today. His
durable offering is the question itself, asked stubbornly: what IS a field, beneath the
mathematics that predicts it?

## The two elder registers

**Plotinus** (Enneads, 3rd century) teaches that all things flow from the One by emanation, and
that the source stays undiminished by all it pours forth (Ennead V.2.1). In the intelligible
order, each part carries the whole (Ennead V.8.4). This is monism with a geometry: one
substance, self-differentiating, entire at every point. It is the same shape the topos essay
finds in the sheaf. It is also the shape Wheeler reaches for when he names the ground by what
exceeds its appearances. The Neopythagorean current beneath Plotinus adds that number and
harmony structure the real. That is the oldest form of the claim that mathematics is found in
the world rather than draped over it.

**Akasha**, in the Samkhya and Bhagavata accounts the topos essay already carries, is the first
and subtlest gross element. It is the field that bears sound and gives every later form its
room. Tesla's 1907 paper borrows exactly this word. So the electrical lineage and the Vedic one
meet in a primary document rather than in a modern analogy.

## Where the lens stands, honestly

The observation: mainstream physics is itself field-monist at the level of ontology. Einstein's
1920 Leiden address allowed that spacetime, endowed with physical qualities, may be called an
ether -- with the one caveat that it has no state of motion. Quantum field theory's textbook
position (Weinberg, The Quantum Theory of Fields, 1995) holds that fields are the basic
entities. Particles are their quanta: excitations of a continuous substrate. The inference: the
revival figures' directional intuition sits closer to the textbook than either camp tends to
admit. Their divergence lives in specific testable claims -- superluminal waves, unquantized
charge -- where measurement has already ruled. The lens is usable exactly there. Keep the monist
reading, and let the falsifier win every named contest.
