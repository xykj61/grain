Three rooms, one language. Everything beneath this door is Glow -- the userland the implementation exists to carry. **A room is named for what its files are, in the word the files already wear**, so a reader predicts the room from any filename and the filename from any room; the subject stays in the middle of the name, where it always was. The elder desk shape that inspired this floor plan is honored in gratitude -- **except `web`**, which stays refused (Realidream / Brushstroke / `.brush` are not an HTML stack).

*Named `20260827` on Keaton's word.* The room was six -- `lib`, `sur`, `app`, `sec`, `til`, `gen` -- and five of those were borrowed three-letter abbreviations the [Comlink tendency](../.claude/rules/comlink-tendency.md) released on `20260808.205404`. Every file in `lib/` was already a `gate-`, every file in `sur/` a `shape-`, and `app/`, `sec/` and `til/` held `shape-` files too, so four rooms held one kind of thing split by a subject the filename already carried. The three elder doors keep their READMEs as pointers, and the subject each room taught is carried whole into `shape/`. Charter: [`../active-designing/20260827-174816_the-glow-tree-and-the-rooms-that-say-what-they-hold.md`](../active-designing/20260827-174816_the-glow-tree-and-the-rooms-that-say-what-they-hold.md).

- [`shape/`](shape/README.md) -- every **shape**: the data-shape museum, each shape in isolation, in beauty. Its files are the `shape-*.glow` desks, so the room and its members say one word.
- [`gate/`](gate/README.md) -- every **gate**: the shared arms many desks call and none should re-carve. Its files are the `gate-*.glow` desks.
- [`gen/`](gen/README.md) -- **generators**, the runnable demonstrations (desks arrive here from `glow/gen/` when O3 speaks).

**Refused:** `src/web` -- will not open. Surface language lives under `brushstroke/` and Glow/Bron/TAME/Radiant -- never JS/HTML/CSS/JSON as home tongue.

The O3 desk migration remains designed, declared-not-begun, under [`../context/BREACH.md`](../context/BREACH.md). Until the breach speaks, `glow/gen/` remains the desks' one true address.

*May every room fill in its own season, and no address move before its witness stands.*
