Three living rooms and three pointer doors, one language. Everything beneath this door is Glow -- the userland the implementation exists to carry. **A room is named for what its files are, in the word the files already wear**, so a reader predicts the room from any filename and the filename from any room; the subject stays in the middle of the name, where it always was. The elder desk shape that inspired this floor plan is honored in gratitude, room for room, save one, which the note at the close of this page names and explains.

*Named `20260827` on Keaton's word.* The room was six -- `lib`, `sur`, `app`, `sec`, `til`, `gen` -- and five of those were borrowed three-letter abbreviations the [Comlink tendency](../.claude/rules/comlink-tendency.md) released on `20260808.205404`. Every file in `lib/` was already a `gate-`, every file in `sur/` a `shape-`, and `app/`, `sec/` and `til/` held `shape-` files too, so four rooms held one kind of thing split by a subject the filename already carried. The three elder doors keep their READMEs as pointers, and the subject each room taught is carried whole into `shape/`. Charter: [`../active-designing/date/20260827/20260827-174816_the-glow-tree-and-the-rooms-that-say-what-they-hold.md`](../active-designing/date/20260827/20260827-174816_the-glow-tree-and-the-rooms-that-say-what-they-hold.md).

- [`shape/`](shape/README.md) -- every **shape**: the data-shape museum, each shape in isolation, in beauty. Its files are the `shape-*.glow` desks, so the room and its members say one word.
- [`gate/`](gate/README.md) -- every **gate**: the shared arms many desks call and none should re-carve. Its files are the `gate-*.glow` desks.
- [`gen/`](gen/README.md) -- **generators**, the runnable demonstrations (desks arrive here from `glow/gen/` when O3 speaks).

Three **pointer doors** stand beside them, each keeping its path so every reference that ever
pointed there still lands, and each naming on its own face what it carried into `shape/`:

- [`app/`](app/README.md) -- what it taught about **Pond agents**.
- [`sec/`](sec/README.md) -- what it taught about **security and policy**.
- [`til/`](til/README.md) -- what it taught about **tilaks**.

**Refused:** `src/web`, the one elder room this floor declines to open. Surface language lives under `brushstroke/` and Glow/Bron/TAME/Radiant -- never JS/HTML/CSS/JSON as home tongue, and Realidream / Brushstroke / `.brush` carry that surface rather than an HTML stack.

The O3 desk migration stands designed and awaits its declaration under the breach law at [`../context/BREACH.md`](../context/BREACH.md); [`gen/README.md`](gen/README.md) holds the room's own reading of where that word stands. Until the breach speaks, `glow/gen/` remains the desks' one true address.

*May every room fill in its own season, and may every address wait for the witness that lets it move.*
