## The three-name discipline, in one breath

**New Gauge Civic TAME** is one order of values worn three ways: Gauge writes prose that
measures and stays worth reading, Civic names what a system rewards, and TAME bounds every
allocation and says why. When they pull against each other, safety wins, then the measured
claim, then the joy of the craft.
