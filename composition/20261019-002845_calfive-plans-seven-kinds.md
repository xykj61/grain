# Calfive plans -- seven kinds

**Stamp:** `20261019.002845`
**Voice:** Kyli (Teacher Style, Door setting)
**Status:** Living
**Room:** mixed

---

The Calfive calendar keeps time in five rings: fifteen rounds to an orbit, seventy-five
to a quest, three hundred seventy-five to a journey, and so on. Five goals rotate through
the rings -- heart, aesthetics, talent, law, fun. Five senses name how each lap is read:
aether hears, air feels, fire sees, water tastes, earth breathes in. The machinery is
simple. What it plans is not.

Looking across the waymark ladders -- STOA through DISC, AMIR through WADE -- the plans
carry seven distinct kinds of work.

**Language.** The runes, the stdlib, the lowering pass that carries Glow facts through
Mantra into something a product can read. Language work is slow and precise and multiplies
everything else once it holds.

**Product.** The Simple, Lovable, Complete order: readable receipt, changeable consent,
followable value, findable offer, portable bundle, trustworthy books, settleable return,
separated entities. Each milestone is a complete thing -- Linengrow and Dimeroll running
over the same Mantra facts without braiding their types.

**Identity and network.** Kumara, Comlink, the d12/d60 topology, settlement. The signed,
addressed layer. The reason two ships can pass a fact and both trust it.

**Surface and rendering.** Skate, Brushstroke, the glass keyboard, Wired Glass. What a
person sees and touches. Brushstroke declares the grid and the motion budget. Skate
renders the bounded frame.

**Hardware.** HUNK opens the image-decode path toward a parts marketplace and a Photos
surface. DREY seats the Mikrophone's forgetful capture buffer: memory that holds only
while powered, dissolved on power-down. These laps touch metal.

**Creative suite and open protocols.** ALES names the audio wire shape -- a self-describing,
sealed frame carrying a PCM buffer. DISC is the SLH-DSA hash-based signature ladder,
built rung by rung in Rye against the published FIPS 205 answers. Infrastructure the
suite and identity layer will eventually stand on.

**Long return and meta-season.** AMIR rewrites the front door. CION molts count-up IDs
into chronological labels. The harvest seasons prove built modules on real data and
open toward commons and trade. These are the rounds that make the tree legible to anyone
arriving late.

What Calfive plans, underneath all of it, is a sequence of complete things: each one
small enough to finish, useful enough to matter, and built so the next one can start from
solid ground. The rings keep time. The goals rotate the lens. The waymarks name where
each chapter lives. And the SLC order is the promise that nothing lands until it is whole.
