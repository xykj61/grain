# Gratitude — GNU/Linux & Unix

**Language:** EN · **Style:** Radiant · **Kind:** gratitude note (concepts honored, clean-room; no code copied)

Unix is the idea that a system should be **small tools that compose**, and GNU/Linux is the gift of that idea made free for everyone. Our whole way of working descends from this lineage, and we hold it with deep thanks.

**From Unix, the philosophy:**

- **Do one thing well** — a tool with a single clear job, joined to others by a pipe. Our Rishi shell (`sh`-family kin to `bash`, `zsh`, `fish`) speaks this directly, and our witnesses are small tools that each prove one thing.
- **Everything is a file** — one uniform surface for devices, data, and communication. A clarity we still learn from.
- **Text streams as the universal interface** — plain, inspectable, pipeable. Our Bron notation and plain-text disciplines keep faith with it.
- **POSIX** — a small standard that let a thousand systems agree, so a script written once runs across a fair slice of the world. Some of our own tools stay POSIX `sh` on purpose, at exactly the seams where that agreement is worth more than a richer language.

**From GNU and Linux, the freedom:**

- **Software freedom as a moral stance** — the freedom to run, read, change, and share. The GPL made the commons defensible, and the whole free-software world grew in the space it protected.
- **A kernel the planet could build on, together** — Linux showed that an open, gift-economy project could out-build the closed ones, in the open, over decades.

Our enclosure, our shell, our host guides, and our very habit of building in the open are children of this lineage. We study its ideas and write our own; understanding crosses the boundary, never code.

*Thank you, Unix, for the philosophy — and thank you, GNU and Linux, for making it free.*
