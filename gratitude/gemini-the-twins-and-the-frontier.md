## The Thanks

This house uses **Google's Gemini** models often and gladly — in research, in drafting, in the daily craft beside the other frontier hands this tree already thanks — and this page says so plainly: to the Gemini teams at Google and DeepMind, thank you for capable tools generously built, and for carrying a beautiful name well.

## The Prayer of the Shared Name

Gemini is older than any of us: the Twins have stood in the sky since before writing, and the zodiac's names are the commons of every culture that ever looked up. This project now uses **Gemini as an astrological seat-name** — the Gemini fund, one of twelve on the constellation's wheel, named for the tropical sign the way its siblings will be named for theirs. We record here the hope, the wish, the prayer — in the same open spirit as this tree's bow to Qwen — that our use of the sky's own word beside Google's is received as the kinship it is: two hands borrowing from the same ancient commons, each honoring the other's work, all well between every open hand.

---

*May the Twins keep both meanings gladly — the model and the seat, the frontier and the wheel — and may every name drawn from the sky remind its borrowers that it belongs, first, to everyone.*
