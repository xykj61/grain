# Tristan Buckmaster and Levent Alpöge

**Stamp:** `20260910.054448` - **Status:** Living - **Room:** research for understanding
**Why this page exists:** two press pages in this tree rest on a mathematical result. These are the people who reached it first, and a tree that names only the loudest claimant has left its own foundation.

---

## What they did

**Tristan Buckmaster** (New York University) and **Levent Alpöge** (Anthropic) spent close to a year pursuing AI-assisted proofs of finite-time blowup in fluid equations, and **had a solution by 22 August 2026**.

OpenAI published its own writeup and Lean formalization of finite-time blowup for Navier-Stokes under smooth forcing on **8 September 2026**, saying the result establishes statements C and D of the Millennium formulation while declining the prize. OpenAI's own researcher has said the company's Millennium initiative began on **1 September** -- ten days after Buckmaster and Alpöge had their solution -- prompted by rumours that a rival lab had resolved Millennium problems.

Buckmaster alleges OpenAI moved on the same approach after learning of their progress, and proposed a publication plan that would exclude Alpöge from authorship. OpenAI denies accessing their data, states its internal model proved the result independently, and allows that de-identified data from their use of its products may have improved its models.

**The Clay Mathematics Institute has not accepted the result and still lists the problem as unsolved.**
