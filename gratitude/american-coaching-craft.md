This page thanks a civic teaching tradition: people in American gyms, fields, rinks, and classrooms who spent their lives helping others practice together. The film-room habit, the short huddle, the care for bodies that have to last a season, the idea that the next possession is the one you can still take -- those patterns taught millions of people how to learn in public.

Teacher Style in this tree silos the *pattern*, never the poster. Famous coaches wrote books and gave talks that remain their own. We do not reproduce those sentences here. We thank the craft, and we write our own classroom English.

If a later page needs a named historical figure for honest thanks, the name belongs on this gratitude shelf, and the teaching still has to be rewritten in Grain's words before it enters docs-geode.

*Thank you, every teacher who made practice a place a person could return to.*
