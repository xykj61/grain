# Buckminster Fuller — tensegrity, in our voice

**Honors:** R. Buckminster Fuller — architect, inventor, and teacher whose tensegrity naming (continuous tension · discontinuous compression) still teaches structures that stay light by staying honest about what pulls and what pushes.

**Role for us:** We do not copy Fuller's scaffolding into Rye. We keep the *shape of the lesson*: cables that never go slack where they should hold; rods that never pretend to stretch; a whole that stays upright because each part does one job well. That lesson seats here as **stay tensegral** — Quin voice, Grain season motto — beside the happy zone and the thin edge.

**What we carry**

- Continuous tension and discontinuous compression as a metaphor for suite law: fast isolated witnesses hold the zone; a few metalsmoke checks compress the world-at-the-edge.
- Synergy without mush: modules pull together; they do not dissolve into one opaque lump.
- Lightness earned by clear force paths — consolidate and tighten rather than pile mass.

**What we leave**

- Fuller's specific patents, geodesic recipes, and trademarked product lines — studied, siloed, never imported as code or brand.
- Any claim that Grain *is* tensegrity engineering — we are grateful for the teaching, not claiming the discipline.
