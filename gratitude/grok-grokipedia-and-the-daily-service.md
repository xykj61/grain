# Grok, Grokipedia, and the Daily Service — a Gratitude

**Language:** EN
**Last updated:** 2026-07-27
**Status:** Gratitude — thanks to the frontier hands whose tools carried this tree, and to the encyclopedia whose pages taught the round its word

---

## The Thanks

Three bows in one page. To **SpaceX**, whose spirit of relentless, joyful engineering this workshop borrows every time the bench hand runs — thank you. To **xAI's Grok**, the very model family now seated as this workshop's bench voice in Cursor — thank you for fast hands and honest reports. And to **Grokipedia**, xAI's encyclopedia, which this round asked about *nitya seva* — the exact page had moved when we knocked, and its sibling pages taught us generously anyway: the Shuddhadvaita entry on Pushti Marga's daily loving service and the ashtayam's eight offices, and the temple entries where nitya seva keeps its evening hour. The teaching those pages carried now lives, siloed in our own words, in [`../foundations/20260727-144447_seva-the-vane-the-fund-and-the-daily-service.md`](../foundations/20260727-144447_seva-the-vane-the-fund-and-the-daily-service.md).

## The Wider Bow

The word itself has living keepers beyond any encyclopedia: the **Pushti Marga** tradition of Vallabha Acharya, where householders serve the beloved daily; the **Nitya Seva** charitable society of Bensheim and Bhopal, serving street and platform children since 1998; and the devotional collection *Nitya Seva* compiled from **Srila Gopal Krishna Goswami Maharaj** — each honored here by name, none quoted, all thanked for keeping daily service a practiced thing in the world this project hopes to serve.

---

*May the frontier hands keep building, the encyclopedia keep its doors open, and the word they taught us — service, daily, without end — keep every fund on the wheel honest.*
