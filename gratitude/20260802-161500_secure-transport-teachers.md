This room holds names and thanks, and nothing else. The ideas these teachers gave us cross into our grain stripped of their origin — studied in `../external-research/20260802-161500_ssh-mosh-ipv6-secure-transport.md`, siloed in our own voice in `../active-designing/20260802-161500_comlink-ipv6-and-the-roaming-wire.md`. Here we simply say whose shoulders the transport vane stands on.

## The Mobile Shell

Deep thanks to **Keith Winstein and Hari Balakrishnan**, and the Mobile Shell project, for the observation that a remote terminal's screen is a small synchronizable value, and for the state-synchronization protocol and roaming datagram carrier that follow from it. This is the shape our roaming wire grows toward, and the insight it rests on is theirs. The source, should the pier choose to clone it whole into this room on metal, lives at `github.com/mobile-shell/mosh` under the GNU GPL.

## The Secure Shell

Thanks to the **OpenSSH** authors and the broader Secure Shell community for the clean three-layer separation — transport, authentication, connection — that has kept remote work safe for a generation, and for making Ed25519 a first-class identity, the very key family our tree already signs with. The portable source, if cloned whole here on metal, lives at `github.com/openssh/openssh-portable` under a BSD-style license.

## The Internet Protocol, Version Six

Thanks to the authors of the IPv6 specifications and the Happy Eyeballs guidance for the wider address space that ends translation's necessity, and for the dual-family grace that lets a host try the newer path and fall back kindly. These are open standards; we study them from their published form and thank the many hands behind them.

## A Note on Cloning

Per the silo technique, a teacher's own source is kept whole and unaltered in this room, never edited, and its ideas enter our design only after being rewritten entirely in our own words. The full-source clones above are a pier-metal step, at Keaton's discretion and by his hand — counsel names the teachers and thanks them here; the wire and the disk are the pier's to touch. Whether cloned or only cited, the gratitude stands.
