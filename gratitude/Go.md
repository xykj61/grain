# Gratitude — Go

**Language:** EN · **Style:** Radiant · **Kind:** gratitude note (concepts honored, clean-room; no code copied)

Go taught a generation that a language can be *small on purpose* and lose nothing for it. Its gifts we hold with thanks:

- **Fast compilation as a feature, not an afterthought** — a build so quick it keeps the maker in flow.
- **One format, settled** — `gofmt` ended a thousand style arguments by simply deciding, and the whole ecosystem exhaled. Our own Radiant and TAME disciplines chase the same peace: a settled way, so the argument is over.
- **Concurrency you can reason about** — goroutines and channels brought Hoare's CSP into daily practice, "share memory by communicating."
- **Readability over cleverness** — a language that would rather be plain than impressive. The Comlink tendency shares that heart.

A small, honest note on the name: **Glow** rhymes with **Go**, and we say so plainly rather than pretend otherwise. Short language names crowd happily together — Go, Nim, Zig, V — and readers hold them apart with ease. We are glad to stand near Go on the shelf.

We study Go's ideas and write our own; the boundary between reading and building is never crossed by code, only by understanding.

*Thank you, Go, for proving that clarity scales.*
