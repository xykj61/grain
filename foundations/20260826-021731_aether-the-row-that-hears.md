Aether is the Jupiter row, and its question is the first question: why does the work
exist? The other four rows ask what the work touches, sees, tastes, and carries; this
row asks what the work is *for*, and it asks before anything else moves. A lap that
opens here reads the three oldest kinds of conviction the tree holds -- the return
habit, the long view, and the grain itself -- so that every later row inherits a
direction rather than inventing one. Jupiter is the teacher planet in the old scheme,
and the row keeps that office plainly: it teaches the lap its purpose, once per orbit,
from documents that have already survived being taught.

## The sense: sound

Aether's sense is sound -- the tanmatra mapping seated on Keaton's word (kyri/receipt.rye,
20260826): aether hears, air feels, fire sees, water tastes, earth breathes in. A lap on this
row *listens*. Listening is a distinct act from scanning: a scan asks whether the words
are present, and a listen asks what the work is saying underneath them. Practically, the
lap reads each seated document aloud in its head and attends to three things -- the claim
the page keeps repeating, the question it keeps answering, and the silence where a claim
used to be and a witness now stands. When the row's read is done well, the lap can say in
one sentence what the tree sounds like this orbit, and that sentence steers the four rows
that follow.

## The working vocabulary

The row speaks in six words, three light and three dark, drawn from the two fifteen-seat
palettes in kyri/receipt.rye (seated 20260826, paired seat for seat under "dark euphoria,
light terra"):

- **mind / mystery** -- the cardinal pair. Mind initiates: the deliberate read, the
  compass taken in hand. Mystery is its night side: the part of *why* that stays open
  on purpose, the question a good foundation keeps alive rather than closes.
- **sound / silence** -- the fixed pair, and the row's own sense. Sound is the work
  speaking; silence is the work holding. A green witness is sound; a claim retired
  because it could no longer be checked is an honest silence, and the lap records both.
- **dream / hush** -- the dual pair. Dream dissolves the read onward: what the *why*
  wants next, carried loosely enough to adapt. Hush is the dream at rest -- the horizon
  named and then left alone, the deferred door that stays a door.

A lap tags its receipts from these six. A reading that initiates uses mind; a reading
that finds the tree quieter than its documents claim uses silence; a reading that opens
a horizon uses dream, and one that deliberately closes the lap's ears to a tempting
tangent uses hush.

## The handoff

Aether closes its lap holding one sentence: what the work is for, this orbit, in the
tree's own words. It hands that sentence to Air -- the row that feels, whose triad is
arc, touch, and senses -- so that the next lap can ask how the purpose moves through
hands and surfaces. The order is deliberate: purpose is heard before motion is felt,
the way a note sounds before the arc of the phrase can be played. What Aether leaves
in hush, Air may take up in touch; what Aether records as silence, a later row may
answer with a witness.

*May the lap that opens here always hear the work before it handles it, and may the
why keep sounding long after this orbit's readers have handed the compass on.*
