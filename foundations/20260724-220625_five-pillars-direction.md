## Non-harming as the ceiling

Every feature meets a harm ceiling before it meets a feature list. What we store, who may see it, how long it stays, and how it leaves -- each answer is measured against whether a person is safer for our having built the thing. The custody doctrine already seated on this pier is that ceiling in code: minimize first, encrypt with the key held elsewhere, delete by destroying the key, and keep ordinary release under a published schedule that a hold can suspend. Non-harming is not a motto on the wall; it is the first filter in the design door.

## Devotion as service

The work faces outward as service rather than extraction. Tools earn their place by helping someone stand more upright in their own life -- an artist paid fairly, a newcomer who can clone and see green, a reader who finds a plain word where a jargon wall might have stood. Devotion here means staying with the person's good when a clever shortcut would harvest them. The civic register and the stranger's lap both lean this way: prove the path helps, rather than prove the path impresses.

## Scripture as compass and silo

The compass documents -- Radiant Style, TAME, the custody gate, the living pins -- are the scripture we keep rewriting carefully and citing often. Silo discipline is scripture's twin: ideas may enter from anywhere; they land in our vocabulary, under our witnesses, without dragging another house's scaffolding into the body of the page. We study widely and write as ourselves. That is how a small pier stays coherent while drinking from a large world.

## Sound as the spoken edge

What we name must survive being read aloud. Radiant Style already asks for that register -- warm, clear, sayable. Auras that earn an outward face earn one a mouth can carry: Crockford's spoken alphabet for stamps is the same instinct wearing number clothes. If a name fails in the ear, it has not finished earning its seat.

## Meditation as pace

Slower to go faster is not delay for its own sake. It is the pause that lets a bound be named, a witness be written, a season close before the next door opens. Season-close rest belongs here beside round-close: the operator's attention is a scarce asset the threat model already names, and meditation is how that asset regenerates. A rested head writes smaller complete rings; a tired head writes aspiration as fact.
