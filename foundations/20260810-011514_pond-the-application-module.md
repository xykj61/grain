## What Pond Is

Pond is where Grain becomes something a person uses. Below it sit the kernel, the compositor, the wire, the store -- each a bounded module doing one thing well. Pond is the floor where those modules compose into a running **whole**: an application a person opens, holds their work in, and trusts. It is the application module of Grain, and from this seat forward it carries that role entire.

The name is a pond because a pond is where small living things run -- kin in spirit to a Play store and an App store, and to the sandbox that first held these agents. It is a plain, warm word a newcomer grasps at once, and it has already grown up in the tree: the `pond/` floor holds `apps/`, a `customs.rye` policy seam, and a first drawn-terminal application. This declaration does not invent Pond; it names, plainly, the role Pond has grown into.

## The Three Teachers, Named With Thanks

Pond inherits from three lineages, and copies the code of none.

- From the elder **Gall**, the *shape of an application host* -- agents that hold state, answer requests, and subscribe to one another, all above one kernel. Grain studies the shape and writes its own; the gratitude is real, the code is ours.
- From the classic hypervisors **KVM** and **Qemu**, the *discipline of isolation* -- a guest runs a whole world while the host holds the boundary. These are GPL; Grain reads their public design in the clean room and never links their code.
- From the modern Rust virtual-machine monitors -- **Firecracker**, **Cloud Hypervisor**, **crosvm** -- the *lightweight, fast-boot, least-privilege* posture a per-application sandbox wants. These are permissive; safe to study, still written in Grain's own hand.

Pond keeps the Glow way throughout: Rishi scripts, Rye modules, Brix composition, TAME's asserted bounds and explicit widths, Radiant prose. It is not a port of any teacher; it is Grain's own floor, grown to carry the whole application role.

## Pool Retires Into Pond

The tree once named an application host **Pool** -- headlined as a content-and-compute market, framed as the "P-vane" in the old Urbit vane grid. Two things changed. The vane grid itself is **released**: the seated reframe says plainly that no module must claim a letter or coin a vane word, and that a clear, warm, safe name is the default. And Pond, the enclosure floor, grew up to hold applications under policy. So the two are one, and the one is Pond.

Nothing of Pool's substance is lost -- only its framing retires. The market surfaces Pool was to host -- **Granary**'s resins, **Mandi**, **Open Asks**, **MUR**, **Neth** -- run exactly as they were meant to: as applications inside Pond. What was "an agent in Pool" is now "an app in Pond." The Pool study stays on disk as a readable writing fossil, its good thinking preserved and its name at rest.

## What Pond Composes

Pond is the floor where Grain's modules become one usable surface:

- **Comlink** (Mantra's wire) carries the sealed datagram between running wholes.
- **Kumara** and the **JARL settlement** give every app an identity that is minted, signed, rotated, and *named* -- a point that wears a spoken name (`../settlement/names.rye`), settled on a ledger whose shared surface stays small.
- **Granary**, **Amphora**, **Tablecloth**, **Open Asks**, **MUR**, **Neth** are the market and store surfaces, each an app.
- **Brushstroke** and **Skate** paint an app's surface onto a host backend; **Realidream** is the single-surface face over the whole graph.

Above them all, **customs** (`customs.rye`) is Pond's own discipline: at receipt, each Tilak mark earns **place**, **hold**, or **refuse** -- plain bytes may be placed, an await-word waits for a human word, an unknown mark refuses whole. Isolation policy meets a running whole exactly here.

## Pond Loads a Sky

The breach's second gift meets Pond here. A **sky** is a loadable topology profile -- a community's chosen shape of its own network, drawn in the silo brief (`../active-designing/20260809-234413_loadable-topologies-and-pond-silo-brief.md`). Pond loads a sky the way a console loads a game: one community runs the civic d12-d60 sky, another a five-element project sky, a third a role-woven social sky. The settlement ledger beneath them is the same code, because it settles by asking the loaded sky where a sponsor sits. Many worlds, one vessel, one honest ledger.
