Every instrument reaches for its subject through a **key** -- the thing it actually looks at. A
census keys on a filename. A control's plant keys on a line of text. A witness keys on a list
somebody typed. The key stands in for the subject, and the reading is only ever as good as that
stand-in.

So an instrument answers the question its **key** asks, rather than the question its **name**
asks. The two agree on the day the instrument is written, which is exactly the day they look identical.

**Choose a key that moves when the subject moves, and rests when the subject rests.**

## Four keys, and what each one reports instead

This tree has now watched all four drift, each caught by running the instrument rather than by
reading it. Every reading below is a command.

## What the four have in common

Each key is **free** -- something else in the tree is entitled to move it, for a reason of its own. A convention gets a better name. A ratchet earns a lower
number. A room grows a file. Testimony accumulates, as testimony is meant to.

Each repair is the same move: **key on what the subject itself holds still.** The form of a line
rather than its value. The room rather than the roster of it. The property rather than the name
somebody chose for it. The cell a hand can actually repair rather than the whole count.

And each reading is believable on the way down, which is the reason this earns a page. A stale
name reports **a clean search**. A stale plant reports **a guard that held**. A short list
reports **every member green**. A growing population reports **a line crossed**. Three read
green, the fourth reds where testimony holds still, and one cause stands behind all four.

## How to choose a key, in one question

> **What else in this tree is allowed to move this, for a reason my subject never touches?**

A key whose honest answer is *the subject alone* is a good key. A key with any other answer
wants the answer written beside it, so the next reader knows what the number will do before it
does it -- which is Gauge's fifth thing about a figure, one room over: name what holds it still.

## The taste of it

Water tastes up close, and this is a page the water row keeps handing back. An instrument is a
sense organ the tree grows for itself, and the gentlest way one goes astray is by reporting the
shape of its own nerve rather than the world. The cure is the row's own instruction: **run the
actual thing**, on the actual tree, and read what came back rather than what the name led you to
expect.

*May every meter we grow reach its own subject, and report what it found there.*
