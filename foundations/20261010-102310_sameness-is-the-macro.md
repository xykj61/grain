## The Tension, Stated Honestly

One tradition compresses by letting a program rewrite itself toward the domain: fewer lines, higher altitude, and a private dialect that each reader must learn first. Our discipline weighs that privacy at its true cost. Explicitness, assertions any reader can check, and control flow kept in plain view are the gifts we keep. The arithmetic holds too. Total lines are a load-bearing cost, and a system too large to hold in mind behaves differently from a small one.

## Where Our Stack Already Compresses

Read our own tree and compression appears everywhere, and all of it is public. **One value model** -- string, integer, boolean, list, record -- means every module speaks one grammar; Brix surface, wire records, and shell literals are three faces of one value, so a tool written once reads everything. **One fold** -- state as a pure fold over signed facts -- makes undo, history, sync, and audit one mechanism instead of four; the drawing-room harvest and the stack record arrived at the same shape from different doors. **One vocabulary grown in canon** -- season, lap, bench, gate, toll -- compresses the way a macro compresses, with one difference: the new words live in prose any reader can open, not in syntax only the compiler expands. That is the reconciliation. We accept the goal and keep the instrument in plain view.

## The Principles

**Sameness is the macro.** Compress the system by making things publicly similar -- one value model, one fold, one vocabulary -- so that learning one part is learning many. Grow the language in the canon, where a new word costs a page of prose, and keep the syntax shared by every reader. When a compression can be had only through magic, keep the lines and keep the honesty.

**Count the whole cost of a line saved.** A line removed by sameness stays free for every reader; a line removed by cleverness is a loan against every reader to come.
