## First, the Civic Question

Every reward measures something, so name the outcome before the mechanism: **more kindness enacted** — plant-kingdom meals actually chosen, sanctuaries actually sustained, seva hours actually given. Frequent-flier miles reward *loyalty to a carrier*; this lane rewards *loyalty to the living*, and it holds the reward and the outcome aligned under every condition the way Civic Style demands: the ledger records the **deed**, verified at the seam where it happened, and never a proxy a clever hand could farm.

## The Shape, Invariants First

**A merit fact is append-only and two-handed.** One enacted kindness becomes one signed fact on the pilot's MUR (was MALA): the pilot's hand and the seam's hand — a merchant's, a sanctuary's, a seva host's — both sign, the same symmetric marriage the identity bind taught us. No countersignature, no fact. Fields: the pilot · the seam (a plant-kingdom purchase at a Mandi merchant · a Linengrow-routed sanctuary gift · an attested seva hour) · a **class**, never a price — aparigraha records the deed's kind, not the wallet's weight · the clock reading.

**Standing is a fold, never a counter.** The pilot's merit is a projection recomputed over the log — state as a pure fold over immutable signed facts, our oldest law — and WOV pins that standing **at a nib**, so *how much kindness has this hand enacted* is always a named landed edge anyone can recompute and no one can quietly edit.

**Merit never expires.** The airline mile decays to keep you flying; kindness done is done forever, so the ledger keeps it forever — accrete-never-break, applied to goodness. What expiry did for the airline, **tiers of lifetime standing** do here without the treadmill: recognition deepens as the fold grows, and nothing ever counts backward.

**Every earn is bounded.** A per-day, per-seam ceiling — named as a constant, checked at the edge — so the honest pilot never notices it and the gaming hand saturates early. Where a seam cannot yet countersign honestly, it does not earn at all: an unverifiable kindness is still a kindness, and still not a fact.

## Redemption Is Steering, Never Spending

Merit does not cash out — it **aims**. A redemption is one more append-only fact: the pilot points their standing at a sanctuary, a seva program, or a Mandi maker, and a **sponsor pool** — funded by patrons, merchants, and the platform's own tithe — releases matching support in that direction. The merit stays on the ledger forever; only its *steering* is spent, and the steering history is itself public, signed, and foldable. Three gifts fall out at once: the pilot's kindness compounds into more kindness rather than converting into a coupon; the sponsor's money moves only where enacted kindness pointed it — the reward aimed straight at the good, in Civic Style's own words; and the legal face stays gentle, because nothing here is a currency, a security, or a promise of money — a stance each pilot's and each pool's **JURISDICTION block** still confirms on its own ground, professionals consulted where pools grow serious.

## The Guards, Because Every Reward Bends

Named plainly so the design survives its own success: **two-hand signing** defeats self-dealing; **class-not-price** defeats big-spender capture; **ceilings** defeat farming; **fold-not-counter** defeats edit-in-place; **steering-not-spending** defeats the secondary market that would turn merit into a commodity and the commodity into a lie. And the adversarial dashboard rule applies from birth: anomalies — a seam countersigning at impossible rates, a pool releasing off-pattern — must be as easy to find as the successes are to celebrate.
