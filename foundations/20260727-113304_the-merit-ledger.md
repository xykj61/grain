Every reward measures something, so name the outcome first: **more kindness enacted**. That means plant-kingdom meals chosen, sanctuaries sustained, seva hours given. ## Redemption Is Steering, Rather Than Spending

Merit aims; it does not cash out. A redemption is one more append-only fact. The pilot points their standing at a sanctuary, a seva program, or a Mandi maker. A **sponsor pool**, funded by patrons, merchants, and the platform's own tithe, then releases matching support in that direction. The merit stays on the ledger for good. Only its *steering* is spent, and the steering history stays public, signed, and foldable.

Three gifts follow at once. First, the pilot's kindness compounds into more kindness, and it converts into no coupon. Second, the sponsor's money moves only where enacted kindness pointed it, so the reward aims straight at the good, in Civic Style's own words. Third, the legal face stays gentle. ## The Guards, Because Every Reward Bends

Named plainly, so the design survives its own success. **Two-hand signing** defeats self-dealing. **Class-not-price** defeats big-spender capture. **Ceilings** defeat farming. **Fold-not-counter** defeats edit-in-place. **Steering-not-spending** defeats the secondary market, which would turn merit into a commodity and the commodity into a lie.
