## Why fifteen -- the rota lap

The council rota is the 5x3 grid seated in the autonomous-loop seed on 2026-08-21 (stamp
`20260821.211423`): five elements -- aether, air, fire, water, earth -- crossed with three
modalities -- cardinal, fixed, dual. Fifteen cells, fifteen canonical documents. Each round
deep-reads one row, three documents seen from three modalities, and across an orbit each of the
fifteen documents takes its turn in the deep-read seat. An orbit therefore returns the whole
canon to awareness exactly once.

That property is the reason the orbit is the base ring. A unit of planning should close with
the planner's beliefs freshly re-read, and fifteen rounds is the smallest count at which the
rota guarantees it. The orbit is the tree's unit of *remembering everything it holds true*.
Smaller units forget; larger units are built from this one.

## Why fives -- elements, and tree rings

Five is already the tree's working number. The rota's rows are the five elements. The public
seed ships every fifth round (foundation `20260823-111029`). The rings extend the same count
upward: a quest is five orbits, a journey five quests, an equinox five journeys, a chapter five
equinoxes.

The image is a tree ring. A tree grows by adding one ring around the whole of what it was, and
each ring contains every ring before it. The Panchanga grows the same way: a chapter contains
its equinoxes whole, an equinox its journeys, down to the round at the center. Growth is
containment rather than succession.

## The rename -- season becomes chapter

The old ladder counted in powers of two: a journey was 16 rounds, an equinox 64, a season 256
(unit: rounds; seated `20260729.203719`; source: `context/LEXICON.md`). The container word was
upside down. In the sky, an equinox belongs to a season -- it is the day a season turns. A
"season" holding four equinoxes inside it had the containment backwards, and the word could
never honestly carry it.

**Chapter** carries it plainly. A chapter is a container of written work, which is what this
tree produces, and the word follows the literary line of Anthropic's own model names -- Mythos,
Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku -- a register the tree already lives beside every working day. The
equinox keeps its name and its place inside the larger ring, which is where an equinox belongs.

## Why the word Panchanga

The name passes the three tests of the Comlink tendency. It is **clear** once its plain
function is named: five limbs, for a calendar whose every ring is a five. It is **fun** -- a
warm word from a register the tree already keeps, beside Mantra and Rishi. And it is **safe**:
it collides with nothing seated. The network-topology words -- Constel, Compass, Kumara,
Mycelium -- name how piers find each other, and the calendar deliberately stands clear of all
four. **Almanac** was also passed over, since that word is already spent: it names the learning
record at `rye-learning-process/`.
