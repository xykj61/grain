Fire is the row of Mars, and its question is the unflinching one: what must stop,
and what must be cut? Every tree that grows
fast accumulates two kinds of trouble: the defect it is tempted to build over, and
the dead tissue it is tempted to keep. Fire's lap exists for both. Where the other
rows listen, feel, taste, and smell their way through the tree, this row looks --
hard, directly, at the thing most laps would rather route around -- and then it
acts once, cleanly, at exactly the depth the truth requires. The three documents
seated here are one discipline read at three moments: the stop, the allocation the
stop buys, and the cut that follows when something has truly died.

## The sense: vision

Fire's sense is vision, the tanmatra of sight (seated with the palettes in
`kyri/receipt.rye`, 20260826, Keaton's word). A fire lap uses its eyes the way a
welder uses them: it looks straight at the bright, uncomfortable point where the
work is joining or failing. In practice that means reading the REDS ledger before
reading anything new, running the witnesses and watching what they refuse, and
looking at a fault until the root is visible rather than glancing and guessing.
Vision here is the discipline of seeing what is, before deciding what to do --
the lap that sees clearly cuts once; the lap that squints cuts twice.

## The working vocabulary

The row's light triad is **rockstar, vision, risk** -- the rockstar ignites,
vision is the sense, risk adapts. The dark twins at the same three seats are
**hermit, night, surrender** (grain and twilight palettes, `kyri/receipt.rye`,
paired seat for seat under "dark euphoria, light terra"). Read them as one
vocabulary with a day side and a night side:

- **rockstar / hermit** -- the cardinal pair. The rockstar starts the
  fire in front of everyone; the hermit tends it alone, holding a stopped line
  in place while the root is found. A Standfast is hermit work with rockstar
  stakes: the whole line waits on one patient watcher.
- **vision / night** -- the fixed pair, and the row's own sense. Vision needs night to matter;
  a defect is easiest to see against the dark, and the honest lap looks into
  the night of the tree -- the ledger of what went wrong -- before the daylight
  of what might go right.
- **risk / surrender** -- the dual pair, the seat that dissolves onward. Risk is
  the willingness to cut; surrender is the willingness to let the cut thing go.
  The three depths of removal are exactly this pair walked downward: risk
  chooses the depth, and surrender releases what the depth removes.

A lap tags its receipts from these six. A reading that pulls the cord uses
rockstar; a patient watch over a stopped line uses hermit; a fault seen whole
uses vision, and the ledger it was seen against uses night; a cut chosen uses
risk, and the dead tissue released uses surrender.

## Dual: the three depths of removal

`foundations/20260818-081438_the-three-depths-of-removal.md` names the cut. Molt
grows the new skin and destroys nothing; breach renames while the history keeps
its honest account; debride reaches all the way down, removing dead tissue from
every commit so the tree reattaches around the absence -- and before any depth
past the first, a cairn marks the way back. The page's hardest lesson is a
seeing lesson: a dead name lives on three surfaces at once -- the living tree,
the history's metadata, and the history's content -- and a removal is finished

whole art is cutting exactly as deep as the truth requires and no deeper. The
dual seat adapts and dissolves onward, and this is the dissolving done with

## The handoff

Fire's lap closes when the line that had to stop has stopped, the reds hold the
allocation they are owed, and any cut has been made at its named depth with the
walk-back lit. What it hands the next lap is ground a reader can trust: water
follows fire in the rota, and the river runs best through a channel fire has
already cleared. Where fire sees and severs, water tastes and connects -- the
tide, the nectar, the longing -- so the row that cut hands a clean bed to the
row that flows. A lap that opened through this door leaves knowing the oldest
trade in the grid: a pause that fixes is the truest kind of speed, and a cut
made cleanly loses nothing that was ever true.

*May this row's eyes stay open in the night, may every stop be glad and every
cut exact, and may what fire releases feed the river that follows.*
