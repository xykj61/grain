The fascia is the tree's connective tissue: every link, kin line, citation, and path-home
pointer that lets a reader standing in any document walk back to the root and out to every
relation. The word comes from the body, where fascia is the sheet that binds muscle to bone
and each part to the whole -- cut it and the parts still exist, yet nothing moves together.
A tree of eight thousand documents lives or dies the same way. The writing can be excellent
page by page while the whole is unreadable, because reading a tree *is* following its
relations, and a relation that does not resolve is a hallway that ends in a wall.

## Confidence is a measurement, never a mood

The question this page seats: **how much can a reader trust a document's relations?** The
tree's answer is the same answer it gives every other question -- a witness harness that
turns the feeling into numbers, read fresh each lap, refusals proven from both sides. Under
New Gauge every figure carries unit, date, and source; under Civic the metric names what it
rewards; under TAME every bound is named and every surprising number says why.

Five instruments take the reading today, from the door inward:

- **The path home.** Every room's front page carries a *Where this sits* line, and
  [`../tools/fixtures/f/fascia_home_link_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/f/fascia_home_link_scan.sh)
  holds rooms without one at **zero** -- measured `20260826` at 118 of 118 with a ceiling of
  none missing. A reader anywhere is one link from home.
- **Living links resolve.** [`../tools/fixtures/r/readme_reach_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/r/readme_reach_scan.sh)
  and [`../tools/fixtures/f/foundations_link_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/f/foundations_link_scan.sh)
  hold broken links in living pages at **zero**, while
  [`../tools/fixtures/t/tracked_link_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/t/tracked_link_scan.sh) sweeps
  the tracked tree; dated testimony keeps its elder spellings and is *resolved* rather than
  rewritten, by [`../tools/d/dated_path_resolve.rish`](../tools/d/dated_path_resolve.rish).
- **The census.** [`../tools/fixtures/d/dated_path_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/d/dated_path_scan.sh)
  reads every dated reference in the tree -- 22,523 on `20260826` -- and classifies each:
  home, recoverable, ambiguous, gone. Its **lost** count sits under a no-slack ceiling in
  [`../tools/d/dated_path_witness.rish`](../tools/d/dated_path_witness.rish), so a cut that
  strands references reds on the lap it lands rather than years later.
- **The health percent.** The front door's **fascia** figure -- *can a reader follow any
  thread home* -- rendered by [`../tools/r/readme_metrics.rish`](../tools/r/readme_metrics.rish)

  and kin) watches the same tissue from inside a season. Read **51 of 100** on `20260826` --

- **The card, per document.** [`../tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh`](../tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh)

  reaches the page and a reader actually passes through it. A page's confidence is its card,
  and the card cites the scans above rather than measuring twice.

## What the metric rewards

Civic asks the question of every meter, so it is asked here: the fascia readings reward
**repointing the living and resolving the dated** -- the two moves that keep relations true
without rewriting testimony. They deliberately give no credit for deleting a hard page, for
padding kin lists with links nobody follows, or for rewriting a dated log so its references
look fresh; the first is measured by the census's lost ceiling, the second by the card's
reach budget, and the third is refused outright by the one-clock law. A meter that rewarded
tidiness over faithfulness would teach the tree to shed its history, which is the opposite
of confidence.

## The kin habit

A relation is strongest when it is reciprocal: the page that leans on a law names the law,
and the law's own kin line names the page back. That is why this foundation, and every
seated page after it, carries a deliberate **Kin** line at the door -- the fascia is grown
one named relation at a time, and the witness harness holds what the habit grows. When a
document's relations change, [`../.claude/rules/docs-implementation-sync.md`](../.claude/rules/docs-implementation-sync.md)
asks for the repoint in the same commit, and *references are promises* is already the
collaboration law's own sentence.
