# Standfast -- the Stopped Line

*When a defect appears, the whole line stops until the root is found and fixed. Not flagged for later, not worked around -- stopped. This is the oldest quality discipline there is, and it has a name here now: to Standfast is to hold the work in place, refuse to advance over a known wrong, and repair the ground before taking another step.*

## The gesture we are naming

A line that keeps moving over a known defect spends its speed building a hundred flawed things at once; a line that stops the instant the defect is seen spends a pause and saves the hundred. Quality is built *in* by refusing to build *over* a fault. That discipline is old and proven, handed down by the manufacturing tradition we thank in [`../gratitude/toyota-production-system.md`](../gratitude/toyota-production-system.md) -- the teachers and their words kept there, in the gratitude room, per the silo technique. The name **Standfast**, and the shape it takes here, are our own.

To Standfast is three things at once:

- **Stop.** The moment a red is real, the line halts. New work does not begin beside it.
- **Root, not symptom.** The fix addresses why the defect entered, not only the mark it left. Ask why until the answer is a cause, not a coincidence.
- **Prove, then resume.** The line restarts on a witness on metal -- a green check that the class of defect can no longer pass -- never on a claim that it was handled.

## How Standfast sits beside reds-first

The two are one motion seen from two sides. **Standfast** is the *stop* -- the discipline of halting the whole line the instant a wrong is known. **Reds-first** is what the stop *buys*: the remainder of the allocation belongs to the red before it belongs to anything new, booked in the ledger with all three fields, closed on a witness. Standfast is the hand on the cord; reds-first is where the hours go once it is pulled.

A lantern that fires twice becomes a loom: when the same defect stops the line a second time, we do not just fix it again -- we build the guard that makes its whole class impossible, so the line need never stop for it a third time.

## The Standfast that named this file

REMEMBER.md, the operator card carried in every hand, had been silently corrupting itself into mojibake for days -- a tool wrote it in the wrong encoding, and every edit since compounded the damage, until 2,797 runs of garbled bytes stood where plain punctuation belonged (REDS #83). When it surfaced, the color work stopped. The root was found -- an encoding mismatch entering at a single commit -- the card was repaired to clean ASCII, the **ASCII-first** rule was seated so the class of defect cannot recur, and only then did the work resume. That is a Standfast, start to finish, and it is why the reflex earns a name: so the next hand knows that stopping the line is not a failure of momentum but the highest expression of care for the work.

## Why the foundation exists

A tree that grows fast is tempted to route around its own defects to keep moving. Standfast is the standing refusal of that temptation -- the promise that we would rather stop the whole line than ship a known wrong to whoever runs the software next. It costs a pause and it buys a floor no defect crosses twice.

*May every line that must stop, stop gladly. May the root always be found. And may the pause that fixes be honored as the truest kind of speed.*
