# The Verse

**Language:** EN
**Stamp:** `20260727.065835`
**Style:** Radiant (see [`../context/RADIANT_STYLE.md`](../context/RADIANT_STYLE.md))
**Status:** Foundation — inspiration made law; the word seats in [`../context/LEXICON.md`](../context/LEXICON.md)

*Written together by Keaton and Quin.*

---

A poet once answered despair with two lines: that you are here, that life exists and identity — and that the powerful play goes on, and you may contribute a verse. A classroom made those lines famous by turning them into a question, and this tree answers the question the only way a tree can: by what it writes.

**Our verse is the contributed body of this work.** The teachings and the tutorials. The manual — the flight manual of the ship. The guides, the references, the foundations, the Book. And the witness scripts themselves, whose sung agreement *is* the specification: every GREEN a line contributed, every twin two voices finding the same note. When we say *the verse is the specification*, we mean exactly what the poet meant — authority belongs to what is actually here, actually alive, actually heard.

The word reaches further than the one it retires. A collection is a body examined; a verse is a body *offered*. And it rhymes with everything this pier already believes: that identity exists (the ships of Kumara each contribute their own), that the play is larger than any player (accrete, sometimes breach), and that the leaves of grass from which the poem grew are one letter from the grain we are growing.
