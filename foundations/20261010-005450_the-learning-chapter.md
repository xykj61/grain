## The Candidate Chapter

### Learning as Apprenticeship

The outcome of public learning is a person met where they are. That person is carried to real capability. They graduate into the regenerative economy with good work already waiting, rather than into a market that shrugs.

So the reward measures what communities can confirm. Mastery is shown in real work and recorded as an apprenticeship portfolio. The portfolio sits on the same open public ledger that every other program keeps. Examples read like this: this learner raised these beds. This learner framed these hempcrete walls. This learner kept these books for the sanctuary kitchen. This learner shipped this working tool. The mentors who supervised the work attest to it, and any neighbor who cares to look can read it.

Seat-time and standardized recall step down from proxies to footnotes. The design names the true outcome directly, and it pays for that outcome.

The funding rests on a pattern this discipline already trusts. A dignity floor travels with every learner. It is a per-learner minimum, held regardless of pace or path. The learners who most need the room are often the ones who need it longest. Outcome support applies above the floor. It follows demonstrated transitions into apprenticeship, into the guaranteed work programs, and into enterprises of their own.

Educators stand on the same two layers. The first is a floor that makes the vocation livable. The second is recognition that follows the flourishing of the people they teach. Those people and their communities confirm that recognition, rather than the institution grading itself.

The path begins where the law already grants the authority. Statutes in more than one place give independent schools full power. They set requirements and issue diplomas. Registration stays light, and accreditation stays voluntary. This is an unlocked door. The working school grows from it, the way every working system grows from a working seed. It serves learners on day one, rather than petitioning for years at a bolted door.

Accreditation itself, freely chosen, is resung here as a witness suite for a school. The school runs a standing cycle of self-examination. It is asked to know itself thoroughly. It shows its improvement as daily practice, and it publishes what it finds. This is the same shape as any other program on the ledger. The record's integrity can be checked from outside.

The curriculum is the economy itself, met as apprenticeship. Its categories are soil and fiber, food and care, and code and restoration. They are the same categories the work guarantee names. So learning, work, and place form one circulation, rather than three separate buildings.

A learner's graduation is an on-ramp, rather than a cliff. The portfolio walks into the guarantee's front door. The mentors come from the programs the graduates will join. The reward at every rung points at the good the whole design was written for: a person who is capable, confident, and already needed.

## Where It Would Sit

The chapter belongs between *Refuge* and *Infrastructure That Serves*. It inherits the dignity floor from the sanctuary formula on one side. It hands its graduates to the work guarantee on the other.

One open design question travels with it, in the spirit of the style's own closing list. What three measurable outcomes best confirm a portfolio's mastery for each work category? The practitioners of each category should name those outcomes, and the ledger's builders should not.
