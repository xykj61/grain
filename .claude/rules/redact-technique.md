# Redact Technique -- our own particular, set down as a public general

**Seated:** `20260908` on Keaton's word - **Status:** Living - **Room:** vision (the technique is
seated; the manifest verdict it proposes is not built)
**Full guide:** `context/REDACT_TECHNIQUE.md` - **Sibling:** [`silo technique`](../../context/SILO_TECHNIQUE.md) - **Register:** [`bhakta-style`](bhakta-style.md)

**Redacting is siloing pointed the other way.** Silo receives an idea from the world and sets it down
in our own words, so a particular method becomes a general principle we own. Redact takes *our own*
particular -- a real portfolio, a real rate, a real parcel -- and sets it down as a general principle
anyone can use. Silo protects us from borrowing; redact protects a person from being published.

## Why it exists

A page carrying real figures has one honest destination today: withheld. `template-manifest.bron` is
an allowlist, so a room absent from it never ships. That wall is right and it wastes something --
**the specifics are private, and the method rarely is.** A tree that only knows how to withhold
teaches nothing from its own hardest thinking.

## What a redaction lets go, and keeps

**Let go:** names, entities, addresses and parcels, account identifiers, exact sums, exact dates tied
to a person, counterparties, and anything that with one search names a household.

**Keep:** the shape of the calculation -- the variables and what each does, the direction each
relationship moves, the thresholds where a decision flips, the base rates and where a reader finds
their own, and the refusals naming what the method cannot tell you.

**Turn a figure into a level.** A sum becomes *a portfolio at a scale where one sale realises a
material gain*; a rate becomes *a benchmark read on the day and cited to its publisher*. The reader
learns to find their own numbers.

## The multivariable reading

Name the terms and their degrees -- assets and their stage, capital and its cost, opportunity and how
long it stays open, network and how far it reaches, time and what it forgives -- then show what each
kind of calculation honestly does with them, and where it stops.

## Register and discipline

Written in **Bhakta**: no assumed background, first principles in order, one honest sentence about
why it matters. Gauge still holds -- a figure carries unit, date, source, and what holds it still; a
projection carries horizon, assumptions, falsifier, confidence.

## What it is not

**Not anonymising.** A page without a name that still says *4.94 acres on a named highway, under
contract in August* has published a parcel. The check is whether the page could describe a hundred
different households.

**Not vagueness.** A redacted page is *more* exact about method, since the specifics that carried the
argument are gone.

**Not a second copy.** The private page is the record; the redaction is a projection of it,
regenerated rather than maintained in parallel.

## Not built

The manifest carries `scrub`, `template` and `personal`. Redact proposes a **fourth verdict** whose
tool and witness are their own lap -- named here so that lap has a standard to build against.
