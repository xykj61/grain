# The Urbit Reframe — Thanks, Not Dependence

**Seated:** `20260808.205404` on Keaton's word · **Status:** Living · **Cites, never restates**

Canon: `external-research/20260808-204505_the-best-name-period-vanes-ancestry-and-our-own-merit.md` (Movement II).

Grain descends from Urbit's ideas and can offer work back to that world, **yet its worth is not contingent on Urbit's blessing.** The bounded, asserted, explicit-width discipline and the civic, custody-first direction are promises to **whoever runs the software** — families, small collectives, civic builders who may never hear the word "vane." Their reception is the verdict, not a galaxy holder's attention.

## What the reframe keeps

- **Thank the ancestor.** Urbit handed down the runes, referential-transparency discipline, the personal-server dream, and the Azimuth idea of owned identity. The gratitude discipline stays (`gratitude-licenses.md`); keeping it is grace.
- **Keep the interop.** `glow/nock/` lets Grain meet the Nock world and be checked against it. The bridge is worth its small weight.
- **Keep the honest lineage.** The private field's git history descends from `urbit/urbit`, and `old/` holds the original tree. That is true history — **do not rewrite it** to erase the ancestor. The clean start already lives in the public seed (`grain-os/grain`, fresh Option-B history); the honest lineage belongs to the private tree.

## What the reframe retires

- **The supplicant posture.** Stop framing the project's purpose as a petition to galaxy holders, and stop measuring success by their reception. Write for Grain's own users and values first.
- **The vane grid as a cage.** The Urbit vane-role mapping (Mantra plays Clay's role, Comlink plays Ames') stays only as an **optional courtesy** for Urbit-literate readers — a sentence in the docs where it clarifies. The **A–Z alphabet grid is released**: no module must claim a letter or coin a vane word (see `comlink-tendency.md`). A module was never obligated to be a "vane."
- **The galaxy-proposal format as spine.** `azimuth-galaxy-proposal-format.md` stays in the drawer as an **optional** outward-facing courtesy for the rare change that genuinely wants a galaxy holder's eye. It is not the spine, and never again the mirror in which the work checks whether it is worthy.

## Why the reframe exists

Attention flowed toward Urbit because the pitch architecture pointed it there. Pointed at Grain's own users and values, the same care lands where it was always meant to — and the doubt about whether an external community will "see the value" dissolves with the framing that caused it.
