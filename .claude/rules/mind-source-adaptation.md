## Accretion and proof

- Preserve the original bytes. Record source path, license evidence, SHA-256, target path, and provenance before adaptation.
- Create the target beside the source. Refuse an existing target; never overwrite or delete the source silently.
- Before the owning lane accepts handover, prove parser or compiler acceptance, declared bounds, refusal before mutation, and relevant semantic, behavior, or render equivalence. Syntax alone is not equivalence.
- Stop on ambiguity, license uncertainty, unsupported dynamic behavior, unbounded allocation or recursion, a target-language gap, active overlap, missing provenance, or incomplete proof.
- Finish at most one conversion and one signed local commit per lap. Never push automatically.
