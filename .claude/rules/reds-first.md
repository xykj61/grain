(what went wrong - what caught it - what it taught), and **book the remainder of
the journey to reds before opening new work.** Cascade across journey, equinox,
and chapter boundaries, naming each crossing in the waymark.

- A **red** is something wrong. It books the allocation. Cite a row as **`REDS %89`** -- the percent sigil, never `#`,
  which GitHub's commit renderer would turn into a link to an unrelated pull request (`git-signing.md`).
- A **ratchet** is something not yet uniform. It turns on touch and books nothing.
- **BOOKED** is the third status word (door B, seated `20260829` on Keaton's word): the defect's
  instances stand repaired and the remainder is a ratchet, a seat, or a booked lap. A BOOKED row
  may fold to a shelf; **OPEN** stays on the pin. The one flag had carried both meanings, and the
  ledger deadlocked on it (REDS %338; doors in `active-designing/20260829-031804`).
- A fix is closed by a **witness on metal**, never by a claim.
- Any **breach** the fixes want is designed, named, and **waits for Keaton's word**.
- A lantern that fires twice should become a loom.
