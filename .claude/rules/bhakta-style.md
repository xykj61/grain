# Bhakta Style -- the register that assumes nothing (third alternate to Radiant)

**The working style of this tree is [Gauge](gauge-style.md)**, whose first rule governs Bhakta
hardest: **don't be too smart about it**. Bhakta is the third register beside
[Radiant](radiant-style.md) and [Twilight](twilight-style.md).

**Full guide:** `context/BHAKTA_STYLE.md` - **Seated:** `20260908` on Keaton's word - **Status:** Living

Bhakta is Radiant teaching someone who has never written a line of code, and who deserves the whole
truth anyway. The name is the Sanskrit for one who walks the path of devotion, whose discipline is
that the path opens to anyone and needs no scholarship to enter -- which is the promise this register
makes a reader.

## Where it is reached for

**Specification and design reference, API documentation, the OS user manual, onboarding and
first-hour pages** -- any writing whose reader may be meeting computing itself for the first time.
Radiant carries the day, Twilight the threshold, Bhakta the open door.

## Core moves (all of Radiant, plus)

- **Assume no background, and let that stay quiet.** Explain the thing; a reader who already knows
  should still enjoy the sentence.
- **First principles, in order.** Nothing rests on a term introduced later.
- **One new idea at a time**, concrete before abstract. Show the thing, then name it.
- **Say why it matters to a life, once.** Every description roots back to what the work serves --
  the ecology, the household, the grower who owns their own records. Placed where it belongs and
  said once; repeated in every section it becomes a slogan.
- **Encourage by being accurate.** *Here is the step, and here is what you will see when it works*
  serves a reader where *this is easy* fails them the moment it is not.
- **Courtesy in the words themselves** -- reach past *simply*, *just*, and *obviously*.

## What it keeps from Gauge

Every measurement discipline holds. A figure carries unit, date, source, and what holds it still; a
projection carries horizon, assumptions, falsifier and confidence. **Accessible means measured too**
-- the reader who needs the plainest words most needs to be able to check what they are told.

## The lens

Bhakta writes through the lens this tree already holds: devotion, grace, and the path with heart,
with **biocyclic vegan ecology** as the world the software serves. A bound exists so the program runs
on a machine a small farm can afford; a key nobody can revoke is what lets a grower own their records.
One honest sentence, where it belongs.
