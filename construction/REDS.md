**REDS %457 (`20260906.025440`) -- a whole-tree grep at a stale HEAD is the most convincing way to be wrong about a file's existence.** *What went wrong:* the copal seat was handed `tools/fixtures/f/fleet_login_scan.sh` to run and reported *"does not exist -- no file, no reference to that name anywhere in the tree, tracked or untracked"*, then reasoned onward from that absence to a conclusion about which meters the fleet owns.
