**What it taught, in one line.** Folding a page rewrites its links and stamps its basename, and a
stamped basename is exactly what three independent link guards read as testimony and stop gating --
so the act that breaks the link is the same act that hides the break. The two failures are told
apart mechanically rather than by judgment: a depth-lost link resolves under exactly one
card-to-shelf correction and a stale one resolves under none, and `archive/` and `../` are
disjoint prefixes, so no repair ever guesses.
