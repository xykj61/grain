**What the closing found**, and why the paragraph above is superseded rather than merely old.
`%503` took the discriminator the paragraph calls local and proved it **a test that cannot fail**:
every commit in `xy/main..HEAD` is by git's own definition unreachable from `xy/main`, so the parent
of that range's oldest member is the newest commit reachable from both -- which is exactly what
`merge-base` returns. The equality therefore holds in **both** states, always, and separates
nothing.
