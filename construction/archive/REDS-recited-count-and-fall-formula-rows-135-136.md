**REDS %135 (`20260822.102337`) -- a one-clock stamp cited in a meter's prose was refused as a recited count, and the same lantern had already fired once.** *What went wrong:* the widening column split landed, and `tools/ca/caravan_ladder_carry_witness.rish` updated its queue prose to cite the design read behind it -- `active-designing/20260822-101058_what-a-widening-costs-a-rungs-door.md`. `tools/fixtures/caravan_ladder_prose_count_scan.sh` reads every digit run in a `say` line and refuses any not held by an `assert` in the same file, so the stamp's `20260822` and `101058` both reddened `tools/ca/caravan_ladder_copy_witness.rish` and through it the whole choir of one hundred and eight. Neither digit run is a count. A one-clock stamp is fixed by `context/specs/20260627-102012_one-clock-naming-law.md` at the moment a file is named and can never drift, which is the only fault that scan exists to catch. *What caught it:* running the full Caravan choir after the split rather than only the witness the split touched. The copy witness named the file, the line, and both values. *What it taught:* **this is REDS %131 arriving a second time in a different shape, and the first repair was written narrower than its own reasoning.** That row exempted a `%`-sigilled row name because `%` marks a constant term that is exactly itself and never varies -- yet the sentence it was really proving is that **a digit run belonging to a name cannot drift, so it is not a count**. Written as `%`-only, the rule covered one of the two immutable-name shapes this tree writes and left the other to fire. A lantern that fires twice becomes a loom, so the exemption is now stated at the level of the reasoning rather than at the level of the one example that prompted it.

**REDS %135 CLOSED (`20260822.102337`) -- both one-clock shapes read as names, and a bare number beside a stamp still refuses.** *The repair, on metal:* [`../tools/fixtures/caravan_ladder_prose_count_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/caravan_ladder_prose_count_scan.sh) strips the two shapes the naming law defines -- `YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS_` in a filename and `YYYYMMDD.HHMMSS` in a body field -- from a `say` line before extracting any number, exactly as it already strips `%<digits>`, and the comment beside the strip now names the reasoning both shapes share rather than the sigil alone. The bound stays as tight as the sigil's: only a **full** stamp is exempt, so a bare eight-digit number is still a count named at its own value. Two legs prove it on generated controls in [`../tools/ca/caravan_ladder_copy_witness.rish`](../tools/ca/caravan_ladder_copy_witness.rish), sitting directly beneath the sigil pair whose reasoning they extend: a meter citing a dated path and a body stamp reads `LADDER_PROSE_COUNT_OK`, and the same sentence with a bare `20260822` beside the stamp is refused at that value -- one refusal, on the bare number alone, which is what proves the exemption is a name rather than a doorway. **REDS %136 (`20260822.103942`) -- the fall formula named two populations where three stand, and overpriced every fold with more than one rung home.** *What went wrong:* `tools/fixtures/ladder_reach_visibility_scan.sh` predicted a fold's realized fall as `(copies - 1) * lines - (folding - 1) * stub`, on the stated reasoning that a rung standing **below the fold line** keeps its whole body and *leaves the family*, so the carry it contributed goes entirely. It leaves the family only when fewer than two such rungs remain. Two or more identical bodies are still a family and go on carrying `(held - 1) * lines` between themselves. The `load_one` fold was the first of this arc with more than one rung below the line -- seven of them -- so the meter priced **549** and the carry fell **483**, the gap being exactly six bodies of eleven lines. *What caught it:* the carry scan disagreeing with the widening meter's prediction for the second lap running, and the two disagreements having different causes. The lap before, the gap was the two asserts a repair restored and both numbers were right; this time the arithmetic was re-derived from the three body populations on disk and the corrected formula reproduced all three folds the arc has measured -- `load_one` 483, `read_count` 487, `take_up_again` 540 -- where the elder formula matched only the two that had fewer than two rungs home. *What it taught:* **a formula that names its populations in prose must be checked against the populations that actually stand, and an assumption is invisible exactly where the data has never tested it.** The elder reasoning was written down honestly and read correctly for eleven folds, because every one of them had zero or one rung below the line. The error was never latent in the prose; it was latent in the corpus. A prediction that has only ever been tested on one side of its own conditional is a prediction with an untested branch, and the way to find the branch is to name the conditional out loud -- which the comment did, one clause short of the case that mattered.

**REDS %136 CLOSED (`20260822.103942`) -- three populations counted, and the correction proven against every fold the arc has measured.** *The repair, on metal:* [`../tools/fixtures/ladder_reach_visibility_scan.sh`](../tools/fixtures/ladder_reach_visibility_scan.sh) computes `held_after` as `(held - 1) * lines` when two or more rungs stand below the fold line and zero otherwise, and subtracts it beside the stub carry: `fall = (copies - 1) * lines - ((folding - 1) * stub + held_after)`. The comment beside it names all three populations rather than two, and names the condition under which the third disappears. Checked against the arc's own record before the edit shipped: `load_one` corrected 549 to **483**, matching the measured carry exactly; `may_pass` corrected 574 to **536**, three rungs standing below its line; `read_count` and `take_up_again` unchanged at 487 and 540, which is what proves the fix touches only the folds that were wrong. `may_pass` stands asserted in [`../tools/ca/caravan_ladder_reach_visibility_witness.rish`](../tools/ca/caravan_ladder_reach_visibility_witness.rish) as the living row carrying the correction, so a formula that forgets the third population again reddens on the lap it enters.
