## The aligned expedition -- the bound before the flight

The general mechanism also serves memory, allocation, latency, energy, and network budgets.

The Vortex writings add four durable rules:

1. **State has one center.** A pure transition computes `new_state = transition(old_state, event)` over memory allocated within declared bounds.
2. **Local facts glue into one whole.** Each measurement keeps its source and unit; compatible local records combine into one verdict.
3. **Two descriptions move together.** The concrete input record and the abstract policy stay in witnessed correspondence.
4. **The first ship is the witness.** A small measured trial decides the order of later moonshots.

This expedition serves Grain rather than orbit alone. A farmer's water loop, a small computer's memory, a community network, and a spacecraft radiator can share one honest habit: name the vessel, name the ceiling, name who measures it, and let the machine refuse excess in public.
