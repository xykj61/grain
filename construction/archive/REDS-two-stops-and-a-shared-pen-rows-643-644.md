**What the two taught together, and why they share a shelf.** Each is a lesson about **reach**
rather than about quality. `%643` found a stop sentinel only its own author could set, so a hand
asking a loop to finish and stop was answered by the loop erasing the request. `%644` found ten
careful, specific witnesses guarding the tree's front doors, rostered nowhere, so one of them stood
red for sixteen days while a 190-green pass ran beside it every twenty minutes.

Neither was a badly built thing. Both were built well and could not be reached -- by a hand in the
first case, by a pass in the second. A guard's reach is the roster; a stop's reach is whoever may
set it.

Proven by `tools/fixtures/f/fleet_drain_control.sh` -- 9 behaviors, every sentinel planted and lifted, the two sentinels proven distinguishable. **CLOSED** on that control.
