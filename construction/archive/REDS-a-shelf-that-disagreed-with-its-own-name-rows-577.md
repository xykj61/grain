A number written once is a fact. A number written four times is four facts that have to be kept
equal by hand, and this tree keeps them equal by hand every time the derived spine renumbers an
unshared row. Three of the four are retyped in the same motion. The fourth -- the title line, read
by every visitor and by no instrument -- is the one that stays behind.

The lesson is the one its neighbours keep learning in different materials. `%524` found three link
walls reading one row shape. [`%566`](REDS-the-meter-that-read-one-of-two-instruments-rows-566.md)
found a meter reading one of two instruments. Here a guard reads one of four spellings, and the
three it does not read are the three a reader actually meets: the address they arrive at, the title
they see first, and the header beneath it.

**What separates this from its kin is which spelling is authoritative.** The filename is the link
target -- a path that moves breaks a promise, and `reds_citation` guards those promises from the
outside. So the reading asks whether each label agrees with the address, rather than asking the
labels to agree among themselves; that choice is what makes a repair obvious when the guard bites.
