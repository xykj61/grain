# REDS %403, %404, %407 -- what a reading is allowed to skip

*Folded from the living pin [`../REDS.md`](../REDS.md) on `20260904.174748`, three **CLOSED** rows
written across one night, to bring the pin back under its bound as `%408` landed. Read together
they are one lesson in three rooms: **a repair to what a reading measures never reaches what that
reading is allowed to skip.** `%403`: a hook ran its generator with both streams to /dev/null and
then told a jailed reader to read the error it had just discarded -- so the refusal now carries the
last twelve lines it refused on. `%404`: `%402` fixed the report card's prose extractor and left the

than asserting one member of it. Link depth moved with the text.*

**REDS %403 (`20260831.113534`) -- the hook told a jailed reader to read an error it had discarded.** *What went wrong:* `refresh_page` in `tools/hooks/pre-commit` ran its generator with both streams to /dev/null, then printed *read its error before committing* -- and inside the jail nobody can rerun it, so Mind and Mystery stood twelve hours on swallowed words. *What caught it:* the morning helper read, then a host replication of the jail's PATH shape passing clean -- the evidence had died in the hook, nowhere else. *What it taught:* **a refusal carries the words it refused on.** *Repaired (`20260831.113534`):* the captured output's last twelve lines ride the refusal (pen-proven); `/private/var/select` mapped read-only in the supervisor's jail plan on Keaton's word. **CLOSED.**

**REDS %404 (`20260831.121741`) -- the repair that taught a reading to find its prose left, one line below, a typed word that switches the reading off.** *What went wrong:* `tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh` frees BOTH scored readings at Meter -- `register=100; reach=100` -- for any artifact that is not a program, so a `.kyri` roster read with `--setting meter` returns a composite fixed by Truth and Service alone. Measured across the nineteen tracked notation files carrying 200 words or more of comment prose, every one read **exactly 94** with Service held at 75: one number across nineteen different inputs, which is precisely the disconnected reading REDS %402 had repaired eighteen hours earlier, still available for the typing. The same nineteen read **81 to 92** at Field, an eleven-point spread with every one at or above B, so the class never needed the relief it could ask for. *What caught it:* answering the live card's own named door for this seat -- *which setting a roster takes* -- by measuring the class at all three settings rather than asserting one. Door and Field discriminated; the Meter column printed 94 nineteen times, and a column that constant is a reading nobody is taking. *What it taught:* **a repair to what a reading measures does not reach what a reading is allowed to skip.** %402 fixed the extractor and left the exemption beside it, and an exemption is a second door onto the same fault -- reachable by a flag rather than by a bug. *Repaired (`20260831.121741`):* the setting comes from the file's own grammar, the way a program's already does. A notation carrying a comment block has a document, so Meter is refused and the reading falls to Field, this card's default, since Meter carries no ceiling to fall back on; a notation with no comment block is all record and the free pass stands. The split is the family's rather than an invention -- of 4,090 tracked `.bron` and `.kyri` files, **3,983 carry zero comment lines** (3,928 session logs, which `.claude/rules/session-logs.md` already seats at Meter, plus 55 data corpora) and 107 carry a document. Field rather than Door because twelve of the nineteen exceed Door's 20% register ceiling and eight exceed its grade ceiling of 9; a typed `--setting door` is still honored, since whether that ceiling of 9 is right at all is its own open question on the live card and settling it inside this repair would be reaching. Ninety-three behaviors in a throwaway pen, eight of them new, with the biting direction shown both ways: a card carrying the elder test reads two opposite rosters to one composite, and the repaired card does not. **CLOSED** on `tools/q/qa_report_card_witness.rish`, GREEN on metal.

**REDS %407 (`20260831.132335`) -- one card, one page of prose, two scored readings, and only one of them asks whether there is enough prose to read.** *What went wrong:* `tools/fixtures/q/qa_report_card.sh` reads Register and Reach over the same prepared prose. Register honors a floor published by `tools/fixtures/p/prose_register_scan.sh` -- under eight sentences the share is reported rather than scored, since a share needs a denominator big enough to mean something. Reach is a Flesch-Kincaid grade, which is two rates of exactly that kind (words per sentence, syllables per word), and it had no floor at all -- so one run over one file printed `register=100 ... reported, not scored` beside a scored grade from those same words. The reach awk's empty exit put the reach itself in field one and printed zero, so a file with **nothing to read scored 0**, the worst reading on the scale: `active-designing/docs/glow/fixtures/anchor_bad.rye` printed `meter_reach=100 (0 words reported, not scored)` and `reach=0 (grade 0 against 9 ... 0 words)` in the same card. *What caught it:* measuring the door this seat was handed -- whether a record-only notation should DEFAULT to Meter -- across 385 artifacts read by the card itself (55 record-only notation, a 60-log sample, 163 programs, all 107 documented notation). **207 sat under the register floor and 137 took a scored Reach below 100 anyway**; 17 carried zero words and every one read reach=0. Worst are data corpora, where the syllable heuristic counts vowel groups inside a 128-character hex string: `mycelium/corpora/braid_dag.bron` reads 6.1 syllables per word and grade **59** over 30 words, and 41 of 55 read D+ on that alone. *What it taught:* **a free pass one reading earns by measurement is a free pass its twin has already argued for.** The card had written the argument twice -- the register floor's comment, the index door's -- and applied it to one term of one reading. The door answers itself: session logs moved **0 of 60**, so the family the question named was never the one bleeding, and defaulting 3,985 notation files to Meter would hand them the constant `%404` had just removed. *Repaired (`20260831.132335`):* the grade term takes the same cited floor; the cross-reference term keeps its index door -- two terms, two doors, independent, so a twenty-word page with five links stays penalized for link density while it stops being graded on a readability score no twenty words can carry. All four index-door legs pass unchanged. Eight new control legs, 93 -> **101** behaviors, the bite proven by carrying the elder reading back into a copy of the card. Across the same 385: **137 readings rose, none fell**, 99 crossed to B. Witness GREEN on metal. *Named, not taken:* the card's live front attributes below-B programs to Door's ceiling of 9; of 115 sampled programs below B, **51 sat under this floor** and 64 are genuinely scored at grades 9 to 23 -- the ceiling question is the second number's alone. **CLOSED.**
