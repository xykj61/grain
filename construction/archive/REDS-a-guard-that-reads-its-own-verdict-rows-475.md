# REDS %475 -- a guard that reads its own verdict can never take it back

*Folded from the living pin [`../REDS.md`](../REDS.md) on `20260906.093338` at the moment it was written,
for the reason the two shelves before it give: every other row standing on that pin is **OPEN**, and
the pin holds under 350 bytes of slack. This row is **CLOSED** on a witness that reds before and
greens after, so the shelf is where it belongs.*

*The clause is about a fixed point. `standing_equipment` is itself a rostered guard, so
`standing_equipment_run.sh` writes `ran standing_equipment <stamp> <verdict>` into the run card
that `standing_equipment_scan.sh` then reads and counts. While the guard was green that loop was
invisible. The first time it went red, the loop closed: the reading that produced the red became the
reading that reproduced it, and no state existed anywhere else to clear it.*

**REDS %475 (`20260906.093338`) -- `standing_equipment_scan.sh` counted its own run record among the reds it refuses on, so its first red was absorbing: nothing the tree could be repaired to would take it green again.** *What went wrong:* the scan walks the run card and counts every row whose verdict is neither `green` nor `gated`, refusing at `verdict=roster_broken` if the count is past zero. Its own row is in that card, written by the runner after the scan answers. So a red verdict becomes the next pass's evidence for the same red, and the pass after that, with the roster and the tree perfectly repaired in between. *What caught it:* the hot pass of this lap. The guard's genuine red -- `runs_unrostered: copy_lag`, a receipt for a guard a dead lap's stash had carried out of the roster -- was repaired, `runs_unrostered` read **0**, and the guard refused anyway on one line: `red: standing_equipment red`. *Proven on metal, one variable:* with that row in the card the scan reads `runs_red=1 verdict=roster_broken`; removing that row alone, changing nothing else in tree, roster or card, reads `runs_red=0 verdict=ok`. *What it taught:* **a guard's own verdict is its output, never its evidence** -- and the failure mode is invisible for exactly as long as the guard is green, which is the whole time anyone would think to look. Same family as `%458` one room over, where a scan read its own header as tree evidence, and `%451`'s, where an instrument was green because it could not see enough of its subject to disagree. *Repaired:* the self row is **reported and never counted** -- `runs_red_self` beside `runs_red`, and a `red_self:` detail line, so a hand still sees it and nothing is hidden. The exemption is **one name wide**. *Proven both ways, and the second half is what makes it a repair rather than a hole:* five control legs on planted rosters -- the self red free, reported and uncounted, and a **peer's** red in the same card still refusing and still counted exactly once. Three plants, each bitten by the legs that should catch it and no others: the exemption removed, the exemption aimed at a name that never matches, and a peer's red routed into the self counter, which takes `peer_red_still_bites` to `no` and is the shape a careless exemption would actually have. **CLOSED.**
