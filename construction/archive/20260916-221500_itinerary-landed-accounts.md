## The mechanism

Each of the nine ASCII-family scans answered `--list` with

```sh
printf '%s' "$report" | sort -rn | head -40
```

and printed nothing about the rows that `head` removed. Eleven such listings stand across the nine
files. Each now computes `list_total` from its own report, caps at forty unless the mode is
`--list-all`, and emits `list_shown`, `list_hidden` and `list_total` after the rows. The two
irregular members keep their shape: `rye_comment_ascii_scan.sh` carries two listings in one mode and
wears `list_` and `trail_list_` prefixes, since one number standing for two populations is the same
fault one layer down; `ascii_document_scan.sh` carries two modes and gains `--list-all` and
`--enforce-list-all`.

## The meter, and why the class earned one

Petrichor's `seed_link_scan.sh` printed a ratchet site only while its counter stood at or under
five, so 847 sites showed five (`20260916.210332`). Two firings in one day, so the class gets a loom.

`tools/fixtures/l/listing_census_scan.sh` reads every tracked `*_scan.sh` under `tools/` for a
**cap** -- a sort piped into head, printed rather than redirected, taking more than one row -- and
asks whether that file also **emits** a key ending `hidden=` or `shown=`. Three exclusions are drawn
where counting would be wrong rather than merely hard, and each is proven in the pen:

- **`head -1`** is a scalar read. Nine stand in the live tree and none is a fault.
- **A redirected cap** bounds an input set, which is TAME asking a collection to name a maximum. It
  drops rows nobody was reading.
- **A comment naming the shape is not the shape.** Without this the meter refuses its own header.

The nine ASCII scans are a **wall at zero**; **13 caps across nine files in nine peer lanes** ratchet
under a ceiling that only falls -- `prose_register` 4, `sealed_digest` 2, and one each in
`width_check`, `tame_style_long_fn`, `standing_equipment`, `socket_dialect`,

## Three faults of the instrument's own, each found by running it

- **The claim priced the remainder at four.** It was counted by a grep wanting digits after `head`;
  the meter reads `head -n "$var"` too and answers thirteen. The correction rides with the reading.
- **Assignments counted as censuses.** `list_shown=$a` sets a variable and says nothing to a reader,
  so a repaired block read as three censuses and a verbose file could earn slack from two extra
  caps. The census is an emit now, and leg `assignment_is_not_a_census` holds it there.
- **One mutation leg read a false pass.** Its `sed` matched nothing, and the unmutated count
  happened to equal the number the leg wanted. Caught by diffing each mutant against the scan rather
  than trusting the leg's own answer.

## What this does not reach

Whether a particular `head` belongs to a particular `echo` -- that is control flow rather than a
scan -- so the reading is **per file**, and a file whose two caps share one census line is
undercounted on purpose, the way every meter in this family undercounts and says so.

And whether a cap's **number** is well chosen. Forty may be too few for a lane and too many for a
reader; this meter proves only that the number is spoken aloud.
