**REDS %207 (`20260824.193815`) -- a number reported without its bins meant three things at once, and stood 2.8x the truth for a day.** *What went wrong:* the comment-dial round of `20260824.170904` reported **13,235 asserts with no stated reason** against the seated `// invariant:` law, and that figure was carried into the living card, named to Keaton twice as the standing crux, and used to argue that a wall wanted his word before it could go up. The real figure is **4,757**. Two faults compounded, and neither was an arithmetic slip. **First, three promises shared one denominator.** A **contract** assert inside an ordinary function is what the law is about; a **selftest** assert inside `main` or a `selftest` function has the test's own name as its reason; a **witness** assert inside a `*_witness.rye` has the witness header as its reason. The three sum exactly to the old figure -- `4,757 + 3,944 + 2,568 = 13,235` -- so nothing was ever miscounted, and three different things were added together. **Second, 211 symlinks were counted as modules.** Zig refuses an import that escapes the root file's directory, so a module several rooms need is symlinked into each; `image/photos.rye` is the file and two rooms hold links to it, and counting the links tripled its 185-assert gap to 555. **11% of the 1,891 modules were links.** *What caught it:* binning the reading before acting on it, and then **three identical rows in the worst-ten table** -- 234 contract, 49 covered, 185 gap, three times over. A symlink looks like a coincidence to a counter. *What it taught:* **care about the method does not substitute for care about the population.** The round that produced 13,235 said so explicitly in its own log -- *counted directly, walking up from each assert rather than dividing two line counts* -- and that sentence was true, careful, and beside the point, because the question it never asked was *what am I counting over*. This is the denominator lesson of `20260824.170904` arriving at the numerator: **a share is a claim about its denominator, and a count is a claim about its population.** *Repaired:* [`../tools/fixtures/invariant_coverage_scan.sh`](../../tools/fixtures/invariant_coverage_scan.sh) bins the three promises, skips symlinks, and reads in two seconds. The verdict lives at [`../active-development/20260824-193815_a-number-without-its-bins.md`](../../active-development/20260824-193815_a-number-without-its-bins.md). **The law runs at 79%**, the gap is **4,757 across 570 modules with a median of 3**, and **half of it sits in 50 files**. *And the shape is the part worth keeping:* coverage is a **room** property. A discipline was carried into some rooms and never into others, which makes the remedy a per-room sweep rather than a tree-wide ratchet grinding 570 files with a median of three. *Named, not fixed:* whether 92.8% coverage in `caravan` is a number about **reasons** or about **vocabulary** is unmeasured -- an invariant line restating its own assertion counts here exactly like one naming the failure it prevents.
