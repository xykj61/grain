## REDS %667 (`20260909.155028`) -- the pronunciation roll was listed beside the lexer table rather than derived from it, so two runes entered the language unpronounced and the guard that watches the table stayed green

**What went wrong.** `tools/g/glow_rune_alphabet_worker.sh` binds Glow's rune alphabet to the lexer.
Its check walked a hand-written heredoc of 28 `glyph<TAB>spoken` rows and asked, of each, whether
`glow/tokens.rye` `match_rune2` carries that glyph. That direction catches a pronunciation row whose
lexer head has gone. It can never catch a lexer head that arrived with no pronunciation row -- which
is the only direction a language actually grows in.

Two heads arrived exactly that way. `?&` and `?|` entered `match_rune2` on `20260830`, recorded in
[`../../foundations/20260830-011530_a-rune-is-earned-by-a-law.md`](../../foundations/20260830-011530_a-rune-is-earned-by-a-law.md)
as *the ancestor's spellings, already spoken by Rishi*, with four proof legs and twin desks agreeing.
The lexer's `COUNT` check was updated 28 -> 30 in the same arc. The roll was not, and the two numbers
stood **30 against 28** for ten days with nothing able to read the difference.

**The reason the difference was unreadable is that one name carried both meanings.** The worker
published `rune_heads=$n` counting **pronunciation rows**, and two peers bind that field --
`tools/g/glow_digraph_twin_witness.rish` asserts `rune_heads=28`, `tools/g/glow_shop_nest_tokenize_witness.rish`
asserts the field is present. A reader taking `rune_heads` for *the runes this language has* read 28
and was wrong by two, and no field named the gap.

**What caught it.** The aether rota row, read through its own sense. Aether hears, and its threshold
page states the ear's hard rule: *a guard that cannot red guards nothing -- a witness must be proven
able to make a sound before its silence means anything.* Asking that question of this lane found
`glow_rune_alphabet` GREEN, fast, and **rostered nowhere** since STOA90 -- so the sound it could make
reached no one. Proven on metal before the repair, in a throwaway pen holding a doctored `tokens.rye`:
deleting `?&` from `match_rune2` left the gate **GREEN**, because no roll row named it to be missed.

**What it taught.** **Derive the population from the thing you are guarding, never list it beside.**
A list beside a table is a second copy, and two copies drift the first day one of them is edited --
the same lesson `%532` booked, arriving here through a heredoc rather than a grep. The repair reads
`match_rune2` on every pass and walks **its** glyphs, looking each up in the roll; the elder direction
is kept beside it, so a pronunciation row whose head has gone still refuses.

**And a gap is bounded by NAME rather than by count.** A ceiling of two cannot tell `?&` from a head
somebody swapped in beside it: planted in the pen, deleting `?&` and adding `!!` read `unnamed_heads=2`
and passed. `UNNAMED_EXEMPT='?& ?|'` refuses a third unnamed head wherever it arrives -- the shape
[`../../.claude/rules/gratitude-licenses.md`](../../.claude/rules/gratitude-licenses.md) already uses
for Microkit's two GPL files, bounded by path so a new one anywhere else reds on the lap it arrives.

**Proven both ways**, six legs on real files in a throwaway pen, every refusal planted and then lifted:
a clean tree passes; a swap of either unnamed head refuses by name; a 31st head refuses on the count;
a pronounced head deleted from the lexer refuses by its spoken name; the tree walks free again after
every plant. The gate is on the roster at `tier lap`, 0.6 seconds.

**What stays open, and it is not this row.** `?&` and `?|` have no pronunciation in the closed table,
and what this tree calls them is a language custody ruling that stays Keaton's word -- a rune is
earned by a law, and a name by his word. The exemption holds the two still and names them out loud;
the day they are named, the list empties and the gate tightens to zero by deletion.

**CLOSED** -- the guard reds now where it could not, and it is heard now where it was not.
