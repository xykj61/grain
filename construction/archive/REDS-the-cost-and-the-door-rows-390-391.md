*Folded from the living pin [`../REDS.md`](../REDS.md) on `20260831.062210`, both **BOOKED** -- one lap's two
findings about instruments that reported faithfully and measured the wrong thing. The first is
cost: the roster recorded what every guard answered and never what it took, so a lap sizing its
own pass extrapolated from whatever window it had watched, over a distribution whose mean sits
fifteen times its median. Three laps in a row projected a full pass at three and a half hours, then
at fifty-five minutes; held still and measured whole, it is forty-six, and four guards of a hundred
and eight hold fifty-five percent of it. The second is a door: a pin that declares itself
newest-first opened on the oldest shelf in the tree, because its top row was copied from a page
whose table runs the other way -- and every loop's first instruction is to open the newest shelf
named at the top of that pin. Together they say the same thing twice: an instrument can be honest
in every field it prints and still hand its reader the wrong number, and only its own count tells
the two apart. Both rows name their repair and both landed it. Moved here to bring the pin back
under its bound after a rebase carried two more rows in beside them. Link depth moved with the text.*
