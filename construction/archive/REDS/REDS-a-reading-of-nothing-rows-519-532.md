# REDS -- a reading of nothing

**Language:** EN
**Style:** Gauge, Meter setting
**Voice:** Kyri
**Status:** Shelf -- two folded rows, immutable once written
**Room:** checkable -- two ledger rows, each repair proven by a control on real trees
**Folded:** `20260915.215500` from [`../REDS.md`](../../REDS.md)

Two rows, folded to hold the pin under the bound eight ships share, on the lap that met the same
lesson from a third side. They belong together because each is an instrument that had quietly
stopped reading its subject, and **an instrument reading nothing is indistinguishable from a
healthy tree.** `%519` found a control whose plants were literal lines of the module they broke, so
an ordinary edit to one of those lines turned the plant into a no-op -- and a plant that plants
nothing passes exactly as a law that holds does. `%532` found the only guard that runs Glow desks
naming its 218 desks by hand against a corpus of 352, so the list stood in for the population and
read as coverage from every side.

The pair is worth reading before building any guard. The cure in both is the same shape and it is
cheap: **make the instrument prove it saw something.** Assert the plant took, derive the population
from the room rather than typing it, count the legs beside the failures. A guard that cannot tell
you what it looked at has told you nothing, and it has told you nothing in the voice of good news.

Read them beside [`REDS-fold-recital.md`](REDS-fold-recital.md), which keeps each shelf's record.



**REDS %519 (`20260906.210016`) -- a control's plants are literal lines of the module they break, so the lap that edits one of those lines turns its plant into a no-op, and a plant that plants nothing reads exactly like a law that holds.** *What went wrong:* `tools/fixtures/m/mantra_diff_control.sh` broke `mantra/src/diff.rye` seven ways in a pen, one of them by rewriting `var deletes: std.ArrayListUnmanaged(u32) = .empty;` into the Zig 0.15 form the toolchain retired. This lap changed that declaration's type from `u32` to `LineId`, so the `sed` matched nothing, the pen was copied byte for byte, and the phase built and ran the **unmutated** module -- `elder_arraylist_exit=0`, the same reading a healthy law gives. *What caught it:* the control's own verdict loop, which happens to demand that phase be non-zero, so it answered `break_not_caught` rather than `ok`. **That is luck rather than design**: the loop reads the exit code of a run that never tested anything, and had the plant been one whose absence still exits non-zero for an unrelated reason, the reading would have been `ok`. *What it taught:* **a plant is a claim about a file, and nothing was checking that claim.** Every phase asserts what the pen does AFTER the break; not one asserted the break landed. This is `%463`'s family -- a census that reads nothing and says nothing is wrong is indistinguishable from a healthy file -- moved into the instrument that is supposed to prove the census can red. *And it is a loom rather than a lantern.* Measured `20260906.210016` at `e567cbc128`: **102 of 155 tracked controls mutate a pen with `sed`, and 8 carry a `cmp -s`** -- and reading all eight, **every one of them uses it to prove a plant landed**, in five lanes and five spellings (`did not apply`, `the innocence patch matched nothing`, three `_planted` readings). Five hands met this fault independently and each fixed it locally; **94 controls still have no such check**, so the next type change silently disarms whichever plant names the line it moved. *Repaired, in this lane only:* all three Mantra controls now `cmp -s` the mutated copy against the original inside `run_pen`, answer the literal `plant_matched_nothing`, and read that word ahead of every numeric verdict -- a word rather than a number, so it can never be mistaken for an exit code. Proven from the failing side on metal by aiming a plant at a declaration that does not exist: `control_verdict=plant_matched_nothing`, exit 1, against the real control's `ok` and exit 0. The elder-form plant itself is repaired to name the **form** rather than one line's spelling, capturing the variable and its type, so it survives the next widening. *Not taken, and it is the larger half:* the 94 remaining controls span every lane, and `run_pen` is written fresh in each -- so the fix is either 94 edits or one shared helper the fixtures import, which is a shape decision rather than a lap. *Read `20260910.152143`:* `tools/fixtures/p/plant.sh` landed; **27 of 250** controls source it and all 27 call it. `plant_liveness_scan.sh` runs each resolvable plant against its source: **46 resolved, 46 live, 0 dead**. *Repaired this lap:* its writing test read `>&2` as a file redirect, so two diagnostic READS stood in `plants_unresolved` -- the inflation its own comment declares avoided. Duplications strip first: **67 -> 66**, two removed, one added by this lap's pen. `--list` names the lines behind each count. Control **74 legs, faults=0**, mutation-proven. *Open:* the ~66, most pen-authored with no tracked source. *Read `20260915.175900`:* `plant_liveness_scan.sh` answers **56 resolved, 56 live, 0 dead**, up from 46 at the last reading, and `tools/fixtures/p/plant.sh` keeps spreading by touch. The remainder is that ratchet. **BOOKED.**

**REDS %532 (`20260907.005950`) -- the only guard that runs Glow desks names its desks by hand, and the corpus grew past the list without a sound.** *What went wrong:* `tools/g/glow_run_desk_witness.rish` is 1,133 lines of hand-written blocks, each naming one desk, running it through `tools/g/glow_run.rish` and asserting GREEN. It names **218**. `glow/gen/` holds **352**. Five of the remainder declare in their own heads that they must never be run, which leaves **129 runnable desks that no guard speaks for** -- and run bare on metal, **89 pass right now** while 42 want only a sample argument. *The deeper shape, and the reason this is a red rather than a chore:* a desk's run-contract -- may this file be handed to `glow_run`? -- was written in **three places no instrument could read together**. Arity lived in `tools/g/glow_run_worker.sh` as a hand-written `case` over stem names. Runnability lived in a `::` prose comment inside the desk (`Parse-only; do not glow_run`). Coverage lived in the witness's 218 blocks. *What caught it:* `construction/ITINERARY.md` named rostering that witness as this seat's lap, and measuring its reach before rostering it is what found the gap -- the same move that caught `two_rooms_doorway` a day earlier. **Rostering it as it stood would have published a 218-desk green as coverage of a 352-desk corpus.** *What it taught:* **a hand-written enumeration standing in for a population is a proxy, and it reads as coverage from every side.** The sharper half is underneath: the three enumerations **agreed perfectly**. The five self-declared desks are exactly the five the witness declines, confirmed by running all 139. That agreement was real, complete, and stored in a person's memory -- the one place a check cannot stand. *Repaired (`20260907.005950`):* `tools/fixtures/g/glow_desk_reach_scan.sh` derives the population from the room and reads each desk's contract from **both** markers it carries -- `refuse` in its stem, `Refuse` / `do not glow_run` in its head. Three readings gated at zero: `norun_disagree`, `phantom`, `contradicted`. `uncovered` is a **ratchet at 129** rather than a gate, because the backlog was not made today; what it buys is that a desk landing tomorrow is covered, declares itself unrunnable in both markers, or reds on the lap it arrives. `tools/fixtures/g/glow_desk_reach_control.sh` proves **23 behaviors** on miniature desk rooms in a pen, every refusal planted then lifted and the ceiling shown both ways. `tools/g/glow_desk_reach_witness.rish` rostered `tier lap` at **6s**, reading desks where the witness it measures would cost a quarter hour of Zig builds. *An error of my own, caught by the control before it shipped:* the scan first read **10 phantom desks** all present on disk, because `find | sort` ran under `LC_ALL=C` and `comm` did not, so the two disagreed about collation. One `export LC_ALL=C`. **A meter that sorts and a meter that compares must agree about the alphabet.** *Not closed, named rather than taken:* a **fourth kind** stands in the corpus -- `glow/gen/s/sample-demo-fact-line-lits.glow`, `sample-demo-fixture-lits.glow` and `sample-digraph-table.glow` fail `unsupported Glow head` and are data fixtures rather than desks. They carry **no marker in either name or head**, so nothing tells them from a desk that ought to run, and they sit inside the 129. Giving them one changes what declares a desk's kind, which is a language custody ruling. *Read `20260915.175900`:* `tools/g/glow_desk_reach_witness.rish` runs GREEN on metal this lap, `uncovered` holds as a ratchet, and a desk landing tomorrow is covered or reds on arrival. The remainder -- a fourth kind of file with no marker in either name or head -- is a language custody ruling. **BOOKED.**
