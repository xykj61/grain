*Three rules this tree had already written, and three walls that reached past the case.* A pin's own
directive, overwritten by a rewrite aimed at the line beneath it, in a list no instrument reads. A
process-signalling clause that named a spelling, so the hand carrying it walked around the wall and
took a peer's pass with a different one. And a report card whose Meter branch refused a program by
name while its register ceiling admitted the same program through a side door.

The shape they share: **a rule is enforced where it is stated, and obeyed where it is read.** Each
of these three was stated correctly and enforced somewhere the failing case never passed through --
a guard reading a nib by its key rather than by its neighbours, a scan reading tracked sources
rather than a session prompt, a branch reading a flag rather than a threshold. None of the three
firings broke a rule that was missing; each broke a rule whose wall stood one step away from where
the hand actually was.

The rows stand here exactly as they were written. They leave the living pin so the pin stays the
length a reader will actually read; the lessons travel forward in the guards those rounds built, and
the rows themselves stay one click away.
