report card read a line's first character and skipped a marker ALONE, where Markdown asks for a

benediction, which is how `radiant-wishes-ending` closes an earned page, were both thrown away as
bullets. The second half had stood unnamed in any row.

cure the row itself proposed was refused by measuring it.** Adopting the register scan's line rules
fixes both blindnesses and admits a third kind -- the `**Key:** value` header line, carrying no
sentence -- at 232 lines against 155, so the obvious repair would have let in more noise than
signal. A question with no cost attached is answered by whoever feels strongest; this one was
answered by a number.
