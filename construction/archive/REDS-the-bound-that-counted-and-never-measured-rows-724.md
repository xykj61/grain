**REDS %724 (`20260912.003734`) -- a weave bounds how many lines it holds and never how large their positions may grow, so the next rise of the counter is an integer overflow.** *What went wrong:* `max_weave_lines` in `mantra/src/weave.rye` is `1 << 20` and bounds a COUNT. A position and a run are each a `u32` carried in a record read off disk, and nothing read their VALUE. So a `mantra-weave-v1` record of one row at position 4,294,967,295 passed the length check, passed the rising check, passed the generation check, and met `next_pos = row.pos + 1` as a panic -- proven on metal in a pen, **exit 134 at `weave.rye:592`**. The same fault stands one function over: a `mantra-weave-v2` record whose header counters sat at the ceiling restored cleanly, and one insert then met `self.next_pos += 1` -- **exit 134 at `weave.rye:1063`**. *What caught it:* the air rota, row 1 -- the row that feels -- running its own threshold test, *a claim of a boundary is tested by pressing on it; if the hand passes through, the boundary was a wish.* The hand went looking for the fence around `max_weave_lines` and found a fence around one of the two things that number governs. *What it taught:* **this module names its refusals beautifully and had named the wrong unit.** Every other malformed blob is a refusal that speaks -- `V1PositionsDoNotRise`, `V2IdentityRepeated`, `V2CounterBelowLine` -- and these two were the same class of blob at the same edge, aborting the process instead. Fourth firing of *a bound stated in one unit bounds nothing in another* in this room (`%678`, `%688`, `%689`); the unit here is the number a line is named by rather than the count of lines. *The ceiling is derived rather than invented:* a position rises once per line, a run once per inserting edit, and `merge` takes the MAXIMUM of two counters rather than their sum -- so a weave inside the line bound is inside the counter bound by construction, and the number was already true and simply unwritten. *Repaired:* one named error `CounterPastCeiling` and three edge checks -- `from_v1` refusing a row at or above the ceiling before the counter rises off it, `from_v2` refusing either header counter above it, and `apply` refusing an insert whose positions would pass it, written as a SUBTRACTION rather than a sum so the arithmetic is a real number where the sum is what overflows. Two entry asserts state the invariant the subtraction rests on. *The case the line bound cannot see, and why the two checks stand apart:* `from_v1` accepts positions that RISE without being dense, so a record of two rows at 0 and `max_weave_lines - 1` lifts to a weave holding **two lines** whose counter has spent to the ceiling -- the count passes at two against a bound of a million and the counter refuses. *Proven:* `weave_v1_lift_witness` **eleven claims, six refusals** (the ceiling row, and the sparse lift asserted at both halves -- it stands, and the insert on it refuses); `weave_v2_witness` **twelve claims, six refusals** (both header counters apart, **and a counter standing exactly at `max_weave_lines` welcomed**, since a bound proven only in the refusing direction cannot be told from one that refuses everything). **Three mutations planted and each bit**, all exit 134: `from_v1`'s check to `if (false)`, `apply`'s position guard to `if (false)`, `from_v2`'s position-counter check to `if (false)`. Fifteen mantra witnesses GREEN, `tame_style_check` and `width-check` GREEN. *Not reached:* the CLI's own disk path was already guarded -- `store.rye` recomputes each blob's SHA3-256 on read -- so what this reaches is the module's published edge and the door a peer's weave arrives through, which is the door `merge` exists to open. The run counter takes the same ceiling, exact for positions and generous for runs. **BOOKED.**
