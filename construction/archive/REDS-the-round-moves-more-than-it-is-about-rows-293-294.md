the round is about, and a round moves more than it is about.*

so `equinox_e123_living_pin_guard` went unrun and a 24,676-byte page shipped against the 24,576 its

broke was one the round had touched without being *about*.

whose diff touches a path under `living_pin_max_bytes` runs that guard before it sends, and a
cross-history adoption reads the tree object rather than the blob. The rows themselves stay one
click away.
