Two rows, folded `20260916.200508` to keep the living pin under the byte bound its own header
declares. They arrived from two lanes on one day and they teach one thing: **a reference in this
tree carries two promises, and the tree had built an instrument for exactly one of them.**

`%777` is the citation half. A compass station names a path and a SECTION, and every guard in the
tree reads the path -- so three stations sent a reader to headings a molt had renamed four days
earlier while every link on the line opened green.

`%779` is the comment half. A pen's generator promised reproducibility and variety in one word, and
the reproducibility half was true the whole time; the half nobody could check was the one that had
quietly gone. A comment is where a promise goes when no instrument will take it.

What they taught together outlives either repair: **the unguarded half of a promise is the half
that reads perfect forever**, because nothing is left to disagree with it.

**REDS %779 (`20260916.112900`) -- a pen's generator kept its promise of reproducibility perfectly while its promise of variety had quietly gone, and the word *deterministic* carried both.** *What went wrong:* `tools/fixtures/t/torus_fold_control.sh` planted its population from `seed = (seed * 1103515245 + 12345) % 2147483648`, evaluated by awk. awk carries every number as an IEEE-754 double, exact to `2^53`; that line's largest intermediate is `1103515245 * 2147483647` = 2.37e18, **263 times past the exact range**, so every step rounds. Measured on metal `20260916` on this pier, gawk 5.4.1: the rounded map has a tail of **3,253 draws** and then a cycle of **10,466** -- **13,719 states in all** against the exact generator's `2^31` -- and the byte the pen consumes, `int(seed / 65536) % 256`, differed from the exact stream in **4,079 of 4,096 draws**. The pen draws 2,048 values, so it stood inside the tail, and the margin was one growth of the plant wide. A second site, the discarded spin loop in `tools/fixtures/w/workload_trial_scan.sh`, spelled the same arithmetic where nothing consumed the result. *What caught it:* a fire rota lap reading for what a comment asserts that no instrument checks. The comment beside that line said *the pen must plant the same population on every host*, and **it was true the whole time** -- IEEE-754 specifies multiply, add and `fmod` exactly, each rounding once by a rule every conforming implementation shares, so the rounded stream is perfectly reproducible on any awk carrying doubles. The author's reasoning that led to writing a generator by hand rather than reaching for `rand()`, whose seeding differs between implementations, was sound. The fault entered one layer below the question anybody was asking. *What it taught:* **a generator makes two promises, and this tree had a word for one of them.** Reproducibility is *two runs agree*, and a rounded map passes that check on every host, every time, forever. Variety is *the sequence visits enough distinct states that the population means what the leg says it means*, and reading it takes a COUNT of states rather than a COMPARISON of runs. Two readings, two instruments, and the tree had written a comment where the second instrument should stand -- the `single-stranded` shape, one word carrying two claims, so satisfying either reads as satisfying both. *What landed:* both sites moved to MINSTD, `48271 % 2147483647`, whose product is 1.04e14 and which matches the spelling `aurora_placement_scan.sh` and `topology_growth_scan.sh` already use; the spin loop's floor was measured across the change and the two orderings disagreed on sign, so the difference sits inside this pier's own spread. `tools/fixtures/a/awk_lcg_exact_scan.sh` sorts every tracked awk multiply-then-mod site into three classes and gates one: a **fed-back** site, where the variable assigned is the variable multiplied, is bounded by its own modulus, so the largest intermediate follows from the line alone and the reading stays pure arithmetic -- held at **zero** past `2^53 / (modulus - 1)`, a wall rather than a ratchet, since the class was two sites wide and both were repaired on the seating lap. An **index hash** -- `h = (i * 2654435761) % 4294967296` -- is bounded by the range of `i`, which a scanner cannot know; **17** stand in the tree, reported and gated by nothing, and printing that number is the point, since a reader there cannot otherwise tell an empty blind spot from a large one. *And the paper's own figures were read against the instrument rather than trusted:* the draft claimed **five** exact sites where the repaired tree holds **seven**, and claimed every site stood clear of the borderline band *by a factor of two or more* when the two `key_trade_control.sh` sites stand at **1.260x** of headroom, using 79 percent of the multiplier their modulus allows -- the one place in the tree where an ordinary edit crosses the line. Both are corrected and the tightest site is now named in the scan's own header, so a zero cannot read as slack. *What this does not reach:* cross-implementation agreement, asserted from the IEEE specification rather than measured, since this pier carries one awk and an x87 80-bit intermediate would round differently; and whether a generator inside the range is a GOOD generator, which MINSTD's known lattice structure says it is not, for anything but planting a pen. *And the wall's own first red was its control:* the scan reads every tracked `.sh` and `.rish` source, the control is one, and proving a refusal requires containing the thing refused -- so staging the control took the live reading from `overflowing=0 exact=7 unread=17` to `overflowing=5 exact=8 unread=18` and reddened the wall on five generators that exist only to be refused inside a pen. **The author had solved this exactly one class over:** the scan reads past a `#` line because its own header spells three generators to teach the rule and would otherwise count itself, and nobody asked the same question about the control, whose plants are code rather than prose.
