# LINENGROW ITINERARY -- valuable data with a visible receipt

**Stamp:** `20260912.142909` (EDT)
**Status:** Living product itinerary -- fleet paused
**Style:** Bhakta with Radiant warmth; Gauge at Meter
**Design system:** [`../active-designing/20260912-142909_the-linengrow-receipt-cloth-design-system.md`](../active-designing/20260912-142909_the-linengrow-receipt-cloth-design-system.md)

Linengrow lets a person offer a small data product on terms they understand, see each permitted use, receive a signed statement of value, and carry the complete record away. The first wholes use fake identities and simulated value. Money, real personal data, and public deployment remain custody gates.

## Growth ladder

Each milestone receives its real one-clock stamp when it lands. The name stays independent of its place in the sequence.

### The receipt you can read

**Simple:** one local sample data product, one consent statement, one value amount, one expiration.

**Lovable:** a warm ASCII receipt card shows what, who, why, value, and until when in one view.

**Complete:** sign the offer fact, append it through Mantra, bound it through Tally, project it, render it through Brushstroke and Skate, verify it, and refuse an incomplete receipt.

**Modules:** Kyri, Mantra, Tally, Brix, Brushstroke, Skate, Linengrow.

### The consent you can change

**Simple:** one holder grants and revokes one purpose-bound use.

**Lovable:** the consent rail makes control feel present; the expiration and revoke action remain visible.

**Complete:** Caravan checks the capability, Pond encloses the app, Mantra appends grant and revoke facts, and the view proves a revoked use refuses while history remains whole.

**Modules:** Caravan, Pond, Mantra, Tally, Brushstroke, Skate.

### The value you can follow

**Simple:** one permitted use creates one use receipt and one promised return.

**Lovable:** the person sees value move without exposing the private data itself.

**Complete:** Comlink seals the use receipt, Dimeroll recognizes the corresponding value event, and both surfaces reconcile to the same signed facts.

**Modules:** Comlink, Amphora, Dimeroll, Mantra, Tally, Brushstroke, Skate.

### The offer another person can find

**Simple:** one data product listing with purpose, price basis, consent terms, and expiration.

**Lovable:** Mandi presents an honest offer rather than an attention trap.

**Complete:** list, inspect, accept with fake identities, produce a receipt, and preserve an expired listing as testimony.

**Modules:** Mandi, Caravan, Comlink, Mantra, Linengrow, Dimeroll.

### The bundle you can carry

**Simple:** one export containing the holder's offers, consents, uses, returns, and receipts.

**Lovable:** leaving is a first-class action rather than an account closure ritual.

**Complete:** Amphora seals the bundle, Granary addresses it, import rebuilds the same projection, and a digest witness compares meaning.

**Modules:** Amphora, Granary, Kyri, Mantra, Tally, Brushstroke, Skate.

### The return you can settle

**Simple:** one earned return tied to one use receipt.

**Lovable:** value arrives with its reason and remains understandable later.

**Complete:** simulated settlement reconciles first. MUR or an outside payment rail enters only after legal and custody gates open.

**Modules:** Dimeroll, MUR, Comlink, Mantra, Tally.

## Product walls

- The mechanism stays visible; personal data stays private by default.
- Every valuation names its basis, unit, date, source, and party responsible for it.
- A price is an offer, a use is a fact, a return is an obligation or settlement, and each keeps its own record type.
- Lantern may explain or classify only data the consent fact admits.
- Mycelium waits until several independent holders need shared order.
- A milestone ships only when its narrow loop is Simple, Lovable, and Complete.

## First aligned move

Build **The receipt you can read** from a tiny synthetic dataset. Its witness proves the same meaning across the signed fact, Mantra replay, Linengrow projection, Brushstroke frame, Skate snapshot, and Dimeroll intake.
