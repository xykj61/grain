# DIMEROLL ITINERARY -- books for receipt-shaped value

Dimeroll turns Linengrow's signed offer, consent, use, return, expiration, and correction facts into books a steward can understand. It records and reports value. It holds no money, generates no real keys, and opens no payment rail.

## Growth ladder

Each completed whole receives its live stamp and name when it lands.

### The receipt the books can recognize

**Simple:** one verified Linengrow value receipt enters one entity's journal.

**Lovable:** the receipt card and journal line share language, amount, source, and status.

**Complete:** verify, classify, append, fold, and refuse duplicates or missing deciding fields without changing the prior books.

**Modules:** Linengrow, Dimeroll, Kyri, Mantra, Tally, Amphora.

### The promise kept apart from payment

**Simple:** one promised return becomes an obligation while cash remains untouched.

**Lovable:** the steward can see what is owed and why.

**Complete:** recognize the obligation, link it to its use receipt, age it to expiration, and close it through a simulated settlement or correction.

**Modules:** Dimeroll, Mantra, Tally, Caravan.

### The valuable data statement

**Simple:** one period reports offers, permitted uses, recognized value, open obligations, and expired consent.

**Lovable:** a calm Skate statement answers the first questions without accounting jargon.

**Complete:** every total drills down to signed facts, the trial balance closes, and the statement regenerates identically from replay.

**Modules:** Dimeroll, Brushstroke, Skate, Mantra, Tally.

### The correction that keeps the story

**Simple:** one mistaken valuation receives one superseding fact and adjusting entry.

**Lovable:** the reader sees what changed while the elder receipt remains reachable.

**Complete:** correction, replay, report, and receipt views agree; deletion and silent overwrite remain unavailable.

**Modules:** Dimeroll, Mantra, Amphora, Cellar, Brushstroke, Skate.

### The books you can carry

**Simple:** one entity and one closed period travel as a sealed bundle.

**Lovable:** a steward can leave one machine and reopen the same books on another.

**Complete:** export, authenticate, import, replay, compare statements, and refuse unknown versions.

**Modules:** Amphora, Granary, Comlink, Dimeroll, Mantra, Tally.

### The two entities that stay apart

**Simple:** Linengrow PBC and Siya Fund keep distinct charts, journals, periods, and receipts.

**Lovable:** the steward changes rooms without losing the sense of where they stand.

**Complete:** an inter-entity fact appears explicitly on both sides, balances independently, and never becomes a shared silent balance.

**Modules:** Dimeroll, Caravan, Comlink, Mantra, Tally, Brushstroke, Skate.

## Product walls

- Offer value, recognized value, obligation, and settled value remain distinct.
- Every amount keeps unit, basis, source receipt, entity, period, and recognition rule.
- Tally bounds entries, receipt bytes, memo bytes, periods, exceptions, and view rows before a fold begins.
- Mantra holds facts; Dimeroll projects books; Brushstroke describes reports; Skate renders them.
- Real payroll, disbursement, custody, tax filing, and investment action remain human and licensed gates.

## First aligned move

Build **The receipt the books can recognize** against the same synthetic receipt used by Linengrow. One dual-product witness proves Linengrow's value meaning and Dimeroll's accounting meaning correspond without becoming one braided type.
