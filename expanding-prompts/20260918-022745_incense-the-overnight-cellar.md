## What this page is, and how the three fit

Three files steer one overnight lap, and each carries one job.

The **outer prompt** is the seat's lane stanza. The loop prepends the baton to it and hands both to
the agent. It names the inner prompt once and stays unchanged all night.

The **inner prompt** is what the loop lives inside: its rite, its two laws, its state, and its
ordered next. **The loop updates its `state` and `next` sections itself**, lap by lap, which is the
recursion -- each lap leaves the next one better directed.

**This page** is the plan behind them, read once at the first lap. It holds the reasons, so the
inner prompt can stay short enough to act on.

## Why tonight is different from last night

Last night's work was measured and landed, and much of its prose was written first and made
affirmative second. Several pages were swept to pass after they were drafted. The same held for
code: a style check run at the end of a lap rather than kept in mind from its first line.

**Tonight writes both habits in from the start.** The inner prompt states them as laws with the one
command that measures each, so a lap drafts affirmatively and codes to TAME in the first pass. The
cost of a habit built in is one sentence of attention; the cost of a habit repaired afterward is a
lap.

## The captain's reading of the fleet

**Command, measured before issued.** Keaton granted full trust to direct every itinerary. The wise
use of that trust is to point each ship at what its own lane already owns, rather than moving work
between lanes in the dark.

**The fusion build belongs to bakery, and it is the number-one fleet priority.** Its itinerary names
content-keyed compilation, the moved proof closure, and the fusion toward Caravan's scheduler. Its
content-keyed receipt has **landed and reads green** --
[`../tools/r/ryekey_witness.rish`](../tools/r/ryekey_witness.rish) at 37 legs, a warm receipt
falling from about 230 ms to 120. **Incense supports that lane with research rather than building
beside it**, because two ships building one instrument is the collision the baton was written
after.

**What that research is, precisely.** The receipt caches a build *inside one tree*. Eight trees on
this pier compile the same modules, so a module proven once is compiled up to eight times. The open
question is a content-addressed store the eight could **share between rounds and across trees**, so
a build proven once serves all of them.

**And the wall it meets, named up front.** Each ship runs inside an enclosure that binds exactly one
tree ([`../.claude/rules/the-baton.md`](../.claude/rules/the-baton.md), *a tree carries its own
keys*). A shared store outside every tree is invisible from inside each jail. So the research
answers a real design question -- where a shared cache may live, and what it costs the isolation
that makes each ship safe -- rather than assuming the answer.

## The product lane, where no ruling is owed

The milestone is *the receipt you can read*. Its contract is sound on every claim it makes, and two
of its four public types stand in code while two stand at zero
([`../active-designing/20260918-000154_three-numbers-and-a-name.md`](../active-designing/20260918-000154_three-numbers-and-a-name.md)).

**The Dimeroll intake is buildable tonight.** Its *name* waits on Keaton; its *shape* is written in
the contract already -- an intake that reports `recognition_status=unrecognized-offer` and
`journal_entry_count=0`, because an offer is evidence while no use or settlement has happened. A lap
may build that shape under a working name, witness first, TAME from line one, holding the braid
guard green -- and rename on Keaton's word, which the guard follows automatically because it reads
the type from the contract's own table.

## Inspiration from the new shastra room

[`../shastra/README.md`](../shastra/README.md) founded tonight as a siloed study library of the six
Sandarbhas. Their order is the lesson worth carrying: **Tattva** (what is true) comes first, then
**Bhagavat**, **Paramatma**, **Krsna**, **Bhakti**, and **Priti** (love) last -- each resting on the
one before it.

That is the shape of a good lap too. Measure what is true before building on it; build before
claiming it works; prove it works before loving it. The room stays siloed: its structure of
understanding inspires, and none of its text enters code.
