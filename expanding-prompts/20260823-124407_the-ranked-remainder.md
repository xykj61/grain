Across one session Keaton named roughly twenty directions, from a database consensus protocol to a
philosophy essay to a Delaware PBC round. Each is real. Ranked by hand they would sort by whichever
was named last.

**The measurement that changed my mind.** I had been calling **Mycelium** the crux, on the reasoning
that Caravan, Tablecloth, and the world-surface all rest on it. Measured `20260823.124407`:

| Module | Authored `.rye` | Witnesses |
|---|---|---|
| **Caravan** | 110 | 109 |
| **Mycelium** | 98 | 80 |

Both are far past the sketch stage, and Caravan carries near one-to-one witness coverage. So the
crux is **neither of them being written** -- it is that a reader arriving today cannot tell what
either one *does*, and the tools room that holds their proofs is seven times past the bound a

That reordering is the whole value of this page, and it is checkable: if a newcomer can clone the
tree and describe Caravan's job in one sentence within an hour, the ranking below is wrong and
building should resume at the top.

### The shape, decided and measured -- `20260823.130056`

Keaton chose **first sprig letter**, and measuring it produced one amendment and one reassurance.

**The amendment.** A single letter leaves two rooms still over bound: `a` holds **289** files and
`c` holds **330**, against a bound of 256. So the shape is *first sprig letter, with a second
letter only where the first exceeds the bound* -- `tools/al/` at 241, `tools/ca/` at 117,
`tools/cr/` at 107, `tools/co/` at 69. Twenty-one letter rooms and a handful of two-letter ones,
every one under bound.

**Why not group by first word**, which was the obvious alternative: it yields 296 rooms of which
**205 hold one or two files**. That trades one unbrowsable room for two hundred near-empty ones,
which serves a reader worse than the problem does.

**The reassurance, and it is what makes the fold safe.** The `date/YYYYMMDD/` fold works because
the basename carries the key to compute the new path, so a stale reference is *resolved* rather
than rewritten. **A first-letter fold has exactly that property**: `caravan_x.rish` yields `c`
yields `tools/c/caravan_x.rish`, computable from the basename alone, with no index. So
`tools/dated_path_resolve.rish` gains a sibling and the 2,866 files of dated testimony keep every
word they wrote.

## The three rules this session paid for, in blood

1. **Grep a name whole-word before seating it**, and read the Lexicon and Zig's std. Three rounds
   nearly gave a seated word a second meaning; one of them was `KYRI`.
2. **A sweep meets its own instruments.** Control pens plant stale paths on purpose, and five
   rounds broke one. Exclude `*_control.sh` from path sweeps the way censuses already do.
3. **A rename must ask which occurrences mean the thing being renamed.** Four renames, four
   different answers -- the seated word survived, then a dated filename did, then the word itself
   moved.

## What would show this ranking wrong

- A newcomer describing Caravan's job in one sentence within an hour of cloning would show
  legibility is already sufficient, and building should resume at the top.
- A tools fold that lands and changes nobody's ability to find a witness would show the bound was
  a tidiness metric rather than a reader metric.
- Someone outside this tree adopting a module would move that module's documentation above the
  fold, since a real user outranks a hypothetical one.
