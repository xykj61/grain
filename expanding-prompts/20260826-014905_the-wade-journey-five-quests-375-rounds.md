rounds of witnessed work, with every look decision resting at DJINN's hand.

**The clock.** This plan counts in the panchanga units seated on Keaton's word, 2026-08-26:
one orbit is 15 rounds, one full lap of the 5x3 council rota, so each orbit returns the whole
canon to awareness exactly once. A quest is 5 orbits (75 rounds); a journey is 5 quests (375
rounds). Orbit n covers rounds (n-1) x 15 + 1 through n x 15. The journey is the map; the
rota is the clock.

design, and a round reaching a look decision records the question and stops at the gate.

## What would falsify the pacing, and how orbits re-plan

The arithmetic is fixed -- 15 rounds per orbit, 75 per quest, 375 for the journey -- and the
content is the movable part. Three observations would falsify this pacing. First, an orbit
whose definition of done slips past one further orbit: the arc was two orbits of work
wearing one name. Second, a gate staying closed past its quest -- DJINN choosing another
direction at gate %6, or the consent gate holding through quest four -- which converts
dependent orbits to exemplar or study work rather than stalling them. Third, a witness
count behind the orbit count for two consecutive orbits: a plan outrunning its proofs is
narrative. Re-planning happens at orbit boundaries, every 15 rounds, never mid-orbit: a
redraw rewrites the remaining entries in a fresh dated mutant while the finished entries
keep every word. The plan is the map; the rota is the clock; and when they disagree, the
clock keeps ticking and the map gets redrawn.
