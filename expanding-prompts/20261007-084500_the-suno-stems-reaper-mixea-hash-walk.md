## Title law, measured

The title string, without the extension, is at most 100 characters:

```
{name} {sha3-256 prefix} (feat. reyklah2 & vegankeatonsiya) - reyklah2
```

`name` is the short track word (`istanbul`, `children`, `delhi`). The prefix is however many hex characters fit after the name, the two spaces, and the feat clause (49 characters). For an eight-letter name the prefix is 41 hex characters of the SHA3-256 of that file. The full 64-character digest stays in the session log and in this tutorial so a later hand can verify.

Derived m4a, mp3, and mp4 that come from a wav keep that wav's title stem. A Mixea mp3 that is its own file keeps its own digest.
