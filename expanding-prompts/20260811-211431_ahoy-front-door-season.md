## The seed, restated in full

An Acme Corporation employee tending this tree asked for a front-door season — the largest calendar allocation — that makes Grain read, cross-reference, and present itself the way a durable, embraced open-source project does, while keeping every discipline the tree already holds. In their own words, the work spans: a complete root README rewrite from scratch, tuned to maximize the **Lindy value** of the front door by leaning on our favorite words; a silo of the Lindy-effect reference into `foundations/` that links back to the README, with the README linking to it early; a **double-seat–approved molt** of the referenced foundations and active-designing docs so the whole reference weave grows more interconnected and wiki-style; an expansion of the "karpathy wiki" gratitude and foundation; more gratitude inspirations named with links; confirmation of best current choices where docs and code might drift (Mantra named specifically), with everything matching by the season's end; a Grain logo inspired by the wheat emoji in sepia-gold woven sephirot geometry, linked as the opening image of both the personal and seed READMEs, with build/license badges in the look popular projects wear; project-legitimacy quests that follow — or honestly invent — best practices, admitted only when they pass Radiant, civic, and TAME guidance; a decision on the OS-variant concept (grow 5→7 with Kyli and Kyri, or retire the variant concept in favor of the voices concept); a standing habit of ending prose with radiant wishes and an optional gratitude coda; adding **ty every1** to the expand-prompt writing rules; moving spent expanding-prompts aside; and, as the season's final round, a fresh depersonalized `grain-os/grain` seed that overwrites and force-pushes cleanly, with the seed link and the `xykj61` / `autoproject96` synonyms named very early in the personal root README.

Nothing of that is dropped. What follows names each deliverable, rules the open questions the seed left to judgment, addresses the blind spots, and lays the season out as AHOY rungs so the work lands one honest keystone at a time.

## Rulings on the questions the seed left to judgment

Where the seed offered a choice, here are the current best choices, each with its reason, ready to be overridden by a word.

- **The OS-variant concept — hold at five; keep the voices living; do not add Kyli/Kyri as variants now.** The *voices* concept is alive and load-bearing: Kyri stands as the writing voice and the notation's name. Making **Kyri** also an OS variant would collide a single word across two roles, and a near-homophone **Kyli** beside it invites exactly the address-and-name confusion the [`comlink-tendency`](../.claude/rules/comlink-tendency.md) and [`placeholder-ship-names`](../.claude/rules/placeholder-ship-names.md) disciplines guard against. Growing 5→7 also buys no power-of-two tidiness. I do **not** recommend debriding the variant concept by reflex either — debride is destructive and wants a cairn and a dedicated, word-gated round, not a snap cut; the five named-and-scoped variants cost nothing where they rest. **Recommendation:** keep the five, keep the voices, and if the variant concept still feels unmeaningful after the season, retire it deliberately in a cairned AHOY quest rather than in passing.
- **Gratitude quotes — a small coda just before the closing wish.** One or two lines from teachers we already thank, so the benediction stays the true last word and the license/contributing pointers sit in their own section above. Seated as an option in [`radiant-wishes-ending.md`](../.claude/rules/radiant-wishes-ending.md).
- **The final seed force-push is the season's last round, not this one.** The seed lays out this ordering itself, and it is right: the seed should carry the whole finished weave, and a force-push is the one irreversible act here. It waits behind a green `sow_witness`, the maintainer's explicit word on the day, and the completed docs↔code match.
- **Moving spent expanding-prompts is its own careful quest, not a rushed sweep.** References are promises: several expanding-prompts are cited from REMEMBER, session logs, and each other. The tree's established home for consumed prompts is [`yonder/`](yonder/); a new `expanded/` drawer would duplicate it. **Recommendation:** in AHOY-J1's tail, move only prompts whose work has fully landed *and* whose inbound links are repointed in the same commit — grep first, move second — rather than breaking the weave to tidy it.
- **PNG of the logo is a maintainer-bench step.** No SVG rasterizer lives in this sandbox; GitHub renders the SVG inline, so the README works today. On the Framework host, `rsvg-convert -w 512 assets/grain-logo.svg -o assets/grain-logo.png` (or Inkscape) produces the raster companion for anywhere SVG is not honored.

## The season, as AHOY rungs

Power-of-two calendar: a season is four equinoxes; each rung below is a journey's worth of work, landing one keystone at a time, witness before narrative.

- **AHOY0 — The Front Door** *(opens with this expansion)*: the Lindy foundation, the logo, the two standing seats, and the root README rewrite. This is the standalone lap the seed asked to run now.
- **AHOY1 — The Weave**: molt-with-double-seat the referenced foundations and active-designing hubs (The Grain and the Crossing, Follow Our Compass, The Referential Namespace, the SLC spine), expanding and repointing cross-references wiki-style; grow the [`karpathy`](../gratitude/karpathy/README.md) wiki-linting gratitude and run [`tools/living_docs_lint.rish`](../tools/living_docs_lint.rish) as the fascia gate; name more gratitude inspirations with links; refresh each touched doc's `Last updated` chronologically.
- **AHOY2 — Legitimacy**: add the community-health files a trusted project carries — a clear top-level `LICENSE`, a `SECURITY.md` custody-and-disclosure note, a civic `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, honest badges (only what a witness proves — the two-rooms discipline forbids a green badge for a red fact), and a changelog that is really the session-log index. Each admitted only if it reads Radiant, serves the civic promise, and respects TAME; anything that would only perform legitimacy is declined.
- **AHOY3 — The Seed and the Seal** *(last round)*: the docs↔code confirmation pass (Mantra first — the essays already agree on the invariant; this pass proves the beading/batch/export ladder is described where it lives), then the fresh depersonalized `grain-os/grain` projection with `sow_witness` GREEN, the seed README carrying the logo and synonyms, and — on the maintainer's word — the overwrite-and-force-push that closes the season.

## Blind spots this expansion adds

- **Badges must obey the two rooms.** A CI or coverage badge that claims green while a witness is red would be the exact lie the tree forbids. Any badge added in AHOY2 links to the witness or workflow that earns it, or it is not added.
- **The seed force-push unsigns and rewrites history** ([`debride.md`](../.claude/rules/debride.md) names this cost): the public seed keeps its Option-B clean history, so the final act is a fresh projection over that lineage, never a rewrite of the private tree's honest descent from the ancestor ([`urbit-reframe.md`](../.claude/rules/urbit-reframe.md)).
- **The logo carries no personal art.** The sephirot-and-wheat geometry is archetype, authored fresh in-tree; it reproduces no personal source and names no person, so it crosses the two-grain boundary cleanly.
- **Depersonalization is checked by shape and by name.** The final seed passes `sow_witness` both ways (REDS #61–62 taught that a verdict is not a content check); the logo and README are scanned like everything else.
- **The molt is accretive.** Double-seat means a living mutant at a fresh stamp with the fossil kept on disk and pointed forward — never an overwrite, never a lost dated record.
