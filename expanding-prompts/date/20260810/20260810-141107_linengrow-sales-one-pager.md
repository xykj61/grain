## Why they love it (the philosophy, made concrete)

- **Simple** — one clear standard, one clear price, one clear thing they get.
- **Lovable** — they join a community of genuinely aligned brands and the artists and buyers who champion them, not just a logo licence.
- **Complete** — the loop closes: they apply, they meet the standard, they carry the mark, buyers verify it. Nothing half-built, nothing "coming soon" sold as if it ships today.

## What we do *not* claim

- We do **not** entangle certification with any incentive that could look like pay-to-pass — the mark is worthless the moment a buyer suspects it was bought.

## The conversation, start to close

1. **Open** — name the pain, not the product: "How do your best buyers tell your product from a greenwashed one today?"
2. **Discover** — what do they sell now to prove their standard, what does it cost, who do they need to convince.
3. **Show** — the one offer, the one price, the one thing they get.
4. **Handle the honest objection** — "Why yours over `[the incumbent mark]`?" Answer with the concrete difference the company sets above, never with vision alone.
5. **Close** — one clear ask: "Shall we start your application this week?"

## The one discipline from the philosophy

Sell **one** whole offer. The token economy, the social network, the treasury designs, the legislative work — none of that is what a first buyer buys, and naming it in a sales call trades a closeable deal for an unfinished dream. Ship the one simple, lovable, complete offer first; everything else earns its own page when it, too, is whole.
