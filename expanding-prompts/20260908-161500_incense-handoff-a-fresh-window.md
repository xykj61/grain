## Three habits this session earned the hard way

**Wait on the process, never on a string.** An `until grep -q run_verdict` fired against a growing
file and produced four phantom reds. Wait for the process to exit.

**Ask whether a rebase is standing before any amend.** `[ -d .git/rebase-merge ] || [ -d
.git/rebase-apply ]` answers in one test. An amend mid-rebase rewrites a peer's commit.

**Read a refusal whole.** The commit hook prints the vocabulary it counted and the rule it applies;
reading its output through `tail -2` hides both, and three refusals in a row came from that habit
rather than from the message.
