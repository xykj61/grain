## The habits this session paid for

- **Read the tree's own text before inventing.** A primitive was proposed, approved, and already
  present. A bound was proposed and already ruled the wrong instrument. A room was offered and
  `design-rooms.md` had already decided it.
- **Ask what population a number covers.** Five readings this session were *true* about something
  other than what their reader believed: a map capped at sixty of 9,767; orphans counted against a
  roster blind to the walk; `cross_type=0` over two types nobody has written; a three-guard
  extrapolation; a verdict called at nine readings of eleven.
- **Predict against the number the guard GATES**, never the one printed beside it. `refs_lost` is
  diagnosis; the gate is the repairable class.
- **A site is not a count until its loop is measured.** Three identical lines removed 36 of 515
  spawns; the rest sat in a loop running twenty times more often.
- **Compare against a held-still input.** Stash, run, unstash, run. A baseline captured earlier
  measures the change plus everything else that moved.
- **Never wait on a command-line pattern** (`%824`). `until ! pgrep -f "<name>"` matches the waiting
  shell's own line and waits forever, silently. Wait on a content predicate, or ask
  `sh tools/f/fleet_call.sh --pattern <substring>`, which refuses the caller by name.
