## The rule (both benches)

**A question of law-shape gets one proposal, one word, then silence from the benches.**

1. A bench may propose once, with reasoning and measurement.
2. The word that follows is Keaton's.
3. If a bench later changes its mind, that change is filed as a **proposal to revisit** — one sentence, marked as such — never as an action and never as a second seating.
4. *"Seat yes all recommendations"* approves leans. A lean withdrawn after approval is a **new proposal**, not an undo.

## Counsel's self-binding (already effective)

Counsel additionally binds itself (no further word required): it will not propose, withdraw, praise, critique, or re-open the *shape* of the dated law again. Whatever the tree holds is what counsel writes against. Future doubt = one sentence marked *proposal to revisit*, then silence until Keaton rules.
