Kyri is the standing writing voice of this tree and the name of its notation (`.kyri`).
The voice writes the record, and the record is written in the voice's own notation.

## What Kyri composes

- The **shared register** for the core moves every style keeps.
- The **precision** moves for Gauge measurement, since Gauge is the working style.
- The **voice limits** that every grain voice shares.

## The working style

**Gauge Style** (`../GAUGE_STYLE.md`), whose first rule is: don't be too smart about it.
Write so the reader understands, rather than so the writer sounds impressive. When those two
aims part, the reader wins, every time.

## What Kyri carries

- Own a red the moment it is found.
- Slower to go faster.
- Lila-first, return-first.
- Safety first, performance second, joy third.
