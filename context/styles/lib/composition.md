# Composition -- which shared pieces each kind of page uses, and what it states alone

**Status:** Living
**Room:** checkable

The single statement of how voices, styles, and roles compose from the shared library.
Every kind of page points here and does not restate it. Referenced from the clean-slate
tree's `context/lib/composition.md` (`grain-ww/20261015-grain`), adapted for petrichor's
existing structure.

## The shared pieces

- **The header shape:** [`page-header.md`](page-header.md). Stamp, Status, Room fields.
- **The section names:** [`section-names.md`](section-names.md). Role in the tree, Register,
  Limits, Outcome, Open tasks.
- **The core register:** [`shared-register.md`](shared-register.md). Lead with what is,
  active voice, varied rhythm, coined terms named on first use.
- **The precision moves:** [`precision.md`](precision.md). Gauge measurement: bound claims,
  unit-date-source, what holds it still.
- **The voice limits:** [`voice-limits.md`](voice-limits.md). What every voice keeps out:
  source fidelity, G-friendly, ASCII-first.
- **The devotional register:** [`devotional-register.md`](devotional-register.md). Sincerity,
  practice named, passed studies, sacred text rules.

## Voices

A voice composes its header from the shared header shape and names which library pages it
draws from. It states alone: its register (the prose it writes in), and what it carries (the
four or five commitments that are its own). Its limits come from [`voice-limits.md`](voice-limits.md),
not from its own text.

Petrichor's standing voice is [`../../voices/kyri.md`](../../voices/kyri.md), which composes
from shared-register, precision, and voice-limits.

## Styles

A style composes its header from the shared header shape. It states alone: the moves that
are particular to its register (Twilight's aim-then-dissolve, Radha's love-first), and the
settings that belong to it (Gauge's Door, Field, Meter). Its shared moves come from the
library pages it names, not from its own text.

## Roles

A role composes its header and section names from the shared pieces. It states alone: what
the job is, its limits particular to the job, and its outcome. Petrichor has no `context/roles/`
room yet; this section is the standard for when one opens.

## What is not composed

- Dated testimony keeps the header and the words it was written with.
- The full guides (`context/GAUGE_STYLE.md`, `context/RADIANT_STYLE.md`, and peers) remain the
  canonical references for their own rich history. The library is the single source for the
  shared moves; the guides are the full story, each pointing at the library for what they share.
- Rule pages (`.claude/rules/`) keep their own shape, which serves a different reader (an agent
  at lap open). This composition rule governs `context/` pages.
