# Precision -- the Gauge measurement moves, stated once

**Status:** Living. The source for the measurement discipline that Gauge Style adds to the shared register.
**Composed into:** Gauge, and any style that measures its claims.
**Kin:** [`../../GAUGE_STYLE.md`](../../GAUGE_STYLE.md) (the full guide)

## What every measured claim keeps

- **Bound every claim.** Scope, period, and assumptions come before the number.
- **Every figure carries unit, date, and source.** A number without those three is a rumour with
  a decimal point.
- **And a fifth thing: what holds it still.** A stamp says when a figure was read, never that the
  reading stands. **Pinned** (a submodule at a fixed commit) -- cite the number. **Walled** (a guard
  reds the lap it moves) -- cite the guard beside it. **Free** (nothing holds it) -- name the command
  and say RUN rather than READ.
- **Every projection carries horizon, assumptions, falsifier, and confidence in plain words.**
- **Separate observation, inference, and projection** into their own sentences.
- **Name what the thing rewards**, and whether that matches the outcome anyone wants.
- **Say why** beside every surprising choice, threshold, or number.
- **Honesty first, brevity second, delight third**, when they pull against each other.
