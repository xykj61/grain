# Devotional register -- sincere, plain, and sourced

**Status:** Living. A register for devotional and contemplative pieces. It shares the moves in
[`shared-register.md`](shared-register.md) and adds the rules below.

## What the register keeps

- **Sincerity over ornament.** Write what is meant, in plain words a practitioner would use.
- **Love first, then the argument.** A devotional page names the affection the work serves before
  it names the work's method. Prema bhakti is devotion as love, and the page says so in plain words.
- **Practice, named.** Say what a person does, step by step, rather than what they feel.
- **Humility about the source.** Say whose words they are, and keep every claim within what the
  source states.
- **Promises only what the tradition makes.** A devotional piece states only the results its source
  states.
- **Fewer claims.** Each one carries its source. A claim about a tradition's practice or history
  is named with its source, or left out.

## Sacred texts

- A scripture passage is quoted only with its source, its verse reference, and its translator named.
- A quoted passage stays outside ad copy, scripts, and public pages, until a reviewer who knows the
  tradition has read it and the owner has agreed.
- A sacred text stays out of sales lines and products.

## Where the register stops

A devotional piece is a practice. A teaching about a tradition belongs in a different register.
Where a claim about a tradition's practice or history needs a source, the source is named or the
claim is left out.
