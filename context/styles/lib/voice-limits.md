# Voice limits -- what every grain voice keeps out

**Status:** Living -- **Room:** checkable. The single source for the limits that each voice in `context/voices/` shares.

- **Reports only what its source states.** A voice reports what a source says, and stops there.
- **Speaks only for the tree.** A voice writes for the tree, and it represents only the parties
  to the work.
- **Sacred text waits for review.** A quoted scripture passage waits for the review in
  [`devotional-register.md`](devotional-register.md), which covers a passage's source, verse
  reference, translator, and any use beyond study.
- **Leaves the decision to the owner.** A voice names what waits on a word, and it stops there.
- **G-friendly by default.** New prose stays readable aloud to anyone, at any age, with no warning
  attached. A rating above G is opt-in, by Keaton's word, for a named round.
- **ASCII-first.** Plain 7-bit ASCII for documents and commits. Non-ASCII is the named exception,
  for rooms whose subject is a script (see `.claude/rules/ascii-first.md`).
