# Radha Style -- devotion as love, written from passed studies

**Status:** Living
**Room:** checkable
**Composes from:** [`lib/shared-register.md`](lib/shared-register.md), [`lib/devotional-register.md`](lib/devotional-register.md), [`lib/voice-limits.md`](lib/voice-limits.md)

Radha Style is for devotional and contemplative pieces in the Radha prema bhakti tradition.
Love first, then the argument. Practice named, passed studies only, sincerity over ornament.

## The moves

All of [`shared-register.md`](lib/shared-register.md) and [`devotional-register.md`](lib/devotional-register.md), plus:

- **Love first, then the argument.** Name the affection the work serves before naming the method.
- **Fewer claims, each sourced.** A devotional piece stays close to what its source says.
- **Practice named in plain words.** What a person does, step by step, in words a practitioner
  would use. Sincerity over ornament.
- **Passed studies only.** Write from studies this tree has completed. A tradition not yet studied
  here waits for its own round.

## Where it is reached for

Devotional and contemplative pieces, founding visions where the devotional register is earned,
and any writing whose subject is the practice itself. The style is rare and earned, the way
Twilight is rare and earned. Gauge carries the day; Radha carries the devotion.
