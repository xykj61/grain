## The one sentence

**Redacting is siloing pointed the other way.** To silo is to receive an idea from the world and set
it down in our own words, so that a particular method becomes a general principle we own. To redact
is to take our *own* particular -- a real portfolio, a real rate, a real parcel -- and set it down as
a general principle **anyone** can use, so the teaching ships while the specifics stay home.

Same operation, opposite direction. Silo protects us from borrowing; redact protects a person from
being published.

## Why this is better than withholding

Today a page carrying real figures has one honest destination: withheld. `template-manifest.bron`
allows a room or it does not, and a room absent from the allowlist never ships. That is a good wall
and it wastes something.

**The specifics are private. The method rarely is.** *When does borrowing against a portfolio beat
selling?* is a question anyone with any portfolio faces, and the answer -- borrowing wins while the
kept return exceeds the loan rate, with deferral adding a small cushion -- belongs to everyone. What
must not ship is whose portfolio, how large, at which rate on which date, and where the parcel sits.

A tree that only knows how to withhold teaches nothing from its own hardest thinking.

## What a redaction keeps and what it lets go

**Let go:** names, entities, addresses and parcels, account and instrument identifiers, exact sums,
exact dates tied to a person, counterparties, and anything that with one search names a household.

**Keep, and this is the whole point:** the shape of the calculation. The variables and what each one
does. The direction of each relationship -- which way the answer moves when a term rises. The
thresholds where a decision flips. The base rates and where a reader finds their own. The refusals:
what the method cannot tell you, and the conditions under which it stops being true.

**Turn a figure into a level.** A specific sum becomes *a portfolio at a scale where a single sale
would realise a material gain*. A specific rate becomes *a benchmark rate, read on the day, cited to
its publisher*. A named year becomes *a horizon long enough for the deferral to matter*. The reader
learns to find their own numbers, which is more useful than being handed ours.

## The multivariable reading

Value creation runs on several terms at once, and the teaching lives in how they compose rather than
in any one of them. A redacted page names the terms and their degrees plainly: **assets** and what
stage they are at, **capital** and what it costs, **opportunity** and how long it stays open,
**network** and how far it reaches, **time** and what it forgives.

Then it shows what each *kind* of calculation can honestly do with them -- a comparison, a
sensitivity, a base rate, a projection with its horizon and falsifier -- and where each stops. A
reader at a different scale, with different terms, can run the same method on their own life.

## The register

Redacted pages are written in **Bhakta**: no assumed background, first principles in order, one new
idea at a time, and one honest sentence about why the work matters -- the household it steadies, the
ecology it serves. Every Gauge discipline still holds. **Accessible means measured too**: a figure
carries unit, date, source, and what holds it still, and a projection carries horizon, assumptions,
falsifier and confidence.

## What the seed would call

The manifest carries three verdicts today -- `scrub`, `template`, `personal`. Redact proposes a
**fourth**: a page marked for redaction ships as its generalisation rather than as itself, and the
private original stays in the field where `xy` and `debrided` already hold it.

**Not built, and named as not built.** The verdict, the tool, and the witness that proves a redacted
page carries no figure traceable to a household are their own lap. What is seated here is the
technique and its boundary, so the lap that builds it has a standard to build against.

## Where it serves

Counsel that reasoned about real money, property decisions, fund shapes, and any page whose value is
its method rather than its numbers.

## What redacting is not

**It is not anonymising.** Removing a name from a page that still says *4.94 acres on a named
highway, under contract in August* has published a parcel. Redaction rewrites at the level of the
idea, and a rewrite is checked by asking whether the page could describe a hundred different
households.

**It is not vagueness.** A redacted page runs *more* exact about method, because the specifics that
carried the argument are gone and the reasoning must carry it alone.

**It is not a second copy to maintain.** The private page is the record. The redaction is a
projection of it, regenerated rather than edited in parallel -- the same discipline the seed already
keeps.

*May the lesson travel further than the ledger, and may the household stay home.*
