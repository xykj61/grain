## What Bhakta is

Bhakta is Radiant Style teaching someone who has never written a line of code, and who deserves the
whole truth anyway. **Everything Radiant asks, Bhakta keeps** -- lead with what *is*, active voice,
`yet` over `but`, a coined term named in plain words on first use. Gauge's first rule governs it
harder than either sibling: **don't be too smart about it.**

The name is the Sanskrit for one who walks the path of devotion -- and a bhakta's whole discipline is
that the path is open to anyone, needing no scholarship to enter. That is exactly the promise this
register makes to a reader.

## The one difference, said plainly

- **Radiant** -- daylight. The default for teaching, reference, and product prose.
- **Twilight** -- dusk. Reached for when the subject is sacred, nocturnal, or contemplative.
- **Bhakta** -- the open door. Reached for **specification, API reference, and user-manual prose**,
  where the reader may have no computing background at all and must still be able to follow every
  step and know why it matters.

A newcomer meets a Bhakta passage and feels *invited and trusted*. A line that leaves a reader
feeling behind has left the register.

## What Bhakta always does

- **Assume no background, and let that stay quiet.** Explain the thing itself; a reader who already
  knows it should still enjoy the sentence.
- **First principles, in order.** Nothing rests on a term introduced later. If a word is needed, it
  is given its plain function the first time it appears -- and given once, not apologised for twice.
- **One new idea at a time**, concrete before abstract. Show the thing, then name it.
- **Say why it matters to a life.** Every description roots back to what the work is *for*: the
  ecology it serves, the household it lightens, the person whose data stays theirs. A specification
  that cannot say why it matters is a specification nobody will finish reading.
- **Respect the reader's time and intelligence.** Short sentences are kindness, and padding costs a reader twice.
- **Encourage by being accurate.** *Here is the step, and here is what you will see when it works*
  serves a reader where *this is easy* fails them the moment it is not.

## The lens -- why the descriptions matter

Bhakta writes technical prose through the lens this tree already holds: **devotion, grace, and the
path with heart**. Concretely, that means a reference page answers a *why* alongside its *what*, and
the why reaches past the machine.

The tree's own subject supplies it. **Biocyclic vegan ecology** -- growing food from plant matter
alone, returning what is taken, harming nothing that can suffer -- is the world this software is
built to serve. So a page about a bounded buffer may say, plainly and once, that the bound exists so
the program can run on a small machine a small farm can afford. A page about identity may say that a
key nobody can revoke is what lets a grower own their own records.

**Said once, and meant.** A reason belongs where it lands; repeated in every section it becomes a
slogan, which is the opposite of what this register is for.

## Where Bhakta holds its line

**Courtesy, in the words themselves.** Bhakta reaches past *simply*, *just*, and *obviously*, each of
which tells a struggling reader they should already be through the thing they are stuck on.

**Exactness is the kindness.** Accessible prose runs *more* precise than expert prose, because a
reader without background fills a gap from nothing. Every gap closes on the page.

**One sentence of why, placed where it belongs.** The lens earns its place by appearing once. A page that preaches has stopped teaching, and a
reason repeated in every section has become a slogan.

**G-friendly, like every register here** ([`../.claude/rules/vocabulary-aroma.md`](../.claude/rules/vocabulary-aroma.md)) --
the standing default, and the one Bhakta has least occasion to leave.

## What Bhakta keeps from Gauge

Every measurement discipline holds: a figure carries unit, date, source, and what holds it still; a
projection carries horizon, assumptions, falsifier and confidence; observation, inference and
projection stay in their own sentences. **Accessible means measured too.** The reader who needs the plainest
words most needs to be able to check what they are told.

*May the door stay open, and may everyone who walks through it be trusted with the whole truth.*
