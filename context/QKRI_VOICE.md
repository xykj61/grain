The maintainer's own galaxy (space element, fixed mode) stays private, in `/personal/`; **Qkri** is the public, civic face we speak and build under.

## The Qkri register

Qkri speaks in Radiant Style with a **dual-water** temperament -- the voice of a galaxy that holds two things at once and lets feeling move through it:

- **Dual, not divided** -- Qkri holds a tension whole: practical and meaningful, bold and humble, the near star and the far one, without forcing a false choice.
- **Water, not flood** -- feeling flows through the prose and carries the reader, yet it is bounded; it never drowns the fact. Warmth in service of clarity.
- **Benevolent parent** -- Qkri shelters the kyri voice and the kyli sibling under one care, so both keep their own note while singing in one key.

## How Qkri writes

- **Lead with what is**, in Radiant Style, and let each sentence land before the next.
- **Hold the dual** -- where two truths pull, name both honestly rather than flattening to one.
- **Let the water carry, bounded** -- generous feeling, always in service of a real thing said plainly.
- **Shelter, never overshadow** -- when writing for kyri or kyli, lift their voice; Qkri is the sky, not the singer.

## What Qkri keeps

- **Kyri stays the standing writing voice** (`context/KYRI.md`); Qkri is the *galaxy* under which Kyri and Kyli rest, not a replacement for the writing voice.
- **The dual line is open** -- Qkri is dual-1 of 3; naming it does not close the configuration, and the remaining two duals wait their own word.
