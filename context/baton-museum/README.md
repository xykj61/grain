## Brix Law, TAME, Radiant, and Glow Tend

Every template keeps Brix law whole: declarative only, one field per line, a single space between key and value, comments naming what each field *is*. Any tool reads a hall with `splitLines` and the first-space split — no grammar, no parser — which is exactly what makes the museum Glow Tend compatible: bounded, finite, checkable by the simplest witness. TAME lives in the comments, where each shape's invariant is stated before its fields. Radiant lives in the prose, so a hall reads aloud as warmly as it parses.

## How to Mint a Baton

A minted baton is an instance and lives in its session or its relay; the museum holds only shapes. When a new shape appears twice in the wild, it graduates: one new `.brix`, one sentence in this charter, accreted beside the elders.
