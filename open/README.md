## What this room is, plainly

This room holds a **vision**, marked exactly that way throughout, per the same Two Rooms
discipline that governs every other proposal in this tree. A vision page states an honest hope
and stays checkable against what has actually been proven -- which today is a teaching layer,
built on top of real research, rather than a running system. The harness name this room's
rationale leans on (Tiller) stays a candidate from that research; seating it for real is
Keaton's word alone to give.

The dated study this room teaches from lives at
[`../external-research/20260920-021139_harness-letta-open-weight-dst-alignment.md`](../external-research/20260920-021139_harness-letta-open-weight-dst-alignment.md).
That page is the source of record; this room exists to carry its findings to a reader who was
somewhere else when the research happened.

## A note on the room's own name

Under [`comlink-tendency.md`](../.claude/rules/comlink-tendency.md), a new room earns the
clearest, warmest, safest word available, at whatever length that word wants. `open/` is four
plain letters that say exactly what the room holds -- material about *open*-weight models and
*open* harnesses -- earning its place on clarity alone. It sits beside `shastra/`, `risala/`, and
`classical-vedic-astrology/` as one more plainly-named study room at the tree's root.

May whatever hand opens this room next find the door exactly as wide as this first draft left it
