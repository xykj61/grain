## The reading, in one paragraph

The frozen field already holds most of the answer to "Grain running Grain." **Pond** is the enclosure that runs one composed whole under policy, and **Puddle** is the explored layer that hosts many Ponds, each with its own Kumara key, Tally budget, and Caravan supervision. Beneath every surface sits one shape: state as a pure fold over an append-only log of signed facts, which the field calls **Weave**. That same shape is what makes TigerBeetle-style deterministic simulation natural here, because a fold over a log replays exactly. **Realidream**, drawn by **Skate**, is the surface where a person and their agents read and write that one graph, and it can grow into a free operating-system product built for the agentic age. **WASM** earns a narrow, honest role as an outward door that lets anyone verify or replay Grain's work in a browser they already have. Underneath, Rye on Zig goes all the way down, exactly as TAME Guidance asks. The heart of it all is the path the field has walked from the start: work offered as service, freedom held as a gift, and living ground honored in every choice.

## 1. Grain within Grain -- what the field already holds

### What stands

**Pond is the enclosure, and it is partly proven.** The field states the rule plainly: "Pond's enclosure aspect runs what Brix composes under policy" (`pond/README.md`). Its customs layer (place, hold, or refuse per Tilak), its enclosure policy, and a drawn terminal app all landed with witnesses. The daily seat still belongs to ai-jail, and the field names the standard Pond must meet to take it: "Pond earns it by out-refusing the jail -- witnessed, never by feature list" (`docs/ENCLOSURE.md`).

**Puddle is the layer that hosts many Grains, and it is exploration.** The study at `external-research/date/20260702/20260702-035018_puddle-sandboxed-rye-containers.md` describes it as "the layer that *hosts* many Ponds -- the way a host runs many QEMU guests, yet each guest believes it owns the machine." Each Puddle world carries a full Rye environment, a sandboxed Pond, and a Kumara keypair scoped to that world. Its state is a snapshot: the machine image plus the append-only logs that fold into it, sealed for export, verify, and restore. A demo placement algorithm already lives at `mycelium/puddle.rye`.

**Foreign kernels are guests, never mergers.** `docs/PROVEN_SEAT.md` holds the law: no Genode or seL4 code in `caravan/`, and no copyleft teacher linked into shipped Rye binaries. The one permitted link is seL4's BSD-licensed userlevel, `libsel4`, which the field compiled freestanding for riscv64 on `20260821`.

### What is proposed

Read together with the new naming of `20261015`, the stack becomes one sentence a reader can hold. **Aurora** wakes the machine. **Caravan** is the kernel, first as a root task on seL4 and later as its own microkernel, following Gall's Law. **Tally** hands out memory as bounded gardens. **Pond** encloses each application. **Puddle** hosts many whole Grain worlds side by side. Each Puddle world is itself a complete Grain, so "Grain within Grain" means one Caravan supervising Puddle worlds whose interiors are Caravan, Tally, and Pond again.

The milestone worth naming is **self-hosting**: the Rye toolchain building Grain from inside a Puddle world, and the result folding back into a resin the host can verify. The first rung is already measured. On `20261015`, a freestanding Zig RISC-V boot witness ran three times under QEMU's instruction counting and produced one identical output, while three free-running boots produced three different ones. A Puddle world run the same way inherits that determinism for free.

## 4. Deterministic simulation, after TigerBeetle

### What TigerBeetle teaches

TigerBeetle describes its simulator, the VOPR, as "a simulated environment where an entire cluster, running real code, is subjected to all kinds of network, storage and process faults, at 1000x speed," and it runs "24/7 on 1024 cores" [1]. All clusters, replicas, and clients run in one process, with the network and storage mocked. A failure found once can be replayed for debugging, because the run is deterministic from its seed [2].

### What the field already did

The field adopted TigerBeetle's lint discipline in Tally and elsewhere, and it named the VOPR "the largest structural idea not yet reflected here" (`external-research/date/20260707/20260707-053212_tigerbeetle-alignment-study.md`). A first VOPR-shaped lap already landed for one module, `linengrow/neth_sim.rye`. The open-harness research ranks the VOPR first among references and holds that "deterministic simulation testing is the honest bar for trusting an agent's own tool calls" (`open/HARNESS_RATIONALE.md`).

### What is proposed

Grain can hold determinism at two layers, and each layer catches a different kind of fault.

**The machine layer** is the one measured on `20261015`. QEMU's instruction counting fixes virtual time to the instruction stream, so the same image produces the same bytes. QEMU refuses multi-threaded translation while counting, so each such run uses one host thread, and parallelism comes from running many runs side by side.

**The protocol layer** is TigerBeetle's own. Caravan's simulator would run many Puddle worlds as simulated nodes inside one process, with Comlink's network, Tally's storage, and the clock all supplied by the simulator and steered by one seed. Comlink would meet dropped and reordered datagrams, Tally would meet torn writes, Mycelium would meet partitions, and Caravan would meet dependents that stumble mid-turn. Same seed, same bytes, every time.

The deepest reason this fits is the fold. A system whose state is a fold over a log is already a replay machine, so deterministic simulation is the same idea seen from the testing side.

## 5. The fold beneath Weave -- an evaluation

### What stands

`mantra/src/weave.rye` exists, and the Mantra and Weave sample passes seven of seven. The redraw law landed as a paper on `20260728`: a fact enters Weave, Mantra projects it, and `render(state) -> Frame` is a pure fold drawn by Brushstroke. The field is candid that today's redraw test is "a **thin stand-in** for the fold" that walks no Weave edges yet. The vision of Weave as "the append-only DAG of immutable signed facts -- the streaming graph that unifies program, page, and interface" rests in `foundations/20260826-024939_the-graph-beneath-the-surface.md`, with the engine held for later.

### The evaluation

The fold is the right unified interface for Glow, Rye, and Rishi, because it is the one shape all three already share. Glow evaluates values, Rye compiles them to the machine, and Rishi scripts them at the shell. Each can emit a signed fact into Weave and read a projection back out, and none needs to know how the others are built. The field has no single page that defines this cross-language interface yet, so here is the smallest proposed form, ready to become one single-stranded spec in the new tree:

- **One fact shape:** a Kyri value, its Kumara signature, its resin address, and the addresses of its parents.
- **One fold signature:** `fold(state, fact) -> state`, pure, bounded by Tally.
- **Projections as pure functions:** Mantra's names, Realidream's frame, and Lantern's metering all read the folded state and write nothing back.

**What would weaken this reading:** a language among the three that needs to mutate shared state outside a fact. If such a case appears, it belongs in the spec as a named exception with its reason.

## 6. Realidream and Skate as a product for the agentic age

### What stands

Realidream is "the place where you read the living web and write the living program at once, both drawn over a single graph" (`foundations/20260825-233310_realidream.md`), a far-horizon vision. Skate's return proposal names it "the **DAG rendering platform GUI**" that paints Mantra and Weave state, and the field's macOS study chose Swift with AppKit for its native shell. The web stack stays outside the home tongue: the field refuses `src/web` and holds that Realidream and Brushstroke "carry that surface rather than an HTML stack."

### The evaluation

The product shape is coherent and unusually honest for an operating system meant for working beside AI agents, and three of its properties are hard to find elsewhere.

- **Every agent action is a signed fact.** An agent working in Realidream writes into Weave like a person does, so its work can be read, replayed, and reverted, line by line.
- **Every agent runs inside limits.** Lantern serves inference inside Pond, and Tally budgets bound each agent's memory, so an agent's footprint is known before it begins.
- **Every agent session can be rehearsed.** Deterministic simulation turns "trust the agent" into "replay the agent," which is the honest bar the field's harness research named.

The open question worth naming is the living web itself. The field chose a reader over "a from-scratch engine rivaling the few the world maintains." A harmonious path is to draw Grain-native pages directly from Weave, and to let a full outside browser engine run as a **guest inside its own Puddle world**, its pixels handed to Skate as a texture. The web then arrives as a well-fenced visitor, and Grain's own surface stays Rye all the way down. The product's license follows the license fork reading of `20261015`.

## 7. The WASM question

### What stands

- Zig 0.16.0 emits `wasm32` and `wasm64` among its 58 target architectures (measured `20261015`).
- The field proved one portable seam: a receipt verifier built as a `wasm32-freestanding` guest and run under Wasmtime (`active-designing/yonder/date/20260712/20260712-063558_receipt-verify-wasm-hammock.md`).
- The surface law of `20260728` holds "**no Wasm/Web backend** for Realidream / `.brush`."
- TAME's voiced canon keeps "a **zero dependencies** policy, apart from the Zig toolchain" (`external-research/TAME_GUIDANCE.md`).
- TigerBeetle compiled its whole simulator to WebAssembly with Zig's toolchain to run it in a browser, and fit the simulated cluster under 2 GiB to suit WebAssembly's 32-bit memory [2].

### Three roles, weighed

**WASM underneath Grain** would place a virtual machine between Rye and the metal. The field's Urbit gratitude names the opposite choice as a founding difference: Grain compiles to RISC-V "rather than to a virtual machine; determinism moves from the VM into the discipline" (`gratitude/Urbit.md`). TAME's dependency rule agrees. This role stays declined.

**WASM outward** costs TAME nothing, because Zig emits it with no new dependency. A receipt anyone can verify, a simulation anyone can replay, a lesson anyone can run, all reachable from a browser a person already has. This is exactly how TigerBeetle used it: the system stays Zig, and WASM becomes a window for visitors.

**WASM inward**, hosting untrusted outside code inside Pond, would need a runtime: either an outside dependency, which TAME declines, or a Rye-written interpreter, which is large work. Puddle already offers a cleaner answer for outside code. A whole guest machine under emulation or virtualization fences visitors by the machine boundary itself, with no second runtime to trust.

### The verdict, proposed

**Rye on Zig, all the way down, for everything Grain trusts.** WASM serves as an outward door only, a build target for verification, replay, and teaching. Inward hosting rests parked until Pond and Puddle stand, and then a guest Puddle world is the first choice.

## 10. Living ground -- Atthowe ecology and biocyclic vegan practice

Helen Atthowe's *The Ecological Farm* gave the field a phrase that reads like TAME for soil: pay more attention and do less work (`gratitude/helen-atthowe.md`). The field's front door defines biocyclic vegan ecology as growing from plant matter alone, building nothing that has to harm a creature able to feel it. `foundations/20260824-003828_every-climate-has-a-fiber.md` argues that fertility raised from plants protects water, and maps a fiber for every climate, from flax and linen to hemp, jute, coir, and bamboo.

Software meets soil in small, real places already. `linengrow/retting_timer.rye` bounds a fiber-retting batch to 64 entries over an append-only log, and the field calls it "the first shippable candidate." The proposed next step joins this to section 4: a seeded simulation of a growing season, with weather and water as the injected faults, so a farm's provenance ledger is rehearsed before it is trusted.

## 11. Public money, public works, and the ecology economy

**What the field studied.** `external-research/date/20260729/20260729-231500_money-spent-into-being.md` reads Modern Monetary Theory as research for understanding: a government spends money into being and taxes it back, a job guarantee can anchor the currency's value, and inflation follows demand outrunning real capacity. The field marks this as contested economics, and so does this page. `external-research/date/20260703/20260703-043912_the-issuers-ledger.md` reads a currency as "an append-only ledger of issuer-signed marks, where balance is a fold." That is Grain's own architecture, discovered again in public finance.

**What the market shows.** Project research of `20260703` and `20260822` surveyed public companies whose revenue flows from public works: water pumps, pipes, valves, and stormwater; grid and fiber contractors; coastal restoration; and recovery lanes that turn ash into low-carbon concrete, wastewater nutrients into struvite fertilizer, and sludge into biochar. Its sharpest finding is the gap. Veganic farming, fiber building materials, and transparent civic ledgers have no public pure-plays at scale. As one project survey put it, "There is no ticker for veganic no-till." That gap is exactly where software infrastructure for ecological work belongs, and it is what Linengrow was drawn for. Every survey here is a landscape map rather than investment advice.

**Siya Solutions, as principles.** The family vehicle, now shaping as Siya Solutions on legal advice, keeps a discipline the operating system mirrors. Five gates stand before any money moves: securities counsel, a fee-only fiduciary with a written policy, the public-benefit charter, entity separation, and Keaton's word. A glass ledger lets every co-investor read a deal's books from the log. Five screens weigh each candidate: the public share of its revenue, the durability of that spending, wages over rents, circulation over extraction, and ahimsa. Figures stay with the family and its advisors.

## What stands, what is proposed, what would show this wrong

**What stands.** Pond's enclosure is partly proven; Puddle is exploration with a demo placement algorithm; foreign kernels are guests only, with `libsel4` the one permitted link. Weave exists inside Mantra with a green sample, and the redraw fold is a landed paper with a thin stand-in. A VOPR-shaped simulator landed for one module. A RISC-V boot witness runs deterministically under QEMU instruction counting. Zig emits WASM, and one receipt verifier ran as a WASM guest. TAME keeps zero dependencies beyond the Zig toolchain.

**What is proposed.** Grain within Grain as Aurora, Caravan, Tally, Pond, and Puddle nested whole, reaching self-hosting inside a Puddle world. Determinism at two layers, machine and protocol, with a harness name chosen under the Comlink tendency. One fact shape and one fold signature as the unified interface for Glow, Rye, and Rishi. Realidream and Skate as an operating system for the agentic age, with the outside web arriving as a guest in its own Puddle world. WASM as an outward door only. The international table as a reading list.

**What would show this wrong.** A Puddle world that cannot reproduce its host's output under instruction counting would move determinism back to the drawing board. A Glow, Rye, or Rishi case that must mutate shared state outside a fact would amend the one-fold interface. A browser engine that refuses to run fenced inside a guest world would reopen the question of how Realidream reads the living web. And a WASM runtime written in Rye, small enough to read in one sitting and proven by witness, would reopen the inward role on TAME's own terms.
