## What this is

This is a folder tree you can walk the way you walk a Finder window on a Mac. You open a folder,
you see what is inside, and you find your way back up. Everything you download, everything the
machine generates, and every artifact a session produces lands in one place you can name.

The tree lives under `tablecloth/`, beside the module of the same name that holds content by its
address. Here, the folders are plain and human. The documents inside them carry one-clock names, so
each file says when it arrived, even when it was renamed by a hand later.

## The first shape

```
tablecloth/
  documents/
    date/
      20261017/                  <- a day shelf: what arrived or was made that day
        20261017-144713_the-documents-filetree.md
```

Each day gets its own shelf. A file is born on its day's shelf, and it stays there. A folder you
name yourself (for example `receipts/`, `artwork/`, `from-the-bank/`) can sit beside `date/` and hold
what you choose to sort by hand. Both kinds of folder have a place, and neither takes the other's.

## Naming a file

Use the one-clock shape, `YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS_short-sprig.ext`:

- The stamp orders the file. A later stamp is a later file, in every sort order.
- The sprig says what the file is, in two or three plain words.
- The extension names the kind, so `.md` reads as a note and `.pdf` reads as a document.

A generated artifact keeps the stamp of the moment it was made. A download keeps the stamp of the
moment it arrived. The name tells you when, and the folder tells you where it sat.

## What belongs here

- **Downloads** -- files you fetched from the open web or received from a friend.
- **Generations** -- what a model or a tool wrote for you, kept whole.
- **Artifacts** -- pages, reports, and notes a session published or saved.
- **Your own writing** -- drafts and notes you keep to return to.

## What stays elsewhere

Secrets, keys, and identity files stay in their own rooms, never in this tree. Session logs stay in
`session-logs/`, and the Kyri records stay in `scribe/`. This folder is for the things you hold and
browse, and it does not hold the keys to them.
