The brief asked for a measurement before a move, and the measurement answers plainly: **keep two
rooms, and repair the boundary at three named pages.** A merge is available and costs more than it
returns.

## What each room holds, read rather than inferred

`manual/` holds **33** tracked files across four Diataxis rooms -- tutorials, guides, reference, and
a pointer at `foundations/` for understanding -- plus `grain-os/` onboarding and three video
scripts. Its centre of gravity is **your machines**: FileVault, a self-hosted VPN, macOS ai-jail,
GrapheneOS on a Pixel, key cards, first-day personal ops.

`docs-geode/` holds **42** tracked files across twelve rooms. Its centre of gravity is **the
shipping product**: the Rishi language reference, the libraries index, demos, the sangha patterns,
press, and the wiki that maps them.

**The boundary already exists in the manual's own words.** Its front matter points a reader at
`docs-geode/tutorials/the-first-hour.md` for the first hour, and its README says the manual
*"teaches what already runs"* while docs-geode ships the crushed product pages. Two audiences, two
promises, and each room states its own.

## The proposal, for Keaton's word

1. **Keep both rooms.** `manual/` is the operator's house; `docs-geode/` is the shipping shelf.
   Two audiences with two promises read better as two doors.
2. **Name the boundary in both READMEs** in one sentence each, so the next page arrives in the right
   room by reading rather than by asking.
3. **Molt the three drifted pairs one at a time**, each leaving a bannered fossil and a Class M row,
   rather than as one sweep -- three separate laps, each with its own reference repoint.

**The tension this lap created, named rather than hidden:** *Running the fleet* is operator prose,
and by the boundary above it reads manual-shaped. It sits in `docs-geode/tutorials/` because the
booking said so in Keaton's own words -- *update docs-geode with all of these operating user manual
instructions*. If the boundary is seated as proposed, that page is the first candidate to move, and
it is one file with three inbound links rather than sixty-nine.

**Falsifier:** if a reader can state the difference between the two rooms in one sentence after
reading only their front doors, the boundary is real and this proposal is right. If they cannot,
the merge is the honest answer and the 249 references are the price of saying so.
