## Lap H -- context and foundations are already separate, so nothing moves

Keaton granted, if recommended, a Brix-mutant alias placing every `context/` file in `foundations/`
as well, and later asked the sharper version: are any individual files misfiled either way, with
renames and fascia repointing allowed.

**Three independent tells agree that each file sits where it belongs.**

**By name.** `context/` holds **28** files, every one a living-named constant a lap
reads. `foundations/` holds **84**, and **81** carry a one-clock stamp. The three exceptions are the
room's own README and two civic-horizon universals whose Status lines already record the decision in
their own words: *reviewed and kept -- civic horizon under foundations; name
and path unchanged*.

**By citation.** Eleven `foundations/` briefs are cited by `.claude/rules/`, and that citation is
the design at work: [`reds-first`](../.claude/rules/reds-first.md) opens *Canonical
home: `foundations/20260729-224828_reds-first-and-the-allocation.md`*. **The rule is the law and the
foundation is its why**, the way a statute cites its preamble.

**By register.** Fourteen `context/` files are cited by no rule, which is the shape a misfile would
take -- and reading each one, all fourteen are normative or machine-read: styles, techniques,
voices, the threat model, the spellbook, three Brix data files. The one candidate,
`context/APARIGRAHA.md`, carries `Status: Living pin` and a byte bound over four named budgets,
which is guidance rather than reflection.

**So the alias is declined, and the reason is the measurement rather than the rule.**
[`alias-sameness`](../.claude/rules/alias-sameness.md) seats one referent, one implementation, and
*peers* -- and peers are what two names for **one** thing are. A hundred-odd pages duplicated into a
second room would be a second **copy**, the fault that rule exists to prevent. Two rooms already
cleanly separated stay clearest apart, and a reader keeps one place to look.

**One thing is worth recording rather than moving:** the two universals are unstamped names in a
room whose whole shape is stamped, and their exception lives only in their own Status lines. A
reader meeting `foundations/` for the first time deserves to find that named at the room's front
door, so the next person inherits it rather than re-deriving it.
