# expanded-prompts — the cold record

**Language:** EN
**Status:** Living — seated `20260810` (Keaton's word)
**Voice:** Kyri

This folder holds **cold** prompts: intents that were expanded and have come to rest — past records, kept for the memory of how the work grew. Its warm twin, [`../expanding-prompts/`](../expanding-prompts/), holds the **hot** ones: plans still active and live, in motion now.

The two are the same shape at different temperatures. A plan begins **expanding** (hot, active); when its season passes and it becomes a record rather than a direction, it may move here to **expanded** (cold, at rest). Filing by temperature, not only by stamp — a way to tell what is still being built from what is remembered.

Accrete-never-break governs the move: a cold record is never rewritten, only re-housed with its stamp intact; a dated artifact stays readable wherever it rests.

*Thank you everyone.*
